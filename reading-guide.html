YOUR READER'S GUIDE

Make yourself at home.

Everything you need to find a story, follow a series, and make the reading experience your own.

01

Find a story

Start on Home for featured picks, or visit the Library to browse the available light-novel editions by series.

02

Choose a volume

Series pages group related volumes together. Use the volume selector and previous/next navigation where available to move through a series in order.

03

Set your theme

Use the theme control in the top navigation to switch between dark and light appearance.

04

Return to a story

The home page can show recently opened stories on the same browser when reading history is available. This feature uses browser-local storage, so it may not follow you to another device or browser.

05

Read at your own pace

Some stories explore romance, loss, friendship, uncertainty, or heavier personal experiences. Check a story’s opening and context if you want to know its tone before starting.
