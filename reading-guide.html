HELP & READING INSTRUCTIONS

Using Tidewritten.

Learn how to browse the catalogue, navigate volumes, change reader settings, and return to recently opened titles.

01

Find a story

Start on Home for featured picks, or visit the Library to browse the available light-novel editions by series.

02

Choose a volume

Series pages group related volumes together. Use the volume selector and previous/next navigation where available to move through a series in order.

03

Set your theme

Use the theme control in the top navigation to switch between dark and light appearance.

04

Return to a story

The home page can show recently opened stories on the same browser when reading history is available. This feature uses browser-local storage, so it may not follow you to another device or browser.

05

Review story themes

Stories may include romance, loss, friendship, uncertainty, or other mature themes. Review the opening and available story information before reading if you want to check a title's subject matter.

For project information, visit About Tidewritten. The Creator page contains the public project details and repository link.
