Shorot aste aste shit-e porinoto holo: thanda train platform, dui haate dhora gorom drink, ar dokaner janala-te hotat scarf. Group chat assignment niye complain ar dekhte bhalo kintu khete baje emon khabarer photo-te bhore gelo.

Sota ar Airi ekhono kotha bolto. Kokhono matro duita message, kokhono aadha ghonta film, oddbhut advertisement, ba Yuto group calendar use korte shikhechhe kina ta niye. Tader bondhutto kono secret code chhilo na. Eta shudhu bondhutto, ar Sota cheshta korto etake onno kichhu howar dabi chara enjoy korte.

Tobu kichhu shondhya beshi kothin chhilo.

Ek raat-e laptop-e file khujte giye purono chithir photo pel. Hoyto bhaj korar age chhobi tulechhilo, shahosh kore lekha exact kothagulo mone rakhte. Kagoj-ta onek agei hariye gechhe, kintu photo-te tar uneven handwriting, beshi chap dewa bhaj, ar shei line chhilo—Airi-r eki rokom feel korte hobe na.

Se abar porlo. Prottekta kotha tokhon-o shotti chhilo, ekhono shotti.

Kintu arekta shotti se kokhono lekheni: Airi-r uttorer por tar moner ekta chhoto jayga khola rekhechhilo. Se Airi-r jonno opekkha korar decision neyeni, abar confession korar plan-o koreni. Shudhu nijer moner dorja bondho koreni, karon bondho kora mane golpo shuru howar agei shesh bole mene neowa.

Dui bochhor kete gechhe. Se Airi-r shathe heshechhe, assignment-e help korechhe, group outing korechhe, ar Airi-ke aro confident hote dekhechhe. Tar shathe chhoto muhurter ekta private collection-o baniyechhilo, ja eka lagle mone korto. Oi muhurto-gulor shei ortho chhilo na ja Sota gopone cheyechhilo.

Eta bujhte kosto holo, kintu Airi-r dosh chhilo na.

Se blank document khule likhlo:

Ami kokhono arekta chance chai ni. Hoyto bhoy peyechhilam je tomake oshostite felbo, ar abar eki uttor shunbo. Hoyto nijeke bolechhilam, jodi onek din opekkha kori, tahole asha ar mene neowar moddhe ekta beche nite hobe na.

Ektu theme abar likhlo:

Kintu proshno na korar mane ei noy je ami asha korini. Shudhu shei asha nijer moddhe rekhechhilam.

Se document save korlo na, pathalo-o na. Eta Airi-r jonno confession chhilo na; nijer kachhe shikar korar moto ekta shotti chhilo.

Porer shokale Yuto phone kore lunch-e jabe kina jiggesh korlo. Sota haan bollo. Tarpor Yuto tin minute dhore bujhalo keno notun restaurant-er menu “structurally suspicious”. Sota eto haslo je phone kaan theke dure nite holo.

Jibon take shadharon kaj diye jete thaklo. Se bujhte laglo, egiye jawa hoyto emoni—ekta boro decision diye noy, hajar chhoto muhurte shei din-ta bacha ja asole tar kachhe ache.

CHAPTER NINE
Bhule Bujho Na Please

Budhbar shondhay message-ta elo, group chat-e Mei-r pure jawa pancake-er photo ar Yuto-r reminder-er majhe je porer outing-er date ekhono thik hoyni.

Sota tokhoni bari phirechhilo. Bag desk-er pashe, juto ekhono pa-e, ar table-e aadha glass pani. Na bhebei Instagram khullo.

Airi Tanaka tomake message pathiyechhe.

Hey, ekta kotha bolte pari? Asha kori tumi bhul bujhbe na.

Sota boshe porlo. Obosshoi. Ki hoyechhe?

Typing-er tin dot elo, gelo, abar elo. Se dorkarer cheye beshi shomoy ogulor dike takiye roilo.

Tarpor Airi-r message elo:

Ami kichhu din dhore eta niye bhabchhi. Shudhu chai amra eki bishoy bujhi. Tomar shathe kotha bolte ar shobar shathe ghurte amar shotti bhalo lage, kintu chai na tumi kichhu bhul bujho. Amra shudhu bondhu, thik ache? Ami tomake bhul asha dite chai na.

Sota ekbar porlo, tarpor abar. Message-e kono nishthurata chhilo na, obhijog chhilo na, moja chhilo na, ba se bhul kichhu korechhe emon kothao chhilo na. Airi shabdhan hote chayechhilo. Hoyto keu abar moja korechhe, hoyto tar byabohare kichhu dekhechhe, ba hoyto shadharon bondhutto complicated howar age sposto korte cheyechhe. Sota janto na, ar karon niye nijer moto kore siddhanto nite parto na.

Se shudhu janto kothagulo tar kemon laglo.

Dui bochhor age Airi bolechhilo je Sota-r feelings-er uttor dite parbe na. Sota mene niyechhilo. Abar jiggesh koreni, raji korar cheshta koreni, onno uttor dabi koreni. Dhire dhire bondhu hoyechhilo. Sota nijeke bolechhilo bondhutto jothesto, karon se Airi-r shonggo pochhondo korto ar take oshostite felte chayni.

Se bujhte pareni je tar bhetore chhoto, na-bola ekta possibility ekhono khola chhilo.

Ebar mone holo emon ek proshner jonno ditiyo bar reject holo, je proshno se kokhono koreni.

Se phone namiye rakhlo, abar tule nilo. Emon shanto uttor dite chayechhilo ja Airi-ke guilt theke ar nijeke embarrassment theke bachabe. Prottek sentence hoy beshi thanda, noy beshi shotti mone holo.

Shesh porjonto likhlo:

Bujhte perechhi. Chinta koro na, tumi kichhu bhul koroni. Ami jani amra bondhu, ar kokhono chai ni tumi pressure feel koro. Shotti kore bolar jonno dhonnobad.

Mon diye pore send korlo.

Ek minute por Airi reply dilo: Bujhte parar jonno dhonnobad. Ami amader bondhutter shotti-i mullo dei.

Sota “bondhutto” shobdo-tar dike takiye roilo, jotokkhon na eta shudhu shobdo na theke ekta boundary hoye uthlo, ja se obosheshe sposto dekhte pachchhilo.

Phone abar vibrate korlo. Group chat active. Haru emon sandwich-er chhobi diyechhe ja majhkhane dhose gechhe. Mei bread-ke dosh dichchhe. Yuto scientific investigation-er proposal dilo.

Sota pray heshe fello. Shadharon prithibi ekhono chhilo, nijer hasyokor chhoto shomossha niye. Kintu tar bhetore kichhu chup hoye gelo—shantir moto noy, borong emon ek possibility shesh howar moto ja se kokhono karo shamne shikar koreni.

Se Airi-r upor rag koreni. Airi shotti bolechhilo. Sota tar uttor shomman korar promise korechhilo, ekhono sheta korte chay. Kosto chhilo bujhte para je se emon ek bhobishshot-er shok korchhe ja kokhono tar chhilo na.

Screen bondho kore se adho-andhokar ghore boshe roilo. Prothom bar puro disappointment-ta feel korte dilo, shonge shonge nijeke bujhiye felar cheshta na kore. Se asha korechhilo, jodio dekhayni. Tar care chhilo, jodio shabdhan chhilo. Ekhon oder majher jayga-ke emon proshno bhaba bondho korte hobe jar uttor ekdin bodle jete pare.

Baire building-er opash diye ekta train chole gelo, tar janala-gulo andhokare chhoto, durer ghorer moto shore gelo.

CHAPTER TEN
Je Mukhta Ami Kokhono Bhulini

Porer shokale Sota onno manush hoye ghum theke uthlo na.

Se ekhono bichhana theke othar age phone check korto, chabi kothay rekhechhe bhule jeto, ar shopping list-er bodole lunch niye bhabte bhabte class-er jonno bhul notebook kine felto. Nijer mukhe kokhono na bola ashar nishchup shesh-er jonno prithibi theme thakeni.

Koyek din Airi message dile se uttor dilo, kintu kotha shuru korar bahana khujte thaklo na. Take punish kora ba bhabano je she kichhu korechhe, emon kichhu noy. Shudhu nijer chinta shonar jonno jayga dorkar chhilo, prottek notification-ke chhoto test na baniye.

Ek shondhay station theke hante hante Yuto-ke shotti-ta bollo.

“O message korechhilo,” Sota bollo. “Chayechhilo ami jani je amra shudhu bondhu.”

Yuto-r mukh norom holo. “Tui ki bolechhili je aro kichhu asha korchhili?”

“Na. Prothom barer por ar jiggesh korini.”

“Tahole arekta rejection mone hocche keno?”

Sota traffic light bodlate dekhlo. “Karon mone hoy mathar moddhe ekta chance bachiye rekhechhilam. Oita niye kichhu korini. Shudhu… purota chhere dite parini.”

Yuto kichhukhon chup. “Eta kosto dey.”

“Dey.”

“Tui ki oke ghina koris?”

“Na,” Sota shonge shonge bollo. “O amake kichhu promise koreni. Prothom bar-o sposto chhilo. Mone hoy ami amar feelings theke toto-ta egiye jai ni, joto-ta bhebechhilam.”

Yuto matha narlo. Clever kono solution dilo na. Prothom barer moto dukkho-ke joke bananor cheshta korlo na.

“Ajkei bondhutto bondho korte hobe na,” se bollo. “Shudhu bondhutto-ke emon kichhu howar asha kora bondho korte hobe ja eta noy.”

Sota kothata niye bari gelo.

Ek shoptaho por se eka purono school-e phire elo. Alumni afternoon-er por classroom-ta shrine-er cheye shadharon jayga mone hoyechhilo. Se abar dekhte chayechhilo—uttor khujte noy, borong shei nijer version-ke goodbye dite je bhabto sahosh mane kauke nijer feelings bole fela.

Room faka. Desk-gulo abar sajano. Dwitiyo row-te roddur porechhilo, jekhane Airi notebook khule boshto. Sota dorjay dariye roilo, tarpor pechhone giye nijer purono seat-e boslo.

Char bhaj kora chithi mone porlo, Airi-r shabdhan uttor, ar chhoto muhurto-gulo—dhar kora note, room-er opash theke ekta hashi, science project-e angul pray chhuye jawa. Porer bochhor-gulo-o mone porlo: joke, group photo, rater proofreading, penguin plush, ar message-gulo ja tader shadharon bondhu korechhilo.

Smriti-gulo shotti chhilo. Tader ortho romantic hote hobe emon noy.

Se bhebechhilo egiye jawa mane mukh-ta jhapsha hoye jawa, smriti-ta eto halka howa je ar kosto dibe na. Hoyto etai uddeshyo chhilo na. Airi tar mone thakbe. Se chhilo kauke shotti kore bhalobashar prothom cheshtar ongsho, ar ei shikkhar-o je feelings shotti hote pare, jodio shegulo fire ashe na.

Kintu oke mone rakha mane tar jonno opekkha kora noy.

Se phone ber korlo. Group chat abar outing plan korchhilo. Mei bookstore-e jete chay, Haru kom dame khabar, Yuto emon jayga suggest korechhe ja bookstore-o noy, kom dam-o noy. Airi durer dike takiye thaka rabbit sticker pathalo.

Sota haslo. Ready hole shobar shathe jete parbe. Lukono ortho khujte na giye Airi-r shathe bhalo byabohar korte parbe. Dorkar hole ektu distance nite parbe, ar bondhutto abar shotti mone hole phire aste parbe. Egiye jete kauke villain banano ba otit-ke orthohin bola dorkar nei.

Se group chat-e likhlo:

Age bookstore. Tarpor khawa. Ar keu jodi tax document-er moto menu ache emon restaurant suggest kore, ami chole jabo.

Yuto shonge shonge reply korlo: O Haru-ke bolchhe.

Haru menu-r chhobi pathalo.

Mei likhlo: Ami tomader shobai-ke mute korchhi.

Sota heshe uthlo, ar nijer hashir shadharon shobdo shune obak holo.

Classroom chharar age se janalar dike shesh bar takalo. Ebar room-er kachhe onno ending chai na. Chithi lekha chele-ta sahoshi chhilo. Ekhon ekhane bosha jubok-o sahosh dekhate pare: uttor-ke promise bananor dabi na kore mene neowa, ar baki jibon-ke cholte dewa.

Baire dupur ujjol. School gate khola, students-ra shobdo korte korte group-e courtyard par hochchhilo. Sota pechhone na takiye gate diye ber holo.

Se Airi Tanaka-ke mone rakhbe. Hoyto onek din. Hoyto shobshomoy.

Kintu je mukh se kokhono bhuleni, tar jonno sara jibon opekkha korte hobe emon noy.

Kichhu feelings amader shathe thake. Kichhu asha chhere dite hoy. Kokhono boro howa mane je manush jibon bodle diyechhe take bhule jawa noy, borong take smriti hoye thakte dewa—nijer bhobishshot bananor dabi na kore.

Onubhuti-ta orthohin chhilo na.

Shudhu shesh hoye gechhilo.
