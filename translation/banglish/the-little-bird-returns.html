Chapter One The Girl at the Gate Again

Ek bochhor kete gelo.

Ami junior high shesh korlam, konomote entrance exam par holam, ar Seiran Academy-r high-school division-e chole elam. Classroom-gulo onno building-e chhilo, assignment-gulo aro tough hoye gelo, ar shobai future niye emon bhabe kotha bolto jeno ota ekta train—chhere jawar age jeta dhortei hobe.

Ami formula shekha ar chemistry bujhi emon bhab korar moddhe busy chhilam. Tobuo station-er pasher shoru rastata diye gele amar sei meyetar kotha mone porto, je ek shomoy ek kandhe schoolbag jhuliye amar age age haatto.

Hina-r baba-r transfer-er jonno o onno jaygay chole gechhilo. Ami or jonno bhalo cheyechhilam. Mon thekei cheyechhilam.

Tao majhe majhe school gate-er dike takano bondho korte partam na.

Tarpor August-er ekta bhyapsa shokale oke dekhte pelam.

Summer break-er por fire asha student-der bhire nicher courtyard bhore gechhilo. Entrance-er pashe umbrella theke jol porchhilo, gachhe cicada-ra shobdo korchhilo, ar ekjon teacher shobaike homeroom-er dike pathanor cheshta korchhilen. Duto chhoto student-er majhe ekta chena meye navy-blue bag-er strap thik korchhilo.

Hina.

Or chul ektu chhoto hoye gechhilo. O-ke ektu lombao lagchhilo. Ekhon o junior high-r second year-e, ar ami eki campus-er onno pashe high school-er first-year student hoye gechhi.

Amar phone-ta pray haat theke porei jachchhilo.

O fire eshechhe.

Tin din dhore upper corridor theke oke dekhechhi, kintu kotha bolar jonno kichhui korini. Nijeke bolechhi school-er building ekhon alada. Bolechhi ami boro hoyechhi, tai mature-er moto behave kora uchit. Courtyard-er dike proyojoner cheye onek beshi bar takate takate nijeke onek convincing excuse diyechhi.

Choturtho dine cafeteria amar shob excuse shesh kore dilo.

Ami line-e dariye phone scroll korchhilam, emon shomoy ekta chena gola shunlam, “Line-er dike thik kore takale taratari egobe.”

Ami matha tule takalam.

Hina lunch box hate amar pashe dariye chhilo. Or mukhe shei chena, shanto dushtumi.

“Tumi…” ami darun ekta kotha bollam.

“Ami,” o bollo. “Obak hoyechho?”

“Kobe fire ele?”

“Goto shoptaho. Bhebechhilam tumi aro taratari notice korbe.”

“Prothom din-i notice korechhilam.”

“Tahole hello bolte char din laglo?”

“Building alada.”

“Cafeteria to alada na.”

Amar ar kichhu bolar chhilo na.

O bollo, or baba-r transfer cancel hoye gechhe. Oder family abar ei area-te fire eshechhe, ar o Seiran-e abar admission niyechhe. Kothata emon shoja bhabe bollo, jeno weather-er khobor dichchhe.

“Tumi fire eshechho, eta amar bhalo lagchhe,” beshi bhabar agei bole fellam.

O kichhukhon amar dike takiye thaklo. “Tomar jonno, na amar jonno?”

“Asha kori dujoner jonnoi.”

Ebar o shotti kore hese uthlo.

Amra cafeteria-r janalar pashe boshe lunch korlam. O nijer lunch theke ek tukro tamagoyaki amake dilo, ar ami ek tukro dim-er jonno joto ta thanks dewa uchit tar cheye onek beshi grateful hoye seta nilam. Teacher, summer homework, ar nijer charao je jayga cholte thake sekhane fire ashar odd feeling niye kotha bollam.

Konodhoroner dramatic kichhu holo na.

Kintu cafeteria theke beronor shomoy campus-ta ek bochhor por abar chena chena laglo.

Chapter Two A Routine We Never Planned

Amader notun routine kono meeting ba promise charai toiri hoye gelo.

Lunch-er shomoy ami cafeteria-te jetam, Hina-o asto. Kokhono line-e dekha hoto, kokhono o friends-der shathe table theke amake dakto. Ami late korle o bolto amar timing ekdom kharap. Ar o late korle bolto “junior high-r important kaj” chhilo.

Or friend Yui shob kichhu kheyal korto.

Ekdin amake aste dekhe o jiggesh korlo, “Ei tomar senior?”

“Haruto,” Hina shudhre dilo. “Ar haan, senior.”

“Shudhu senior?”

“Ektu odd senior.”

Yui amar dike takalo. “Tomake special mone hochhe.”

“Believe me, ami mostly ordinary.”

“Odd manush thik ei kothatai bole,” Hina bollo.

Amar shondeho hote laglo, Seiran-e fire eshe o aro dangerous hoye gechhe.

Ek brishtir Thursday-te cafeteria bhir ar shobde bhore gelo. Ami duita building-er majher covered walkway-te chole gelam, earphone lagalam, ar chhader dhar diye jomte thaka jol dekhte laglam.

Keu amar kandhe tap korlo.

Hina bhaj kora umbrella hate dariye chhilo.

“Ki shunchho?”

“Emon kichhu ja manushke ignore korte help kore.”

“Tahole nishchoi bhalo.”

O amar theke ektu dure shukno ledge-e boslo. Music, or notun class, ar amar science track beche newa niye kotha holo—jodio lekhalekhi-te ami beshi bhalo, kintu mukhe nijer kotha bojhate gele atke jai.

“Tumi bhalo lekho,” o bollo.

Ami or dike ghurlam. “Tumi janle ki kore?”

“Tomar notebook dekhe. Ekdin lunch-er shomoy tumi likhchhile.”

“Tumi porechhile?”

“Shudhu je line-ta khola chhilo. Ami kono criminal na.”

Ami lojja peye hese fellam. O nijer upor besh khushi mone hochhilo.

Hawar jhapta brishti-ke chhader niche niye elo. Jol theke dure shorbar shomoy Hina-r haat amar haat chhuye gelo. Ek muhurto amra keu norlam na. Tarpor o haat shoriye amader haater dike takiye bollo, “Tomar angul thanda.”

“Brishti hochhe.”

“Gloves niye ashbe.”

“August cholchhe.”

“Tahole excuse dewa bondho koro.”

O abar brishtir dike takalo, kintu or kaner doga golapi hoye gechhilo.

Ami bhabini je etar mane nishchoi kichhu. Ami shikhechhi, asha-vora mon chhoto ekta moment-ke puro imagined future baniye felte pare.

Tobuo shei moment-ta shondhe porjonto amar mone chhilo.

Chapter Three The Little Bird in Her Sketchbook

September-er moddhe amader friendship eto normal hoye gechhilo je friends-rao ar kichhu na dekhar bhan korte parchhilo na.

Daichi jiggesh korte shuru korlo, ami prottek lunch break-e hariye jai keno. Yui Hina-ke jiggesh korlo, jodi lunch-er aadhek amakei diye debe, tahole lunch pack kore labh ki. Amra dujonei emon answer dilam ja keu bishwas korlo na.

Ekdin ami lunch ante bhule gelam. Hina kono kotha na bariye nijer sandwich aadha kore dilo. Porer din ami or jonno ekta extra canned coffee anlam. Tarpor theke jar kache ja thakto, share kora emon ekta rule hoye gelo ja keu likhe rakheni.

Ek shoptaho por amra cafeteria-r baire bench-e boshe chhilam, ar Hina chhoto sketchbook-e drawing korchhilo. O shadharonoto sketchbook-ta amar theke ghuriye rakhto, tai jokhon amar dike ghuriye dilo, ami obak holam.

Page-e ekta chhoto pakhi—sotorko, ekta dal-e boshe. Niche porishkar handwriting-e lekha: Little Bird.

Ami chhobitar dike takiye roilam.

“Oi nickname-ta mone achhe?”

“Oboshshoi.”

“Bhebechhilam tomar kharap lagte pare.”

“Odd legechhilo.” O matha kat korlo. “Kintu kokhono kharap lageni.”

“Shurute tomar naam jantam na bolei naam-ta diyechhilam.”

“Jani. Daichi aste kotha bolte khub-i kharap.”

Ami ek haat diye mukh dhaklam. “Or shathe serious kotha bolte hobe.”

“Please bolo na. O entertaining.”

O abar drawing-e mon dilo, pakhir dana-te aro kichhu shoru line jog korlo.

“Eta rekhechho keno?” ami jiggesh korlam.

“Karon eta mone koriye dey amader kotha bola prothom kivabe shuru hoyechhilo.” O ektu thamlo. “Ar naam-ta emon mone hoy, keu tomar shathe bhalo hote chay kintu nervous hoye normal kichhu bolte pare na—tokhon je naam dey.”

Ki bolbo bujhte parlam na, tai abar drawing-er dike takalam.

“Tumi amake ei naam-e dakte paro,” o jog korlo. “Kintu courtyard-er majhe dariye announce koro na.”

“Nijer upor control rakhar cheshta korbo.”

“Bhalo. Nijer reputation harate chai na.”

Shei shondhay o amake or number dilo. Bollo, dorkar hole message korte pari, tarpor jog korlo je “bore lagchhe” tao valid reason. Kintu rate or ma phone check koren, tai 11tar por message pathano jabe na.

Amader prothom conversation shuru holo ekta simple Hey diye.

Tin minute por reply elo: Likheo ki eto boring?

Porer koyek shoptaho dinner ar bedtime-er majher chhoto shomoyta amader message-e bhore uthlo. School, gaan, boi, ar classmate-der ajob kotha niye golpo hoto. 10:55-e Hina bolto, ekhon jete hobe. 10:59-e abar ekta last message pathato—kokhono joke, kokhono random sticker, kokhono shudhu Hmm.

Oi last message-tar jonno amar opekkha kora uchit-er cheye onek beshi hoye gelo.

Ek rate o jiggesh korlo, “Kokhono bhabo agami bochhor amra kemon hobo?”

Ami koyekta answer type kore shob delete korlam.

Sheshe likhlam, “Asha kori amra evabei kotha bolte parbo.”

Typing indicator elo, chole gelo, abar elo.

10:59-e reply elo.

Tahole amar kachh theke hariye jeo na, Haruto.

Ami duibar porlam.

Chapter Four The Senior Who Came to Cheer

Seiran-er sports festival elo bright banner, loudspeaker ar eto enthusiasm niye je puro campus kapte laglo.

High-school student-ra stand-er ekta section-e boshe prottek event-ke shoman support korar bhan korchhilo. Hina-r badminton match shuru hole amar bhan-ta khub ekta tiklo na.

Court-e neme Hina emon focus dekhala ja lunch-er shomoy ami khub kom dekhi. Or footwork druto, return-gulo precise, ar expression eto serious je August-e gloves niye amar shathe torko kora meyetake pray bhulei gechhilam.

O straight game-e match jite gelo.

Pore stand-er dike takale ami thumbs-up dilam. O water bottle tule chhoto salute korlo, tarpor teammate-ra tease korar agei mukh ghuriye nilo.

Teammate-ra tao oke tease korlo.

Pore Yui bollo, Hina naki baki bikel-ta hashi mukhe katiyechhe. Ami shonar bhan korlam na.

Koyek din por amar cold holo, tai lunch-er shomoy high-school classroom-ei roilam. Hina-ke message kore bollam ami thik achhi, beshirbhag ei jonno je jantam o jiggesh korbe ami kothay.

Panch minute por dorjay knock holo.

O hallway-te ekta chhoto thermos niye dariye chhilo.

“Ma extra soup baniyechhen,” o bollo. “Eta kono special delivery na. Weird kore tulbe na.”

“Ami emon kichhu korte jachchhilam na.”

“Tomar chehara dekhe to tai mone hochhe.”

O thermos-ta amar desk-e rekhe chole jete laglo. Tar niche bhaj kora ekta note chhilo.

Taratari bhalo hoye jao. Tumi na thakle cafeteria boring lage.

Ami eto jore hashlam je gal betha korte laglo.

October-e duita division mile school festival-er preparation-e help korlo. Ami decoration committee-r poster banachchhilam; Hina supply organise korchhilo ar konobhabe shobaike or plan follow korachchhilo. Class-er por amra eki hall-e kaj kortam—paper lantern, rongin string ar notun paint-er gondher majhe.

Ekjon teacher decoration-er box niye pas diye gelen. Amader dujonke dekhe nijer mone hese fellen.

Hina kheyal korlo.

“Shuru korbe na,” o amake warning dilo.

“Ami kichhu bolini.”

“Tumi jore jore bhabchhile.”

“Eta abar hoy naki?”

“Tumi korle hoy.”

Amra hese fellam, teacher-o chole gelen.

Shei bikel-e bujhlam kichhu ekta bodle gechhe. Ekhon prottek conversation-e ami bhabtam na je bhul kichhu bole fellam kina. Ami shudhu Hina-r shathe thakte partam, or golpo shunte partam, ar din-ta bhalo hote ditam—setake aro kichhu bananor pressure charai.

Festival shesh hole amra ekshathe gate porjonto haatlam. Shondher hawa thanda hoye gechhilo. Hina bag theke ekta earbud ber kore amar dike baralo.

“Share korbe?”

Ami nijer earphone-er ekta side oke dilam. Amader majhe ekta purono gaan bajte laglo, campus theke student-der beronor shobder niche shanto bhabe.

Amader kandh eke oporke chhuye chhilo.

Keu-i shore gelam na.

Chapter Five Our Kind of Normal

December-e campus-e thanda neme elo. Zelkova gachher beshirbhag pata jhore gechhilo, ar student-ra coat-er moddhe scarf guje building-er majhe taratari cholto.

Hina ar ami eke oporer diner ekta ongsho hoye gechhilam. Shomoy pele dekha kortam, na parle message ditam, ar emon chhoto chhoto kotha share kortam ja onno karo kache hoyto important mone hoto na.

Ami janlam, chinta hole o drawing kore, khub mishti drink pochhondo kore na, ar lunch-er shesh bite-ta pore khawar jonno rekhe dey. O janlo ami notes-er margin-e story likhi, forecast-e brishti thakleo umbrella bhule jai, ar moner kotha shotti kore bolte gele chup hoye jai.

Ek shondhay amra school gate-er baire dariye chhilam. Akasher rong dhire dhire winter blue hoye aschhilo.

“Agami bochhor niye tension hochhe?” o jiggesh korlo.

“Mostly exam niye. Ar exam-er por ki hobe seta niye.”

“Tumi figure out kore felbe.”

“Emon bhabe bolchho jeno tumi sure.”

“Sure na.” O scarf-ta thik korlo. “Bolchhi karon amar mone hoy tumi parbe.”

O je bhabe kothata bolechhilo, ami seta hubohu mone rakhte chailam.

Tarpor o amake bhaj kora ekta kagoj dilo.

“Bari giye khulbe,” o nirdesh dilo.

Bhetore sketchbook-er Little Bird drawing-ta chhilo, ebar puro shesh—shundor palok ar pata-bhora dal shoho. Niche o likhechhilo:

Je Little Bird-ke ekshomoy dure mone hoto, shey tar chena dal khuje peyechhe.

Dubar porar por bujhlam ami haschhi.

Porer din ami oke ekta chhoto notebook dilam. Plain blue cover-er notebook bechechhilam, karon jantam o etateo drawing korbe.

“Tomar sketch-er jonno,” bollam.

O notebook-ta hate ghuriye dekhlo. “Ar tumi eta diye ki korbe?”

“Shombhoboto writing.”

“Tahole amra page compare korte pari.”

“Challenge naki?”

“Obviously.”

Amra prothombar coincidence-er bhan na kore ekshathe gate diye ber holam. Hina amar bam dike haatchhilo. Corner-er kache o haat bariye amar coat-er sleeve duto angul diye dhorlo.

Ami theme gelam. O-o theme gelo.

Ek muhurter jonno mone holo o kichhu bolbe. Kintu tar bodole o haslo ar sleeve chhere dilo.

Chhoto ekta touch, kintu station porjonto amar shathe roilo.

Amader relation-er kono official naam chhilo na. Amra ekhono student—samne exam, family-r kotha shunte hoy, ar future ki hobe jani na. Hina junior high-r second year-e, ami high school-er first year-e. Boro howar shomoy amader chhilo; ekhono ja bujhini, tar naam taratari dewar kono dorkar chhilo na.

Kintu amader majher distance-ta ar dewal-er moto lagto na.

Shei rate 10:58-e amar phone jole uthlo.

Good night, Haruto.

Tarpor arekta message.

Ar kal umbrella bhule jeo na. Brishti hobe.

Ami aste hese phone-ta notebook-er pashe rekhe dilam.

Little Bird fire eshechhe. Amader ordinary din-gulo abar nijeder rhythm khuje peyechhe.

Shared lunch, brishtibhija corridor, ar thandar moddhe ekta earbud share korar majhe ami bujhte shuru korlam je relationship shob shomoy confession diye shuru hoy na.

Kokhono kokhono eta chupchap bere othe, jotokkhon na dujon manush nijeder ekshathe bari ferar pothe haatte dekhe.

Ekhon etai chhilo amader normal.

Ami tokhono jantam na, ordinary din kotota taratari important hoye uthte pare. Porer school year ekta alada proshno niye ashbe: Hina ar ami abar eke oporer kache fire aste parbo kina, seta noy—borong amader majhe je feelings bere uthechhe, segulo niye shotti kotha bolar moto shahosh amader hobe kina.
