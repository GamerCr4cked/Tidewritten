Chapter One The Last Year at Seiran

Seiran-e amar final year shuru holo Hina-r ekta message diye.

Tomar high school-er third year. Amar junior high-er second year. Eto important hoye jeo na, jeno amar shathe lunch-o korte na paro.

Corridor-e message-ta pore ami phone-er dike takiye idiot-er moto smile korlam.

Ek bochhor age ami shei cheleta chhilam je school gate-er dike takiye thakto ar emon conversation-er practice korto ja kokhono hoyni. Ekhon Hina amar habits, amar terrible jokes, ar shei exact time-ta janto jokhon ami weather check korte bhule jetam. Schedule allow korle amra cafeteria-te dekha kortam, ar beshirbhag raat-e or eleven o'clock limit-er kachhakachhi porjonto kotha boltam.

Kintu graduation ar dure kono idea chhilo na. Entrance exam, university application, ar hoyto onno city-te chole jawa—school year-er sheshe egulo amar jonno wait korchhilo.

Ami cheshta korlam egulo niye na bhabte.

Lunch-e Hina notun navy-blue hairband pore elo. Ami shonge shonge notice korlam, kintu kichhu bollam na.

Panch minute por o chokh chhoto kore jiggesh korlo, “Tumi notice korechhile, tai na?”

“Ki notice korlam?”

“Amar hairband.”

“Ami onek kichhui notice kori.”

“Apparently important jinisgulo na.”

Ami heshe fellam. O annoyed dekhate cheshta korchhilo, kintu or thoter konar smile shob bole dichhilo.

“Bhalo lagchhe,” shesh porjonto bollam.

“Thank you.” Or voice-ta soft hoye gelo. “Bhabchhilam tumi notice korbe ki na.”

Kothata simple chhilo, kintu ami sheta secret-er moto class porjonto shathe niye gelam.

Shei afternoon-e Daichi amake notes-er dike takiye thakte dekhlo, jodio ami ekta page-o ultaichhilam na.

“Abar or kotha bhabchhis.”

“Ami exam-er kotha bhabchhi.”

“Tahole margin-e or naam likhechhis keno?”

Ami notebook bondho kore dilam.

O hashlo, tarpor serious holo. “Shob shomoy guess korte thakte hobe na, you know?”

Ami jantam. Kintu jana ar kore fela duita alada skill.

Dui bochhore Hina ar ami eke oporer shathe comfortable thakte shikhechhilam. Amra worries share kortam, eke oporer chhoto preferences mone rakhtam, ar silence-ke excuse diye bhore na diye chup thakte ditam. Ami oke rush korte chai ni. Amader friendship-ke emon question korte chai ni jar answer oke ready howar age dite hobe.

Kintu honest na hoye graduate-o korte chai ni.

Chapter Two The Rooftop

Daichi high-school building-er pichhoner rooftop-er kotha bollo. Technically okhane jawa allowed chhilo na, jodio purono access door-ta pray unlocked thakto. Hina-ke invite korar age ami rules check korlam; amader favourite jaygata jeno trouble-er karon na hoy. Allowed thaklei amra gelam, ar edge theke dure thaklam.

Oikhan theke Seiran chhoto dekhato. Courtyard, cafeteria, gate, ar station-er pasher shoru road—shob ekta map-er part-er moto lagchhilo, jegulo diye amra hajar bar hetechi.

Hina railing-er pashe dariye late-afternoon light campus-er upor neme asha dekhchhilo.

“Jaygata bhalo,” o bollo.

“Bhebechhilam tomar bhalo lagbe.”

“Amake view dekhate enecho?”

“Mostly.”

O amar dike ghurlo. “Mostly?”

Ami nijer haater dike takalam. “Ekta quiet jaygay kotha bolte cheyechhilam.”

“Tahole bolo.”

Instruction-ta unfairly direct chhilo.

Ami rooftop-er safe side-e wall-e pith diye boslam. Ektu por Hina-o amar pashe boslo, majhe alpo space rekhe.

Amra graduation, or upcoming exam, ar ami protidin school-e na thakle campus-ta kemon different lagbe—egulo niye kotha bollam. O shikar korlo, sheo ei niye bhebechhe.

“Ami chai na shob kichhu ekshathe change hoye jak,” o bollo.

“Ami-o na.”

Sun niche namte thaklo. Ami amader majher floor-e haat rakhলাম, or haat chhuye noy.

Hina oi gap-ta dekhlo, tarpor nijer ekta finger amar haater pashe halka kore rakhlo.

Gesture-ta eto chhoto chhilo je onno keu miss korte parto. Ami korini.

Amra kichhu bollam na. Sky amber theke pink, tarpor pale evening blue hoye gelo.

Shiri diye namte namte o eto kachhe hatchhilo je amader sleeve eke oporke brush korchhilo.

Door-er kachhe o amar dike takalo. “Amake abar ekhane invite korte paro.”

“Really?”

“Erokom question korte thakle mind change kore felte pari.”

Ami heshe fellam, o-o hashlo.

Shei shondhay bujhlam future ekhono amake bhoy dekhay—but khali bole noy. Bhoy lagto karon kichhu manush ar jayga chhilo jegulo ami shathe niye jete chai, kintu kivabe korbo ekhono jantam na.

Chapter Three Things We Had Not Said

Rooftop-er por amader friendship overnight change hoyni. Shudhu ektu beshi honest hoye uthlo.

Amra ekhono music ar cafeteria food niye argue kortam. Ami overthink korle Hina amake strange bolto, ar ami oke mone koriye ditam je o-i ekta bird draw kore nijer naam-e naam diyechhilo. Kintu majhe majhe amader chokh mile gele keu shonge shonge chokh soratam na.

Ek shondhay phone-e amra everything and nothing niye kotha bolchhilam. Teacher onek homework diyechhen; Yui notun hobby shuru korechhe; ar Daichi emon kharap joke bolechhe je shesh porjonto funny hoye gechhe.

Sheshmesh Hina jiggesh korlo, “Amader first time kotha bolar kotha mone ache?”

“Gate-er kachhe.”

“Tomake terrified lagchhilo.”

“Intelligent kichhu bhabar cheshta korchhilam.”

“Kaj hoyechhilo?”

“Clearly na.”

O heshe uthlo, tarpor or voice ektu quiet hoye gelo.

“Ami-o nervous chhilam, you know.”

Ami soja hoye boslam. “Tumi?”

“Of course. Tumi ar tomar friends-ra shob shomoy edike takate. Bujhte partam na tomra amake niye hashchho ki na.”

“Ami kokhono tomake niye hashi ni.”

“Ekhon jani.”

Amar friends-ra jokhon beshi loud hoye jeto, shei shomoygulor jonno sorry bollam. O bollo, o kokhono chayni ami oder shathe friendship chhere di—shudhu mone rakhte cheyechhilo je o chay na shobai oke niye joke koruk.

“Amar agei bujhe jawa uchit chhilo,” bollam.

“Tumi bujhechhile. Tumi nijer behaviour change korechho.”

Eta amar kachhe praise-er cheye beshi matter korto.

October-e school festival abar elo. Hina poetry reading-e participate korlo. Hall-er light-er niche du-haate paper niye dariye or voice steady rakhlo. Porar shomoy shudhu ekbar chokh tule audience-er opare amar shathe chokh melalo.

Pore ami corridor-e wait korlam.

“Poem-ta ki karo niye chhilo?” jiggesh korlam.

“Maybe.”

“Emon keu, jake ami chini?”

“Emon keu, je age khub quiet chhilo.”

“Eta toh je keu hote pare.”

“Tahole guess korte thako.”

Amar reply-er agei o chole gelo.

Ami or pichhone douralam na. Shikhchhilam je protita question-er answer shonge shonge dorkar hoy na. Kokhono keu tomake ektu truth dey, ar shobcheye kind response holo sheta carefully dhore rakha.

Winter kachhe ele permission thakle amra rooftop-e aro beshi dekha kortam. University, ki shikhte chai, ar kon jayga dekhte chai—egulo niye kotha boltam. Thanda barle Hina or scarf amar shathe share korto; ami vending machine theke hot drinks antam.

Ek scarf-er niche amader shoulder touch korto.

Ar pretend korte parchhilam na je amar feelings shudhu temporary fascination. Ekta real friendship-er shathe shathe ogulo aste aste grow korechhilo. Ami Hina-ke ekjon real person hisebe chintam—not a mystery, not amar banano kono story, borong emon ekjon je amar shathe disagree korte pare, amake surprise dite pare, ar nijer path nije choose korte pare.

Ei jonnoi ami oke truth-ta bolte cheyechhilam.

Chapter Four A Notebook for Two

Winter break-er ager shesh school day-ta cold wind ar station-er pasher stall theke asha roasted sweet potato-r gondho niye elo.

Lunch-e Hina amake chhoto ekta wrapped package dilo.

“Ekhane khulbe na,” o bollo.

“Ami khulte jachchhilam na.”

“Tumi definitely khulte jachchhile.”

Bari pouchhano porjonto wait korlam.

Bhitor-e handmade cover-er ekta notebook chhilo, jar upor branch-e bosha tiny bird-er design. First page-e Hina-r careful handwriting-e lekha:

Tumi prothom manush je ei school-ta-ke ektu less ordinary korechhile.

Baki page-gulo blank chhilo. Back cover-er bhitore ekta chhoto note chhilo.

Story, thought, ja khushi lekho. First line ami likhechhi, karon kauke na kauke toh shuru korte hoto.

Ami koyekbar pore fellam.

New Year's Eve-e amader family midnight-er preparation korchhilo, ar amra phone-e kotha bolchhilam. 11:55-e Hina jiggesh korlo amar resolution ki.

“Exam pass kora.”

“Boring.”

“Umbrella bhule jawa bondho kora.”

“Impossible.”

“Tomar shathe kotha bolte thaka.”

Ekta pause holo.

“Eta bhalo,” o bollo.

Minute-gulo kete gelo. Baire dure fireworks-er shobdo holo. Amra eksathe countdown korlam, dujonei taratari bolchhilam ar count hariye fele hashchhilam.

Midnight-e Hina bollo, “Happy New Year, Haruto.”

“Happy New Year.”

“Ar… tomake ekta kotha janate chai.”

Ami wait korlam.

“Tumi amar kachhe matter koro. Ami shadharonoto jotota bolte pari, tar cheye beshi.”

Amar gola tight hoye elo. “Tumi-o amar kachhe matter koro.”

Amra taratari aro kichhu jog korlam na. Silence-ta full chhilo, empty na.

January-te rooftop-e ami or deya notebook-e likhchhilam, ar Hina nijer book-e sketch korchhilo.

“Ek minute move koro na,” o bollo.

“Keno?”

“Karon ami tomake draw korchhi.”

Ami emon freeze hoye gelam je o heshe uthlo.

O focus kore draw korte thaklo, majhe majhe amar face ar page-er dike takalo. Shesh-e sketch-ta dekhale koyekta careful line-e amake dekhlam—amar messy hair, chokher shape, ar chhoto ekta smile ja amar nijer-o kheyal chhilo na.

“Amake amar cheyeo kinder baniyechho,” bollam.

“Tumi nijeke jotota bhabo, tar cheye beshi kind.”

Ki reply debo bujhlam na, tai notebook-e likhlam:

Kokhono onno keu tomar bhalo dik-ta age dekhe, tar age je tumi nijey dekhte shekho.

Hina amar shoulder-er upor diye pore nilo.

“Bhalo line,” o bollo.

“Cheshta korbo jeno mathay na chore.”

“Too late.”

Amra heshe fellam. Baire winter sky pale ar wide chhilo. Notebook-ta amader majhe pore chhilo, ar first page-gulo ar blank chhilo na.

Chapter Five The Truth on the Page

February elo exam revision, cold morning, ar Valentine's Day decoration niye ja mone holo raatarati appear korechhe.

Ami grand gift kinini. Ami likhlam.

Hina-r notebook-er porer blank page-e ami shei chhoto jinisgulo likhlam ja amar kachhe precious hoye uthchhilo: or details mone rakha, rainy lunch break-ke warm kore tola, ar joke kora easy holeo truth bola.

Tarpor sheta likhlam ja bolte bhoy pachchhilam.

Hina, I like you. Tumi-ke chenar age nickname diyechhilam bole na, ar protita glance-er meaning thakuk boleo na. Ami shei manush-ta-ke pochhondo kori jake ami ekhon chini. Tumi amar kachhe answer dite obligated feel koro bole ami chai na. Ami shudhu honest hote cheyechhilam.

Class-er por notebook-ta oke ferot dilam.

O rooftop-e page-ta porlo. Ami wait korlam, possible ending niye bar bar bhaba bondho korar cheshta korte korte.

Pora shesh hole o notebook-ta coat-er shathe chepe dhorlo.

“Tumi shotti onek time niyechho,” o bollo.

“I know.”

“Bhabchhilam kokhon bolbe.”

Ami takalam. “Tumi bhabchhile?”

O matha narlo, ekhon ektu shy. “Amaro tomake bhalo lage, Haruto. Ami shudhu cheyechhilam amra bujhe ni, amra ki bolchhi. Baki shobai protita jinis-er label dite chay bole rush korte chai ni.”

Amar bhitore je relief elo, sheta eto quiet chhilo je prothome calm bole bhul korechhilam.

“Tahole… ekhon ki hobe?” jiggesh korlam.

“Amra nijeder motoi thakbo.” O smile korlo. “Ar hoyto ekta earphone share korake accident bole pretend kora bondho korbo.”

Ami heshe fellam. O-o hashlo, ar amader majher tension-ta soft hoye gelo.

Kono audience chhilo na, announcement chhilo na, dramatic music-o na. Shudhu winter coat pora dujon student, majhe ekta notebook, ar ekta truth jar jonno thik word khujte bochhor legechhe.

Amra railing-er pashe dariye chhilam, haat kachhakachhi. Eibar or haat dhorar age jiggesh korlam.

“May I?”

Hina matha narlo.

Or haat amar haater bhitore warm chhilo.

Chapter Six Growing Up

March elo exam schedule ar ei uneasy feeling niye je protita ordinary day ekhon countdown hoye gechhe.

Hina-r nijer test chhilo. Amar university entrance exam. Lunch-e ager moto oto dekha hoto na, kintu message choltei thaklo: exam-er age good-luck note, pore complaint, vending machine-er ridiculous drink-er photo, ar midnight-er age ghumate jawar reminder.

Physics exam diye beriye amar mone holo ami jibone ja shikhechhi shob bhule gechhi. School building-er pichhone ekta bench-e Hina amar jonno wait korchhilo.

O exam kemon hoyechhe jiggesh korlo na. Amar pashe boshe silence-ta settle hote dilo.

Kichhukhon por o haat bariye dilo.

Ami dhore nilam.

“Bad?” o jiggesh korlo.

“Very.”

“Tahole ajker din-ta bad chhilo. Tar mane ei na je er porer protita din-o emon hobe.”

Ami or dike takalam. “Tumi kothata simple kore dao.”

“Simple na. Ami shudhu ekhane achhi.”

Amra kichhukhon boshe roilam, school wall-er opash theke traffic-er shobdo shunte shunte. Or haat amar haate roilo—not a promise je kichhu change hobe na, borong proof je protita difficult jinis amake eka face korte hobe na.

Exam shesh hole shei question-ta samne elo ja amra avoid korchhilam: university-r jonno ami kothay jabo?

Same city-te ekta engineering programme-e amar admission holo, train-e pray forty minute dure. Prothom jokhon chinta shuru korechhilam, future-ta emon bhabini, kintu ekhon ei future-ta imagine korte parchhilam.

Hina amar kotha shunlo.

“Tahole tumi ekhono nearby thakbe,” o bollo.

“Visit korte parbo emon nearby.”

“Good.” O smile korlo. “Onno city-te giyeo tomake umbrella bhulte debo na.”

Amra heshe fellam, kintu or voice-e relief shunte pachchhilam. Amar-o eki rokom lagchhilo.

Boro howa mane kichhu change hobe na emon promise noy. Fear-ke decision nite dewar age change niye kotha bola shekha. Hina-r nijer studies, friends, family ar plans chhilo. Amar-o chhilo. Shob shomoy eki jaygay, eki shomoy thaka hobe na.

Kintu amra important kotha bolte shikhechhilam.

Eta ekta beginning, guarantee noy. Etai enough chhilo.

Chapter Seven Where Our Story Begins

Seiran-er final day-ta elo ekta clear evening sky-r niche.

Amar friends-ra courtyard-e photo tulchhilo. Teachers-ra last-minute advice dichchhilen. Jara bochhorer por bochhor school-er complaint korechhe, tara ekhon classroom-er pashe emon bhabe dariye chhilo jeno protita wall mone rakhte chay.

Chole jawar age amar ekta jaygay jawa dorkar chhilo.

Rooftop.

Campus khali hote shuru korar por Hina amar shathe okhane dekha korlo. Late sunlight railing-ke gold kore diyechhilo. Niche courtyard—jekhane ami oke prothom notice korechhilam; cafeteria—jekhane o amar excuse challenge korechhilo; ar gate—jekhane o ekdin jiggesh korechhilo, o kachhe ele ami chup hoye jai keno.

Tin bochhorer ordinary moment-gulo jeno oi view-er moddhe dhore gelo.

“Bhebechhilam tumi ekhanei thakbe,” o bollo.

“Hope korechhilam tumi ashbe.”

O amar pashe daralo. Amader haat railing-e chhilo, kachhakachhi kintu touch korchhilo na.

“Bhoy lagchhe?” o jiggesh korlo.

“Ektu.”

“Amaro.”

Ami or dike ghurlam. “Next year exactly kemon hobe jani na. Promise korte parbo na je amader konodin difficult day ashbe na.”

“Promise korle ami believe kortam na.”

“Kintu ami eta choose korte thakte chai. Busy thakleo kotha bolte. Worry hole nijer mathar moddhe hariye na giye tomake bolte.”

Hina onekkhon amar dike takiye roilo. Tarpor amar haat dhorlo.

“Ami-o etai chai.”

Sun rooftop-er opare neme gelo. Sky gold theke orange, tarpor soft pink howa porjonto amra chup kore dariye roilam. Amar shei purono cheletar kotha mone holo—je dure theke takato ar bhabto feeling kokhono mukhe na bolle safe thake.

Ami ekhono nervous chhilam. Ekhono shob answer jantam na.

Kintu next step newar age shob answer jana ar dorkar chhilo na.

Hina aloto kore or forehead amar forehead-e thekiye dilo. Amra ek muhurter jonno chokh bondho korlam, diner shesh alo fade howar shathe oi quiet moment share kore.

O pichhiye ele smile korchhilo.

“Eta niye likhle beshi dramatic kore likho na,” o bollo.

“Kono promise nei.”

“Haruto.”

“Fine. Try korbo.”

Amra eksathe rooftop theke beriye gate-er dike gelam. Shei jaygay, jekhane amader story ekta nervous question diye shuru hoyechhilo, alada pothe jawar age thamlam.

Hina or street-er dike ghurlo. Ami station-er dike.

Koyek pa jawar por amra dujone eki shomoy pichhone takalam.

O heshe uthlo. Ami-o.

Tarpor o wave korlo, ami-o back wave korlam.

Ami oke Little Bird bolechhilam karon dure theke dekhe feelings-ta dhore rakhar onno upay jantam na. Ekhon ami or naam, laugh, stubbornness, quiet kindness, ar Seiran-er boundary-r baire niye jawa dreams jantam.

O kokhono amar imagination-er chhilo na. O shob shomoy nijer chhilo.

Ar somehow, tin bochhorer chhoto conversation, awkward silence, rainy afternoon, shared song, ar shesh porjonto bola kothar por, o amar pashe haatar choice korechhilo.

School year shesh holo. Amader life change hote thakbe, ar shamner protita din easy hobe—emon promise keu korte parto na.

Kintu amra honestly kotha bola, eke oporke nijer moto thakte dewa, ar time-ke freeze korar pretend na kore eke oporke choose kora shikhechhilam.

Amader story continue korbe shei ordinary day-gulote jegulo amra ekhono banchini. Eta shei chapter-er end ja shuru korte ami ekshomoy bhoy petam—and Seiran-er oi bochhor-gulor complete ending, ja amake kono idea-ke noy, ekjon real person-ke bhalobashte shikhiyechhe.
