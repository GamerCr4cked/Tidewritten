Chapter One Bhul Flat

Aarav Mehta duita suitcase, ekta backpack ar ekta plan niye B-204-e pouchhalo: Delhi-te tar prothom raat-ta kono kichu na kore katabe. She freelance designer. Kaj kokhono ekshathe onek ashe, kokhono abar ekdom thake na; taka ashar nijer alada, onishchit calendar ache. Apartment niye tar dorkar chilo matro tin-ta jinis—ekta desk, reliable Wi-Fi, ar bhabar moto jothesto shanti. Broker Pandey take nishchit korechilo je flat-ta ekdom perfect. “Chinta korben na, sir.” Aarav-er tokhoni shondeho kora uchit chilo. Keu jiggesh korar agei jodi bole chinta korar kichu nei, tahole tar upor bhorsha kora kothin. She dorja khule bag-gulo namiye rakhlo, tarpor bedroom-e ekta pink suitcase dekhte pelo. Thik tokhoni main door khule gelo. Ekjon toruni grocery-r bag niye bhitore dhuklo. Aarav-ke dekhei she theme gelo. “Tumi ke?” “Ami ekhane thaki.” “Na, tumi thako na.” Dujone nijeder rental agreement miliye dekhlo. Flat ek, move-in date ek, broker-o ek. Duita security deposit ekoi protarona-r moddhe hariye geche. Owner phone dhorlo na. Pandey-r phone switched off. Raat ghoniye ashchilo, ar hotel-e thakar moto extra taka dujoner karo kache chilo na. Meyeta nijer naam bollo—Zara Khan. She junior architect, shifting korte korte klanto, ar nijer barite hotat dhuke pora ek ochena cheler proti bhalo howar moto mejaj tar chilo na. Aarav-o khub ekta alada kichu feel korchilo na. Room, chabi ar ke age esheche—ei shob niye dujone torko korlo. Sheshe practical kothatai jite gelo: owner-er shathe jogajog na howa porjonto flat-ta share korte hobe, ar shob kichur record rakhte hobe. Ora ekta kagoj-e niyom likhe fridge-e lagiye dilo. Onnojoner room-e dhokar age knock korte hobe. Ja use korbe, sheta replace korte hobe. Na jiggesh kore kichu borrow kora jabe na. Raat barotar por work call-e gola nichu rakhte hobe. Zara aro ekta niyom jog korlo. “Bondhu howar natok korbo na.” “Easy,” Aarav bollo. Etai chilo tader prothom promise—jeta rakha dujoner jonnoi kothin hote jachchilo.

Chapter Two Niyom Ar Chhoto Chhar

Prothom shoptaho-ta chilo ghorer kaj niye chola ekta cold war. Aarav tar shelf-e label lagiye dilo. Zara-o tar shelf-e. Ora pala kore kitchen porishkar korto, jeno kono border niye diplomat-ra negotiation korche. Kotha-barta beshir bhag-i grocery ar Wi-Fi router niye reminder-er moddhe shimaboddho chilo. Tarpor chhoto chhoto ghotona tader majhe dhuke porte laglo. Zara ekta building model niye raat porjonto kaj korte korte khete bhule gelo. Aarav tar dorjar shamne bhat ar shobji rekhe dilo. Tarpor message pathalo je extra khabar noshto hoto. Porer shokale Zara thank you bollo, kintu ekta kothao bollo na je she shobta kheye felechilo. Ekdin Aarav puro shondha ekta jedi design file thik korar cheshta korchilo. Zara raat duitar shomoy tar dorjar niche diye alo berote dekhe cha-er mug niye knock korlo. “Eta kono solution na,” she bollo. “Shudhu cha.” “Cha shadharonoto evabei kaj kore.” Zara pray heshe felchilo. Dhire dhire ora ekjon arekjoner routine bujhte shikhlo. Zara mon diye kaj korar shomoy aste aste gun-gun korto. Client-er reply-er opekkhay Aarav room-er moddhe paychari korto. Zara janala khola rakhte pochondo korto; Aarav fan chaito. Temperature niye torko hoto, tarpor ora bujhte parlo dinner-er por dujonei beshi reasonable hoye jay. Shesh porjonto owner reply dilo. Totodine flat-ta ar ager moto oshohay ba oshostikor lagchilo na. Aarav Zara-r bhari model-ta tar studio-te niye jete help korlo. Zara Aarav-er portfolio dekhe bollo, tar shera design-gulo shei gulo ja she nijer jonno baniyechilo—sei client-der jonno na, jara prottekta idea-ke shudhu expensive dekhate chay. “Tumi bhalo kaj koro,” she bollo. “Kintu lokjon tomake ja banate bole, tar arale tumi nijeke lukiye rakho.” Zara room theke chole jawar por-o kothata Aarav-er mone roye gelo. Ora ekhono nijeder temporary roommate bole porichoy dito. Kintu Aarav jokhon bari fire dekhlo Zara ekta khub baje cooking video dekhe hashche, tar nijer-o ichchha holo nijer diner golpo take bolte. Fridge-e niyom-gulo ekhono lagano chilo. Shudhu byatikrom-gulo berei cholchilo.

Chapter Three Hote Hote Hoyni

Koyek mash kete gelo. Aarav-er kaj aro stable holo, ar Zara architecture firm-e beshi responsibility pete laglo. Chhoto chhoto success ora balcony-te takeaway kheye celebrate korto. Aarav Zara-ke presentation-er jonno ready hote help korlo. Zara take ekta difficult client contract negotiate korte help korlo. Ekjoner din kharap gele arekjoner jana thakto—advice dite hobe, cha banate hobe, na ki chup kore pashe boshe thakte hobe. Ek shondhay power chole gelo, ar baire shohorer alo flat-ta alokito kore rakhlo. Zara sofa-y Aarav-er pashe boshe tar purono design-er folder dekhchilo. “Onno keu eta kinbe ki na, sheta niye bhabar age jokhon tumi kichu banate, tokhon tomake beshi khushi lagto,” she bollo. “Rent artistic fulfilment diye deya jay na.” “Na, kintu tar jonno nijer pochonder shob kichu hariye felao uchit na.” Aarav uttor dite ghurlo. Zara tar dharona-r cheye onek kache chilo. Ek muhurter jonno keu norlo na. Tader majher nistobdhotay chilo shei shob kotha, ja ora kokhono mukhe aneni. Tarpor Zara uthe daralo. “Amar presentation-ta shesh korte hobe.” “Achha, thik ache.” Dujone emon bhab korlo jeno kichui hoyni. Ek shoptaho por Zara tar confident colleague Dev-er shathe dekha kora shuru korlo. Dev janto kivabe kono room-e dhuklei shobar attention nijer dike ante hoy. Aarav supportive thakar cheshta korlo. Nijeke bojhalo, mon kharap korar kono odhikar tar nei; Zara tar kache koto ta important, sheta she kokhono bolei ni. Dev flat-e asha shuru korlo. Shobar shamne she charming chilo, kintu kichu chhoto byapar Aarav-ke bhabato: Dev Zara-r kaj-ke chhoto kore dekhto, tar shomoy-ke nijer shompotti mone korto, ar Zara emon kichur jonno-o sorry bolto ja tar dosh chilo na. Aarav nijer chinta nijer moddhei rakhlo. She jealous roommate hote chayni, je nijer feelings-kei shotti bole dhore ney. Shesh porjonto Zara jokhon jiggesh korlo Dev-er bepare Aarav ki bhabe, she bhebe-chinte uttor dilo. “Amar mone hoy tumi emon ekjon-ke deserve koro, jar shathe moter omil hole tomake guilty feel korte hobe na.” Zara onno dike takalo. “Ami jani, o perfect na.” “Ami-o na. Kintu kothata sheta na.” Prothombar Zara bujhte parlo, Aarav torke jitte chaichilo na. She Zara-r jonno chintito chilo.

Chapter Four Onno Cheleta

Shotti-ta ek Brihoshpotibar raat-e shamne elo. Zara deri kore bari firlo, bag-ta mejhete chhere dilo, ar light na jaliye kitchen table-e boshe porlo. Aarav pani nite eshe take okhane dekhlo. Zara jante perechilo Dev onno karo shatheo relationship-e chilo. She jokhon Dev-ke shamna-shamni jiggesh korlo, Dev ulto Zara-ke dosh dilo—busy thakar jonno, beshi kaj korar jonno, take jothesto attention na deyar jonno. Zara relationship chhere jawar kotha bolar por-i she sorry bollo. Aarav shob shunlo. Tar ichchha korchilo bolte je age thekei kichu ekta bhul mone hochchilo. Kintu tar bodole she jiggesh korlo Zara chay she pashe thakuk, na ki take eka thakte dik. “Thako,” Zara bollo. Aarav cha banalo. Kitchen-er ghori modhyorat periye gelo, tarpor-o Zara kotha bole gelo. Aarav nijer feelings-er kotha bollo na—Zara jokhon aghat peyeche ar ki ghotechhe sheta bujhar cheshta korche, tokhon na. Porer koyek dine Dev flat-e ja ja rekhe gechilo, shegulo ferot dite Aarav Zara-ke help korlo. She mone koriye dilo, relationship shesh howa mane failure na. Zara dhire dhire abar nijer moto hoye uthlo. She beshi hashte laglo, ar proti koyek minute por por phone check na kore kaj korte laglo. Tarpor ek shondhay Zara jiggesh korlo Aarav kokhono karo shathe date koreni keno. “Jani na,” Aarav bollo. “Mone hoy ami chai na keu amake shudhu convenient bole beche nik.” Zara tar dike takiye roilo. “Tumi ki bhabo amader moddheo tai hoyeche? Amra shudhu ekjon arekjoner jonno convenient?” “Na. Amar mone hoy ekshathe thakar karone comfort-ke onno kichu bhebe fela shohoj hoye geche.” “Ar tumi ki feel koro?” Aarav chaile moja kore kothata eriye jete parto. Kintu ebar she shotti bollo: she Zara-r care kore, kintu kono uttor dabi korche na. Dev take koshto diyeche bole Zara jeno shudhu take beche na ney—shetao she chayni. Zara-r chokh norom hoye elo. “Ami ki feel kori, sheta bujhte amar ektu shomoy lagbe.” “Nao.” Prothombar ora porer chapter-ta jor kore shuru korte chailo na.

Chapter Five Alada Rasta

Shomoy-er shathe uttor-ta aro porishkar holo, kintu Aarav mone mone je uttor asha korechilo, sheta na. Zara take bollo she tar khub care kore. Aarav flat-e thakto bolei flat-ta tar bari hoye uthechilo. She Aarav-ke bhorsha korto, shomman korto, ar tar pashe nijeke safe mone korto. Kintu she shotti kore bolte parto na je she Aarav-ke bhalobashe. “Ami amader moddhe ja ache, sheta harate chai na,” she bollo. “Kintu bhan-o korte chai na.” Aarav-er mon kharap holo. She feeling-ta thakte dilo, kintu sheta diye Zara-ke shasti dilo na. “Tahole bhan korbo na.” Ora thik korlo lease shesh hole ekjon move korbe. Karon keu kichu bhul koreche bole na, borong eki arrangement-e thakle nijeder alada jibon gora aro kothin hoye jachchilo. November-e shifting holo. Zara office-er kache ekta chhoto apartment pelo. Aarav tar boi ar gachh pack korte help korlo. Flat-e tader shesh dinner-ta mejhete holo, karon table-ta agei niye jawa hoyechilo. Dorjar kache Zara take joriye dhorlo. “Thank you. Tumi kokhono amake emon feel koroni je tomar kache amar kichu dhar ache,” she bollo. Aarav age theke bhebe rakha kothagulo gile niye shotti uttor dilo. “Thank you, tumi shotti-ta bolechho.” Ora bondhu roye gelo, jodio kotha-barta dhire dhire kome elo. Pore Zara abar dating shuru korlo. Aarav nijer kaj-e mon dilo, portfolio aro strong korlo, ar nijer prothom boro branding award jite nilo. Koyek mash por Zara phone korlo. She notun kore single hoyeche, ar bhabchilo ora abar cheshta korte pare ki na. Aarav shanto bhabe tar kotha shunlo. She bujhte parlo, Zara ekdin take beche nibe ki na—ei proshne she ar fire jete chay na. Obosheshe she emon ekta jibon goreche, jeta thik mone howar jonno onno karo approval dorkar hoy na. “Ami tomar care kori,” she bollo, “kintu amar mone hoy na abar ager jaygay fire jawa amar jonno thik hobe.” Phone-er opashe nistobdhota. Tarpor Zara bollo she bujhte pereche. Call shesh kore Aarav cha banalo ar balcony-te giye daralo. November-er roddur tar notun desk ar shelf-er upor rakha framed award-er upor porchilo. She eka chilo, kintu ekhon eka thaka mane tar jibone kichu ekta kom ache—emon proman mone hoto na. Flat-ta tader jibon-ke age ar porer duita bhage vag kore diyechilo. Bishshash-bhangar ghotona Zara-ke shikhiechilo she ar ki mene nebe na. Ar shei adhoora relationship Aarav-ke shikhiechilo, kauke bhalobasha ba care korar mane holo na je she tomake beche nebe bole ononto kaal opekkha korte hobe. She shei ending-ta payni, ja ekshomoy cheyechilo. Kintu emon kichu peyechilo ja tar dorkar chilo: nijeke. Kichu manush tomar jibone thakar jonno ashe. Keu keu tomake bujhte shahajjo kore tumi nijer jaygay kothay acho. Ar kokhono kokhono shobcheye shotti ending protishodh na, abar mile jawa-o na—borong kono tiktota bohon na kore shamne egiye jawar shanto siddhanto. Ekta flat. Dujon manush. Duita alada future. Ar shesh porjonto, dujonei nijeder jiboner malik.
