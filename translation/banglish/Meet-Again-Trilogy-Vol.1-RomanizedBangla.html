Chapter One The Empty Seat

Akito Mori-r college-e tike thakar ekta fixed system chhilo: shomoy-er age pouchhano, pichhoner dike ekta seat bechhe neowa, earphone lagiye rakha, ar dorkar chara karo shathe kotha na bola.

Atharo bochhor dhore ei system kaj korechhilo.

Tarpor Seihoku College-er orientation day-te ekta meye deri kore elo. Or iced coffee-r ordhekta pore gechhilo, ar mukhe emon ekta expression chhilo jeno shokal-er shathe or agei ekta jhogra hoye gechhe.

Auditorium-er shob seat bhora chhilo—shudhu Akito-r pasher seat-ta khali.

“Ei seat-ta khali?” meye-ta aste kore jiggesh korlo.

Akito matha narlo.

“Darun. Ami jodi ghumie pori, important part-er age amake jagie dio.”

“Important part-ta ki, jano?”

“Na. Tai to tomake lagbe.”

Or naam chhilo Mio Tachibana. Dean-er speech-er majhe majhe o fisfis kore comment korte laglo, auditorium-er kharap air-conditioning niye ekta serious announcement dilo, ar kono ekbhabe dui ghontar orientation-ta Akito-r mone rakhar moto kore tullo.

Program shesh hole Akito earphone nite haat baralo.

“Ami lunch korte jacchi,” Mio bollo. “Chaile tumi-o aste paro.”

Or shadharon uttor hoto na. Room unpack korte hobe, schedule guchhate hobe. Eka khete or kono oshubidha chhilo na.

Kintu mukh diye ber holo, “Thik ache.”

Mio emon bhabe hashlo jeno o age thekei janto Akito raji hobe.

Cafeteria-te onek shorogol, curry motamuti, ar Mio dujoner hoyei kotha bollo. O jiggesh korlo Akito kotha theke eshechhe, ki pore, ar o ki shobshomoy emon dekhay jeno jekono shomoy room theke beriye jabe.

“Ami emon dekhai na.”

“Ekdom emon-i dekhay.”

Akito nijeke thamate na pere hese phello.

Tokhon-o o janto na, kintu oder moddhe shobkichu jotil hoye gele ei hashi-tai or mone pora chhoto chhoto smritir ekta hoye thakbe.

Chapter Two A Habit Called Mio

Mio porer din-o elo. Tar porer din-o.

Prothom shoptaho shesh hote hote Akito-r pasher seat-ta unofficially or hoye gelo. Akito age ele pasher chair-e bag rekhe dito. Mio age ele emon confidence niye haat nere dakto jeno nijer barite table reserve korchhe.

Bar bar dekha hote hote oder bondhutto bere uthlo. Lunch protidiner routine hoye gelo. Ora eksathe porto, lecturer-der niye complain korto, ar library theke ferar shomoy lomba rasta dhorto, karon Mio-r dabi chhilo garden-er kachher vending machine-er drink beshi bhalo.

Mio machine-er shob drink try korto. Akito khet black coffee.

“Tumi protibar eki jinis order koro,” Mio bollo.

“Karon ami jani eta amar bhalo lage.”

“Baki-gulo bhalo lagbe na, seta ki kore jano?”

“Amar chokh ache.”

“Professor Mori, taste evabe kaj kore na.”

Rate message ashte shuru korlo: Mio-r noshto hoye jawa instant noodles-er chhobi, assignment-er proshno, ba majhrate kono explanation chara pathano meme. Akito nijer ojantei phone check korte laglo.

Or roommate Kenta eta kheyal korlo.

“Aajkal tomake khub available mone hoy.”

“Ami message-er reply dei.”

“Age tumi message-ke tax office-er chithi mone korte.”

Akito or dike ekta pillow chhure marlo.

O etake obhyash bolto. Shobcheye shubidhar ekta shobdo. Obhyash hole oke bhabte hoto na, Mio-r hashi ki kore kharap din-tao bhalo kore dey, ba o na thakle chupchap cafeteria-ta eto faka lage keno.

Tokhono o bujhte pareni, ekta routine aste aste tomar jibone kono manusher jonno alada jayga hoye uthte pare.

Chapter Three Best Friends, Apparently

Prothom semester-er shesh dike Seihoku-r shobai Akito ar Mio-ke ekshathe dekhte obhyosto hoye gechhilo.

Ora dating korchhilo na. Dating niye kothao boleni. Tobe lecture hall-er onno pranto theke ekbar chokhachokhi kore puro conversation bujhe felar skill oder hoye gechhilo.

Mio student card bhule gele Akito gate-e wait korto. Akito report shesh korte giye lunch skip korle Mio extra rice ball niye hajir hoto, ar or mukher expression dekhe mone hoto torko korar kono sujog nei.

“Shudhu coffee kheye tumi bachte parbe na,” o bolto.

“Cheshta kore dekhte pari.”

“Fail-o korte paro.”

Oder bondhura eto sabdhani chhilo na, karon shobkichui oder kache sposto chhilo.

Kenta Akito-ke jiggesh korlo, o ki Mio-ke biye korar plan korchhe, naki porer char bochhor shudhu or pasher seat reserve kore rakhbe.

Mio-r bondhu Yuna aro kharap. “Tomra hoy secretly relationship-e acho, noyto prithibir shobcheye slow dujon.”

“Amra friend,” Mio uttor dilo.

O kothata halka bhabei bollo. Akito kheyal korlo, bolar por ek second-er jonno o tar dike takiyechhilo.

Shei rate Akito ceiling-er dike takiye muhurto-ta niye bhabte laglo. Emon shob detail o kheyal korte shuru korechhilo jegulo mone rakhar kono karon chhilo na: mon diye kichhu korar shomoy Mio-r kaner pichhone chul guje deowa, shotti chintito hole chup hoye jawa, ar shobshomoy jiggesh kora o kheyechhe ki na.

Upolobdhita dhire dhire elo, tarpor ek muhurte shob clear hoye gelo.

Mio-ke or bhalo lage.

Shudhu moja kora classmate hisebe na. Shudhu convenient routine hisebe na. Or bhalo lage shei manush-ta-ke, je or pasher khali seat-e eshe boshechhilo ar or guchhano din-gulo-ke aro jibonto kore tulechhilo.

Shomossha holo, oder bondhutto or kache khub dami hoye gechhilo. Nijer feelings-er naam diye shob bodle felte o bhoy peto.

Chapter Four The Space Between Us

Spring elo, campus-er rastar dhare cherry blossom phute uthlo. Lecture-er por Mio Akito-ke baire tene niye gelo. O ghoshona korlo, je manush protita roder dupur ghore katiye dey, tar biruddhe complaint kora uchit.

Ora phule bhora ekta gachher niche boshe convenience store theke kena sweets-er packet share korlo.

“Ekta kotha jiggesh kori?” Mio bollo.

“Ki jiggesh korbe tar upor depend kore.”

“College shesh hole ki hobe, kokhono bhabo?”

“Job. Rent. Hoyto aro coffee.”

“Ami serious.”

“Ami-o. Coffee-r dam beshi.”

Mio or kandhe halka dhakka dilo, kintu beshikkhon hashlo na. O bollo graduation-er por shobai je jar moto chole jabe bhebe or bhoy lage; eki schedule ghire gore otha bondhutto schedule bodle gele hoyto hariye jabe.

Akito oke bolte chailo je o Mio-ke harate chay na. Kothagulo uthlo, gola porjonto elo, tarpor theme gelo.

“Manush cheshta korle jogajog rekhe jete pare,” o shesh porjonto bollo.

Mio or dike takalo. “Tumi korbe?”

“Korbo.”

Eta confession chhilo na. Or feelings bojhanor jonno jothesto-o chhilo na. Kintu Mio-r mukh norom hoye elo, ar o gachher gaye helan diye boslo.

“Bhalo. Ami-o korbo.”

Muhurto-ta koyek shoptaho dhore Akito-r mone roye gelo.

O odd lagar moto na hoye aro jotnoshil howar cheshta korlo. Class eksathe shesh hole Mio-ke station porjonto niye jeto. Or pochonder drink mone rakhto. Majhe majhe age message pathato, bolar moto khub smart kichhu na thakleo.

Mio bodol-ta kheyal korlo. Ek thanda shondhay Akito oke gorom canned drink dile o prothome drink-er dike, tarpor Akito-r dike takalo.

“Aajkal tumi besh thoughtful hoye jaccho.”

“Gujob chhorio na.”

“Ekhon deri hoye gechhe. Shobaike bolbo.”

O hashlo, kintu or chokhe jeno ekta proshno-o chhilo.

Prothombar Akito bhablo, Mio-o ki or mukh theke kichhu shonar opekkhay ache?

Chapter Five Say It Before the Train

Prothom bochhor-ta grishmer halka brishtir moddhe shesh holo. Exam shesh, dormitory-r hallway-te suitcase dekha jacchhe, ar student-ra bari ferar plan korchhe.

Mio porer shokale chole jabe. Or poribar koyekta prefecture dure thake, tai chhutite ora koyek shoptaho alada thakbe.

Packing shesh kore ora station-er baire dekha korlo. Brishti tokhon halka jhirijhiri. Mio-r hate transparent umbrella; Akito Kenta-r kachh theke umbrella dhar niyechhe.

“Tomake dekhe mone hochhe speech dite jaccho,” Mio bollo.

“Amake bola hoyeche amar aro kotha bola uchit.”

“Ke bolechhe?”

“Shobai.”

“Buddhiman manush.”

Akito hese uthlo, tarpor chup hoye gelo. Platform theke train-er announcement bhese elo.

“Mio, tomake amar bhalo lage.”

Kothagulo khub shundor kore bola holo na. Taratari beriye elo, jeno or sahosh shesh hoye jawar age bole felte hobe.

Mio-r hashi theme gelo—mon kharap hoye noy, borong o thik shunechhe ki na seta bojhar cheshtay.

“Tomake amar bhalo lage,” o abar bollo. “Friend-er cheye beshi. Ekhoni uttor dite hobe na. Shudhu tomake na janiye chole jete dite chaini.”

Koyek second umbrella-r upor brishtir halka shobdo holo.

Tarpor Mio chokh namalo, eto aste hashlo je Akito pray kheyal-i korto na.

“Akito, tomar shotti onek deri hoye gelo.”

Or buk dhok kore uthlo. “Eta ki kharap kichhu?”

“Na.” O mukh tule takalo. “Eta khub bhalo kichhu.”

Mio bollo, or-o Akito-ke bhalo lage. Age bole dite bhoy lagchhilo—jodi oder bondhutto-te aro kichhu shudhu o-i kolpona kore thake.

Train eshe gelo. Ora hotat kore onno rokom manush hoye gelo na. Ekhono awkward, ekhono eke opor-ke niye moja kore, ar relationship shuru korar niyom-o jane na.

Kintu Mio train-e othar shomoy dorjar kachhe fire bollo,

“Aaj rate phone korbe.”

“Korbo.”

“Kal-o.”

“Thik ache.”

“Ar bhule gechho emon bhan korbe na.”

“Korbo na.”

Dorja bondho holo. Train Mio-ke niye chole gelo, ar Akito platform-e dariye roilo—jama-r hata brishtite bhija, mukhe lukono jay na emon ekta hashi.

Chapter Six Meet Again

Oder relationship kono announcement charai shuru holo.

Na kono dramatic post, na cafeteria-te kono declaration. Oder bondhura bujhte parlo jokhon summer break-er por Mio fire eshe soja Akito-r pasher seat-e boslo, tarpor table-er niche or haat dhorlo—jeno etai prithibir shobcheye shadharon byapar.

Kenta-r cha pray golay atke gelo.

“Tomra eto din dhore dating korchhile?”

“Puro shomoy na,” Akito bollo.

Mio matha kat kore bollo, “Tumi kobe theke count korchho tar upor depend kore.”

Oder din-gulo aro ushno hoye uthlo, kintu perfect holo na. Music, study schedule, ar Akito-r coffee-ke thikmoto drink bola jay ki na—egulo niye ekhono motbirodh hoto. Protita misunderstanding-ke competition baniye na fele sorry bola ora shikhte laglo.

Ek dupure brishti oder cafeteria-te atke dilo. Mio janala diye pani goriye pora dekhchhilo.

“Baire jabe?” o jiggesh korlo.

“Brishti hochhe.”

“Bah, ki darun observation.”

Pach minute pore ora courtyard-e khola akasher niche hashchhilo. Mio dui haat chhoriye ekbar ghure daralo, ar Akito oke emon ekta oshohay bhalobashar chokhe dekhlo, ja o ar lukiye rakhar cheshta korchhilo na.

Mio or kache fire elo. Or chule brishti, chokhe hashi.

“Shei prothom din amar shathe lunch-e eshechhile bole ekhono khushi?”

“Ha,” o bollo. “Khub.”

Mio aste kore nijer kopal or kopale thekiye dilo. Brishti oder charpashe porte thaklo, ar campus-ta rupali kuashar moddhe jhapsa hoye gelo.

Prothom bochhor shesh hole grishmo oder abar alada shohore niye jabe. Ora janto eta kothin hobe. Etaw janto je ora cheshta korte chay.

Station-e Mio-r train ashte dekhe o haat narlo.

“Taratari dekha hobe,” o bollo.

Akito hashlo. “Phir milenge.”

Ora abar dekha korbe.

Tokhono ora keu janto na, pore ei kothagulo koto boro ortho niye ashbe—ba durotto-r karone shadharon shotto kothao bola koto kothin hoye uthte pare.
