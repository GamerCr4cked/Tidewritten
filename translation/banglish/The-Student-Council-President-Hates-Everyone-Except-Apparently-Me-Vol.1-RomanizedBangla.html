“Mone hoy ami eta chai,” o bollo.

Chapter TenThe Truth About Breathing

Kaito reply dilo: Plan chhilo na.

Good.

Hanako message-ta duibar porlo. Shudhu jodi tumi chao.

I do.

Shei shondhay Kaito janalo doctors treatment plan-er ekta part adjust korechhen, ar symptoms kharap hole prescribed instruction follow korte bolechhen. Shob detail share korlo na; Hanako-o demand korlo na.

Dujone station-er pashe bench-e boshe evening commuter dekhchhilo, jokhon Kaito kiss-er kotha tullo.

Hanako-r drink pray pore jachchhilo. “Eta keno tulchho?”

“Karon kotha bola uchit.”

“Fine. Bolo.”

Kaito bollo kiss tar lungs thik koreni. Oshustho hole medical help dorkar, kiss na. Hanako chokh namiye bollo, “Obviously.”

“Ami tomar kachhe thakte cheyechhilam. Honest answer etai.”

Hanako still hoye gelo.

Kaito bollo o nervous chhilo, feelings explain korte parto na—kokhono ekhono pare na. Kintu Hanako jeno bhabe na je take rescue kora tar responsibility, ba Kaito-r kachhe thaka mane control-er baire jinis-er responsibility newa.

Baire city evening light-er streak hoye pichhone chole jachchhilo. Prothom bar clever kichhu bolar dorkar mone holo na.

Chapter ElevenThe Debt That Didn't Exist

Hiro take, door-ke, abar take dekhlo. “Sure.”

Chapter TwelveStay With Me

Monday shokal ekta broken pen diye shuru holo. Hanako student council-er jonno note likhte giye dekhe pen kaj korchhe na. Dui bar click korlo, jeno formal warning-e pen shune felbe. Shunlo na. Beshi press korte tip-ta snap kore gelo.

This is ridiculous.

Dui din dhore she Saturday niye bhabchhilo—not café, walk, ba Kaito-r thank you. Tar mone porchhilo Kaito bolechhilo je she ar fine thakar pretend korte chay na. Hanako tar honest life-er part hote chayechhilo—not nurse, not rescuer, ar emon keu-o na jar pashe thakar jonno useful howa dorkar.

Shudhu nijer moto.

Lunch-e she courtyard-e Kaito-ke khuje bollo, “Amar shathe esho.”

“Private jaygay.”

Silence.

Kotha beriye gechhe. Ar ferot neya jabe na.

AfterwordA Small Thing, Repeated

Kichhu golpo grand gesture-er jonno mone thake: brishtite dramatic confession, fireworks-er niche promise, ba kichhu na bola rekhe dite na pere puro city cross kore asha.

Ei golpo-teo shei feelings ache, kintu shobcheye important gesture-gulo chhoto.

Keu pore jawar age haat bariye dewa.

Emon apology ja forgiveness demand kore na.

Answer assume na kore question kora.

Shei friend je jane kokhon joke bondho korte hoy.

Shei chele je onno keu notice korar age bolte shekhe, “I'm not feeling well.”

Ar desk-e coffee-r can rekhe dewa.

Hanako quiet person hoye jayni. Kaito-o protita feeling perfectly explain korte shekhini. Dating shuru korlei oder problem gayeb hoyni, ar affection life-er difficult part theke immune koreni.

Change hoyechhilo ora pretend kora bondho korte shikhchhilo.

Hanako shikhlo control na kore care kora jay.

Kaito shikhlo care accept korar bodole kauke nijer puro life-er debt dite hoy na.

Coffee can, argument ar broken pen-er majhe ora bujhte parlo, understood hote hole perfectly understood howa dorkar nei.

Kokhono shudhu onno ekjon-ke try korar chance dewa enough.

Oder golper beginning shetai.

Kiss-er moment na.

Confession-er moment-o na.

Shei moment jokhon ora eke oporer hoye decide kora bondho korlo je ke ki handle korte parbe—and eke opor-ke nijeder choice korte dilo.

One can of coffee. One honest answer. One day at a time.

Shuru korar jonno etai enough chhilo.

— Kaito

Koyek mash por Kaito-r mother visit-e eshe drawer-er pichhone rakha ekta purono photograph-er kachhe theme gelen. Ek muhurter jonno tar expression bodle gelo.

She janto na, je past niye kokhono proshno koreni, shetai fire ashar pothe.
