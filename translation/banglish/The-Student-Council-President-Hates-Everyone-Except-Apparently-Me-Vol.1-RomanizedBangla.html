Ora heshe felllo.

Tarpor Kaito bollo, “Keu kokhono amake jiggesh koreni boshe thakte chai ki na, emon bhabe jeno ami kichhu fail korini.”

O bollo kono din easy, kono din na. Onkdin dhore difficult day-gulo nei emon act korechhe.

“Ami ar emon korte chai na,” o bollo. “Tomar shathe na.”

Hanako bollo, “Good.”

Kaito jiggesh korlo bas etai? Hanako bollo take speech invent korte bolbe na.

Tarpor she truth-ta bollo: “Record-er jonno, amar jonno worry korar debt tomar nei.”

“Tumi bolechhile ache.”

“Jhoot bolechhilam.”

“Disappointing.”

“You're welcome.”

Hanako tar dike takiye roilo. She ekhono oi words bolar jonno ready chhilo na ja koyek shoptaho dhore avoid korchhilo. Kintu ager moto bhoy-o lagchhilo na.

Chapter TwelveStay With Me

Monday shokal ekta broken pen diye shuru holo. Hanako student council-er jonno note likhte giye dekhe pen kaj korchhe na. Dui bar click korlo, jeno formal warning-e pen shune felbe. Shunlo na. Beshi press korte tip-ta snap kore gelo.

Hanako pen-er dike, tarpor classroom-er opare Kaito-r dike takalo. Kaito Hiro-r shathe kotha bolte bolte hashchhilo.

This is ridiculous.

Dui din dhore she Saturday niye bhabchhilo—not café, walk, ba Kaito-r thank you. Tar mone porchhilo Kaito bolechhilo je she ar fine thakar pretend korte chay na. Hanako tar honest life-er part hote chayechhilo—not nurse, not rescuer, ar emon keu-o na jar pashe thakar jonno useful howa dorkar.

Shudhu nijer moto.

Lunch-e she courtyard-e Kaito-ke khuje bollo, “Amar shathe esho.”

“Kothay?”

“Private jaygay.”

Kaito-r eyebrow uthe gelo. Hanako-r face warm holo. “Oi meaning na! Kotha bolar jayga!”

“Ami kichhu boli ni.”

“Tumi bhabchhile.”

“Na.”

“Liar.”

She take school building-er quiet side-e niye gelo. Koyek second arms fold kore pavement-er dike takiye roilo. Kaito wait korlo—tar shobcheye irritating habit.

“Kaito.”

“Yes?”

“Ami bhabchhilam.”

“Sounds serious.”

“Serious.”

Hanako breath nilo. “I like you.”

Awaaz khub quiet chhilo. Tai abar bollo, “Ami bolechhi I like you.”

“Shunechhi.”

“Tahole answer korchho na keno?”

“Interrupt korte chai ni.”

Hanako emon chokhe takalo jeno kichhu chhure marbe. Kaito chup thakai bhalo mone korlo.

Hanako bollo, o Kaito-ke pochhondo kore, kintu Kaito frustrating, jinis lukiye rakhe, take worry koray, ar tar expectation moto react kore na. She jane tar problem she fix korte parbe na, tar health-er responsibility tar noy, ar Kaito jeno tar jonno fine thakar acting na kore.

“Kintu ami pashe thakte chai. Truth shunte chai, difficult holeo. Tumi kotha bolte na chaileo pashe boshe thakte chai. Coffee khete, stupid jinis niye argue korte ar—”

She theme gelo.

Kaito-r expression soft hoye gelo, ar shob aro difficult laglo.

Hanako chokh soralo. “So stay with me.”

Silence.

“Shudhu aaj na. Amar mane—”

She chokh bondho korlo. “Let's be together.”

Kotha beriye gechhe. Ar ferot neya jabe na.

Kaito take emon bhabe dekhchhilo jeno she precious kichhu diyechhe ar o bhoy pachchhe fele debe.

“Say something!” Hanako bollo.

Kaito breath nilo. “I like you, too.”

Hanako takiye roilo. “Tahole oi face keno?”

“Karon bhabini tumi age bolbe.”

“Keno?”

“Tumi usually amake jinis chhure marar threat dao.”

“That's not an answer!”

Kaito aste heshe serious holo. “Ami tomar shathe thakte chai. Kintu tomake nijer moddhe dhukte dewa shikhte hobe. Kokhono bhul korbo.”

“Amio.”

Kaito chay na Hanako tar jonno responsible feel koruk. Hanako bollo, “Tomar responsibility hote chai na. Tomar shathe thakte chai.”

Kaito matha narlo. “I think I can do that.”

Hanako take hug korlo. Hug-ta awkward chhilo: she khub tight kore dhorlo, Kaito return korar age hesitate korlo, dujoner-i haat kothay rakhbe bujhte oshubidha holo.

Perfect chhilo—not graceful bole na, borong dujone pretend koreni je eta onno kichhu.

Alada hoye Hanako jiggesh korlo, “Can I kiss you?”

Kaito smile korlo. “Yes.”

Oder second kiss first-er cheye gentle chhilo. Kichhu solve korlo na, protita din easy hobe emon promise-o dilo na. Shudhu emon kichhur beginning mark korlo ja dujone choose korechhe.

Hanako briefly forehead tar forehead-e rakhlo. “Kauke bolle ami shob deny korbo.”

“Understood.”

“Smile kora bondho koro.”

“Try korchhi.”

“Harder try koro.”

Kaito heshe uthlo. Hanako-o smile korlo. Tarpor tar haat dhore dujone classroom-er dike gelo.

Future ki anbe tara janto na. Kintu eibar keu pretend korchhilo na je shob eka face korte hobe.

EpilogueOne Can, Two Straws, and a Broken Pen

Oder relationship-er first week surprisingly ordinary chhilo.

Kaito ekhono umbrella ante bhule jeto.

Hanako ekhono tar handwriting niye complain korto.

Hanako spare umbrella carry korte shuru korechhilo—obviously weather-related risk management-er jonno. Je sheta shob shomoy Kaito-r preferred type chhilo, sheta coincidence; ei niye kotha bolte she raji chhilo na.

Hiro 48 hours-er moddhe truth discover kore emon react korlo jeno romance-ta she personally orchestrate korechhe.

“I knew it!”

Kichhu jinis bodlechhilo. Kaito rest dorkar hole bolte shikhchhilo, automatically fine bolar bodole. Kokhono worry hole withdraw korto; Hanako tar silence-ke rejection bhabto; Kaito kokhono Hanako-r anger-ke chole jawar signal bhabto.

“Don't.”

World choltei thaklo.

Kokhono question kora ar answer accept kora.

Kokhono theke jawa.

Hanako box-er dike takalo. “Tumi eta forever rakhte parbe na.”

“Keno na?”

“Embarrassing.”

“Tai toh rakhbo.”

Hanako chokh chhoto korlo. “I hate you.”

Kaito smile korlo. “No, you don't.”

“Reassuring na.”

AfterwordA Small Thing, Repeated

Kichhu golpo grand gesture-er jonno mone thake: brishtite dramatic confession, fireworks-er niche promise, ba kichhu na bola rekhe dite na pere puro city cross kore asha.

Ei golpo-teo shei feelings ache, kintu shobcheye important gesture-gulo chhoto.

Keu pore jawar age haat bariye dewa.

Emon apology ja forgiveness demand kore na.

Answer assume na kore question kora.

Shei friend je jane kokhon joke bondho korte hoy.

Shei chele je onno keu notice korar age bolte shekhe, “I'm not feeling well.”

Ar desk-e coffee-r can rekhe dewa.

Hanako quiet person hoye jayni. Kaito-o protita feeling perfectly explain korte shekhini. Dating shuru korlei oder problem gayeb hoyni, ar affection life-er difficult part theke immune koreni.

Change hoyechhilo ora pretend kora bondho korte shikhchhilo.

Hanako shikhlo control na kore care kora jay.

Kaito shikhlo care accept korar bodole kauke nijer puro life-er debt dite hoy na.

Coffee can, argument ar broken pen-er majhe ora bujhte parlo, understood hote hole perfectly understood howa dorkar nei.

Kokhono shudhu onno ekjon-ke try korar chance dewa enough.

Oder golper beginning shetai.

Kiss-er moment na.

Confession-er moment-o na.

Shei moment jokhon ora eke oporer hoye decide kora bondho korlo je ke ki handle korte parbe—and eke opor-ke nijeder choice korte dilo.

One can of coffee. One honest answer. One day at a time.

Shuru korar jonno etai enough chhilo.

— Kaito

She janto na, je past niye kokhono proshno koreni, shetai fire ashar pothe.
