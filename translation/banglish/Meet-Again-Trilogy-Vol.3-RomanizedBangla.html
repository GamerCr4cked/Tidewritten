Chapter One Colours of Silence

Shei rate shobkichhu bhenge porar dui bochhor por Akito Mori emon ekta jibon gore tulechhilo, ja bhalo bhabei cholchhilo.

O degree shesh korechhilo, onno ekta shohore chole gechhilo, ar chhoto ekta photography studio-te internship peyechhilo. Or room-ta shadharon chhilo: ekta bed, ekta desk, camera bag, ar boi-r ekta shelf—jegulo porar ichchhe chhilo. Rent deowa ar camera-r film kenar moto taka o rojgar korto. Kenta-r shathe pray prottek shoptaho kotha hoto.

Or photography series, *Colours of Silence*, chupchap popular hoye uthchhilo. Khali station, bondho classroom, brishtir por ekta bench. O emon jaygar chhobi tul to, ja dekhe mone hoto keu jeno ekhoni sekhan theke chole gechhe.

Manush chhobi-guloke shundor bolto. Akito proshongsha grohon korto, kintu edit korar shomoy or mone koto bar ekta nirdishto mukh bhese uthto, seta bojhato na.

Mio-o graduation shesh korechhilo. O onno ekta shohorer chhoto publishing company-te junior editor hisebe kaj korto. Age-r cheye ekhon kotha bolar shomoy beshi bhebe nito, ar shikhechhilo je nijeke doshi mone kora ar nijer bhul shudhre neowa ek jinis noy.

O Akito-r shathe jogajog koreni.

Tarpor oder purono college town-er ekta gallery theke invitation elo. Curator *Colours of Silence* series theke koyekta chhobi group exhibition-er jonno bechhe niyechhilo.

Akito pray na bolei diyechhilo. Kenta oke bollo, past-er shathe jukto protita jayga eriye jawa ar shamne egiye jawa ek kotha noy.

Tai o gelo.

Gallery-ta alokito, bhir-bhara, ar charpashe manusher kotha cholchhilo. Akito dewal-er kachhe dariye chhilo, jotokkhon na nijer ekta chhobi-r shamne porichito ekta manush-ke dekhlo.

Mio.

O sheet-er khali gachher niche ekta bench-er chhobi dekhchhilo. Akito chhobi-ta oder shesh kothabartar onek pore tulechhilo, kintu jayga-ta shei jayga jekhane ekta kharap diner por ora eksathe boshechhilo.

Caption-e lekha chhilo: *Jekhane shuru. Jekhane shesh.*

Mio onekkhon dhore chhobi-ta dekhlo. Tarpor fire takate oder chokh mile gelo.

Koyek muhurto dujoner keu norlo na.

Tarpor Akito or dike egiye gelo.

“Hello, Mio.”

“Hello, Akito.”

Dui bochhorer na-pathano message, afsos, ar mone mone toiri kora kothabarta chhoto charta shadharon shobde eshe theme gelo.

“Kemon acho?” o jiggesh korlo.

“Kaj korchhi. Chhobi tulchhi.”

“Ami online-e series-ta dekhechhi. Khub shundor.”

“Thank you.”

O jiggesh korlo na chhobi-gulo Mio-ke niye ki na. Akito-o jiggesh korlo na o keno eshechhe. Koyek minute ora kaj, gallery, ar shohor-ta niye kotha bollo—jeta oder bhabnar cheye kom bodlechhilo.

Alada howar shomoy Akito-r mon kapchhilo, kintu o bhenge poreni.

Past oder ekta room-e abar ene dari koriechhilo. Prothombar, dujoner keu seta notun kore likhe felar cheshta koreni.

Chapter Two The Apology That Owed Nothing

Porer din Mio ekta message pathalo.

*Kal amar shathe kotha bolar jonno dhonnobad. Ami bar bar contact korbo na. Shudhu bolte cheyechhilam, tomake bhalo thakte dekhe amar bhalo legechhe.*

Akito message-ta porlo, kichhukhon bhablo, tarpor reply dilo: *Tomakeo bhalo thakte dekhe amar bhalo lagchhe.*

Kothabarta-ta chhoto chhilo. Eta oder relationship abar shuru koreni. Shudhu oder ei shotto-ta mene newar sujog dilo je ora dujon-i jiboner shobcheye kharap muhurto periye beche thaka manush.

Ek shoptaho por Akito ekta print nite gallery-te fire gelo. Mio-o sekhane chhilo, ek colleague-er shathe arekta event-er prostuti nichhilo. Shesh porjonto ora eksathe station-er dike hete gelo.

Mio jiggesh korlo, o ki ekta thikthak apology shunte raji hobe—khoma pabe bole asha kore noy, borong ager moto osposto kothar pichhone ar lukiye thakte chay na bole.

Akito bhebe dekhlo.

“Thik ache,” o bollo. “Kintu tarpor amar kemon lagbe, seta promise korte parbo na.”

“Ami bujhi.”

Ora station-er kachher shanto ekta café-te boslo. Mio kono ghuriye na bole shotti-ta bollo. Notun keu oke chaichhe—ei onubhuti or bhalo legechhilo. Akito-ke haranor bhoy peyechhilo bole relationship-er shachchhondo-o rakhte cheyechhilo, abar Ryo-r attention-er uttejonatao. O onek kichhu lukiyechhilo, Akito-r chinta-ke urie diyechhilo, ar shotto ar lukiye rakha na gele tobei sorry bolechhilo.

“Ami nijeke bar bar bolechhi je ami confused,” o bollo. “Ami confused chhilam, kintu ami siddhanto-o niyechhi. Ami tomake koshto diyechhi, ar tumi eta deserve koro ni.”

Akito kono badha na diye shunlo.

“Amar kharap lagchhe bole ami tomake amake trust korte bolte pari na,” o abar bollo. “Ami jani amar siddhanto-r porinam amake mene nite hobe.”

Kichhukhon Akito coffee theke uthe asha bhaap-er dike takiye roilo.

“Obosheshe sposto kore bolar jonno dhonnobad,” o uttor dilo. “Ami bishwas kori tumi eta mon theke bolchho. Kintu tar mane ei noy je amra ager moto hoye jete parbo.”

“Ami jani.”

Ora café theke alada alada ber holo.

Akito-r hotat kono halka mone holo na, past-ke ar koshto dewar moto na mone howar kono cinema-r moto muhurto-o elo na. Kintu apology-ta oke shantona dite ba Mio-r guilt muche dite boleni. Eta shotto-ta-ke tar thik naam diyechhilo.

Etar gurutto chhilo.

Chapter Three Rebuilding Without Shortcuts

Porer dekha-ta chhilo hotat kore, tarpor arekta dekha holo nijeder ichchhay.

Ora majhe majhe shadharon bishoy niye message korte laglo: Akito-r exhibition-e rakha ekta chhobi, Mio-r edit kora ekta boi, ba dujoner mone thaka ekta café. Keu taratari reply dewar dabi korto na. Karor space dorkar hole, sheta bole dito.

Akito kheyal rakhto jeno bhalo byabohar-ke promise na bhabe. Mio-o kheyal rakhto jeno jogajog-ke khoma peye jawa na bhabe.

Prothome oder kothabarta formal chhilo. Dhire dhire aro shabhabik hoye uthlo. Ora abar mone korte parlo eksathe hashte kemon lage, jodio shei hashi ar eke opor-er somporke jana kothin shotto-gulo muche dito na.

Ek shondhay Mio jiggesh korlo, Akito-r chhoto studio-ta dekhte aste pare ki na. Akito raji holo, ar notun series-er jonno toiri kora print-gulo oke dekhlo.

O ekta khali room-er upor diye chole jawa roder chhobi-r shamne theme gelo.

“Eta alada rokom lagchhe,” o bollo.

“Kibhabe?”

“Emon mone hochhe na je keu ekhoni chole gechhe. Mone hochhe keu hoyto ashte jacchhe.”

Akito chhobi-ta abar dekhlo.

“Ami evabe bhabini.”

“Hoyto tai amar eta bhalo legechhe.”

O hese phello. Nijeke defend na kore kono bishoy niye kotha bolata je or koto mone porechhilo, seta bujhte pere obak holo.

Ora chay bolei trust fire asheni. Chhoto, chokhe porar moto siddhanto theke eta bere uthlo. Mio shotto bolto, emonki tate oke kharap dekhaleo. Akito oshosti lagle seta bolto, shob thik ache emon bhan na kore. Ora boundary niye kotha bolto, seguloke shasti hisebe na dekhe.

Kichhu din purono koshto abar fire asto. Tokhon ora sheta hariye gechhe emon bhan korto na. Ora aste hoto, shotto kore kotha bolto, ar jor kore kono uttor ber na kore onubhuti-ta kete jete dito.

Abar gore tola ekta bhanga vase jora dewar moto kom, borong je mati ekbar nore gechhe, tar upor notun bari tola jabe ki na ta shekhar moto beshi chhilo.

Dujoner keu janto na er shesh kothay.

Prothombar, na jana mane paliye jawa chhilo na.

Chapter Four A Visit in Winter

January-te Akito Mio-r shohore gelo. Or ekta weekend faka chhilo, ar ekta proshno chhilo ja o shamna-shamni jiggesh korte chayechhilo.

O Mio-ke agei janalo je ashchhe. O kono announcement chara or dorjay hajir holo na, ba dekha-ta-ke kono test banalo na. Ora nodir pasher park-er kachhe dekha korar siddhanto nilo.

Mio dhushor coat ar college-er shomoykar sei scarf-ta pore elo. Eta dekhe Akito-r mone porlo, o age kemon chhilo—ar ekhon kemon manush hoye uthechhe.

Ora pata-hin gachher niche hete gelo. Hawa eto thanda chhilo je oder nishshash dekha jacchhilo.

“Amra abar ager moto hote parbo ki na jani na,” Akito bollo.

Mio or dike takalo. “Amar mone hoy na amader ekdom ager moto howar cheshta kora uchit.”

Akito bhebechhilo Mio arekta chance chaibe. Tar bodole, o Akito-ke shomoy dichhilo bhebe dekhar—chance mane ashole ki hote pare.

“Tomar proti amar ekhono care ache,” o shikar korlo. “Kintu tomake miss kori bole ja hoyechhilo seta upekkha korte chai na.”

“Tomar uchit-o na.”

“Ar ami promise korte parbo na je abar koshto pawar bhoy kokhono hobe na.”

“Ami promise korte parbo na je tumi kokhono koshto pabe na. Kintu shotto kothin holeo tomar shathe shotto bolbo—eta promise korte pari.”

Ora nodir pashe theme gelo. Sheet-er aloy pani boye cholchhilo, jeno tar dhare manusher neowa siddhanto-gulo niye tar kono mathabatha nei.

Akito ekta lomba nishshash nilo.

“Ami abar tomake notun kore chinte chai. Dhire dhire. Jeno kichhui hoyni emon bhan kore noy.”

Mio-r chokhe jol elo, kintu o tar dike chhute gelo na, ba siddhanto-ta-ke aro boro kore dekhate bollo na.

“Ami-o chai,” o bollo.

Ora abar hatte shuru korlo. Oder haat ekbar lege gelo, tarpor abar. Mio oder majher faka jayga-ta dekhe roilo, siddhanto Akito-r hate chhere dilo.

Akito or haat dhorlo.

Mio aste kore haat-ta dhore rakhlo.

Eta past-e fire jawa chhilo na. Eta emon kichhur dike chhoto, sabdhane neowa ekta pa, jar porinam keu nishchit kore bolte parto na.

Ekhoner jonno, etai jothesto chhilo.

Chapter Five Meet Again

Purono college town-e abar spring fire elo. Station road-er pasher cherry gachh-gulo phule bhore gelo, ar student-ra bag kandhe niye taratari oder niche diye hete jete laglo.

Akito ar Mio ghoshona korlo na je ora abar ekshathe. Ora ekhono shikhchhilo oder relationship ki hoye uthte pare. Ora plan korto ar shegulo rakh-to. Kichhu kharap lagle kotha bolto. Kothin proshno bondho korar jonno affection-er byabohar korto na.

Kichhu din shohoj chhilo. Kichhu din chhilo na.

Akito-r purono shondeho fire ele o mone korar cheshta korto, sabdhanta ar nishchoyota ek jinis noy. Mio bhul korle, seta lukiye boro shomossha hote dewar bodole shikar korte shikhchhilo. Keu past bodlate parto na; kintu shamne ki korbe seta bechhe nite parto.

Ek dupure ora Seihoku College-e gelo. Cafeteria-te notun table eshechhe, ar purono vending machine-gulo ar nei. Auditorium-ta Akito-r mone rakhar cheye chhoto laglo.

Mio shei seat-er pashe dariye roilo jekhane oder prothom dekha hoyechhilo.

“Tumi koto chupchhap chhile,” o bollo.

“Tumi amader dujoner hoye kotha bolte.”

“Ami ekhono boli.”

“Kichhu jinis bodlay na.”

O hashlo. “Kichhu bodlay.”

Ora baire giye cherry gachher niche boslo. Koyekta papri rastay ure porlo. Akito-r prothom bochhorer kotha mone porlo, jokhon future-ke shohoj mone hoto, karon ora keu janto na future oder kachhe ki chaibe.

“Amar shathe dekha hoyechhilo bole tomar afsos hoy?” Mio jiggesh korlo.

O shotto kore uttor dewar jonno bhebe nilo.

“Na. Amra jevabe eke opor-ke koshto diyechhi, tar jonno afsos hoy. Je kothagulo lukiyechhilam, tar jonno afsos hoy. Kintu tomar shathe dekha hoyechhilo bole afsos nei.”

Mio matha narlo. O uttor-ta jemon chhilo temon-i mene nilo, tar theke beshi kichhu mane ber korar cheshta korlo na.

Ora dupur-ta kete jete dilo. Shesh porjonto Mio or kandhe nijer kandho thekiye dilo. Akito oke sheibhabei thakte dilo.

Future ekhono onishchit. Oder eke opor-er trust bar bar arjon korte hobe, ar boro howar kaj theke bhalobasha oder chhar debe na. Kintu ekhon ora ar emon bhan korchhilo na je ditiyo chance-er mane shob bhule jawa.

Station-e pouchhe Mio campus-er dike fire takalo.

“Phir milenge,” o bollo.

Akito hashlo. “Amra to ekhoni dekha korlam.”

“Ami porer bar-er kotha bolchhi.”

“Porer bar-o.”

Train elo. Ora eksathe uthlo—oder golpo perfect hoye gechhe bole noy, borong chokh khola rekhe golpo-ta chaliye jawar siddhanto niyechhe bole.

Kichhu manush dekha kore, alada hoye jay, ar kokhono eke opor-er jibone fire ashe na. Kichhu manush abar dekha kore bujhte pare je past bodle diyechhe tara ekhon eke opor-ke ki dite parbe. Ar kokhono kokhono, shotto bole dewar por ar shomoy-ke nijer moto kaj korte dewar por, dujon manush abar shuru korar siddhanto ney—shuru theke noy, borong tara jekhane shottyikar-e dariye ache sekhan theke.

Phir milenge.

Jokhon abar dekha hobe.
