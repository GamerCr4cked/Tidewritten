Chapter One A Small Server, a Big World

GamerCr4cked-er jonno ekta broken plugin thik kora onek shohoj chhilo, nijer moner kotha bujhiye bolar cheye.

Hoyto ei karonei internet-ta tar kache apon mone hoto. Voice call-e Minecraft niye ghontar por ghonta kotha bolte parto—permissions, hosting, builds, porer update, ba emon kono notun idea jar jonno tar hate thaka shomoyer cheye beshi kaj dorkar hoto. Kintu shamne boshe ekta shadharon kothabartateo thik shobdo khujte giye atke jeto.

Server-ta chhoto chhilo. Late-night kaj, dhar kora dhorjo, ar server chalano tinjoner upor bhor kore tike chhilo. Technical jhamela GamerCr4cked shamlatto. Baki shobai beshi excited hoye gele KingOP shobai-ke stable rakhto. Alone elomelo idea-ke plan-e bodle dite parto—tarpor oi plan niye moja korte korte setake banateo shahajjo korto.

Ora plugin niye torko korto, maintenance announce korte bhule jeto, ar kokhono kokhono ashol problem solve korar cheye server-er icon bachhte beshi shomoy diye dito. Kintu notun kono player join korle, ora cheshta korto jeno jayga-ta tar kache welcoming lage. Eta kono bishal network chhilo na. Eta chhilo oder nijeder jayga.

Chhoto ekta project setup korar proshno niye Bunny eshechhilo. GamerCr4cked practical bhabe bujhiye dilo, bhebechhilo kothabarta ekhanei shesh hobe.

Kintu shesh holo na.

Porer shondhay Bunny abar elo. Tarpor group call-e join korlo, KingOP ar Alone-ke ekdom faltu ekta bishoy niye torko korte shunlo, ar aste aste eto regular hoye gelo je call-e tar naam-ta shobar chena ekta ongsho hoye uthlo.

Cheshta na koreo o funny chhilo. GamerCr4cked chup hoye gele Bunny bujhte parto, ar protibar karon jiggesh kore take explain korte badhyo korto na. Kokhono shudhu kono build ba video niye message dito, tarpor kothabartake nijer moto kore cholte dito.

GamerCr4cked nijer mukhe poriborton-ta shikar korar agei KingOP kheyal korlo.

“Member list abar check korchhile, tai na?” call-e KingOP jiggesh korlo.

“Dekhtesi keu help chaiche kina.”

“Obosshoi.”

Alone emon ekta bhab nilo jeno gobhir kichhu bhabchhe. “Server-er monitoring ajkal besh romantic hoye geche.”

“Tomader dujon-ke mute kore dibo,” GamerCr4cked bollo.

Ora hese uthlo. Bunny shob shune felechhilo; take shudhu ekta laughing emoji pathalo.

Chhoto ekta ghotona. Tobuo tar puro shondhya bhalo hoye gelo.

Nijer onubhuti-r naam dewar dorkar tokhono chhilo na. Shudhu etuku janto, Bunny online ele server-ta ektu beshi jibonto mone hoto.

Chapter Two Messages After Midnight

Oder bondhutto gore uthlo shadharon kichhu bishoy diye: adhura build-er screenshot, emon clip jar context na janle kichhui bojha jay na, school niye ovijog, family-r expectation, ar oi biroktikor onubhuti je shobai dhore ney nijeder puro jibon niye plan oder age thekei jana uchit.

Kichhu rate Minecraft niye kotha hoto. Onno rate Minecraft-er kotha pray uthtoi na.

GamerCr4cked je project-ta onekdin dhore pichhiye dichhilo, Bunny sheta niye jiggesh korlo. Bunny-r ekta technical problem-e se shahajjo korlo. Ora eke-opor-ke gaan pathato, bar bar bola joke-e hasto, ar eke-oporer pattern bujhte shikhlo: ke shathe shathe reply dey, ke byasto dine hariye jay, ar ke “I'm fine” bole jokhon ashole tar ektu space dorkar.

GamerCr4cked Bunny-ke emon kichhu kotha bolte shuru korlo ja shadharonoto nijer moddhei rakhto. Bunny shunto, kintu prottekta problem-ke lecture baniye felto na. Bodole, Bunny-r din kharap gele se pashe thakar cheshta korto—even jokhon tar mathay kono perfect kotha asto na.

Bondhutto-ta emon guruttopurno hoye uthlo je GamerCr4cked aro sabdhane cholte laglo. Dujoner kache er mane ekoi, emon dhore niye bondhuttor upor pressure dite chayni.

Kichhudin porjonto chintar kono karon chhilo na.

Tarpor Bunny-r reply komte laglo.

Prothome bujhte para jachhilo. Bunny bollo she byasto. Ekta call miss korlo, tarpor arekta. Je message-gulo age lomba conversation hoye jeto, segulo ek-dui sentence-e shesh hote laglo. GamerCr4cked jiggesh korlo kichhu hoyeche kina; Bunny bollo onek kichhu cholchhe.

Se uttor-ta mene nilo, kintu onishchoyota roye gelo.

KingOP bollo, koyekta message miss howa dekhe puro golpo baniye felish na. Alone-er poramorsho chhilo, ekbar shoja kore jiggesh kor, tarpor Bunny-ke uttor dewar shomoy de. Oder poramorsho juktijukto chhilo. Kintu prottekta nishchup shondhay GamerCr4cked-er mone hoto check kore dekhe Bunny online esheche kina.

Se ekta funny clip pathalo. Reply elo na.

Jiggesh korlo pore server-e join korbe kina. Bunny bollo “maybe”, tarpor ar elo na.

Koyekdin por Bunny group call-e fire elo, ar Alone-er arekta joghonno joke-e hese uthlo. GamerCr4cked-er mon theke eto taratari shosti elo, jeno koyek minute-r shadharon kothabarta tar mathay jome thaka shob proshner uttor diye debe.

Kintu dilo na.

Call-er por nishchupota abar fire elo.

Se janto, green online dot kono protishruti noy. Screen-er baireo manusher nijeder jibon ache, ar shob shomoy tar jonno available thaka karo dayitto noy. Kosto-ta shudhu Bunny byasto chhilo bole chhilo na. Kosto-ta chhilo, je manusher kache nijeke bojha geche bole mone hoto, tar kache nijer jayga-ta ar bujhte parchhilo na.

Kosto hocche na emon bhan na korei Bunny-ke space dewar cheshta korlo.

Chapter Three “Who Are You?”

Koyek shoptaho por Bunny fire elo emon ekta byakkha niye, ja GamerCr4cked bhabteo pareni.

Bunny janalo je she ekta serious medical problem-er moddhe diye geche ebong tar surgery hoyeche. Tar kotha onujayi, tar memory-te probhab porechhe. Jiboner kichhu ongsho mone korte pare, kichhu pare na.

GamerCr4cked-er prothom protikriya chhilo chinta. Se jiggesh korlo Bunny safe ache kina, ar tar pashe emon keu ache kina je take support korte parbe. Bhoyonkor kono obhiggota-r moddhe diye jawa manush-ke proshner por proshno kore chap dite chayni.

Bunny chhoto kore uttor dilo. Tarpor ekta proshno korlo.

“Sorry… tumi ke?”

Ek muhurter jonno GamerCr4cked bhablo, Bunny moja korchhe. Se Minecraft server-er kotha mone koriye dilo, modhyorater call, screenshot, ar raat periye jawa conversation-gulor kotha bollo. Message-ta norom rakhar cheshta korlo, jeno thik shobdo bolte parle Bunny take chinte parbe.

Kintu Bunny-r uttor durei roilo. She bollo GamerCr4cked-ke tar mone nei.

KingOP ar Alone-o jogajog korlo. Tara nijeder porichoy ar Bunny-r shathe tader porichoyer kotha bujhiye bollo. Bunny janalo, oder dujon-keo mone nei.

GamerCr4cked Bunny-r byakkhay bishash korte chayechhilo. Kintu nijeke proshno kora theke thamate parchhilo na. Mone hochhilo Bunny online community-r kichhu ongsho chinte parchhe, kintu jara tar shobcheye kacher chhilo tader chinte parchhe na. Bunny-r medical obosthar shomporke tar kono dharona chhilo na, se ki mone korte pare ta mapar upay-o chhilo na. Ar Bunny-r obhiggota ashole kemon, sheta thik kore dewar odhikar-o tar chhilo na.

Koyekta shombhabona chhilo, jegulor moddhe parthokko kora tar pokkhe shombhob chhilo na. Hoyto tar memory asholei bodle gechhe. Hoyto she overwhelmed hoye gechhe ar distance chay. Hoyto shotto-ta ei duita byakkhar cheyeo jotil. Se jante parto na; beshi bhable onishchoyota shotto hoye jay na.

Koyekdin dhore se message likhe abar delete korlo. Jiggesh korte ichchha korchhilo, Bunny fire eshe take ochena manusher moto treat korchhe keno. Chayechhilo Bunny bujhuk, tar nishchupotay koto kosto hoyeche. Kintu etao janto, byakkha dabi korlei byakkha pawa jay na.

Shesh porjonto se ekta chhoto message pathalo.

“Umeed kori tumi safe acho ar support pachho. Reply dewa dorkar nei. Shudhu janate chailam, amader bondhutto amar kache guruttopurno chhilo.”

Message-ta unread-i roilo.

Se ar ditiyo message pathalo na.

Chapter Four A Server Full of Echoes

Server cholte thaklo, kintu GamerCr4cked-er kache sheta ar ager moto chhilo na.

Porichito kono channel Bunny-r bola joke mone koriye dito. Adhura build emon ekta conversation-er kotha mone korato ja ar shesh korte parbe na. Kokhono na bhebei member list khule boshto, tarpor ki korchhe bujhte pere shathe shathe bondho kore dito.

KingOP ar Alone tar pashe roilo. Tara take jor kore “move on” korte bollo na, abar Bunny-ke villain banateo utsaho dilo na. Tara game khelto, project niye kotha bolto, ar GamerCr4cked-er kichhu bolar na thakle take chup thakte dito.

Shesh porjonto GamerCr4cked mene nilo je se ar server manage korte chay na. Onno ekjoner hate dayitto tule dewar byabostha korlo, community-ke poriborton-ta janalo, ar eto din dhore je kaj shamle eshechhilo sheta hand over korlo.

KingOP khushi chhilo na. Alone jiggesh korlo se nishchit kina.

“Break dorkar, ei bishoye nishchit,” GamerCr4cked bollo. “Shob shomoy emon lagbe kina jani na. Kintu ekhon prottekta channel-e mone hoy ami kichhur jonno opekkha korchhi.”

Rag kore se server chhere jayni. Jayga-ta tar kache shotti chhilo. Bondhutto, hashi, ar eksathe kora kaj—shob kichhur-i dam chhilo. Kintu Bunny hoyto fire ashbe ar shob kichhu ager moto kore debe, ei ashay se ar bachte parchhilo na.

Tar porer mash-gulo kono dramatic ghotonar moto chhilo na. Hothat kono revelation elo na, perfect closure-o holo na. Majhe majhe Bunny-r kotha ekhono mone porto. Bhavto, she ki oder mone korte pare, bhalo ache kina, ar je byakkha diyechhilo shetai ki puro golpo.

Se shikhlo, karo jonno chinta korar mane tar nishchupotar shomadhan ber kora noy. Uttor pawar jonno Bunny-r pechhone na ghureo tar bhalo chawa jay. Kosto peyechhe eta mene newa jay, kintu tar uddeshyo ki chhilo sheta nijer moto kore thik kore felte hoy na.

Shobcheye guruttopurno kotha, jiboner baki bondhuttogulor jonno dorja khola rakha jay.

Chapter Five The People Who Stayed

Ek shondhay, server-er dayitto onno karo hate jawar onekdin por, GamerCr4cked purono screenshot-er ekta folder khuje pel.

Sekhane blocky build, ajob chat message, adha-toiri spawn-er pashe dariye thaka tin owner-er chhobi, ar support channel-e Bunny-r prothom proshner screenshot chhilo.

Ekta shadharon hello. Eta theke koto kichhu shuru hobe, tar kono dharona-i chhilo na.

Se folder-ta scroll korlo, prottekta memory-r moddhe kono clue khuje ber korar cheshta na kore. Bondhutto-ta guruttopurno chhilo. Hashi-gulo shotti chhilo. Shesh hoye jawa mane ei noy je oi shomoyta muche gechhe, jokhon bondhutto tar din-gulo aro bhalo kore ditto.

Se KingOP ar Alone-ke message pathalo.

“Keu call-e free achhish?”

Alone shobar age join korlo, ar dhuktei game-er ekta problem niye ovijog shuru kore dilo. Ek minute por KingOP elo, emon ekta plugin niye torko shuru korlo ja oder keu ekhon ar use-o kore na. GamerCr4cked nijeke thamate parar agei hese uthlo.

Call-er porichito shobdo-ta ghore choriye porlo.

Bhiri jaygar cheye screen-er pechhone thakte se ekhono beshi comfortable chhilo. Nijer onubhuti bujhiye bolte ekhono majhe majhe oshubidha hoto. Kintu se shikhchhilo, ekta golmele ending-er mane ei noy je porer prottekta notun jogajog-ke unsafe mone korte hobe.

Bunny-r shathe ashole ki hoyechhilo, hoyto se kokhono thik kore jante parbe na. Bunny ja share korechhilo tar shima-ke se shomman korte pare, baki kotha verify korte parbe na eta mene nite pare, ar onno ekjoner jiboner proshno nijeke diye solve koranor cheshta chhere dite pare.

Se chayechhilo Bunny bhalo thakuk. Asha korechhilo, oder bondhuttor bhalo muhurto-gulo tar kacheo guruttopurno chhilo. Nijer jibon pause na koreo ei asha-gulo dhore rakha jay.

Purono server ar tar niyontrone chhilo na, kintu prithibi shekhanei shesh hoye jayni. Ekhono project bananor chhilo, game khelar chhilo, ar emon manush chhilo jader call korle tara uttor dito.

Kokhono manush offline jay, porer shokal-e abar fire ashe. Kokhono porichito username niye fire ashe, kintu majhkhane emon durotto thake ja kono message perote pare na. Kokhono kono sposto biday charai bondhutto shesh hoye jay.

GamerCr4cked beche nite parto na je kon dhoroner ending tar bhagye eshechhe.

Kintu tar por se ki korbe, sheta beche nite parto.

Se screenshot-er folder bondho korlo, call-e join korlo, ar KingOP ar Alone-ke ekdom faltu bishoy niye torko korte shunte laglo.

Onekdin por prothombar, se kono notification-er opekkhay chhilo na.

Se age thekei shekhane chhilo, jekhane thakte chay.
