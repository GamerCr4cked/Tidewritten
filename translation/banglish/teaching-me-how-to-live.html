Chapter One Bus Stop

Arjun Sato-r shokal minute dhore dhore cholto. Tar alarm bajto 7:15-e. Se uthto 7:16-e, 7:30-er moddhe ready hoto, 7:45-e bari theke ber hoto, ar 8:05-er moddhe bus stop-e pouchhe jeto. Bus asto 8:12-e. TechNova Solutions-er office-e shoptom tolay tar desk chhilo, ar noyta bajte na bajtei sekhane thaka lagto. Der bochhor dhore pray kichhui bodlay ni. Arjun kono dramatic bhabe oshukhi chhilo na. Tar ekta stable chakri, chhoto apartment, ar prottekta bill niye chinta na korar moto taka chhilo. Shudhu se prottekta din emon bhabe katato, jeno kono checklist-er task shesh korchhe. Dorkar hole kotha bolto, baki shomoy headphone pore thakto. Ek bhir-bhora shokale bus ashar shomoy keu take thele shamne chole gelo. Footpath-er dhare tar pa pichhle gelo. Se rastay pore jawar agei ekta haat tar bahu dhore take abar footpath-e tene nilo. Je meyeta take dhorechhilo, she jore jore shash nichchhilo. Tar bag ek kandho theke pichhle porechhilo, ar chokhe bhoy sposto chhilo. “Tumi thik acho?” Arjun nijer hater talur khoto-ta dekhlo, tarpor meyetar dike takalo. “Ami thik achhi. Thank you.” Se bus-er dike egiye gelo. Meyeta obak hoye shekhanei dariye roilo—keu take shahajjo korar por eto taratari headphone lagiye chole jay kibhabe? Tar naam Nisha Fujimoto, ar TechNova-te shei chhilo tar prothom din. Dujone eki bus-e uthlo, jodio Nisha tar theke koyek row pichhone boshlo. Office-e giye she janlo, bus stop-er oi chupchap chele-ta tar nijer team-ei kaj kore. Oder colleague Sahil porichoy koriye dilo. “Arjun, e Nisha. Nisha, e Arjun. Kaj-e bhalo, kintu small talk-e ekdomi baje.” “Ami shunte pachchhi,” Arjun bollo. “Tahole porichoy-ta kaj korchhe.” Nisha haslo. Arjun halka kore matha narlo, tarpor abar screen-e mon dilo. Nisha take pore jawa theke bachiechhilo. Se thank you bolechhilo. Tar kache byapar-ta shesh. Kintu Nisha bhabte shuru korlo, kon dhoroner manush onno karo shahajjo eto dure theke grohon kore?

Chapter Two Lunchbox

Nisha-r prothom shoptaho besh byasto chhilo. Se software shikhlo, office-er layout mukhosto korlo, ar bujhar cheshta korlo je printer-er paper keno shobcheye tarahuro-r shomoy-i jam hoy. Se etao kheyal korlo je Arjun eka lunch korto. Prothome tar mone holo hoyto eka thakte pochhondo kore. Tarpor dekhlo, lunch-er shomoy-o se pray kaj korto ar desk-e fire asto shudhu coffee niye. Ek dupure Nisha tar keyboard-er pashe chhoto ekta lunchbox rekhe dilo. “Tumi khete bhule gechho,” se bollo. “Bhuli ni.” “Tahole ichchha kore khao ni. Sheta to aro kharap.” Se bollo, tar maa beshi khabar pack kore diyechhen. Eta ekta bahana chhilo, ar dujonei janto. Arjun lunchbox-ta emon bhabe dekhlo jeno kono guruttopurno document review korte hobe. “Thank you,” se bollo. “Dabba-ta ferot dio. Eta amar favourite.” Porer din lunchbox-ta dhoya, shukno ar poripati bhabe fire elo. Dhaknar niche ekta chhoto note chhilo: *Thank you. Khabar bhalo chhilo.* Nisha note-ta rekhe dilo. Tarpor oder routine-e ek-ek minute kore poriborton aste laglo. Nisha project niye proshno korto, Arjun kajer uttor dito. Se jiggesh korto cha khabe kina, Arjun haan bolto. Kokhono lunch-er shomoy pashe boshe Nisha tar boi niye ba train-e dekha odbhut ghotona niye golpo korto. Arjun nijer personal kotha khub ekta bolto na, kintu mon diye shunto. Nisha ekta important presentation-er kotha bolle tarikh-ta mone rakhto, pore abar jiggesh korto kemon gelo. “Mone chhilo tomar?” Nisha jiggesh korlo. “Tumi bolechhile.” “Beshi bhag manush bhule jay.” “Ami bhulbo keno, bujhi na.” Arjun-er kach theke compliment-er shobcheye kachhakachhi kotha chhilo eta. Nisha nijer desk-e fire giye ojotha khushi hoye roilo.

Chapter Three Brishti, Cake, Ar Chhoto Poriborton

Nisha Arjun-ke outgoing manush bananor cheshta korlo na. Se shudhu jibon-er je chhoto ongsho-gulo Arjun kheyal korto na, shekhane take aste aste jayga dite laglo. Brishti jokhon oder office-er baire atke dilo, Nisha umbrella share korlo. Station-er kache notun café khulle jiggesh korlo coffee judge korte shahajjo korbe kina. Office party-te Arjun janalar pashe dariye thakle, paliye jawar agei Nisha tar jonno ekta plate niye elo. Arjun-o tar bodole Nisha-ke kheyal korte shuru korlo. Nisha shobar shathe friendly chhilo, kintu shob shomoy khushi thakar natok korto na. Kothin dine chup hoye jeto ar nijer chinta mon diye shamlatto. Se ambitious chhilo, majhe majhe ekguyye, ar bhul korle sheta mene nite parto. Tar bhalo byabohar kono performance chhilo na; protidin sheta beche newar ekta siddhanto chhilo. Ek shondhay Arjun tar desk-e cake-er ek slice pel. Kono note nei, announcement nei. Shudhu cake. Se Sahil-ke jiggesh korlo ke rekhechhe. Sahil monitor theke chokh tule bollo, “Tumi-i bolo. Ke tomake bar bar khabar ene dey?” Arjun office-er onno dike takalo. Nisha ekjon colleague-er shathe kotha bolchhilo, screen-e kichhu dekhe hashchhilo. Prothombar se bhablo, Nisha jokhon take nijer jogote dhokanor cheshta korchhe na, tokhon she kemon thake. Se dujoner jonno cha banate shuru korlo. Nisha-r weekend kemon kate jiggesh korto. Office theke eka ber na hoye tar shathe station porjonto hatte laglo. Tar timetable bodlachchhilo—not Nisha dabi korechhilo bole noy, borong se nijer thekei tar jonno jayga korte chayechhilo bole. Tarpor Kaito naam-er notun ekjon employee oder team-e elo. Se confident, funny, ar Nisha-ke shahajjo korte shob shomoy ready. Arjun oder project niye hashte hashte kotha bolte dekhe emon ekta oshosthi feel korlo, jar mane se bujhte parchhilo na. Sahil kheyal korlo. “Tui jealous.” “Na.” “Dosh minute dhore eki spreadsheet-er dike takiye achhish.” Arjun screen-er dike takalo. Ekta cell-o pora hoy ni. Se bujhte shuru korechhilo, keu jokhon tomar kache guruttopurno hoye jay, tokhon prithibi ager moto predictable thake na.

Chapter Four Je Proshno-ta Se Erate Chayechhilo

Koyekdin Arjun abar dure sore roilo. Nijeke bojhalo je shey practical hocchhe. Nisha-r nijer jibon ache, ar tar attention dabi korar kono odhikar Arjun-er nei. Purono routine-e fire jawa, kichhu chawa niye bhoy pachchhe eta shikar korar cheye shohoj chhilo. Nisha poriborton-ta kheyal korlo. “Amar ki kichhu bhul hoyeche?” ek shondhay lift-e se jiggesh korlo. “Na.” “Tahole amake eriye cholchho keno?” “Kaj niye byasto.” “Tumi to shob shomoy-i byasto. Age ontoto ami kotha bolle amar dike takate.” Lift ground floor-e pouchhalo. Keu shathe shathe ber holo na. Arjun chaile abar ekta bahana dite parto. Tar bodole shikar korlo, nijer onubhuti niye confused hoye gechhe, ar ki korbe bujhte parchhe na. Nisha-r mukh norom holo, kintu se kothabarta-ta Arjun-er jonno shohoj kore dilo na. “Aaj raat-ei shob thik korte hobe na,” se bollo. “Kintu tumi amake ja bolo ni, shetar jonno amake shasti dio na.” Arjun khoma chailo. Dujone station-er dike hatte laglo. Crossing-e Nisha tar ager ekta kothin relationship-er kotha bollo, ar kibhabe shei obhiggota take emon manushder niye shabdhan korechhe jara shoja kotha na bole chup hoye jay. “Ami chai na tumi shob shomoy hashikhushi thako,” se bollo. “Ami shudhu jante chai, tomar jibone amar jayga-ta kothay.” Alada hoye jawar poro Arjun kothata niye bhabte thaklo. Porer shokale se prothome message pathalo. *Aaj amar shathe lunch korbe? Office-er baire kothao?* Pray shathe shathe reply elo. *Ami bhabte shuru korechhilam tumi kokhono jiggesh-i korbe na.*

Chapter Five Aro Bhalo Ekta Shadharon Jibon

Oder lunch date-gulo shohoj chhilo. Station-er kache chhoto ekta restaurant. Nodir pashe hata. Ekta bookstore, jekhane Nisha ek ghonta boi dekhto ar Arjun emon bhab korto jeno tar bhalo lagchhe na. Ora dhore newar bodole jiggesh korte shikhlo. Arjun-er chup thakte hole se bole dito. Nisha kosto pele keno peyechhe bujhiye bolto, Arjun nije theke bujhe nebe emon asha korto na. Oder parthokko-gulo hariye jay ni, kintu bujhte shohoj hoye uthlo. Ek shondhay Nisha tar bondhu Sneha-r kotha bollo, je take Arjun-er chup thaka-ke udashinota bhebe na nite bolechhilo. “Se bollo, tumi naki amar dike emon bhabe takao jeno amar prottekta kaj mone rakhar cheshta korchho.” Arjun-er chopstick pray haat theke porei jachchhilo. “Eta barie bola.” “Tai?” Se mithye bolar kotha bhablo. Tarpor matha narlo. Nisha haslo—jite jawar moto kore noy, borong shosti peye. Koyek shoptaho por oder prothom serious jhogra holo. Kajer jonno Arjun shesh muhurte plan cancel korlo, kintu Nisha agei pouchhe jawar por take janalo. Nisha rege giyechhilo—not kaj guruttopurno chhilo bole noy, borong kono byakkha chara take opekkha koranor jonno. Arjun nijeke defend na kore khoma chailo. Byasto dine ki bhabe manage korbe ta niye ora kotha bollo, ar porer bar kajer chap barle Arjun nijer kothar upor kaj korlo. Tokhon Nisha tar poriborton-ke bishash korte shuru korlo. Boro boro protishruti diye noy, borong Arjun asholei pashe thakte shikhchhilo bole. Ek brishtir shondhay ora station-er chhader niche dariye chhilo. Nisha akasher dike takalo. “Amader prothom dekha mone ache?” “Tumi amake pore jawa theke bachiechhile.” “Ar tarpor emon bhabe chole gele jeno ami tomake receipt diyechhi.” “Ami thank you bolechhilam.” “Khub bhodro bhabe. Jeno customer service email.” Arjun heshe uthlo. Nisha khushi holo, tarpor hotat ektu lojja pel. “Bhalo hoyechhilo ami tomake dhorechhilam,” se bollo. “Amaro tai mone hoy.” Arjun dhire kore tar haat dhorte egolo, jate Nisha chaile na bolte pare. Nisha nijer haat tar hate rekhe dilo. Brishti oder charpashe porte thaklo. Dujoner-i tarahuro chhilo na.

Chapter Six Beche Achi

Koyek mash poro Arjun-er routine shuru hoto 7:15-e. Se ekhono pray 8:05-e bus stop-e pouchhoto ar noyta bajbar age office-e asto. Kintu ekhon schedule-ta tar jibon-er charpashe dewal mone hoto na. Kichhu shokale se Nisha-r shathe hatte jeto. Kichhu shondhay dujone mile ranna korto. Arjun bari ferar pothe chhobi tulte shuru korechhilo—dokaner janala-e pore thaka alo, brishtir por akasher rong. Nisha take chhobi-gulo rekhe dite bolto, jodio se segulo shadharon bole urie dito. “Egulo tomar shadharon din,” se bolto. “Tai egulo rekhe dewar moto.” Oder relationship official holo shobcheye kom dramatic bhabe: Nisha jiggesh korlo, ek bondhur dinner-e Arjun-ke nijer boyfriend bole porichoy koriye dite pare kina. Arjun haan bollo. Tarpor dujone dosh minute dhore kon train-e jawa uchit ta niye torko korlo. Ek bochhor por ora abar shei bus stop-e fire elo, jekhane prothom dekha hoyechhilo. Bench-ta notun kore rong kora, footpath-o meramot hoye gechhe. Shohor ager motoi oder charpashe cholchhilo. Nisha jayga-ta dekhe heshe bollo, “Shei shokale tumi ekbaro pechhone takao ni.” “Lojja lagchhilo.” “Tumi oshombhob chhile.” “Jani.” Se Arjun-er haat dhore nilo. Arjun-o tar haat dhore roilo, mone korlo shei chhoto haat-ta je take pore jawar age rastar theke footpath-e tene ene chhilo. Bus elo. Dorja khullo. Keu norlo na. “Ei bus-e uthbo?” Arjun jiggesh korlo. Nisha matha-ta halka kore tar kandhe rakhlo. “Ekhono na.” Bus-ta chole gelo, ar kichhukhoner jonno stop-ta chup hoye roilo. Arjun ekshomoy bhabto, bhalo jibon mane shomoy moto chola ar kono unexpected kichhu na chaowa. Nisha take notun personality dey ni. Se shudhu take shei jibon-ta kheyal korte shikhiechhilo ja age thekei tar charpashe cholchhilo—ekshathe khawa, shotto khoma chawar por thik kora plan, dujoner jonno banano cha, ar kono karon charai dhore rakha ekta haat. Bari ferar poth lomba chhilo, kintu ekhon ora eksathe hatte laglo. Se ar shudhu din par korchhilo na. Se ashole bachte shikhchhilo.
