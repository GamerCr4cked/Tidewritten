Rasta-ta thik shei dupurer moto lagchhilo, jedin prothom take kheyal korechhilam. Eki oshoman footpath, eki dokan, gachher majhe roddur-er eki tukro. Dhire hatte hatte bhablam, keu dramatic shuru charao tomar jibone dhukte pare, ar tobuo ekta chhap rekhe jete pare.

Take chenar agei ami ekta naam diyechhilam. Shomoyer shathe bujhlam, naam ekta smriti dhore rakhte pare, kintu puro manush-ke tar moddhe bondi korte pare na.

Hina shudhu school courtyard-e dekha shei meyeta chhilo na. Se nijer bondhu, chinta ar nijer future niye ekjon student—emon future ja amar onubhuti-ke ghire ghurbe na. Asha korlam notun school tar shathe bhalo byabohar korbe. Asha korlam, emon manush pabe jara take hasabe.

Ami etao chayechhilam je ei bochhor-ta shotyi kore mone rakhi—not emon ekta love story hishabe ja pray hoye jete jete hoy ni, borong boro hoye othar ekta chhoto chapter hishabe.

Kichhu manush thakar jonno ashe na. Kokhono tara tomar poth diye jay, ekta shadharon diner rup bodle dey, tarpor nijer pothe chole jay.

Porer shokale student-ra uwabaki pore ager moto homeroom-e dhuklo. Obhyash boshoto junior student-der dike takalam, tarpor nijeke mone koriye dilam je obhyash-tao ekshomoy kome jabe. School bell bajlo. Bondhura kotha shuru korlo. Din cholte thaklo.

Prothombar bujhlam, kono shesh mane smriti-ke tragedy baniye fela noy.

Little Bird emon ek akasher dike ure gechhilo, jar pechhone ami jete partam na.

Ar take bhalo thakar kamona korte partam, take fire takate na bolei.

Bhebechhilam golpo ekhanei shesh. Jantam na, ek bochhor por abar school gate-er dike takabo—not karon otit bodle gechhe, borong jibon chupchap Hina-ke abar amar chokher shamne ene debe bole.
