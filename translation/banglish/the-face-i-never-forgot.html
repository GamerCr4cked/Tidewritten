CHAPTER ONE
The Girl by the Window

Middle school-er ditiyo bochhor shuru holo chalk, purono desk, ar entrance-er kache shukote thaka brishtir panir porichito gondho niye. Sota-r seat-er upor-er ceiling fan-ta klanto bhabe tik-tik korchhilo. Tar notebook-e adha page class note ar ekta spaceship-er chhobi chhilo, jeta planet theke paliye jachchhe.

“Ei period shesh hote ar koto deri?” se aste kore jiggesh korlo.

Pashe bosha Yuto bhaj kora bahur upor gaal rekhechhilo. “Aro bish minute por jiggesh korish. Totokkhone ami uttor-ta shojjo kore felbo.”

Sota pray jore heshe felechhilo. Hashi lukanor jonno janalar dike ghurlo—ar ditiyo row-er meyetake dekhte pel.

Airi Tanaka poripati, sthir hate likhchhilo. Notebook-er upor jhuklei tar chul shamne pore jeto, ar na takiyei sheta kaner pechhone guje dito. Se kono oshadharon kaj korchhilo na. Shudhu nijer kaj-e mon diye boshe chhilo, jokhon baki classroom arekta shadharon shokal-er moddhe diye dhire dhire cholchhilo.

Sota pray shathe shathe chokh shoriye nilo.

Shudhu classmate, se nijeke bollo. Kal hoyto bhule jabo.

Kintu bhullo na.

Porer koyek shoptaho-te na chaiteo se Airi-r shomporke chhoto chhoto kotha jene gelo. Airi class-er shera student-der ekjon, kintu keu help chaile take boka mone korato na. Teacher proshno korle aste kotha bolto, kintu uttor hoto sposto. Kokhono teacher porer topic-e chole jawar poro se notebook-er margin-e likhe jeto. Ki likhchhe jani na, kintu nijer lekha pore nijer monei hashto.

Ek dupure English teacher passage jore pore shonabar jonno volunteer chailen. Puro room jhuke jawa chokh ar hotat kore khub interesting hoye otha pencil case-e bhore gelo.

Airi haat tullো.

Tar gola shanto chhilo, kintu prottekta shobdo classroom-er shesh porjonto pouchholo. Sota bujhte parlo, puro din-e onno kono kichhur dike eto mon diye shone ni.

Se shesh korle tar chokh ek muhurter jonno Sota-r shathe mile gelo. Sota shathe shathe nijer notebook-er dike takalo, jeno spaceship-er kaj hotat khub joruri hoye gechhe.

Kichhui ghote ni. Tobuo kichhu ekta shuru hoye gechhilo.

CHAPTER TWO
The Small Things That Stayed

School-er ekta bochhor beshirbhag shomoy emon shadharon bishoy diye toiri, ja mone rakhar moto mone hoy na: attendance, dhar kora eraser, lunch bell, ar classroom-er moddhe lomba hata. Sota-r kache ei chhoto chhoto ghotona aste aste Airi-r charpashe joma hote laglo.

Ek shokale se bujhte parlo, history notebook bari-te fele eshechhe. Teacher shobai-ke classmate-er shathe note share korte bolলেন. Yuto nijer notebook tule fusfus kore bollo, “Amar ache. Amar-ta ne.”

Sota take thank you bollo, kintu tar pa totokkhone Airi-r desk-er dike niye gechhe.

“Tanaka-san? Ektu tomar note dekhte pari?”

Airi obak hoye takalo, tarpor notebook-ta dujoner majhe shoriye dilo. “Hya, obosshoi. Lunch-er age ferot dio. Amar-o lagbe.”

“Nishchoi.”

Etotukui. Airi nijer kaj-e fire gelo, ar Sota note copy korte laglo emon monojog diye jeno take desher kono gopon rahoshyo diyeche.

Arekdin science project-er jonno class-ke group-e kaj korte bola holo. Airi diagram dekhate table-er upor jhuklo, thik tokhon Sota ruler nite gelo. Oder angul pray chhuye jachchhilo. Sota eto taratari haat shoriye nilo je Yuto obak hoye takiye roilo.

“Ekhon stationery-keo bhoy pash?” pore Yuto jiggesh korlo.

“Chup kor.”

“Ami to kichhu boli ni.”

“Tor mukh shob bole diyechhe.”

Yuto heshe uthlo, kintu Airi-r shamne Sota-ke niye moja korlo na. Ei chhoto daya-r jonno Sota kritoggo chhilo—ar aro beshi lojja pachchhilo.

Brishtir season shuru hole Sota extra ekta bhaj kora umbrella niye ber hote laglo. Nijeke bolto eta practical. Kintu Airi-ke offer korar thik shomoy-ta kokhono khuje pelo na.

School-e jawar pothe shadharon sentence practice korto: Homework bujhechho? Oi boi-ta interesting mone hochchhe. Aaj brishti besh jore, tai na?

Shesh porjonto kotha bolar moto kache geleo, shob shobdo mile bhodro ekta “Good morning” hoye jeto. Airi protibar eki norom hashi diye uttor dito, tarpor nijer pothe chole jeto.

Se ek second-er ekta hashi-r jonno opekkha korte shuru korechhilo.

CHAPTER THREE
A Letter Folded Four Times

March-er moddhe school-er bochhor pray shesh. Exam classroom-e osthir uttejona eneche, ar shobai porer grade niye kotha bolchhe. Sota-r aro ekta chinta chhilo: se kichhu na bolle bochhor-ta shesh hoye jabe, ar shotto-ta tar bhetorei atke thakbe.

School-er por Yuto take tritiyo bar eki proshno ghuriye phiriye korte shunlo.

“Kauke pochhondo korle bole de,” Yuto bollo. “Shobcheye kharap hole she eki rokom feel korbe na. Prithibi to ar dhongsho hoye jabe na.”

“Tor jonno bola shohoj.”

“Hya, shohoj. Ami to shei manush na je meyeta pencil dhar chailei mathar moddhe speech likhte shuru kore.”

Sota-r bolar moto kichhu chhilo na.

Shei raat-e chhoto lamp-er niche desk-e boslo. Prothom draft-ta kharap romance drama-r dialogue-er moto laglo. Chhire fello. Ditiyo-ta formal application-er moto. Shetao chhire fello. Tritiyo-ta dui page hoye gelo—mone rakha prottekta chhoto muhurto diye bhora. Confession-er cheye beshi lagchhilo jeno diary bhul kore onno karo naam-e likhe felechhe.

Shesh porjonto ekta porishkar kagoj niye shoja kore likhlo:

Airi, eta bola thik kina jani na, kintu ar lukiye rakhte chai na. Tomake amar bhalo lage. Mone hoy onekdin dhorei. Tomar-o eki rokom feel korte hobe na, ar ami amar pochhondo moto uttor chaichhi na. Shudhu chayechhilam tumi jeno jano.

Shesh line-e tar hater lekha kapte laglo. Chithi-ta char bhaj kore pocket-e rakhlo. Raat-e pray ghum-i holo na.

Porer shokale prottekta corridor beshi bhir mone hochchhilo. Lunch-er shomoy Airi boi guchhiye rakhar shomoy se shesh porjonto tar kache gelo.

“Tanaka-san… shomoy pele eta porbe?”

Se bhaj kora kagoj-ta shamne baralo. Nijer haat-ta jeno tar shorir theke alada mone hochchhilo.

Airi chithi theke tar mukher dike takalo. Prothome obak holo, tarpor chokhe dekha dilo shabdhan ar shodoy ekta bhab.

“Thik ache,” se aste kore bollo.

Shei din kon teacher ki bolechhilen, Sota-r ar kichhui mone roilo na.

CHAPTER FOUR
Her Answer

Airi class-e chithi-r kotha tullo na. Classmate-der bollo na, ar Sota-r shathe alada byabohar-o korlo na. Porer dui din school-er shadharon routine cholte thaklo, ar prottekta shadharon muhurto Sota-ke aro nervous kore tullo.

Tritiyo din dupure she jiggesh korlo shoe locker-er pashe ektu kotha bola jabe kina. Sekhane student-ra baire porar juto bodlachchhilo, ar corridor-e bheja umbrella-r halka gondho chhilo.

“Shotyi kore bolar jonno thank you,” se bollo. Bhaj kora chithi-ta notebook-er page-er moddhe jotno kore rekhechhilo. “Ami obak hoyechhilam, kintu bhalo legechhe je tumi amake eto bishash korechho.”

Sota shabhabik bhabe shash newar cheshta korlo. “Achha.”

Se ektu theme shobdo beche nilo. “I'm sorry. Ami ekhon relationship-e jete chai na. Onek kichhu ache jar upor focus korte chai, ar mone hoy na tomar asha moto tomar feelings-er uttor dite parbo.”

Tar golay kono nishthurata chhilo na. Se haslo na, ar muhurto-ta shohoj korar jonno pore haan bolte pare emon mithye ashao dilo na.

Ei karone uttor-ta ekshathe shodoy-o chhilo, abar shona kothin-o chhilo.

“Bujhte perechhi,” Sota bollo. Tar gola nijer moner obosthar cheye sthir shonachchhilo. “Tomake khoma chaite hobe na.”

“Asha kori amra classmates thakte parbo. Ami chai na tumi amake eriye cholo.”

Se matha narlo. “Parbo.”

Ora nijeder routine-e fire gelo. Airi arekbar thank you bole train dhorte chole gelo. Sota locker-er pashe dariye roilo, jotokkhon na corridor faka hote shuru korlo.

Se bhebechhilo rejection mane dramatic ekta scene: lomba speech, hotat jhor, emon ekta muhurto ja jibon-ke age ar pore bhag kore dey. Kintu eta chhilo school-er ekta shadharon diner sheshe dui student-er moddhe nishchup ekta kothabarta.

Porer shokale Airi ager motoi take shubhechha janalo. Sota uttor dilo, ar she class-e chole gelo.

Airi-r jonno kichhu bodlay ni—or ontoto she kichhu bodleche emon dekhate chay ni. Sota-r kache eki classroom, shudhu bhetore notun ekta durotto toiri hoye gechhilo.

CHAPTER FIVE
The Same Face, Years Later

Koyek bochhor por, boshonter shesh dike ek dupure Sota nijer purono school-e fire elo. Ekhon se college-e pore, notun rasta, ochena mukh ar emon routine-er moddhe thake ja school bell-er upor nirvor kore na. Koyek mash dhore Airi-r kotha bhabe ni—or ontoto nijeke shikar korar moto ghonoghon bhabe ni.

Tarpor gate-ta chokhe porlo.

Deowal-gulo mone rakhar cheye chhoto lagchhilo. Rastaar pasher zelkova gachh-gulo aro ghono hoyeche, ar courtyard-ta ekshomoy je puro prithibi dhore rekhechhilo tar cheye chhoto lagchhilo. School-e ekta event cholchhilo, tai gate khola. Kono siddhanto na niyei Sota bhetore dhuke porlo.

Se shoe locker, dupurer alo dhora janala, ar shei corridor diye gelo jekhane je kothagulo kokhono bolte pareni segulo practice korto. Classroom-er sirir kache eshe tar pa dhire holo.

Purono room-ta faka chhilo. Desk-gulo shoja row-te rakha, ar floor-er upor dupurer alor ekta fakashe ayotokhetro pore chhilo. Se ditiyo row, janalar pasher seat, ar Airi notebook khule je jaygay boshto sheta chinte parlo.

Sota pichhoner nijer purono seat-e boslo.

Ek muhurte shob mone pore gelo: prothom take dekha, dhar kora history note, science project-er shomoy angul pray chhuye jawa, pocket-e kapte thaka chithi, ar shoe locker-er pashe tar norom na-bola.

Shob kichhu eto sposto mone ache dekhe se obak holo. Shomoyer shathe onubhuti bodle gechhilo, kintu eta kokhono emon golpo hoy ni ja niye tar lojja lage. Se kauke pochhondo korechhilo, shotyi kore bolechhilo, ar mene niyechhilo je meyetar onubhuti eki rokom noy. Kosto hoyechhilo, kintu eta-o shikhiechhilo je shotto bola shudhu tokhon-i mullo rakhe na jokhon pochhonder uttor pawa jay.

Baire courtyard-e ekta ball laf diye gelo. Ek student heshe tar pechhone douralo, ar khola janala diye shobdo-ta bhetore elo.

Sota haslo. Uthe dariye pant theke dhulo jharlo, ar janalar pasher faka desk-ta arekbar dekhlo.

Se beriye jabe emon shomoy corridor theke ekta porichito gola daklo.

“Mori-kun?”

Se ghurlo. Airi classroom-er dorjar kache dariye, pashe arekjon purono student. Se boro hoye gechhe, obosshoi, kintu tar cheharay ekhono shei shanto, monojogi ushnota chhilo. Ek second-er jonno dujonei bujhte parlo na, otiter kon rup-ta diye prothome shubhechha janabe.

Tarpor Airi haslo. “Onek din hoye gelo.”

“Hya. Onek.”

Prothome kotha ektu oshostikor chhilo. College, teacher, ar classroom-ta ager cheye chhoto lagar kotha bollo. Shathe asha purono student Airi-ke event-e help korte daklo, ar she bole chole gelo. Kono dramatic kichhu ghotlo na. Purono onubhuti hotat fire elo na, ar dorjar pechhone kono notun confession-o opekkha korchhilo na.

Jawaar age Airi jiggesh korlo, “Tumi ekhono Instagram use koro? Class-er shobar shathe contact rakha uchit.”

“Kori,” Sota bollo. “Obosshoi.”

Ora contact details exchange korlo ar ekta group chat-e jog dilo, jeta Yuto naki kauke na jiggesh korei baniye felechhilo. Dupur shesh hote hote chat-e baro jon member, tin rokom naam, ar porer weekend-e kothay khawa hobe sheta niye torko shuru hoye gelo.

“Eta to ekhoni jhamela,” Sota likhlo.

Airi ekta chhoto khorgosh-er sticker pathalo, jar hate sign-e lekha: please cooperate.

Sota heshe uthlo. Hashi-ta porichito chhilo, kintu ekhon eta bortoman-er—shei chele-r noy, je ekta sentence bolar age shoptaho dhore practice korto.

Porer koyek mash oder message-gulo shadharon hoye gelo. Ora joke share korto, story-te reply dito, ar bondhuder shathe ber hoto. Ora khub kom eka dekha korto; group shob shomoy thakto—restaurant niye torko, train miss, ba emon chhobi tola jekhane keu na keu chokh bondho kore fele. Sota ar Airi bondhu hoye gelo—not purono onubhuti jadur moto muche gechhilo bole noy, borong Sota tar uttor-ke shomman korechhilo ar shomporko-ta shotyi bhabe ja hote pare, sheta hote diyechhilo bole.

Kokhono kono smriti take hotat dhore felto. Kokhono Airi-r bhalo byabohar janalar pasher meyetar kotha mone koriye dito. Kintu se shikhchhilo, kono onubhuti mone pora mane sheta abar pursue korte hobe emon noy.

Samner bochhor-gulo ki niye ashbe se janto na. Ekhon message-er uttor dewa, bondhuder shathe dekha kora, ar nijeder moto kore smriti hoye uthte thaka shadharon din-gulo-i chhilo.

Onubhuti bodle gechhilo.

Kintu smriti-ke bujhte shekhanor golpo tokhono shesh hoy ni.

Dui bochhor por Sota bujhbe, karo bondhu hoye jawa ar shei manusher jagano purono onubhuti theke purota ber hoye asha ek kotha noy.
