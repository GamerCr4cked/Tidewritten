Chapter One Eight Hundred Kilometres

Grishmer shuru holo majhrat porjonto chola phone call diye. Mio tar family-r balcony theke drishsho Akito-ke dekhato; Akito nijer hometown-er shondhar akasher chhobi pathato. Ora joke, voice message, ar protidiner chhoto update share korto, jar fole durotto-ta shamle neowa shombhob mone hoto. Tarpor call-gulo chhoto hote laglo. Mio bollo, family-r shathe dekha-shona niye busy. Or kichhu kaj chhilo, purono bondhuder shathe dekha korte hoto, ar kichhu din kotha bolar moto shokti thakto na. Akito or kotha bishwas korlo. Or kache trust-er mane chhilo protita deri-ke shondeher proman na bhaba. Kintu koyek shoptaho por o online-e ekta chhobi dekhlo. Mio purono bondhuder ekta group-er shathe dariye. Ryo naam-er ekta chele or khub kachhe dariye chhilo, ek haat Mio-r chair-er pichhone rakha. Shudhu chhobi diye kichhu proman hoy na. Akito eta janto. O outing-ta niye jiggesh korlo, cheshta korlo jeno kothata shadharon bhabe bolchhe. Mio bollo, moja hoyechhilo, tarpor Akito-ke bollo beshi bhebe felte na. Kothata or bhabnar cheye beshi koshto dilo. Ekta shadharon proshno-ke emon mone holo jeno dosh-ta Akito-r-i. Ditiyo bochhorer jonno ora Seihoku-te fire ashar por Akito asha korlo, train-er jatra shesh hole durotto-o shesh hoye jabe. Dormitory-r entrance-e Mio oke joriye dhorlo, ar koyek minute-r jonno Akito-r mone holo shob thik ache. Tarpor Mio-r phone beje uthlo. O screen-er dike takiye call dhorar jonno baire chole gelo. Dosh minute por fire elo, mukhe emon ekta hashi ja jeno onek bhebe sajano. “Ke phone korechhilo?” Akito jiggesh korlo. “Ek purono bondhu.” “Ryo?” Or mukher expression bodle gelo. “Tumi amake interrogation korchho keno?” “Ami shudhu jiggesh korchhi, karon amar mone hochhe kichhu ekta bodle gechhe.” “Kichhui bodlayni, Akito. Please, shuru koro na.” Akito sorry bollo, jodio nijer kacheo ar sposto chhilo na je kon bishoyer jonno khoma chaichhe. Shei shondhay o nijer room-e boshe oder message thread dekhte thaklo. Grishmer shurur message-gulo ekhono chhilo—chhoto chhoto detail ar ador bhora kothay bhora. Shobdo-gulo ekhono chhilo. Kintu oder pichhone je shachchhondo chhilo, seta ar chhilo na.

Chapter Two The Third Person

Ryo-r naam aro beshi shona jete laglo. Mio-r chhobir niche comment. Akito-r shathe kotha bolar shomoy emon call, jar uttor o dite parto na. Shesh muhurte bodle jawa plan. Akito Mio-r bondhutto-gulo niyontron korte chayni. O emon manush hote chayni je screenshot chay, ba relationship-ke security system-er moto treat kore. Or dorkar chhilo shotota, ar o bishwas korte chayechhilo je shotota chailei take unreasonable mone hobe na. Ek shondhay Akito station-er kachhe ekta café theke Mio ar Ryo-ke ber hote dekhlo. Ora haat dhore chhilo na. Shudhu ekshathe kachhakachhi hete jacchhilo, Ryo-r phone-e kichhu dekhe hashchhilo. Ora eka howar por Akito bishoyta niye jiggesh korlo. Mio chup hoye gelo. Tarpor shikar korlo, Ryo oke bolechhilo je or Mio-ke bhalo lage. Mio bollo, o confused chhilo, attention-ta or bhalo legechhilo, ar Akito-ke kibhabe bolbe bujhte pareni. “Tomader moddhe kichhu hoyechhilo?” o jiggesh korlo. Mio onekkhon chokh onno dike rekhe roilo. Uttor-ta tukro tukro kore elo: ekta kiss, ekta siddhanto jar jonno or afsos chhilo, ar kichhu message ja o lukiyechhilo karon Akito-ke haranor bhoy peyechhilo. Kono ojuhat-i ghotonagulo-ke nirdosh korte parto na. Akito or kotha shesh howa porjonto shunlo. “Tomake amar bola uchit chhilo,” Mio fisfis kore bollo. “Ha.” “Ami bhoy peyechhilam.” “Bhoy pawar bishoyta ami bujhi.” Nijer kaan-eo Akito-r gola dure theke asha mone holo. “Kintu tumi amake emon ekta bishoy bishwas korte diyechho, ja ashole shotto chhilo na.” Mio kandte shuru korlo. Akito-r oke shantona dite ichchhe korlo; oi instinct-ta shathe shathe elo, ar koshto-o dilo. Or ekhono Mio-r proti care chhilo. Thik shei karonei shotto-ta eto koshto dichhilo. O bollo, or kichhuta space dorkar. Ebar Mio oke shanto hote bollo na, ba ghotonatar kono mane chhilo na emon promise-o korlo na. Shudhu matha narlo. Ora dujon du dike hete chole gelo. Shei rate Akito oke block korlo na, opoman korlo na, ba sheshbarer moto lomba kono speech pathalo na. O phone-ta rekhe dil ar ei bastobota niye boshe roilo je bhalobasha theke jete pare, emonki trust emon bhabe khotigrosto holeo ja shei din thik kora shombhob noy.

Chapter Three The Shape of Silence

Porer koyek shoptaho Akito-r kolponar cheyeo beshi chupchap chhilo. O library-te porto, campus-er charpashe chhobi tullo, ar Kenta-r shathe beshi shomoy katate laglo. O photography-r ekta chhoto series shuru korlo: khali bench, class shesh howar por corridor, janalar upor brishti, ar emon jayga jar moddhe diye manush kheyal na kore chole jay. O series-tar naam dilo *Colours of Silence*. Online-e chhobi-gulo manusher bhalo lagte shuru korlo. Tara chhobi-r atmosphere-er proshongsha korto, ar protita chhobi-r mane ki jiggesh korto. Akito khub kom-i uttor dito. O ekhono nijer feelings bojhate shikhchhilo. Onnodike Mio jor kore kotha bolar cheshta bondho korlo. O ekbar khoma cheye message pathalo, tarpor ek shoptaho por abar ekta. Akito message-gulo porlo, kintu reply dilo na. O Mio-ke shasti dite chayni. Shudhu emon kono uttor or kachhe chhilo na ja shotto hoto. College-e majhe majhe oder corridor-e dekha hoye jeto. Prothome dujon-i chokh firiye nito. Pore Akito chhoto kore matha narte parto. Mio-o eki bhabe uttor dito, or chokhe emon onek kotha thakto ja shonar jonno o Akito-ke bolto na. Ekshomoy ora chhilo shei juti, jader shobai shobshomoy ekshathe dekhte asha korto. Ekhon oder bondhura shikhe gelo, ekjon-er kachhe onnojon-ke niye proshno na korte. Shobcheye kothin chhilo na je Akito ar Mio-r shathe kotha bolte pare na. Kothin chhilo, ekhono kichhu dekhe or mone hoto Mio-r eta bhalo lagto: kono hashyokor poster, vending machine-er notun drink, shiri-te pore thaka ek tukro roder alo. Relationship shesh hole karo proti care ekdom bondho hoye jay na. Eta theke jay, kintu kothay jabe ta jane na.

Chapter Four No More Excuses

Mash kete gelo. Akito abar nijeke sthir mone korte laglo, jodio ghotonar probhab puro puri muche jayni. O bujhte parlo, bhalo din mane Mio-ke bhule jawa noy; ar kharap din mane dure sore jawar siddhanto bhul chhilo, tao noy. Mio Akito-ke uttor dite badhyo korar upay khujte bondho korlo. O Yuna-r shathe shotto kore bollo je o ki korechhe. Ryo, durotto, eka lagar onubhuti, ba Akito-r proshno-ke dosh dilo na. Egulo chhilo thik-i, kintu siddhanto-gulo or nijer chhilo. “Ami ekta koshtokor conversation eriye jete cheyechhilam,” o Yuna-ke bollo. “Tai nijer hate aro eksho-ta kharap conversation toiri kore fellam.” O Ryo-r shathe jogajog bondho korlo ar nijer kora khotir daitto nite shuru korlo. Tate je oke khoma kortei hobe, emon noy. Eta shudhu thik kaj-ta kora. Semester-er shesher dike Akito ar Mio-r library-r baire hotat dekha hoye gelo. Ora eki chhader niche dariye chhilo, pavement-e brishtir tip-tip shobdo hochhilo. “Ami tomake amake khoma korte bolbo na,” Mio bollo. “Shudhu chai tumi jano, ekhon ami bujhi tumi keno chole gechhile.” Akito or dike takalo. Mio-ke klanto dekhachhilo, kintu o Akito-ke nijeke shantona dewar daitto dichhilo na. “Eta bolar jonno dhonnobad,” o uttor dilo. Eta abar mile jawa chhilo na. Kono promise-o chhilo na. Koyek mash-er moddhe prothombar oder majher nirbota kono ostro-r moto mone holo na. Eta emon ekta boundary chhilo, ja bhangar dorkar dujoner karor-i chhilo na. Ora bhodro bhabe alada hoye gelo. Akito brishtir moddhe bari-r dike hete gelo, ja hoyechhilo tar koshto shathe niye, ar ei shanto shosti niye je nijer smritir shathe ar torko korte hochhe na.

Chapter Five Phir Milenge, Without a Promise

Abar grishmo elo. Ebar ora keu phone korlo na. Akito sei notebook-ta khullo, jekhane o prothom Mio-r shathe dekha howar din niye likhechhilo. Prothom diker page-gulo joke, observation, ar protidiner chhoto detail-e bhora chhilo—jegulo oder bondhutto-ke eto shabhabik kore tulechhilo. O shesh ekta entry jog korlo: *Kichhu manush tomake shundor jinis-o dekhay, koshto dewar jinis-o dekhay. Duto-i tomar jiboner ongsho hoye jay. Pore koshto hoyechhilo bole amake emon bhan korte hobe na je shundor muhurto-gulo mithye chhilo.* O notebook bondho korlo. Mio nijer diary-te erokom ekta kotha likhlo, jodio Akito ki likhechhe ta or jana chhilo na. Mutual friend-der kachhe Akito kemon ache jiggesh kora o bondho kore dilo. O asha korto Akito bhalo ache. Kintu o mene nilo, Akito-r bhalo thakar asha korlei tar jibone nijer jayga dabi korar odhikar hoy na. Notun academic year shuru hole corridor-e oder abar dekha holo. Akito oke chhoto, mon theke asha ekta nod dilo. Mio-o eki bhabe uttor dilo. Ora nijeder pothe hete chole gelo. Eta oder keu je ending kolpona korechhilo, sheta chhilo na. Kono shesh jhogra holo na, perfect apology holo na, ar ora ek muhurte ager manush hoye gelo na. Oder relationship shesh hoyechhilo, ar tar porinam-gulo bastob chhilo. Tobuo prothom bochhor-ta ghotechhilo. Hashi shotto chhilo. Brishti shotto chhilo. Pore ja hoyechhilo, ta oder moddhe thaka bhalobasha-makha onubhuti muche dite pareni. Akito bag-e camera niye library-r dike ghure gelo. Mio notebook buker niche dhore class-er dike gelo. Ora keu janto na, kokhono abar emon bhabe dekha hobe ki na jar kono mane thakbe. Ekhon ora uttor chara bachte shikhchhilo. *Phir milenge* age ekta promise chhilo je durotto taratari shesh hobe. Ekhon er mane aro chhoto, aro shotto: jibon lomba, manush bodlay, ar future-er kachh theke keu abar dekha howar promise jor kore nite pare na. Golpo-ta shesh hoyni. Kintu er ei ongsho-ta shesh hoye gechhe. Shotto beriye ashar por ora ar ager relationship-e fire jete parto na.
