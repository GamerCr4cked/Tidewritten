PrologueThe Girl Who Hated Everyone

Hanako Shimizu ko teen cheezon se nafrat thi. Incompetence. Unnecessary noise. Aur boys. Isi order mein—halanki kabhi-kabhi woh list ko rearrange kar leti thi, depending on ki saamne wala ladka kitna irritating tha. Seiran High School mein, jahan entrance par indoor shoes change karne padte the aur student council lagbhag har complaint ke liye form rakhta tha, Hanako ki reputation morning bell se bhi tez phailti thi. School mein boys ki kami nahi thi. Zyadaatar noisy the, kuch incompetent, aur lagbhag sabko lagta tha ki Hanako ka ignore karna unke liye aur zyada try karne ka invitation hai. Student council president hone ke naate usne ek reliable system develop kiya tha. Woh unhe dekhti. Woh chup ho jaate. Woh dobara dekhti. Woh sorry bol dete. Efficient system tha. Student council handbook intimidation ko official administrative technique nahi maanta tha. Hanako ko lagta tha yeh handbook ki serious oversight thi. Phir Kaito Mizuno aaya. Usne Hanako ko stare nahi kiya, charming introduction dene ki koshish nahi ki, boyfriend ke baare mein nahi poochha, aur books carry karne ki offer bhi nahi di. Bas classroom mein aaya, uske paas wali empty seat par baitha, bag desk ke neeche rakha aur book khol li. Hanako ne wait kiya. Kuch nahi hua. Usne posture adjust kiya. Phir bhi kuch nahi. Aakhir woh uski taraf mudi. “Tumhe pata hai main kaun hoon?” Kaito ne nazar uthayi. “Haan.” “Then?” “Aur tum mere paas baithi ho.” Hanako use stare karti rahi. Kaito wapas book padhne laga. Hanako ki internal committee turant meeting mein aa gayi. Chairperson furious thi. Committee mein sirf Hanako thi. Saalon baad pehli baar use aisa ladka mila jo uski existence mein bilkul interested nahi lag raha tha. Usne decide kiya ki woh usse nafrat karti hai. Perfectly reasonable decision. Kam se kam woh khud ko yahi batati thi. Use abhi coffee cans, hospital appointments, bag mein chhupi medication, ya Kaito ke “I'm fine” ke peeche chhupa quiet fear nahi pata tha. Kaito ko bhi nahi pata tha ki uske paas baithi yeh ladki, jo uski school life miserable banane par tuli lagti thi, ek din crowded hallway mein uske footsteps pehchanne wali insaan banegi. Abhi ke liye woh bas ek desk share karne wale do students the. Ek sabse nafrat karti thi. Doosra khud ko overlook karwana achhi tarah seekh chuka tha. Dono honesty mein khaas achhe nahi the. ---

Chapter OneThe Boy Who Didn't Chase Her

“Question seven,” history teacher ne board par marker tap karte hue kaha. “Alliance kyun collapse hua?” Class mein silence chha gaya. Window ke paas ek student notebook search karne ka drama karne laga. Peeche kisi ne cough kiya. Hiro Takahashi, jo exercise book ke margin mein dragon bana raha tha, idhar-udhar dekhne laga jaise answer kisi chair ke neeche chhupa ho. Hanako ne haath uthaya, phir neeche kar liya. Use answer pata tha, obviously. Hamesha pata hota tha. Lekin teacher Kaito ko dekh rahe the. “Try karoge?” Kaito ne notes se nazar uthayi. “Alliance ek disagreement ki wajah se collapse nahi hua. Disagreements pehle se the. Last dispute ne bas yeh pretend karna impossible bana diya ki woh exist nahi karte.” Teacher ne use continue karne ko kaha. Kaito ne economic pressure, conflicting goals, aur temporary cooperation ko genuine trust samajhne ki galti explain ki. Usne dramatic banne ki koshish nahi ki. Bas use topic samajh aata tha. “Excellent,” teacher ne kaha. Kuch students uski taraf mude. Hiro ne apna dragon drawing Kaito ki taraf ghumaya, jaise creature ko bhi historic event witness karna chahiye. Dragon ne lesson mein Hiro se zyada contribute kiya tha. Kaito notes par wapas aa gaya. Hanako ki fingers pen ke around tight ho gayin. *Kam se kam khud se pleased toh lag sakta tha.* Class ke baad Hiro uske desk ke paas aaya. “Congratulations. Tumne hum sabko academically unemployed dikha diya.” “Mujhe nahi pata tha yeh competition hai.” “Ab hai. Authorities ko complaint file karunga.” “Kaunsi authorities?” “Meri imagination wali. Bahut strict hain.” Kaito ke lips halka sa move hue. Hiro ne notice kar liya. “Wait, smile tha? Date record karo!” “Mat karo.” “Too late. Tumhare grandchildren ko bataunga.” Hanako ne book snap karke band ki. “Kuch logon ke paas impressive free time hai.” Hiro uski taraf muda. “Thank you. Main cultivate karta hoon.” “Compliment nahi tha.” “Main ise compliment hi receive karunga.” “Tum exhausting ho.” “And yet, here we are.” Hanako ne Kaito se kaha ki friends carefully choose karne chahiye. Kaito ne jawab diya, “Woh tumhare sochne jitna bura nahi hai.” Hiro ek pal ke liye chup ho gaya, phir bola, “Dekha? Best friend mujh par believe karta hai.” “Hum chaar din se jaante hain ek doosre ko,” Kaito ne kaha. “Bond already unbreakable hai.” Kaito ne usse pencil wapas maangi. Hiro ne kaha friendship complicated hoti hai. Hanako unhe jaate hue dekhti rahi. Kaito kam bolta tha, popularity ya romance mein khaas interested nahi lagta tha, phir bhi usne Hiro ko bina hesitation defend kiya. Loudly nahi, hero ban kar nahi—bas apni baat keh kar. Hanako ko yeh pasand nahi aaya. Mostly kyunki use samajh nahi aa raha tha ki is baat ka kare kya. ---

Chapter TwoAn Extremely Unfortunate Lunch

Cafeteria crowded thi, aur Hanako ko crowded places se nafrat thi: noise, careless elbows, raaste ke beech rukne wale log, aur woh students jo queue ko competitive sport bana dete the. Seiran ki lunch queue ke teen unofficial rules the: move karte raho, tray protect karo, aur us student par kabhi trust mat karo jo kahe, “Main bas friend ke liye place save kar raha hoon.” Croquette wale ek ugly incident ke baad Hanako ne yeh rule apni notebook mein likha tha. Woh tray lekar khadi thi jab peeche kisi ka balance bigda. Ek shoulder usse takraya, uska shoe slide hua, aur ek humiliating second ke liye floor uski taraf uthta hua laga. Phir kisi ne uska arm pakad liya. “Careful.” Kaito. Of course. Hanako ne balance sambhala aur turant khud ko chhuda liya. “Mere control mein tha.” “Naturally.” Uska expression itna neutral tha ki Hanako samajh nahi paayi woh agree kar raha hai ya mazaak uda raha hai. Usne second option choose kiya. “Khud par itna pleased mat lagna.” “Main nahi hoon.” “Toh aise dekhna band karo.” “Kaisa?” “Waisa.” Kaito ne apne face ki taraf dekha, jaise explanation wahin mil jaayegi. Hiro teen logon ke liye kaafi food lekar aa gaya. “Fight ho rahi hai? Excellent. Main lunch aur bilkul useless advice laaya hoon.” “Go away,” Hanako ne kaha. “Aaj tumne mujhse sabse achhi baat kahi.” Kaito ne ek student ko pass hone dene ke liye side li. Hanako jaane lagi toh uski tray tilt hui aur milk carton edge ki taraf slide hua. Kaito ne girne se pehle pakad kar wapas rakh diya. “Second time.” Hanako ki aankhein narrow ho gayin. “Kuch kehna hai?” “Particularly nahi.” “Toh mat kaho.” “Okay.” Woh chala gaya. Na smug grin, na teasing, na us moment ko saath time spend karne ka excuse banane ki koshish. Hanako cafeteria ke beech tray pakde khadi rahi. *Woh baar-baar aisa kyun karta hai?* Aur zyada irritating question yeh tha ki woh chahti kyun thi ki Kaito ruk kar answer de? Us afternoon usne classroom se Hiro ki awaaz suni: “Tumhe pata hai, jab tum use ignore karte ho toh woh upset hoti hai?” Kaito ne kaha, “Main use ignore nahi karta. Jab woh mujhse baat karti hai, main answer deta hoon.” “Same thing nahi hai.” Kaito ne maana ki use nahi pata Hanako usse kya chahti hai. Hiro ne kaha, “Maybe use khud bhi nahi pata.” Kaito ne book band ki. “Jo bhi ho, shayad main woh person nahi hoon jiski use zaroorat hai.” Hanako door ke bahar ruk gayi. Hiro ne kaha Kaito hamesha doosron ko batane se pehle decide kar leta hai ki unhe kya chahiye. Kaito ne jawab diya ki woh Hanako ki life aur complicated nahi banana chahta. Hanako wahan se chali gayi. Stairs tak pahunchte-pahunchte usne decision le liya: Kaito effort ke layak nahi tha. Agar use uski attention nahi chahiye, toh use milegi bhi nahi. Usne ghar tak yahi decision repeat kiya. ---

Chapter ThreeAn Apology with Poor Timing

Teen din tak Hanako ne Kaito se baat nahi ki. Uski taraf dekha bhi nahi—at least deliberately nahi. Bas usne notice kiya ki woh kab late aata hai, last lesson mein aankhein rub karta hai, aur bag ko usual se thoda paas pakad kar classroom se nikalta hai. In observations ka koi matlab nahi tha. Woh student council president thi; cheezein notice karna job ka part tha. Probably. Thursday afternoon Kaito library mein uske paas aaya. “Shimizu?” Woh padhte rahi. “What?” “Tumse baat kar sakta hoon?” “If you must.” Kaito saamne baith gaya. Kuch pal sahi words dhoondhne ke baad usne kaha, “I'm sorry.” Hanako ne page turn kiya. “Kis liye?” “Tumhe yeh feel karwane ke liye ki main tumhe paas nahi chahta. Mujhe nahi pata tum mujhse kya expect karti thi. Abhi bhi nahi pata. Lekin tumhare baare mein aise baat nahi karni chahiye thi jaise tumhari feelings inconvenience hon.” Hanako ne kai din baad use properly dekha. Woh smile nahi kar raha tha, na charm karke trouble se nikalne ki koshish. Bas uncomfortable tha—reassuringly familiar. “Terrible apology thi,” usne kaha. “I know.” “Khud ko explain bhi nahi kiya.” “Kar sakta hoon. Bas nahi lagta explanation se meri baat better ho jaayegi.” Hanako ne use stare kiya. Apology ke liye usne kai replies prepare kiye the, lekin is reply ke liye nahi. “Fine. I accept.” Kaito ne nod kiya, thank you kaha aur khada ho gaya. “Wait.” Woh ruk gaya. Hanako ko khud se nafrat hui ki usne yeh kaha. “Sorry kyun bola?” “Kyunki main galat tha.” “Bas?” “Aur kya?” Hanako ne book ki taraf dekha. “Nothing. Go away.” Woh chala gaya. Hanako use shelves ke beech disappear hote dekhti rahi. Woh chahti thi Kaito argue kare, defend kare, ya kuch aisa kahe jisse woh angry reh sake. Lekin usne apology di aur use akela chhod diya. Yeh pattern banta ja raha tha. Aur Hanako ko shak hone laga ki uski misery ka reason sirf gussa nahi tha. ---

Chapter FourOne Can of Coffee

Monday ko midterm results lage. Hanako ke 96 aur Kaito ke 98 the. Hiro results board, Kaito aur apne paper ko dekh kar education system ke khilaaf appeal karne laga. Hanako ne list dobara check ki, phir verify kiya ki teacher ne names reverse toh nahi kar diye. Nahi kiye the. “Two points,” woh mutter hui. Hiro ne memorial service organize karne ka suggestion diya. Hanako ne poochha kya woh marna chahta hai; Hiro ne kaha lunch ke baad tak postpone karna pasand karega. Kaito ne Hanako ko congratulate kiya. “Good score hai.” Hanako ne poochha woh mock kar raha hai ya gloat. Kaito ne dono se inkaar kiya. “Toh smile kyun kar rahe ho?” Hanako ne poochha. “Main nahi kar raha.” Woh sach mein nahi kar raha tha. Yahi problem thi. Hanako march karke chali gayi. Agli subah Kaito ke desk par black coffee ka can tha—no sugar, uski usual choice. Koi note nahi tha. Hiro usse dekh kar unbearable delight ke saath grin kar raha tha. “Don't,” Kaito ne kaha. “Maine kuch kaha hi nahi.” “Abhi kehne wale ho.” Hiro ne mysterious beverages ki new school tradition ke baare mein socha. Kaito ne can khola aur sip liya. “Coffee hai,” usne kaha. Room ke doosri taraf Hanako papers organize karne ka pretend kar rahi thi. Usne Kaito ki taraf ek baar bhi nahi dekha. Haan, usne notice kiya ki usne poora can finish kar liya. Us afternoon library mein Hanako ne poochha coffee theek thi? Kaito ne pehle pretend kiya ki use pata nahi kaunsi coffee, phir kaha achhi thi aur thank you bola. Hanako ka face warm ho gaya. “Mujhe nahi pata tum kis baare mein baat kar rahe ho.” “All right.” Usne Kaito ko random cheezein accept karne mein careful rehne ko kaha, aur assume na karne ko ki coffee ka koi meaning hai. Kaito ne dono baatein maan li aur book par wapas chala gaya. Hanako ki internal committee phir meeting mein aa gayi. Woh chahti thi Kaito poochhe kisne coffee rakhi, kyun rakhi, aur ise difficult banaye. Lekin usne bas accept ki, thank you kaha, aur kuch demand nahi kiya. Use nafrat thi ki Kaito ki care karna itna easy lagta tha. Ek week baad usne doosra can chhoda. Is baar Kaito ne empty can turant phenkne ke bajay notebook ke paas rakh diya. Hanako ne notice kiya. Usne pretend kiya ki nahi kiya. ---

Chapter FiveThe Distance Between Two Desks

Cleaning duty simple honi chahiye thi: desks wipe karo, floor sweep karo, chairs wapas rakho. Lekin Kaito chair move karte waqt apna shoulder desk ke edge se takra baitha. Hanako ne chair, phir Kaito, phir chair ko dekha. “Furniture se fight kaise haar gaye?” “Surprise attack tha.” “Iske chaar legs hain. Surprise dene ki capacity nahi.” Chot serious nahi thi, lekin Kaito quiet ho gaya. Hanako ne poochha kya hua. Usne “nothing” kaha, toh Hanako ne yaad dilaya ki question jhoot bolne ka invitation nahi hota. Usne sleeve hata kar shoulder dikhaya. Bruise ban raha tha. Hanako ka annoyed expression badal gaya; usne first-aid kit laakar scrape clean ki aur bandage lagaya. Kaito ne kaha use yeh karne ki zaroorat nahi. “I know,” usne jawab diya. “Tum angry lag rahi ho.” “Ho bhi.” “Mujhse?” “Yes.” Kaito ne sorry kaha. Hanako ne bandage thoda zyada firmly press kar diya; woh wince hua. Phir usne softer voice mein sorry kaha. “Tumhe aisa act karne ki zaroorat nahi ki kuch matter nahi karta,” Hanako ne kaha. Kaito ne inkaar kiya, lekin Hanako ne kaha woh aise behave karta hai jaise kal disappear ho jaaye toh sab apna din continue kar denge. Use turant regret hua. Woh Kaito ki life ke baare mein bahut kam jaanti thi: test scores, preferred coffee, noisy cafeteria se dislike. Medical appointments kyun hote the, bag mein medication kyun rakhta tha, kuch din stairs chadhkar exhausted kyun lagta tha—use kuch pata nahi tha. Phir bhi lagta tha Kaito ne jaan-boojhkar sabko uske baare mein kam se kam jaankari di thi. “Forget it,” usne mutter kiya. Kaito ne thank you kaha. Hanako ne use ghar jaakar rest karne ko kaha aur shoulder ke saath stupid kuch na karne ki warning di. Door ke paas Kaito ne use roka. “Mujhe nahi lagta main disappear ho sakta hoon aur koi notice na kare.” Hanako still ho gayi. “Bas mujhe hamesha nahi pata hota ki jab koi notice karta hai toh karna kya chahiye.” Pehli baar Hanako chahti thi ki uske paas gussa hone ke reasons jitni aasani se sahi words bhi aa jaayein. ---

Use nahi chahiye tha ki Kaito ko trouble ho sirf isliye kyunki use lagta hai har cheez chhupani padegi. Hiro ne kaha, “Jab woh sun sake, tab use yeh kehna.” Hanako ne nod kiya. Filhaal woh bas itna promise kar sakti thi. ---

Chapter SevenA Kiss Is Not a Cure

Medical advice aur recovery plan follow karne ke baad Kaito kuch din baad school lauta. Woh tired lag raha tha. Hanako ne notice kiya ki woh baithne se pehle hesitate kar raha tha, jaise use lag raha ho ki Hanako aise questions poochhegi jin ke answers use nahi pata. Class ke baad Hanako use courtyard le gayi. “Tumne mujhe dara diya,” usne kaha. “I'm sorry.” “Sorry bolna band karo jaise usse sab fix ho jaata hai.” Kaito chup ho gaya. Hanako ne kaha use uski poori medical history jaanne ki zaroorat nahi, aur woh un details ko share karne ke liye force nahi karegi jin ke liye woh ready nahi. Lekin agar woh trouble mein ho, toh use kisi aise insaan ko batana chahiye jo help kar sake. Kaito ne agree kiya. Hanako ne poochha us din tabiyat kharab thi toh bataya kyun nahi. “Mujhe laga handle kar lunga,” usne kaha. “Aur agar nahi kar paate?” Kaito ke paas answer nahi tha. Hanako ne kaha use nahi pata woh dono abhi ek doosre ke liye kya hain—shayad friends bhi nahi. Lekin woh nahi chahti thi ki Kaito uske saamne fine hone ka pretend kare. “Why?” Kaito ne poochha. Woh challenge nahi kar raha tha; use genuinely samajh nahi aaya. “Kyunki mujhe care hai, idiot,” Hanako se nikal gaya. Kaito still ho gaya. “Main nahi jaanta iske saath karna kya hai,” usne dheere se kaha. “Tumhe kuch karna nahi. Unka first kiss brief aur awkward tha—answer se zyada question jaisa. Alag hote hi dono startled the. “Yeh—” Kaito ne shuru kiya. “Don't.” “Main kuch kehne wala bhi nahi tha.” “Good.” Kaito ne kaha usne yeh expect nahi kiya tha. Hanako ka face red ho gaya. Usne use lightly arm par maara, Kaito hans pada, aur is baar Hanako ko use rokne ki zaroorat nahi lagi. Kiss ne kuch cure nahi kiya. Uski medical condition change nahi hui, aur na hi unka fear erase hua. Lekin ek chhoti cheez badli: pehli baar dono ne maana ki unke beech ka distance pehle jaisa nahi raha. Ab aage kya hoga, woh nahi jaante the. Filhaal, itna kaafi tha. ---

Kaito agle week school lauta. Pehle se better lag raha tha, halanki aankhon ke neeche shadows thi. Class khaali hone par Hanako uske desk ke paas gayi aur poochha ab kaisa feel kar raha hai. “Last week se better.” “Better ka matlab well nahi.” “No.” Hanako ne honesty appreciate ki, chahe usse yeh pasand karna annoying lagta tha. Usne medical appointments ke baare mein poochha. Kaito ka expression badla. Itna kaafi tha. ---

InterludeHiro Knows When to Stop Joking

Hiro hamesha maanta tha ki friendship timing ke baare mein hai: kab bolna, kab chup rehna, kab joke kisi ko embarrassment se bacha sakta hai aur kab wahi joke use aur chhota feel karwa dega. Kaito ke saath usne yeh difference seekhne mein saal lagaye. Dono younger the tab mile the, jab Kaito sochta tha ki sick hone ko mehnat karke outwork kiya ja sakta hai. Hiro ko hospital visits, cancelled plans aur late replies yaad the—“Sorry, something came up.” Pehle use lagta tha Kaito hang out nahi karna chahta. Phir use truth pata chala. Uske baad Hiro careful ho gaya—gentle nahi, kyunki Kaito fragile treat hona hate karta tha; bas careful. Jab Kaito ko normal feel karna hota, Hiro jokes karta. Jab questions zyada ho jaate, topic change karta. Har absence ka explanation demand nahi karta. Lekin privacy respect karna aur kisi ko disappear karne mein help karna alag baatein hain. Hiro ko yeh samajhne mein waqt laga. Hospital wale din usne Kaito ki mother ko call kiya. Woh bas jaana chahta tha ki Kaito safe hai. Unhone bataya doctors uska dhyan rakh rahe hain. Hiro ne poochha kya aisa aksar hota hai. “Jitna woh logon ko batata hai, usse zyada,” unhone kaha. Unhone explain kiya ki Kaito doosron ki worry karta hai. Use lagta hai difficult parts chhupane se doosron ko fikr nahi hogi. Lekin worry karna aur hurt hona same nahi; kabhi logon ko tumhari care karne ka chance chahiye, chahe woh kuch fix na kar sakein. Kaito school lauta toh Hiro ne explanation demand nahi ki. Bas uske saath chalta raha. Kaito ne baad mein kaha, “Tumne meri mother ko call kiya.” “Haan.” “Kyun?” “Kyunki main worried tha.” “Tum mujhse poochh sakte the.” “Tum exactly available nahi the.” Kaito chup raha. Hiro ruk gaya. “Suno. Main tumhari har baat jaanna nahi chahta. Tumhare medical records ya private thoughts par mera right nahi. Lekin agar tum disappear ho jao aur mujhe lage tum trouble mein ho, toh main care karunga. Tum mujhe usse mana nahi kar sakte.” Kaito startled lag raha tha. “Main control nahi kar raha,” Hiro ne kaha. “Main bata raha hoon tum mere friend ho. Dono alag baatein hain.” Kaito ke paas clever response nahi tha. Dono chalne lage. “Surprisingly well put,” Kaito ne kaha. “Surprisingly?” “Main sincere tha.” “Ruin mat karo.” Kaito hans pada. Hiro ne decide kiya ki filhaal conversation ne wahi kiya jo zaroori tha. Sab fix nahi hua tha. Bas ek door khula tha. ---

Chapter TenThe Truth About Breathing

Kuch din baad Kaito ka scheduled medical follow-up tha. Hanako uske saath jaane ko kehna chahti thi, lekin usne samjha ki chahne se kisi cheez ka haq automatically nahi milta. Usne message kiya: *Appointment skip mat karna.* Kaito ka reply aaya: *Plan nahi tha.* *Good.* Thodi der baad uska doosra message aaya: *Baad mein bataun appointment kaisa gaya?* Hanako screen ko do baar padh kar dekhti rahi. *Sirf agar tum chaho toh.* *I do.* Us evening Kaito ne bataya doctors ne treatment plan ka ek hissa adjust kiya hai aur symptoms worsen hon toh prescribed instructions follow karne par zor diya. Usne har detail share nahi ki; Hanako ne demand bhi nahi ki. Dono station ke paas bench par baithe commuters ko dekh rahe the, jab Kaito ne kiss ke baare mein baat shuru ki. Hanako ka drink girte-girte bacha. “Woh kyun la rahe ho?” “Kyunki baat karni chahiye.” “Fine. Bolo.” Kaito ne kaha kiss ne uske lungs theek nahi kiye. Agar tabiyat kharab thi, toh medical help chahiye thi, kiss nahi. Hanako ne nazar neeche ki aur kaha, “Obviously.” “Main tumhare paas hona chahta tha. Honest answer yahi hai.” Hanako still ho gayi. Kaito ne kaha woh nervous tha aur feelings explain karna nahi jaanta tha—kabhi-kabhi abhi bhi nahi jaanta. Lekin woh nahi chahta tha ki Hanako soche use rescue karna uski responsibility hai, ya uske paas hone ka matlab un cheezon ki responsibility lena hai jinhe woh control nahi kar sakti. Hanako chup rahi. Usne yeh conversation kai baar imagine ki thi, lekin Kaito itna direct hoga, nahi socha tha. “Tumse angry rehna difficult bana rahe ho,” woh mutter hui. “Sorry.” “Apologize karna band karo.” “Right.” Hanako ne dheere se apna haath aage kiya. Kaito ne poochhne ki koshish ki, toh usne kaha, “Mujhse har cheez explain mat karwao.” Kaito ne uska haath pakad liya. Uski fingers warm thi. Kuch der tak dono chup rahe. Train aayi toh woh saath chadhe aur window ke paas do seats par baith gaye. Hanako ne uska haath nahi chhoda. Kaito ne bhi nahi kaha. Bahar city evening light ki streaks mein peeche chhoot rahi thi. Pehli baar unhe clever kuch kehne ki zaroorat nahi lagi. ---

Chapter ElevenThe Debt That Didn't Exist

“Saturday ko tum mujhe bahar le ja rahe ho,” Hanako ne kaha. Kaito notebook se nazar uthakar ruk gaya. “Kya main?” “Yes.” “Kahan?” “Abhi decide nahi kiya.” “Toh kaise pata main le ja raha hoon?” Hanako ne desk par dono haath rakhe. “Kyunki tum mere owed ho.” “Kis liye?” “Worry karwane ke liye.” Kaito ne socha. “Woh debt nahi hai.” “Ab hai.” “Formal repayment schedule hai?” “Saturday, ten in the morning. Late mat hona.” Woh aur questions ka chance diye bina chali gayi. Hiro, jo next desk se sab sun raha tha, Kaito ko congratulate karne laga: apparently uski girlfriend ka legal system bilkul alag tha. “Woh meri girlfriend nahi hai,” Kaito ne kaha. Hiro ne use dekha, phir door ko, phir wapas use. “Sure.” Saturday ko clear sky aur mild breeze thi. Hanako ne previous evening outfit choose karne mein time lagaya tha, phir morning mein khud ko convince kiya ki usne outfit choose karne mein time nahi lagaya. Kaito 9:55 par station pahuncha. Hanako already wahan thi. “Tum early ho,” usne kaha. “Tum bhi.” “Main check kar rahi thi ki tum late na ho.” “Of course.” Dono shopping district mein chale, bookstore gaye, phir small café mein baith gaye. Hanako ne tea order ki, Kaito ne coffee. Usne cup ko dekhkar kaha, “Predictable ho.” “Tumhe pata tha main kya order karunga.” “Yeh nahi kaha ki buri baat hai.” Unhone school, Hiro ki student council theories aur Kaito ki book par baat ki. Day ordinary tha, aur yahi Hanako ko surprise kar raha tha. Usne awkwardness ke liye arguments aur emergency topics prepare kiye the, lekin conversation expected se easy thi. Bahar nikalne par Kaito quiet ho gaya. Hanako ne poochha tired hai? Usne kaha thoda. “Baithna hai?” Kaito ne question dismiss nahi kiya. “Haan, actually.” Dono park ke bench par baith gaye. Hanako ne use tired hona band karne ko nahi kaha, na outing ruin karne ka blame diya. Bas uske ready hone tak saath baithi. Kaito ne thank you kaha. Hanako ne kaha debt abhi repay nahi hua, use dobara le jaana padega. Kaito ne expensive hone ki complaint ki; Hanako ne kaha places woh choose karegi. “Aur bhi expensive lag raha hai.” Woh hans pade. Phir Kaito ne kaha, “Kisi ne mujhse kabhi nahi poochha ki mujhe baithna hai ya nahi, bina aisa feel karwaye ki maine kuch fail kar diya.” Usne bataya kuch din easy hote hain, kuch nahi. Woh lambe waqt se difficult days ko nonexistent dikhane ki koshish karta aaya hai. “Main aisa karte rehna nahi chahta,” usne kaha. “Tumhare saath nahi.” Hanako ne kaha, “Good.” Kaito ne poochha bas itna? Hanako ne kaha speech invent karne ko mat bolo. Phir usne sach kaha: “Record ke liye, tum mujh par worry karne ka debt nahi rakhte.” “Tumne kaha tha rakhta hoon.” “Jhoot bola tha.” “Disappointing.” “You're welcome.” Hanako use dekhti rahi. Woh abhi ready nahi thi woh words kehne ke liye jo hafton se avoid kar rahi thi. Lekin ab woh pehle jitna nahi darti thi. ---

Chapter TwelveStay With Me

Monday morning ek broken pen se shuru hui. Hanako student council ke liye note likh rahi thi jab pen ne kaam karna band kar diya. Usne do baar click kiya, jaise formal warning se pen maan jaayega. Nahi maana. Zyada press kiya toh tip snap ho gayi. Hanako ne pen ko dekha, phir classroom ke across Kaito ko, jo Hiro se baat karte hue hans raha tha. *This is ridiculous.* Woh do din se Saturday ke baare mein soch rahi thi—not café, walk, ya Kaito ka thank you. Use yaad aa raha tha ki Kaito ne kaha tha woh fine hone ka pretend karna band karna chahta hai. Hanako uski honest life ka part banna chahti thi—not nurse, not rescuer, aur na hi koi aisa person jise paas rehne ke liye useful hona zaroori ho. Bas khud. Lunch mein usne Kaito ko courtyard mein dhoondha. “Mere saath aao.” “Kahan?” “Private jagah.” Kaito ki eyebrows uthi. Hanako ka face warm hua. “Woh matlab nahi! Jahan baat kar sakein!” “Maine kuch kaha hi nahi.” “Tum soch rahe the.” “Nahi.” “Liar.” Woh use school building ke quiet side le gayi. Kuch seconds tak arms fold karke pavement dekhti rahi. Kaito wait karta raha—uski sabse irritating habit. “Kaito.” “Yes?” “Main soch rahi thi.” “Sounds serious.” “It's serious.” Hanako ne breath li. “I like you.” Awaaz bahut quiet thi, toh usne dobara kaha, “Maine kaha I like you.” “Sun liya.” “Toh answer kyun nahi kar rahe?” “Kyunki interrupt nahi karna chahta.” Hanako use kuch phenkne jaisi nazar se dekhne lagi. Kaito wisely chup raha. Hanako ne kaha woh use pasand karti hai, lekin woh frustrating hai, cheezein chhupata hai, use worry karwata hai aur uski expectations ke mutabik react nahi karta. Use pata hai ki woh uski problems fix nahi kar sakti, uski health ki responsibility nahi hai, aur Kaito ko uske liye fine hone ka pretend nahi karna chahiye. “Lekin main wahan rehna chahti hoon. Truth sunna chahti hoon, difficult ho tab bhi. Jab tum baat nahi karna chaho tab tumhare saath baithna chahti hoon. Coffee peena, stupid cheezon par argue karna aur—” Woh ruk gayi. Kaito ka expression soft ho gaya, aur sab aur difficult ho gaya. Hanako ne nazar hata li. “So stay with me.” Silence. “Sirf aaj nahi. Mera matlab—” Usne aankhein band ki. “Let's be together.” Words ab wapas nahi liye ja sakte the. Kaito use aise dekh raha tha jaise usne koi precious cheez di ho aur woh use gira dene se darr raha ho. “Say something!” Hanako ne kaha. Kaito ne breath li. “I like you, too.” Hanako use stare karne lagi. “Phir aisa face kyun bana rahe ho?” “Kyunki nahi socha tha tum pehle kahogi.” “Why not?” “Tum usually mujhe cheezein phenkne ki dhamki deti ho.” “That's not an answer!” Kaito halki si hansi hansa, phir serious ho gaya. “Main tumhare saath rehna chahta hoon. Lekin mujhe tumhe apni life mein aane dena seekhna hoga. Kabhi galti karunga.” “Main bhi.” Kaito nahi chahta tha Hanako uske liye responsible feel kare. Hanako ne kaha, “Mujhe tumhari responsibility nahi chahiye. Main tumhare saath rehna chahti hoon.” Kaito ne nod kiya. “I think I can do that.” Hanako ne use hug kar liya. Awkward hug tha: usne bahut tight pakda, Kaito ne return karne se pehle hesitate kiya, aur dono ko haath kahan rakhne hain nahi pata tha. Perfect tha—not graceful hone ki wajah se, balki kyunki dono ne pretend nahi kiya ki yeh kuch aur hai. Alag hone par Hanako ne poochha, “Can I kiss you?” Kaito smile kiya. “Yes.” Unka second kiss pehle se gentle tha. Usne kuch solve nahi kiya aur har din easy hone ka promise bhi nahi diya. Bas us cheez ki shuruaat thi jise dono ne choose kiya. Hanako ne uske forehead par apna forehead briefly rest kiya. “Kisi ko bataya toh main sab deny kar dungi.” “Understood.” “Smile karna band karo.” “Try kar raha hoon.” “Harder try karo.” Woh hans pada. Hanako bhi smile kiye bina nahi reh paayi. Usne uska haath pakda aur dono classroom ki taraf chale. Future kya laayega, dono nahi jaante the. Lekin is baar koi pretend nahi kar raha tha ki unhe akela face karna padega. ---

EpilogueOne Can, Two Straws, and a Broken Pen

Unke relationship ka pehla week surprisingly ordinary tha. Kaito ab bhi umbrella lana bhoolta tha. Hanako ab bhi uski handwriting complain karti thi. Hanako spare umbrella carry karne lagi—weather-related risk management ke liye, obviously. Ki woh hamesha Kaito ki preferred type hoti thi, coincidence tha jiske baare mein woh baat nahi karti thi. Hiro ne 48 hours ke andar truth discover kar liya aur aise react kiya jaise romance usne personally orchestrate kiya ho. “I knew it!” Hanako ne uski taraf finger point ki. “Tumhe kuch nahi pata tha.” “Mujhe sab pata tha.” “Tumne socha tha woh mujhe eraser lend kare toh hum shaadi kar lenge.” “Meaningful eraser tha.” Kaito ne head shake kiya. “Mujhe doosra friend chahiye.” “Denied,” Hiro bola. “Application rejected.” Kuch cheezein badli thi. Kaito ab rest ki zaroorat hone par batane laga, automatically fine kehne ke bajay. Kabhi-kabhi woh worry hone par withdraw karta; Hanako uski silence ko rejection samajh leti; Kaito kabhi uske anger ko chale jaane ka signal samajhta. Woh argue karte. Sorry bolte. Seekhte. Koi overnight perfect partner nahi bana. Ek afternoon Hanako do coffee cans lekar aayi aur ek Kaito ke saamne rakh diya. “Yeh ek aur debt hai?” usne poochha. “Maybe.” “Is baar kya owed hai?” Hanako uske paas baith gayi. “Nothing. Bas laana chahti thi.” Kaito ne can ko haath mein ghumaya, phir bag se chhota box nikala. Andar kuch empty coffee cans, folded paper scraps aur ek broken pen tha. Hanako ne pen pehchan liya—wahi jo confession wali morning snap hua tha. “Tumne ise rakha?” “Yes.” “Kyun?” “Kyunki yaad rakhna chahta tha.” “Kya?” Kaito ne uski taraf dekha. “Ki tum bhi scared thi.” Hanako ke paas reply nahi tha. Is baar usne invent karne ki koshish nahi ki. Desk ke neeche uska haath pakda; Kaito ne wapas pakad liya. Class ke doosri taraf Hiro ne grin karna shuru kiya. Hanako ne bina mude free hand se use point kiya. “Don't.” Hiro dheere se notebook ke peeche chhup gaya. Kaito hans pada. Hanako ne poochha kyun smile kar raha hai. “Because I'm happy.” “Stupid reason.” “Probably.” Hanako ne apna shoulder uske shoulder se laga diya. Bahar school bell baji. Corridor students se bhar gaya. Koi missing homework ke baare mein chilla raha tha; Hiro kisi innocent classmate par pencil churaane ka accusation laga raha tha. World continue kar rahi thi. Kaito lambe waqt se maanta tha ki logon ki care karne ka safest tareeka apne difficult parts chhupana hai. Hanako ko lagta tha care karne ka matlab logon ko sunne par majboor karne ka tareeka dhoondhna hai. Dono beliefs poori tarah sahi nahi the. Kaito seekh raha tha honesty burden nahi, invitation ho sakti hai. Hanako seekh rahi thi ki love ka matlab har problem solve karna nahi. Kabhi iska matlab question poochhna aur answer accept karna hota hai. Kabhi iska matlab stay karna. Aur kabhi kisi ke desk par coffee ka can rakhna—bina return mein kuch expect kiye. Hanako ne box ko dekha. “Tum ise forever nahi rakh sakte.” “Kyun nahi?” “Embarrassing hai.” “Isi liye toh rakh raha hoon.” Hanako ne eyes narrow ki. “I hate you.” Kaito smile kiya. “No, you don't.” Hanako kuch kehne wali thi, phir ruk gayi. Aakhir woh uske paas jhuk kar dheere se boli, “Don't make me regret liking you.” “I'll try not to.” “Reassuring nahi hai.” “I'll do my best.” Hanako ne apna head uske shoulder par rest kar diya. Is baar dono ne silence fill karne ki koshish nahi ki. Coffee cold thi. Pen broken tha. Aur woh ladki jo sabse nafrat karti thi, aakhir kisi aise insaan ko mil gayi thi jiske peeche bhaagna use nahi pada. Kyunki usne stay karna choose kiya. ---

AfterwordA Small Thing, Repeated

Kuch stories grand gestures ke liye yaad rakhi jaati hain: rain mein dramatic confession, fireworks ke neeche promise, ya koi insaan poora city cross kare kyunki woh kuch unsaid chhod nahi sakta. Is story mein bhi woh feelings hain, lekin sabse important gestures chhote hain. Kisi ko girne se pehle haath dena. Aisi apology jo forgiveness demand na kare. Answer assume kiye bina question poochhna. Woh friend jo jaanta ho kab joke band karna hai. Woh ladka jo kisi aur ko notice karna pade usse pehle kehna seekhe, “I'm not feeling well.” Aur desk par coffee ka can rakhna. Hanako quiet person nahi bani. Kaito har feeling perfectly explain karne wala nahi bana. Dating shuru karne se unki problems gayab nahi hui, aur affection ne unhe life ke difficult parts se immune nahi kiya. Badla bas yeh ki unhone pretend karna band karne ki koshish ki. Hanako ne seekha ki control kiye bina care ki ja sakti hai. Kaito ne seekha ki care accept karne ke badle kisi ko apni poori life ka qarz nahi dena padta. Coffee cans, arguments aur broken pens ke beech unhe pata chala ki understood hone ke liye perfectly understood hona zaroori nahi. Kabhi-kabhi bas kisi aur ko try karne ka chance dena kaafi hota hai. Unki story ki beginning wahi thi. Kiss wala moment nahi. Confession wala moment bhi nahi. Woh moment jab unhone ek doosre ke liye decide karna band kiya ki kaun kya handle kar sakta hai—and ek doosre ko khud choose karne diya. **One can of coffee. One honest answer. One day at a time.** Shuru karne ke liye itna kaafi tha. *— Kaito* Kuch mahine baad, Kaito ki mother visit par aayi toh drawer ke peeche rakhi ek purani photograph par ruk gayi. Ek pal ke liye unka expression badla. “Yeh yaad hai?” unhone poochha. Kaito ne photograph dekhi. Jagah pehchaani, lekin back par likhi date nahi—or apne naam ke paas likha unfamiliar naam bhi nahi. “Mujhe yaad hona chahiye?” usne poochha. Uski mother ne drawer band kar diya. “Aaj raat nahi.” Kaito ne question chhod diya. Kuch baatein abhi bhi thi jinhe poochhna usne nahi seekha tha, aur ab woh maan-ne laga tha ki honesty ka matlab difficult conversations ko apne waqt par aane dena bhi hai.
