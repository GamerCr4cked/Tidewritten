Chapter One Eight Hundred Kilometres

Garmiyon ki shuruaat aadhi raat tak chalne wali calls se hui. Mio apne family balcony se nazara Akito ko dikhati; Akito apne hometown ke shaam wale aasman ki photos bhejta. Dono jokes, voice messages aur rozmarra ki chhoti updates share karte, jisse doori ko sambhalna aasaan lagta tha.

Phir calls chhoti hone lagi.

Mio kehti ki family se milne-julne mein busy hai. Use kaam the, purane doston se milna tha, aur kuch din woh baat karne ke liye bahut thaki hoti thi. Akito uski baat maanta raha. Uske liye trust ka matlab tha har deri ko shak ka saboot na samajhna.

Lekin kuch hafton baad usne online ek photo dekhi. Mio apne purane doston ke group ke saath khadi thi. Ryo naam ka ek ladka uske bilkul paas tha, aur uska ek haath Mio ki chair ke peeche tika hua tha.

Sirf photo dekhkar kuch sabit nahi hota tha. Akito yeh jaanta tha. Usne outing ke baare mein poochha, koshish ki ki uski awaaz casual lage.

Mio ne kaha ki maza aaya, phir usse bola ki woh baat ko zyada na soche.

Yeh jumla uski umeed se zyada chubha. Isne ek bilkul theek sawaal ko uski kami jaisa bana diya.

Jab doosre saal ke liye woh Seihoku laute, Akito ko umeed thi ki train ka safar khatam hote hi doori bhi khatam ho jayegi. Mio ne dormitory ke entrance par use hug kiya, aur kuch minute ke liye use laga ki sab theek hai.

Phir uska phone baj utha.

Usne screen dekhi aur call lene ke liye bahar chali gayi. Das minute baad lauti toh uski muskaan kuch zyada hi soch-samajh kar banayi hui lag rahi thi.

“Kiska phone tha?” Akito ne poochha.

“Ek purane dost ka.”

“Ryo?”

Uska expression badal gaya.

“Tum mujhse interrogation kyun kar rahe ho?”

“Main bas poochh raha hoon, kyunki mujhe lag raha hai kuch badal gaya hai.”

“Kuch nahi badla, Akito. Please, shuru mat karo.”

Akito ne sorry keh diya, halanki ab use khud nahi pata tha ki woh kis baat ke liye maafi maang raha hai.

Us shaam woh apne room mein baithkar unki message thread dekhta raha. Garmiyon ki shuruaat wale messages ab bhi wahan the—chhoti details aur pyaar se bhare hue. Lafz ab bhi maujood the. Unke peeche wali aasaani nahi rahi thi.

Chapter Two The Third Person

Ryo ka naam zyada baar saamne aane laga. Mio ki photos ke neeche comment. Aisi call jiska jawab woh Akito se baat karte waqt nahi de sakti thi. Aisa plan jo aakhri pal par badal jaata.

Akito uski friendships par nazar rakhne wala insaan nahi banna chahta tha. Woh aisa banda nahi banna chahta tha jo screenshots maange ya relationship ko security system ki tarah treat kare. Use bas imaandari chahiye thi, aur woh maanna chahta tha ki use maangne se woh unreasonable nahi ban jaayega.

Ek shaam usne Mio aur Ryo ko station ke paas ek café se nikalte dekha. Dono ne haath nahi pakde the. Bas ek-doosre ke kareeb chal rahe the aur Ryo ke phone par kuch dekhkar hans rahe the.

Akito ne usse is baare mein tab poochha jab dono akele the.

Mio chup ho gayi. Phir usne maana ki Ryo ne confess kiya tha ki woh use pasand karta hai. Usne kaha ki woh confused thi, use milne wala attention achha laga tha, aur use samajh nahi aaya ki Akito ko kaise bataye.

“Kya tum dono ke beech kuch hua?” usne poochha.

Mio ne zaroorat se zyada der tak nazar hataaye rakhi.

Jawab tukdon mein aaya: ek kiss, ek aisa faisla jiska use afsos tha, aur kuch messages jo usne chhupa liye kyunki use Akito ko khone ka darr tha. Koi bhi bahana sach ko be-asar nahi bana sakta tha.

Akito ne uski baat poori hone tak suna.

“Mujhe tumhe bata dena chahiye tha,” Mio ne dheere se kaha.

“Haan.”

“Mujhe darr lag raha tha.”

“Darr lagna main samajhta hoon.” Uski apni awaaz bhi use door se aati hui lagi. “Lekin tumne mujhe aisi baat par bharosa karte rehne diya jo sach nahi thi.”

Mio rone lagi. Akito ka mann use tasalli dene ka hua; woh instinct turant aur takleef dene wala tha. Use ab bhi Mio ki parwah thi. Isi wajah se sach itna dard de raha tha.

Usne kaha ki use thodi jagah chahiye.

Is baar Mio ne na use shaant hone ko kaha, na yeh promise kiya ki uska koi matlab nahi tha. Bas usne sir hila diya.

Dono alag-alag disha mein chale gaye.

Us raat Akito ne use block nahi kiya, gaali nahi di, aur na hi koi aakhri lambi speech bheji. Usne phone rakh diya aur is sach ke saath baitha raha ki pyaar reh sakta hai, chahe trust ko itni chot lagi ho ki us din use theek karna mumkin na ho.

Chapter Three The Shape of Silence

Agle kuch hafton mein khamoshi utni gehri thi jitni Akito ne sochi bhi nahi thi.

Woh library mein padhta, campus ke aas-paas photos leta, aur Kenta ke saath zyada waqt bitata. Usne photography ki ek chhoti series shuru ki: khaali benches, class ke baad corridors, khidkiyon par baarish, aur aisi jagahen jahan se log bina dhyaan diye guzar jaate the.

Usne series ka naam rakha *Colours of Silence*.

Photos ko online log pasand karne lage. Log unke atmosphere ki tareef karte aur poochhte ki har tasveer ka kya matlab hai. Akito shayad hi kabhi jawab deta. Woh abhi apni feelings ko samjhana seekh raha tha.

Udhar Mio ne zabardasti baat karwane ki koshish band kar di. Usne ek baar maafi ka message bheja, phir ek hafte baad doosra. Akito ne dono padhe, lekin jawab nahi diya. Woh use saza nahi dena chahta tha. Bas uske paas aisa jawab nahi tha jo imaandaar hota.

College mein kabhi-kabhi dono corridor mein ek-doosre ke saamne aa jaate. Shuru mein dono nazar hata lete. Baad mein Akito ek chhota sa nod kar paata. Mio bhi nod karti, uski aankhon mein aise lafz hote jinhe sunne ke liye woh usse nahi kehti thi.

Kabhi woh dono woh pair the jise har koi saath paane ki umeed karta tha. Ab unke friends ne seekh liya ki ek ke baare mein doosre se sawaal nahi karna.

Sabse mushkil yeh nahi tha ki Akito ab Mio se baat nahi kar sakta tha. Mushkil yeh thi ki ab bhi kuch cheezein dekhkar use Mio yaad aati: koi funny poster, vending machine mein naya drink, seedhiyon par dhoop ka ek tukda.

Kisi ki parwah karna relationship khatam hote hi band nahi hota. Woh reh jaata hai, bina yeh jaane ki ab jaana kahan hai.

Chapter Four No More Excuses

Mahine beet gaye. Akito phir se stable mehsoos karne laga, halanki jo hua tha uska asar poori tarah mita nahi tha. Usne samjha ki achha din hone ka matlab yeh nahi ki woh Mio ko bhool gaya, aur mushkil din hone ka matlab yeh nahi ki uska door jaane ka faisla galat tha.

Mio ne use jawab dene par majboor karne ka tareeka dhoondhna band kar diya. Usne Yuna se imaandari se baat ki ki usne kya kiya tha. Usne Ryo, doori, akelapan ya Akito ke sawaalon ko dosh nahi diya. Yeh sab cheezein maujood thi; lekin faisle uske apne the.

“Main ek takleef dene wali baat-cheet se bachna chahti thi,” usne Yuna se kaha. “Isliye maine sau aur bhi buri baatein paida kar di.”

Usne Ryo se contact khatam kar diya aur apne kiye nuksaan ki zimmedari leni shuru ki. Isse use maafi milna zaroori nahi tha. Yeh bas sahi kaam tha.

Semester ke aakhir ke paas Akito aur Mio library ke bahar achanak mil gaye. Baarish pavement par tapak rahi thi aur dono ek hi chhat ke neeche khade the.

“Main tumse mujhe maaf karne ko nahi kahungi,” Mio ne kaha. “Bas chahti hoon ki tumhe pata ho, ab mujhe samajh aata hai ki tum kyun chale gaye.”

Akito ne uski taraf dekha. Woh thaki hui lag rahi thi, lekin woh use apni tasalli ki zimmedari dene ki koshish nahi kar rahi thi.

“Yeh kehne ke liye shukriya,” usne jawab diya.

Yeh dobara saath aana nahi tha. Na hi koi promise.

Mahino mein pehli baar unke beech ki khamoshi kisi hathiyaar ki tarah nahi lagi. Woh ek aisi boundary thi jise dono ko todne ki zaroorat nahi thi.

Dono ne tameez se alvida kaha.

Akito baarish mein ghar ki taraf chala, apne saath us baat ka dard liye jo hui thi, aur yeh halki rahat bhi ki ab woh apni yaadon se behas nahi kar raha tha.

Chapter Five Meet Again

Garmiyan phir aa gayin. Is baar unmein se kisi ne call nahi ki.

Akito ne woh notebook kholi jisme usne pehli baar Mio se milne wale din ke baare mein likha tha. Shuruaati panne jokes, observations aur rozmarra ki chhoti details se bhare the, jinhone unki dosti ko itna aasaan banaya tha.

Usne aakhri entry likhi:

*Zindagi mein kuch log tumhe khoobsurat cheezein bhi dikhate hain aur takleef dene wali cheezein bhi. Dono tumhari zindagi ka hissa ban jaati hain. Sirf isliye ki baad mein dard hua, mujhe yeh dikhawa karne ki zaroorat nahi ki khoobsurat pal jhoothe the.*

Usne notebook band kar di.

Mio ne apni diary mein milti-julti baat likhi, halanki use nahi pata tha ki Akito ne kya likha hai. Usne mutual friends se yeh poochhna band kar diya ki Akito kaisa hai. Woh chahti thi ki woh theek ho. Lekin usne maan liya ki uski khairiyat ki umeed karne se use Akito ki zindagi mein jagah maangne ka haq nahi milta.

Naya academic year shuru hua toh woh corridor mein ek-doosre ke paas se guzre. Akito ne use ek chhota, sachcha nod diya. Mio ne bhi waisa hi kiya.

Dono apne raaste chalte rahe.

Yeh woh ending nahi thi jiska dono ne kabhi imagine kiya tha. Na aakhri behas hui, na perfect apology, na hi woh turant pehle jaise ban gaye. Unka relationship khatam ho chuka tha, aur uske consequences sach the.

Phir bhi pehla saal hua tha. Hansi sach thi. Baarish sach thi. Unke beech jo affection tha, baad mein hui baaton ne use mita nahi diya.

Akito apna camera bag mein liye library ki taraf mud gaya. Mio bagal mein notebook dabaaye class ki taraf chali.

Dono ko nahi pata tha ki kya woh kabhi dobara aise milenge jiska koi matlab hoga.

Filhaal, woh bina jawab ke jeena seekh rahe the.

*Phir milenge* pehle ek vaada hota tha ki doori jaldi khatam ho jayegi.

Ab iska matlab kuch chhota aur zyada imaandaar tha: zindagi lambi hai, log badalte hain, aur future se koi dobara milne ka vaada zabardasti nahi le sakta.

Kahani khatam nahi hui thi. Lekin uska yeh hissa khatam ho chuka tha.

Sach saamne aane ke baad woh dono apne purane relationship mein wapas nahi ja sakte the. Agar kabhi phir mile, toh unhe un logon ki tarah milna hoga jo woh ban chuke hain—na ki un logon ki tarah jo ab bhi chahte hain ki past ko badla ja sake.
