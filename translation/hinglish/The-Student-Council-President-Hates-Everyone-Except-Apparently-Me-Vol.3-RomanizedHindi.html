PrologueThe Message He Finally Sent

Kaito Mizuno ne saalon tak yeh maana tha ki khamosh rehna bhi ek tarah ki kindness hoti hai.

Agar woh logon ko nahi batayega ki woh dara hua hai, toh woh pareshan nahi honge. Agar woh apne past ke un hisson ko explain nahi karega jo use theek se yaad nahi, toh koi use un yaadon ko khodkar nikaalne ke liye nahi kahega. Agar woh apni problems ko itna chhota rakhe ki shayad woh ek din khud hi gayab ho jaayen.

Yeh plan bahut hi kharaab tha.

Ab use dheere-dheere iska ehsaas ho raha tha.

Raat ke 12:16 baje, usne Hanako Shimizu ki chat kholi.

Uski profile picture student council room ki khidki ki thi, jise sunset ke waqt liya gaya tha. Hanako ne kabhi kaha tha ki woh photo sentimental nahi hai. Aur phir bhi, woh use do saal se sambhaal kar rakhe hue thi.

Kaito ne ek message type kiya.

Phir delete kar diya.

Doosra type kiya.

Woh bhi delete kar diya.

Aakhir mein, usne likha:

Kaito: Kisi ne mujhe kuch photos bheji hain jo saalon pehle mere hospital mein rehne se judi hain. Mujhe lagta hai mujhe pata lagana chahiye ki tab kya hua tha. Main tumse help nahi maang raha, aur tum par reply karne ki koi responsibility nahi hai. Bas ab dobara koi zaroori baat chhupana nahi chahta tha.

Woh screen ko dekhta raha.

Phir usne send kar diya.

Poore ek minute tak kuch nahi hua.

Kaito ne phone neeche rakha, khada hua, kitchen tak gaya, apne room mein lauta, aur phir phone utha liya.

Koi reply nahi.

“Excellent,” usne budbudaaya. “Ek message bhejne ke baad bilkul normal reaction.”

Phone vibrate hua.

Hanako: Mujhe batane ke liye thank you.

Ek doosra message aaya.

Hanako: Main promise nahi kar sakti ki tumse baat karne par mujhe dard nahi hoga. Lekin main yeh promise kar sakti hoon ki mujhse poochhe bina tum yeh decide nahi karoge ki main kya handle kar sakti hoon.

Kaito ne use do baar padha.

Phir teesra message aaya.

Hanako: Sach ka pata lagao. Jo bhi jaanoge, agar mujhe batana chaho toh pehle mujhse poochhna. Us waqt main khud decide karungi.

Usne dheere se saans chhodi.

Yeh maafi nahi thi.

Yeh unke beete hue rishte mein lautne ka invitation bhi nahi tha.

Lekin yeh imaandaar tha.

Pehli baar, use samajh aaya ki sach bolne se happy ending ki guarantee nahi milti. Bas logon ko use chunne ka ek asli mauka milta hai.

Usne photo dobara kholi.

Hospital ka corridor. Uski maa. Peechhe Aoi. Woh purana naam jise woh peeche chhodne ki koshish karta raha tha.

Kal woh Aoi ko dhoondhega.

Aur is baar, sawaal woh khud poochega.

Chapter OneThe Girl Who Remembered Everything

Aoi Kanzaki ne usse public library mein milne ke liye haan kar di.

“Neutral ground,” usne phone par samjhaya. “Aur agar tumne mysterious banne ki acting shuru ki, toh main tumhe children's section mein bitha dungi.”

“Main mysterious acting nahi karta.”

“Tum ek baar bina kuch explain kiye teen saal ke liye gayab ho gaye the.”

“Woh alag baat thi.”

“Achha?”

Kaito ke paas koi jawaab nahi tha.

Agle din dopahar ko Aoi library ki unchi khidkiyon ke paas do drinks lekar uska intezaar kar rahi thi. Usne ek tea bottle uski taraf badhaayi.

“Tum ab woh coffee brand nahi peete,” usne kaha.

“Kabhi-kabhi peeta hoon.”

“Toh main bina wajah hi dramatic tareeke se galat thi.”

“Tumhe practice toh hai.”

Usne use ek nazar se dekha. Kaito ke honton par lagbhag muskaan aa gayi.

Ek pal ke liye, woh phir se do purane dost the—not woh ladka jo gayab ho gaya tha aur woh ladki jise peeche chhod diya gaya tha, balki do log jo ek hi summers ko yaad karte the.

Phir Aoi ne table par ek folder rakh diya.

“Maine copies sambhaal kar rakhi thi,” usne kaha. “Tumhari maa ne kaha tha, agar kabhi tumhe jaanne ka mann ho toh kaam aayengi.”

Kaito ne folder khola.

Usmein school photographs, appointment cards aur kuch haath se likhe notes the. Ek photo mein uska chhota wala version hospital bed par baitha tha aur camera ko gusse se dekh raha tha. Aoi uske paas khadi thi, uske sir ke peeche rabbit ears bana rahi thi.

“Mujhe yeh yaad hai,” usne kaha.

“Tumne mujhe hospital director se complain karne ki dhamki di thi.”

“Main serious tha.”

“Tum baarah saal ke the. Tumhe lagta tha hospital director saare hospitals ka principal hota hai.”

“Mere paas kaafi kuch chal raha tha.”

Aoi hans padi, aur us awaaz se Kaito ke seene mein jama kuch bojh halka ho gaya.

Phir woh aakhri page tak pahuncha: ek consent form ki photocopy, jis par uski maa aur ek doctor ke signatures the. Uske neeche uski apni handwriting mein ek note tha.

Mujhe yaad nahi ki maine agree kiya tha.

Kaito ki ungliyan paper par kas gayin.

“Maine kis baat ke liye agree kiya tha?”

Aoi ki muskaan gayab ho gayi.

“Woh nahi jo tum soch rahe ho.”

“Toh batao.”

Usne apne haath jod liye.

“Jab tum chhote the, tumhe ek serious illness thi. Tum har waqt dare rehte the, lekin tumhe yeh bilkul pasand nahi tha ki log tumhe naazuk samajhkar treat karein. Ek bahut mushkil raat ke baad, tumne apni maa se kaha tha ki tum woh ladka banna band karna chahte ho jiske liye sab log hamesha pareshan rehte hain.”

“Mujhe beemar rehna yaad hai. Lekin yeh kehna yaad nahi.”

“Tum bilkul thak chuke the. Dawa li hui thi aur neend bhi poori nahi ho rahi thi. Tumhari maa ne doctor se kaha tha ki jab tum thoda theek mehsoos karo, tab options dobara samjhaaye jaayen. Woh form ek treatment plan ke baare mein tha—not koi secret operation, na koi deal, aur na hi kisi ka tumhare choices chheen lena.”

Kaito signature ko dekhta raha.

“Aur woh note?”

“Tumne woh tab likha tha jab doctor ne sab kuch theek se samjhaya bhi nahi tha. Tum gusse mein the kyunki tumhe lag raha tha sabne tumhare liye pehle hi faisla kar liya hai.” Aoi ek pal ruki. “Tumhari maa ne use isliye sambhaal kar rakha, kyunki woh bhoolna nahi chahti thi ki tumhari apni zindagi ke decisions mein tumhe bhi shaamil hona zaroori hai.”

Usne woh sentence dobara padha.

Ab woh kisi saazish ka saboot nahi lag raha tha. Woh ek dare hue bachche ki awaaz lag rahi thi, jo chahta tha ki koi uski baat sune.

“Mere naam ka kya?” usne poochha.

“Transfer ke baad tumne Kaito naam use karna shuru kiya. Tumne kaha tha ki tum aisa naam chahte ho jise sunkar purane hospital ke log tumhe us din ki yaad se na dekhein jo unke liye sabse bura tha.” Aoi ne nazar neeche kar li. “Tumhari maa ne tum par force nahi kiya. Tumne khud choose kiya tha. Lekin baad mein, jab tumhe har detail yaad nahi rahi, tum sochne lage ki kya tumne kabhi kuch apni marzi se choose bhi kiya tha.”

Kaito kursi par peeche ho gaya.

Is mystery mein koi villain saamne nahi aaya tha.

Bas ek dara hua bachcha, ek maa jo use protect karne ki koshish kar rahi thi, aur ek dost jo kabhi samajh hi nahi paayi ki poori kahaani kaise samjhaaye.

“Tumne mujhe pehle kyun nahi bataya?”

“Tumhari maa ne mujhse kaha tha ki tumhe space doon. Shuru mein mujhe laga kuch hafton ki baat hai. Phir tumne jawaab dena band kar diya. Maine letters likhe. Calls kiye. Aakhir mein main gussa ho gayi.” Aoi ki awaaz narm pad gayi. “Maine khud ko samjha diya ki tum mujhe bhool gaye kyunki main tumhare liye itni important nahi thi.”

“Main tumhe nahi bhoola tha.”

“Tum mujhse baat karna bhool gaye the.”

Usne Aoi ki taraf dekha.

“Shayad woh aur bhi bura hai.”

“Tha,” usne bina kisi kadwahat ke kaha. “Lekin hamesha ke liye nahi.”

Photos milne ke baad pehli baar, Kaito ko laga ki past ab ek aisi shape le raha hai jise woh samajh sakta hai.

Perfect shape nahi. Aisi bhi nahi jisme koi regret na ho.

Lekin ab use samajhne ke liye kisi anjaan insaan ke threatening messages ki zaroorat nahi thi.

“Kya main apni maa se baat kar sakta hoon?” usne poochha.

Aoi ne sir hila diya.

“Aur uske baad,” usne kaha, “main tumse properly maafi maangna chahta hoon.”

“Lunch khila kar shuru kar sakte ho.”

“Bas yahi condition hai?”

“Nahi. Woh late fee hai.”

Kaito lagbhag hans diya.

“Theek hai.”

Chapter TwoA Mother's Explanation

Kaito ki maa ne bina beech mein roke uski poori baat suni.

Woh dono uske apartment ki chhoti dining table par baithe the. Wahi table jahan woh use dawa lene, homework khatam karne aur bukhaar ko chhoti si baat samajhne ka natak band karne ki yaad dilaya karti thi.

Jab Kaito ki baat khatam hui, usne purana consent form apni taraf kheench liya.

“Tum bade hue, tab mujhe tumhe aur samjhana chahiye tha,” usne kaha.

“Aapne kuch baatein samjhaayi toh thi.”

“Kaafi nahi. Mujhe laga tum apni zindagi ka woh hissa peeche chhodna chahte ho. Maine tumhari khamoshi ko tumhara pakka faisla samajh liya.”

Kaito paper ko dekhta raha.

“Mujhe laga kisi ne mere liye decisions liye the.”

“Mujhe pata hai.” Uski aankhon mein thakaan thi, lekin awaaz sthir thi. “Tum bachche the. Beemar the, dare hue the aur gusse mein bhi. Doctors ne humein kuch options diye the. Maine unse kaha tha ki final plan banane se pehle tumhare behtar mehsoos karne tak intezaar karein. Tum usmein shaamil the. Bas tumhe har conversation yaad nahi hai.”

“Aapne mujhe naam badalne kyun diya?”

“Kyunki tumne mujhse kaha tha. Tum ek nayi shuruaat chahte the, aur mujhe laga shayad isse tumhe madad milegi. Lekin mujhe yeh bhi samjhana chahiye tha ki nayi shuruaat ka matlab un sab logon ko mita dena nahi hota jo tumse pehle se pyaar karte the.”

Usne mushkil se apna gala saaf kiya.

“Kya aapko lagta tha main phir se beemar ho jaunga?”

“Har din.” Usne table ke paar haath badhaaya, phir ruk gayi—use mauka dete hue ki woh khud uska haath pakadna chahe. Kaito ne uska haath thaam liya. “Lekin main kabhi nahi chahti thi ki tum apni poori zindagi aise jiyo jaise beemar hona hi tumhari eklauti pehchaan ho.”

Saalon tak Kaito ko laga tha ki uski maa koi aisa sach chhupa rahi hai jo use be-bas mehsoos kara dega. Lekin woh toh bas us mushkil waqt ke tukdon ko sambhaal kar baithi thi, aur intezaar kar rahi thi ki Kaito kab unhe dekhne ke liye ready hoga.

“I'm sorry,” usne kaha.

Uski maa ne uska haath dabaaya.

“Darr jaane ke liye tumhe maafi maangne ki zaroorat nahi.”

“Lekin sabko apne se door karne ke liye maafi maangni chahiye.”

“Haan,” usne kaha. “Woh toh karni chahiye.”

Uski seedhi baat sunkar Kaito hairaan reh gaya.

Phir woh muskuraayi.

“Aur tum dobara aisa na karke shuruaat kar sakte ho.”

Usne sir hila diya.

Yeh ek chhota sa promise tha. Lekin sachcha.

Jaane se pehle, uski maa ne use hospital corridor wali original photograph de di.

“Yeh tumhare paas rehni chahiye,” unhone kaha. “Isliye nahi ki tumhe har mushkil baat yaad rakhni hai. Balki isliye ki tum usse guzre ho, aur us poore waqt tumse pyaar kiya gaya tha.”

Kaito ne photograph apne bag mein rakh li.

Is baar use bag ke sabse neeche chhupane ki zaroorat mehsoos nahi hui.

Chapter ThreeThe Student Council's Emergency Meeting

Student council ke saamne ek nayi problem aa gayi thi.

Kisi ne school festival ke annual closing event ka naam badalkar “The President's Grand Emotional Finale” rakhne ka proposal submit kiya tha.

Hanako paper ko ghoorti rahi.

Hiro Takahashi uske kandhe ke upar se jhaankne laga.

“Mujhe lagta hai ismein ek alag hi artistic quality hai.”

“Ismein ek alag hi disciplinary quality hai,” Hanako ne jawaab diya. “Khaaskar punishment wali.”

“Kya ise agenda mein daal doon?”

“Khud ko agenda mein daalo.”

“Main toh pehle se kai agendas mein hoon. Zyadaatar imaginary wale.”

Council room hansi se bhar gaya.

Hanako nahi hansi.

Haan, uske honton ka ek kona lagbhag ek millimetre zaroor hila.

Hiro ne uski taraf ungli uthayi. “Gawah maujood hain! Inhone khushi mehsoos ki hai!”

“Main tumhare festival privileges cancel kar sakti hoon.”

“Tum mera natural charm cancel nahi kar sakti.”

“Tumhara microphone cancel kar sakti hoon.”

“Yeh toh war crime hai.”

Darwaaza khula. Kaito haath mein ek folder liye darwaaze par khada tha.

Room achanak shaant ho gaya—not isliye ki woh koi ajnabi ban gaya tha, balki isliye kyunki kabhi woh yahin sabse zyada apna lagta tha.

Hanako ne uski taraf dekha.

“Kaito.”

“Hanako.”

Hiro ka expression narm pad gaya. Usne dono ke beech nazar ghumaayi, phir woh ajeeb proposal utha liya.

“Mujhe achanak yaad aaya ki mujhe yeh printer tak le jaana hai. Bahut door. Shayad kisi doosre prefecture mein.”

“Baith jao, Hiro,” Hanako ne kaha.

“Main aaj tak itni khushi se kisi ki baat maanne ko taiyaar nahi hua.”

Woh baith gaya.

Kaito ne folder table par rakha. “Main purane hospital incident ke documents council ko dene aaya tha. Ismein koi scandal nahi hai. School ko koi dhamki nahi de raha. Messages ek former staff member ne bheje the, jise laga mujhe apne past ka saamna karna chahiye—but usne tareeka bahut kharaab chuna. Maine aur meri maa ne isse handle kar liya hai.”

“Tum safe ho?” Hanako ne poochha.

“Haan. Aur tumhe pareshan hona pada, uske liye sorry.”

Usne Kaito ka chehra dhyaan se dekha.

“Mujhe batane ke liye thank you.”

Usne sir hila diya, uske aur Hanako ke beech ki doori ko accept karte hue, bina kisi apology se use zabardasti kam karne ki koshish kiye.

Phir woh council ki taraf muda.

“Festival planning notes bhi update karne honge. West side ka emergency exit nayi display stands ki wajah se block ho raha hai.”

Hanako ne palkein jhapkaayin.

“Tumne woh notice kiya?”

“Kal. Maine photos faculty adviser ko bhej di thi.”

Usne folder check kiya. Kaito sahi tha.

Agle bees minute tak sab waise hi kaam karte rahe jaise pehle kiya karte the: Hanako tasks assign kar rahi thi, Kaito chhoti details pakad raha tha, Hiro duniya ke sabse kharaab suggestions de raha tha, aur baaki log use theek kar rahe the.

Kuch jaadui nahi hua.

Kisi ne confession nahi kiya.

Koi roya nahi.

Lekin meeting khatam hone par, revised plan ko dekhte hue Hanako ko ehsaas hua ki Kaito ke saath ek hi room mein rehna ab kisi ghaav ke paas khade hone jaisa nahi lagta.

Ab woh ek insaan ke paas khadi thi.

Yeh sab kuch nahi tha.

Lekin ek shuruaat thi.

Chapter FourThe Apology With No Hidden Request

Kaito ne Hanako se poochha ki kya woh school ke baad usse baat karne ke liye taiyaar hogi.

“Kisi public jagah par,” usne kaha.

“Bilkul.”

“Aur koi dramatic speech nahi.”

“Mera aisa koi plan nahi tha.”

“Achha. Mujhe dramatic speeches pasand nahi.”

“Tumne ek baar stapler ke sahi istemaal par baarah minute ki speech di thi.”

“Woh administrative necessity thi.”

Woh station ke paas ek chhote café mein mile. Hanako ne khidki ke paas wali table chuni. Kaito do drinks lekar aaya, lekin ek ko rakhne se pehle usne Hanako ko choose karne diya.

“Mujhe nahi pata tha ki tumhe ab bhi yeh tea pasand hai ya nahi,” usne kaha.

“Pasand hai.”

“Achha.”

Uske baad ki khamoshi awkward thi, lekin dushmani bhari nahi.

Kaito ne table par apne haath jod liye.

“Main maafi maangna chahta hoon, bina tumse yeh umeed kiye ki baad mein tum mujhe achha mehsoos karaogi,” usne shuru kiya. “Maine baatein chhupaayi kyunki mujhe laga main tumhe protect kar raha hoon. Tumhe choice diye bina maine decide kar liya ki tum kya handle kar sakti ho. Phir jab sab kuch bahut mushkil ho gaya, toh main tumhe sawaalon ke saath chhodkar chala gaya aur umeed ki ki khamoshi sab theek kar degi.”

Hanako use dhyaan se dekhti rahi.

“Main tumse pyaar karta tha,” usne aage kaha. “Aaj bhi tumhari parwah karta hoon. Lekin mujhe pata hai ki meri feelings ka matlab yeh nahi ki tum mujhe ek aur mauka dene ke liye majboor ho. Main yeh baat saaf kehna chahta tha, kyunki pichhli baar jab humne baat ki thi, tab bhi main apne darr ko ek achhe reason ki tarah pesh karne ki koshish kar raha tha.”

Woh ruk gaya.

Koi guzaarish nahi. Koi promise nahi ki woh raaton-raat badal gaya hai. Use maaf karne ki koi request nahi.

Hanako ne sadak ki taraf dekha.

“Main bahut waqt tak gusse mein thi,” usne kaha. “Phir udaas thi. Phir apne aap par gussa hone lagi ki main ab bhi udaas kyun hoon. Mujhe lagta tha agar main sab kuch samajh loon, toh decide kar paaungi ki achhe pal sach mein achhe the bhi ya nahi.”

“Woh sach mein achhe the,” Kaito ne dheere se kaha.

“Ab mujhe pata hai.” Woh uski taraf mudi. “Lekin achhe palon ne takleef ko mita nahi diya.”

“Mujhe pata hai.”

“Aur agar humne kabhi dobara koshish ki, toh main sach se door rakhe jaana accept nahi karungi.”

“Tumhe karna bhi nahi chahiye.”

“Main sawaal poochhungi.”

“Tumhe poochhne chahiye.”

“Aur mushkil hone par bhi jawaab expect karungi.”

Kaito ne sir hilaaya. “Tum iski haqdar ho.”

Hanako apni tea hilaane lagi.

“Maine abhi decide nahi kiya ki mujhe kya chahiye.”

“Yeh theek hai.”

“Aur tumhe tragic chehra banane ki permission nahi hai.”

“Maine aisa nahi kiya.”

“Tum karne ki taiyaari mein the.”

“Main thoughtful dikhne ki taiyaari kar raha tha.”

“Tum zyada sochte ho toh constipated lagte ho.”

Kaito use dekhta reh gaya.

Phir woh hans pada.

Hansi achanak nikli aur bilkul pehchaani hui thi—waisi hi, jo pehle Hanako ke kisi ajeeb se comment ko poori seriousness ke saath kehne par usse aa jaati thi.

Hanako ki apni hansi bhi uske rokne se pehle nikal gayi.

Ek pal ke liye, unke beech ke saal kisi deewar se kam aur aisi sadak se zyada lage jise dono taraf se dekha ja sakta ho.

Café se nikalne ke baad, woh saath-saath station tak chale.

Entrance par Hanako ruk gayi.

“Aaj mujhse jawaab na maangne ke liye thank you.”

Kaito ne sir hilaaya. “Meri baat sunne ke liye thank you.”

Woh jaane ke liye mudi, phir palatkar dekha.

“Aur Kaito?”

“Haan?”

“Agli baar tea khareedne se pehle guess mat karna. Poochhna.”

Woh muskuraaya.

“Main aisa kar sakta hoon.”

Chapter FiveThe Festival Plan That Wasn't a Date

Teen hafton baad, Hiro ne announce kiya ki student council ek festival reunion dinner host kar rahi hai.

“Yeh reunion nahi hai,” Hanako ne kaha. “Hum sab abhi bhi isi school mein padh rahe hain.”

“Yeh hum sabke collective suffering ka reunion hai.”

“Tumne khud volunteer kiya tha.”

“Main tab jawaan aur bewakoof tha.”

“Woh toh pichhle Tuesday ki baat hai.”

“Log badal sakte hain.”

Dinner Saturday ke liye schedule kiya gaya tha. Classmates, former council members aur itna saara khaana aane wala tha ki Hiro kuch der ke liye shaant ho jaaye.

Hanako ne Kaito ko guest list check karte hue dekha.

“Tum aa rahe ho?” usne poochha.

“Agar mujhe invite kiya gaya hai toh.”

“Tum list mein ho.”

“Toh phir haan.”

“Achha.”

Woh intezaar karta raha.

Woh bhi intezaar karti rahi.

Aakhir Kaito ne poochha, “Kuch aur tha?”

“Nahi.”

“Theek hai.”

Woh jaane laga.

Hanako ne uski sleeve pakad li.

Woh turant ruk gaya.

Usne apna haath chhod diya, achanak apne hi haath ka ehsaas hote hi.

“Main poochhne wali thi ki kya tum decorations uthaane mein meri help karoge.”

“Bilkul.”

“Itna khush mat dikho. Yeh ek administrative request hai.”

“Samajh gaya, President.”

“Main ab tumhari president nahi hoon.”

“Toh phir main tumhe former president kahunga, jo filhaal clipboard ko hathiyaar ki tarah pakde hue hai.”

“Excellent. Tum seekh rahe ho.”

Saturday ko reunion dinner bilkul utna hi chaotic tha jitna Hanako ne socha tha. Hiro ne tees second zyada lambi speech di. Kisi ne seating chart par sauce gira diya. Ek former council treasurer aisi photos se bhara scrapbook lekar aaya jo kabhi li hi nahi jaani chahiye thi.

Ek photo mein Hanako apni desk par so rahi thi, aur uske maathe par ek note chipka tha: Disturb na karein: President universe ke laws update kar rahi hain.

Woh Hiro ko ghoorne lagi.

“Yeh tumne likha tha.”

“Mujhe us incident ki koi yaad nahi.”

“Tumne apna naam sign kiya hai.”

“Forgery.”

Kaito uske paas hans pada.

Baad mein, jab sab dessert table ke paas jama the, Hanako ne use khidki ke paas paaya.

“Achha waqt guzar raha hai?” usne poochha.

“Haan.”

“Hiro ke saath bhi?”

“Khaaskar Hiro ki wajah se. Woh har aam event ko yaadgaar bana deta hai.”

“Yeh kaafi generous interpretation hai.”

“Practice karne ka waqt mila hai.”

Usne peeche hans rahe logon ki taraf dekha.

“Mujhe yeh sab yaad aata tha,” usne maan liya.

“Mujhe bhi.”

Un lafzon mein koi demand nahi thi. Woh bas un dono ke the.

Hanako ne gehri saans li.

“Mujhe lagta hai main tumhare saath dobara waqt bitana chahti hoon. Kabhi-kabhi, sirf hum dono. Isliye nahi ki hum pretend kar rahe hain ki past mein kuch hua hi nahi. Balki isliye ki main dekhna chahti hoon ki ab hum kaun hain.”

Kaito ne turant jawaab nahi diya.

Phir usne poochha, “Kya tum agle Sunday mere saath bahar jaana chahogi? Kisi shaant jagah. Hum baat kar sakte hain, ya nahi bhi. Aur agar tumhara mann badal jaaye, toh woh bhi theek hai.”

Hanako ne apni thodi uthaayi.

“Yeh hairaan kar dene wala acceptable invitation hai.”

“Maine is par kaam kiya hai.”

“Dikh raha hai.”

Woh muskuraayi.

“Haan, Kaito. Mujhe achha lagega.”

Room ke doosri taraf Hiro ke haath se ek spoon gir gaya.

Woh aisi awaaz ke saath zameen par gira jaise poore prefecture ko alert kar sakta ho.

Usne un dono ki taraf ungli dikhayi.

“MUJHE PATA THA FESTIVAL DINNER MEIN ROMANTIC POTENTIAL HAI!”

Hanako ne aankhein band kar li.

“Main uska microphone chheen loongi.”

Kaito muskuraaya. “Yeh kaam Monday ko kar sakti ho.”

“Monday ko kyun?”

“Kyunki aaj raat, mujhe lagta hai humein dessert enjoy karna chahiye.”

Hanako ne uski taraf dekha.

Phir usne uska haath pakad liya.

“Theek hai,” usne kaha. “Lekin agar usne speech di, toh main chali jaaungi.”

“Samajh gaya.”

Dono mein se kisi ne haath nahi chhoda.

Chapter SixA Date, Properly Asked

Sab kuch hone ke baad unki pehli date aquarium jaana thi.

Hanako ne aquarium isliye chuna kyunki wahan shaanti thi, train se jaana aasaan tha, aur bina koi scene banaaye woh kisi bhi waqt wahan se ja sakti thi.

Kaito ne wajah poochhe bina haan kar di.

Unhone bees minute jellyfish ko neeli roshni ke beech tairte hue dekha.

“Inke paas koi responsibility nahi lagti,” Hanako ne kaha.

“Shayad jellyfish banne mein hi bahut busy rehte honge.”

“Inka defence mat karo. Inke paas student council nahi hai.”

“Ho sakta hai inki bhi koi bahut strict underwater president ho.”

“Toh mujhe jellyfish par taras aata hai.”

Kaito hans pada.

Lunch ke waqt unhone aam baatein ki: exams, Hiro ki class representative banne ki latest nakaam koshish, aur ek teacher jisne galti se staff group chat mein schedule ki jagah apne lunch ki photo bhej di thi.

Unhone mushkil baatein bhi ki.

Kaito ne use apne follow-up appointments ke baare mein bataya, jinmein woh ab bhi jaata tha, aur kaise kabhi-kabhi woh unhe chhupa leta tha kyunki woh nahi chahta tha ki log uske saath alag tarah se pesh aayein. Hanako ne bataya ki kisi aise insaan ki parwah karna kitna thaka dene wala tha jo yeh batane se hi inkaar kar de ki use kis cheez ki zaroorat hai.

Dono mein se kisi ne conversation jeetne ki koshish nahi ki.

Kisi ne yeh pretend nahi kiya ki ek achhe afternoon ne sab kuch theek kar diya hai.

Aquarium ke exit par Kaito ruk gaya.

“Kya main tumse kuch poochh sakta hoon?”

“Haan.”

“Kya tum dobara dating try karna chahogi? Dheere-dheere. Imaandari ke saath. Hum boundaries set kar sakte hain, aur agar sahi na lage toh ruk bhi sakte hain. Main yeh assume nahi karna chahta ki ek achhi date ka matlab sab kuch theek ho gaya.”

Hanako kaafi der tak use dekhti rahi.

“Tumhari invitation technique behtar ho gayi hai.”

“Mere paas ek achhi teacher thi.”

“Kaun?”

“Tum. Zyadaatar dhamkiyon ke zariye.”

“Achha. Taleem yaadgaar honi chahiye.”

Usne Kaito ke haath pakad liye.

“Haan,” usne kaha. “Main dobara koshish karna chahti hoon.”

Kaito ke chehre ka expression badal gaya, jaise uska jawaab uske andar us jagah tak pahunch gaya ho jo bahut waqt se kisi mushkil cheez ke liye taiyaar thi.

“Lekin,” Hanako ne kaha, “kuch rules hain.”

“Mujhe umeed thi.”

“Rule one: mere liye decisions lena band.”

“Agreed.”

“Rule two: agar tum dare hue ho, toh mujhe batao ki tum dare hue ho. Tumhe har baat perfect tareeke se explain karne ki zaroorat nahi. Bas yeh pretend karna band karo ki kuch galat nahi hai.”

“Agreed.”

“Rule three: agar main kahoon ki koi baat mujhe pareshan kar rahi hai, toh apna defence shuru karne se pehle meri baat sunna.”

“Agreed.”

“Rule four: mere liye black coffee mat khareedna. Mujhe pasand nahi.”

Kaito ne palkein jhapkaayin. “Mujhe laga tumhe pasand hai.”

“Main woh tumhare liye khareedti thi, bewakoof.”

Woh use dekhta reh gaya.

Phir itna zor se hans pada ki paas khade ek bachche ne mudkar dekha.

Hanako ke gaal garam ho gaye.

“Yeh koi funny rule nahi hai!”

“Yeh bahut important rule hai.”

“Aur rule five—”

“Paanch rules hain?”

“Is relationship ki president main hoon.”

“Tumne kaha tha tum ab meri president nahi ho.”

“Mujhe dobara appoint kar diya gaya hai.”

Kaito ki muskaan narm pad gayi.

“Toh main rules accept karta hoon, President.”

Hanako ne uske haath dabaaye.

“Achha. Kyunki is baar main chahti hoon ki hum ek hi side par hon.”

“Main bhi.”

Woh aquarium se saath nikle, ungliyan ek-doosre mein phansaaye hue. Unhone yeh promise nahi kiya ki woh dobara kabhi ek-doosre ko takleef nahi denge.

Bas yeh promise kiya ki jab aisa hoga, toh sach bataayenge.

Unke liye, yeh ek bahut behtar shuruaat thi.

Chapter SevenThe Last Rule

School festival ke din baarish ka forecast tha, extension cords ki kami thi, aur Hiro ke handwritten signs kuch zyada hi suspicious tadaad mein the.

Hanako pehle sochti thi ki ek successful event ke liye har detail ka control mein hona zaroori hai.

Ab woh jaanti thi ki jab cheezein galat hon, toh aise log chahiye jo help karne ko taiyaar hon.

Kaito subah audio schedule theek karne mein laga tha. Hanako volunteer teams ko dobara organize kar rahi thi. Hiro ko decorations ki zimmedaari di gayi thi—aakhir yeh faisla kisne liya, baad mein koi samjha nahi paaya.

Dopahar ko baarish shuru ho gayi.

Students bhaagkar andar aa gaye. Outdoor stalls ko gym mein shift kar diya gaya. Ek display stand gir gaya, paper flowers ka dher udne laga, aur kisi ne announce kiya ki festival ke final performance ki music file hi gayab ho gayi hai.

Hanako afra-tafri ke beech khadi thi.

Ek purane, pehchaane se pal ke liye, uske mann mein sab kuch khud karne ki ichha hui.

Phir usne aas-paas dekha.

Kaito ek first-year student ke saath speakers dobara connect kar raha tha. Council ke do members tables shift kar rahe the. Hiro ne, pata nahi kaise, paper flowers bachane ke liye ek human chain organize kar li thi.

Koi Hanako ka intezaar nahi kar raha tha ki woh aakar sabko bacha le.

Sab milkar kaam kar rahe the.

Usne decorations ka ek box uthaya aur unke saath jud gayi.

Shaam hone tak gym paper lanterns aur warm lights se jagmaga raha tha. Final performance thodi der se shuru hui aur plan ke mutaabik bilkul nahi thi. Phir bhi audience ne taaliyan bajaayin.

Aakhri student ke jaane ke baad, Hanako aur Kaito covered walkway par khade baarish ki boondon ko chhat par girte sun rahe the.

“Tumne achha kiya,” usne kaha.

“Sabne achha kiya.”

“Tumne khud ko bhi usmein include kiya.”

“Iski aadat mat daalna. Meri ek reputation hai.”

Woh muskuraaya.

Hanako railing se tek laga kar khadi ho gayi.

“Pehle mujhe lagta tha ki agar main sab kuch organized rakhoon, toh koi mujhe surprise nahi kar paayega.”

“Kaam kiya?”

“Nahi. Log paperwork se kahin zyada inconvenient hote hain.”

“Mera bhi yahi experience raha hai.”

Woh uski taraf mudi.

“Mera ek aakhri rule hai.”

Kaito seedha khada ho gaya. “Main sun raha hoon.”

“Agar tum dare hue ho, toh gayab mat hona. Agar main gusse mein hoon, toh pretend nahi karungi ki nahi hoon. Agar hum dono mein se koi galti kare, toh uske baare mein baat karenge—isse pehle ki woh photos aur ominous messages wali koi poori mystery ban jaaye.”

“Yeh reasonable lagta hai.”

“Aur hum yeh promise nahi karenge ki kabhi jhagda nahi hoga. Woh unrealistic hai.”

“Agreed.”

“Hum promise karenge ki baat-cheet par wapas zaroor aayenge.”

Kaito ne uski taraf dekha.

“Main yeh promise kar sakta hoon.”

“Achha.”

Usne Kaito ka haath pakad liya.

“Aur jab hum akele hon, toh tum mujhe President kehna band kar sakte ho.”

Usne is baare mein socha.

“Hanako?”

“Haan?”

“I love you.”

Uska expression narm pad gaya.

Pehli baar, usne koi clever jawaab dhoondhne ya shikayat ke peeche chhupne ki koshish nahi ki.

“I love you too, Kaito.”

Woh kareeb aaya, phir ruk gaya.

“Kya main tumhe kiss kar sakta hoon?”

Hanako ki muskaan chamak uthi, thodi shararti bhi.

“Har baar poochhna padta hai?”

“Mujhe assume karne se behtar poochhna lagta hai.”

Woh panjon par uthi aur use kiss kiya.

“Haan,” alag hote waqt usne dheere se kaha. “Kar sakte ho.”

Gym ke andar kahin se Hiro ki awaaz goonji.

“MUJHE PATA THA! MUJHE PATA THA BAARISH MEIN ROMANTIC POTENTIAL HAI!”

Hanako ne aankhein band kar li.

“Kal main uska microphone chheen loongi.”

Kaito hans pada aur uska haath thoda aur mazbooti se thaam liya.

“Kal,” usne haami bhari.

Aaj raat ke liye, baarish ko girte rehne do.

Unhe kahin aur jaane ki zaroorat nahi thi.

EpilogueEveryone, Apparently

Graduation day ek saaf aasman ke neeche aaya.

Hanako Shimizu school gate par khadi thi. Uski uniform bilkul perfect thi, certificate folder mein rakha tha, aur chehre par wahi expression tha jo kisi aise insaan ka hota hai jisne ceremony shuru hone se pehle hi usmein teen problems dhoondh li hon.

Kaito do drinks lekar uske paas aaya.

Usne tea ki bottle aage badhaayi.

“Pehle poochha tha,” usne kaha.

Hanako ne bottle le li.

“Excellent progress.”

“Umeed hai evaluation achha milega.”

“Tumhara performance satisfactory hai.”

“Sirf satisfactory?”

“Lunch ke baad appeal kar sakte ho.”

Woh muskuraaya.

Unke aas-paas classmates photos le rahe the aur ek-doosre se contact mein rehne ke waade kar rahe the. Hiro itni zor se ro raha tha ki baaki sabke liye bhi problem ban gaya tha.

“Main emotional nahi hoon,” usne announce kiya. “Meri aankhein bas hamari academic achievements celebrate kar rahi hain.”

“Tum apni tie par ro rahe ho,” Hanako ne kaha.

“Meri tie bahut absorbent hai.”

Kaito hans pada.

Hanako kuch der use dekhti rahi. Use ab bhi appointments par jaana tha. Hanako ko ab bhi har detail manage karne ki aadat thi. Ek-doosre ko dobara chun lene bhar se dono mein se koi perfect insaan nahi ban gaya tha.

Lekin jab bhi koi baat use pareshan karti, Kaito use bata deta.

Jab bhi koi baat Hanako ko buri lagti, woh keh deti.

Woh sawaal poochhte. Jawaab sunte. Maafi maangte, bina yeh samjhe ki sirf sorry keh dene se sab kuch jaadui tareeke se mit jaata hai.

Unka relationship past se bach nikalne ka koi reward nahi tha.

Woh ek aisi cheez thi jise woh har aam din, milkar banana choose kar rahe the.

Group photos ke baad, Kaito aur Hanako purane classroom ki taraf chale. Dhoop desks par faili hui thi. Woh seat jahan unki pehli mulaqat hui thi, khaali thi.

“Tumhe yaad hai tumne mujhse kya poochha tha?” Hanako ne kaha.

Kaito ne uski taraf dekha.

“Ki main jaanta hoon tum kaun ho?”

“Aur tumne kya jawaab diya tha?”

“Ki tum mere bagal mein baithi ho.”

“Ek had tak irritating jawaab.”

“Tumne bhi toh ek had tak ajeeb sawaal poochha tha.”

Usne uske kandhe par halka sa dhakka diya.

“Mujhe laga tha tum mujhse nafrat karte ho.”

“Mujhe laga tha tum sabse nafrat karti ho.”

“Karti thi.”

“Sabse?”

Usne sochne ka natak kiya.

“Lagbhag sabse.”

“Aur ab?”

Hanako ne uska haath pakad liya.

“Ab maine ek exception bana liya hai.”

Kaito ne unke jude hue haathon ko dekha, phir uski taraf.

“Sirf ek?”

“Zyada laalchi mat bano.”

Woh hans pada.

Dono dopahar mein saath bahar nikle. Future uncertain tha, bilkul aam tareekon se: naye schools, naya kaam, nayi responsibilities, aur anjaan train lines par bhatak jaane ka chance.

Lekin uncertainty ka matlab ab yeh nahi tha ki unhe har cheez akele face karni padegi.

Gate par Hanako ruki aur uski taraf mudi.

“Ek aakhri baat.”

Kaito ne naatak karte hue saans chhodi. “Hamesha aur rules hote hain.”

“Yeh rule nahi hai.”

“Toh kya hai?”

Woh muskuraayi.

“Ek request hai.”

“Main sun raha hoon.”

“Mere saath rehna.”

Usne mazaak nahi kiya. Kisi clever jawaab ke peeche nahi chhupa. Usne Hanako ka haath pakda aur seedha uski aankhon mein dekha.

“Main rahunga,” usne kaha. “Aur agar kuch badla, toh tumhe bataunga. Tumhe andaaza lagane ke liye nahi chhodunga.”

Hanako ki muskaan halki si kaanpkar aur narm ho gayi.

“Achha jawaab.”

School gate ke neeche usne use kiss kiya. Late afternoon ki dhoop un par chamak rahi thi, aur kahin ek ped ke peeche se Hiro chilla raha tha ki woh bilkul bhi chhupkar nahi dekh raha.

Is baar Hanako ne kisi cheez ko revoke karne ki dhamki nahi di.

Woh bas hans padi.

Aur Kaito, jo kabhi maanta tha ki kisi se pyaar karne ka sabse safe tareeka apne mushkil hisson ko chhupana hai, aakhir samajh gaya ki happy ending ka matlab aisi zindagi nahi jahan kabhi darr na ho.

Happy ending woh zindagi hai jahan darr ko ab faisle lene ki zaroorat nahi padti.

Hanako Shimizu ko ab bhi incompetence, bekaar ke shor aur un ladkon se nafrat thi jo persistence ko permission samajh lete hain.

Woh ab bhi maanti thi ki student council handbook mein kai improvements ki zaroorat hai.

Lekin ab woh yeh nahi sochti thi ki kisi ki parwah karne ka matlab har possible outcome ko control karna hota hai.

Kaito Mizuno ko ab bhi help maangne, khamoshi ko deewar banne se pehle baat karne, aur apne past ko apna ek hissa samajhne—poori pehchaan nahi—mein bahut kuch seekhna tha.

Lekin ab use yeh sab akele seekhne ki zaroorat nahi thi.

Dono station ki taraf saath chale, haath mein haath, aur unka future abhi likha jaana baaki tha.

Aur is baar, page palatne se pehle dono mein se koi bhi dara hua nahi tha.
