Chapter One Raat 11:52 Ke Paanch Lafz

Haruki ko khamoshi kabhi raas nahi aayi.

Woh music chala kar padhta, ghar lautte waqt kisi ko call karta, aur aadhi raat ke baad bina matlab ke memes bhej deta—sirf isliye kyunki shaant kamre mein uske khayal bahut shor machane lagte. Khamoshi ka matlab tha kuch galat hai. Khamoshi ka matlab tha koi naraaz hai. Khamoshi ka matlab tha duniya use bataye bina aage badh gayi.

Aaj raat, Saitama ke kinaare uske chhote se bedroom mein, khamoshi ek na-bulaye mehmaan ki tarah uske paas baithi thi. Baarish balcony ki railing par tap-tap kar rahi thi. Desk lamp ki roshni mein aadhi bhari worksheet, bina chhui chai, aur uske haath mein pakda phone dikh raha tha.

Aoi ke saath wali chat abhi bhi khuli thi.

Uska aakhri message mahino ke jokes, badalon wale aasman ki photos, ghar lautte waqt record kiye voice notes aur raat ke sawaalon ke neeche pada tha—woh sawaal jo dheere-dheere Haruki ki routine ka hissa ban gaye the.

Apna khayal rakhna, Haruki.

Paanch lafz. Koi explanation nahi. Aisi koi behas nahi jise woh apne dimaag mein dobara chala kar jeet sake. Koi dramatic goodbye nahi jise woh wajah bana sake. Bas uska naam, seedhe aur saadhe andaaz mein—jaise koi ghar mein kisi ko jagaye bina darwaza band kar raha ho.

Usne message dobara padha. Phir ek baar aur.

Ek pal ke liye usne lagbhag type kar diya, Kya hua? Phir delete kar diya. Phir likhne laga, Kya mujhse kuch galat ho gaya? Woh bhi mita diya.

Screen ki roshni dheemi pad gayi. Haruki ne message ko zinda rakhne ke liye screen chhoo di, halaanki use pata tha ki phone lafzon ko sambhaal sakta hai, unhe bhejne wale insaan ko nahi.

Chapter Two Group Chat Mein Ek Meme

Unki mulaqat sabse bhool jaane layak tareeke se hui thi: ek mutual friend ke group chat mein.

Kisi ne rice cooker ke andar baithe billi ki ek bakwaas photo share ki. Aoi ne reply kiya ki woh billi zyada tar adults se behtar ghar chala sakti hai. Haruki class mein itni zor se hans pada ki uske dost Kenta ne kohni maar kar use chup karaya.

School ke baad Haruki ne Aoi ko private message bheja. Woh reply sach mein achha tha.

Uska jawab lagbhag turant aaya. “Sach mein achha”? Bas itni hi tareef kar sakte ho?

Use kuch clever kehna chahiye tha. Iske bajaye usne likha, Main apni vocabulary bacha kar use kar raha hoon.

Kis liye? University entrance essay ke liye?

Bilkul hi bewakoofi bhari conversation thi. Phir bhi, jab kehne ko kuch nahi bacha tha, tab bhi Haruki usse aage badhana chahta tha.

Dono memes share karte, pizza par pineapple hona chahiye ya nahi is par behas karte, aur badhti hui bekaar baaton ko prove karne ke liye voice messages bhejte. Aoi ki awaaz ke aakhir mein halki si hansi aa jaati jab use pata hota ki woh zidd kar rahi hai. Haruki un messages ko dobara sunne laga, jaise use argument phir se sunna zaroori ho.

Doosre hafte tak woh lagbhag har din baat karne lage. Mahina khatam hote-hote, ek seedha-sa goodnight dono ki routine ka aakhri step ban gaya.

Dono ne is rishte ko koi naam nahi diya. Isse sab kuch safe lagta tha. Bas internet ke shor mein mile do log the, jinhe pata chala ki saamne wala rukta hai—kahin jaata nahi.

Chapter Three Ek Aam Din Ka Sabse Pyaara Hissa

Unke sabse achhe mahine kisi bade ya yaadgaar event se bhare nahi the. Woh chhoti-chhoti cheezon se bane the jo baar-baar hoti rehti thi.

Aoi train platform se aasman ki photos bhejti, aksar mausam ki complaint ke saath. Haruki convenience store ke desserts ki photos bhejta jinhe woh jaanta tha Aoi buri rating degi. Woh batati ki ek teacher ne uski notebook ko textbook samajh liya. Haruki uske classmate ke ajeeb lunch choice ki das minute ki kahani aise sunta jaise koi zaroori report ho.

Ek shaam, Haruki ke uski mushkil din wali baat sunne ke baad, Aoi kuch der ke liye chup ho gayi.

Pata hai, tumse baat karne mein mujhe kya achha lagta hai? Usne aakhir type kiya.

Haruki ka pehla instinct mazaak karne ka tha. Usne khud ko rok liya.

Kya?

Tum sach mein sunte ho. Zyadaatar log bas apni baari ka intezaar karte hain. Tumhare saath mujhe jaldi-jaldi bolne ki zaroorat mehsoos nahi hoti.

Haruki message ko dekhta raha, jab tak screen ki roshni dheemi nahi pad gayi. Group mein woh aksar chup rehne wala insaan tha, jiske lafz itni der se aate ki tab tak conversation aage badh chuki hoti. Usne kabhi nahi socha tha ki sunna bhi koi aisi cheez ho sakti hai jise koi notice kare.

Shayad mere paas kehne ke liye interesting kuch hota hi nahi, usne reply kiya.

Dekha? Compliment se bach nikalna bhi ek talent hai.

Haruki apne andhere kamre mein muskura diya.

Uske baad aam din thode halke lagne lage. Monday phir bhi Monday hi the. Exams ab bhi thaka dete the. Baarish mein station se ghar aana ab bhi bura lagta tha. Lekin ab uske paas koi tha jise woh yeh sab bata sakta tha, aur Aoi ke paas koi tha jo chhoti-chhoti baatein yaad rakhta tha.

Chapter Four Replies Ke Beech Ki Doori

Badlav itni khamoshi se shuru hua ki Haruki hafton tak khud ko samjhata raha ki shayad kuch badla hi nahi.

Jo reply do minute mein aa jaata tha, ab bees minute leta. Phir ek ghanta. Aoi ke lambe paragraphs chhote jawab ban gaye; voice messages ki jagah reactions aur ek jaldi sa haha aane laga. Raat ki calls kal ke liye taal di jaati, aur kal bas aage khisakta rehta.

Haruki har cheez notice karta. Woh online hoti lekin reply nahi karti. Messages mein pehle wale exclamation marks kam ho gaye. Woh ab aasman ki woh chhoti photos nahi bhejti thi jo bina kisi explanation ke aa jaati thi.

Use pata tha ki chat window ke bahar bhi logon ki zindagi hoti hai. Woh khud ko baar-baar yahi samjhata. Phir bhi, har unanswered message ek aisa sawaal lagta jise type karne se woh darta tha.

Aaj busy ho?

Haan, sorry.

Kal call karna hai?

Dekhti hoon kaisa feel hota hai.

Kal aaya. Call nahi hui.

Usne bhi uski pace match karne ki koshish ki, kam messages bhejne laga taaki Aoi ko ghutan na ho. Phir use darr laga ki uski khamoshi ko beparwahi samjha jayega. Usne ek funny picture bheji, phir sirf ek emoji reaction milne par use bhejne ka afsos hua.

Replies ke beech ki khaali jagah naapkar dosti ko samajhna thaka dene wala tha. Aur yeh maanna usse bhi zyada thaka dene wala tha ki woh aisa kar raha hai.

Haruki ko Aoi ki yaad aati thi, lekin use nahi pata tha ki woh yeh keh bhi sakta hai ya nahi.

Chapter Five Aoi Ki Woh Explanation Jo Kabhi Bheji Nahi Gayi

Aoi ne care karna band nahi kiya tha. Isi wajah se yeh doori itni mushkil thi.

Ghar mein mahino se pressure badhta ja raha tha. Uske parents grades, future aur har us faisle ko lekar pareshaan the jo Aoi ke chunne se pehle hi koi darwaza band kar sakta tha. Har behas cheekhne tak nahi pahunchti thi. Kabhi-kabhi sabse zyada takleef tab hoti jab har koi tameez se baat karta, lekin koi yeh nahi maanta ki woh kitna hurt hai.

Aoi theek dikhne mein mahir ho gayi thi. School mein muskurati, sawaalon ke jawab deti, doston ko homework mein help karti, aur apni zindagi ke bhaari hisse ko is tarah chhupa kar rakhti ki koi dekh na sake.

Haruki uske din ka sabse safe hissa ban gaya tha. Woh bina kisi performance ki demand kiye sunta. Chhoti baatein yaad rakhta. Jab Aoi itni thak jaati ki kisi cheez mein mazaak na dikhe, tab bhi woh use hasa deta.

Aur kyunki Haruki uske liye maayne rakhta tha, Aoi ko darr lagne laga ki agar usne use dikhaya ki woh kitni mushkil mein hai, toh kya hoga.

Usne unki chat na jaane kitni baar kholi. Type kiya, Main theek nahi hoon. Phir delete kar diya. Likha, Mujhe darr hai ki main woh insaan nahi hoon jo tum samajhte ho. Woh bhi mita diya.

Usne khud ko samjhaya ki woh Haruki ko aisi problem se bacha rahi hai jise woh samjha nahi sakti. Lekin har din door hote hue, woh Haruki ko apni taraf se wajahen gadhne ke liye chhod rahi thi.

Ek der raat usne Haruki ka purana voice message suna. Woh kisi aise joke par hans raha tha jo Aoi khud bhool chuki thi. Kuch seconds ke liye use laga woh phir se wahi ladki hai jo use aasman ki photos bhejti thi aur mausam ki complaint karti thi.

Phir pressure laut aaya, aur usne reply kiye bina app band kar diya.

Chapter Six Typing Indicator

Ek Tuesday raat, unki ajeeb si doori ke teen hafte baad, Haruki ne aakhir woh sawaal poochh hi liya jo har jagah uska peecha kar raha tha.

Ek baat poochh sakta hoon?

Aoi ne ek minute baad jawab diya. Haan, bilkul.

Kya mujhse kuch galat ho gaya?

Message read ho gaya.

Ek pal tak kuch nahi hua. Phir typing indicator aaya. Gayab ho gaya. Dobara aaya, kuch seconds raha, aur phir mit gaya.

Haruki bilkul chup-chaap baitha raha, un teen chhote dots ko aate-jaate dekhta hua—jaise unke andar unki poori dosti ka sach sama sakta ho.

Shehar ke doosri taraf Aoi apni screen ko ghoor rahi thi. Usne type kiya ki use afsos hai. Type kiya ki ghar par kuch chal raha hai aur use samajh nahi aa raha kaise bataye. Type kiya ki Haruki ki koi galti nahi, woh uski zindagi mein aane wali sabse achhi cheezon mein se ek tha, aur use darr tha ki woh use hurt kar degi.

Har version adhura lagta tha. Har explanation jaise usse ek aise insaan ka intezaar karne ko keh rahi thi jo khud nahi bata sakta tha ki kab lautne ke liye ready hoga.

Usne poora paragraph mita diya.

Haruki ne ek aur message bheja.

Tumhe sab kuch explain karne ki zaroorat nahi. Bas mujhe itna jaan na hai ki kya tum ab bhi mujhe apne aas-paas chahti ho.

Aoi ne apne hont bheench liye. Woh haan kehna chahti thi. Woh kehna chahti thi, Please mujhe thoda waqt do. Woh maanna chahti thi ki in cheezon ko chahna hi inhe mumkin bana dega.

Iske bajaye, lambi khamoshi ke baad, usne ek aisa sentence bheja jise padhna bhi uske liye mushkil tha.

Apna khayal rakhna, Haruki.

Typing indicator dobara kabhi nahi aaya.

Chapter Seven Ek Ending Ka Aakaar

Haruki ne us raat message pachaas baar padha. Agli subah phir padha, aur phir school jaate waqt train mein—phone neeche pakad kar, taaki koi poochhe nahi ki woh kya dekh raha hai.

Usne har lafz mein chhupa hua matlab dhoondhne ki koshish ki. “Apna khayal rakhna” hi kyun? Us silly nickname ki jagah uska naam kyun, jo Aoi ne use diya tha? Koi explanation kyun nahi, koi sawaal kyun nahi, koi chhota sa ishara kyun nahi ki shayad baad mein ek aur conversation hogi?

Uske dost Kenta ne notice kiya ki Haruki kuch zyada hi chup ho gaya tha.

“Kuch hua hai?” bicycle racks ke paas se guzarte hue Kenta ne poochha.

“Khaas kuch nahi,” Haruki ne kaha.

Technically, yeh sach tha. Koi jhagda nahi hua tha. Koi ilzaam nahi laga tha. Koi ek pal nahi tha jise woh point karke ending keh sake. Bas kuch dheere-dheere feeka pad gaya tha, kyunki dono hi yeh naam dene se darte rahe ki aakhir ho kya raha tha.

Kuch waqt tak Haruki aise messages type karta raha jo kabhi bheje hi nahi.

Tum mujhe bata sakti thi.

Main intezaar kar leta.

Umeed hai tum theek ho, chahe mujhe kabhi pata na chale.

Usne unhe ek-ek karke delete kar diya. Aakhir mein usne yeh check karna band kar diya ki Aoi online hai ya nahi. Isliye nahi ki use parwah karna band ho gaya tha, balki isliye ki har chhote ishare ko promise samajhte-samajhte woh thak gaya tha.

Chat uske phone mein ab bhi thi. Woh use delete nahi kar pa raha tha. Unki conversations wahin thi, usi order mein sambhali hui, un jokes se bhari jo kabhi aam dinon ko khaas bana dete the.

Woh samajhne laga ki messages sambhaal kar rakhna, Aoi ko sambhaal kar rakhne jaisa nahi tha.

Chapter Eight Baarish Ke Baad Jo Reh Jaata Hai

Mahine beet gaye. Dard ki teekhi chubhan kam hui, halaanki poori tarah gayab kabhi nahi hui.

Kabhi station ke paas kisi dukaan mein Aoi ka recommend kiya hua gaana bajne lagta. Kabhi aasman bilkul us halke orange rang ka ho jaata jiski woh photos bhejti thi, aur Haruki phone nikaalne lagta—phir yaad aata ki ab bhejne ke liye koi nahi raha.

Woh sochta ki agar usne pehle poochh liya hota toh kya hota. Agar usne kaha hota, Mujhe lag raha hai kuch theek nahi hai. Tumhe explain karne ki zaroorat nahi, lekin main yahin hoon. Shayad Aoi us par thoda aur bharosa karke sach ka kuch hissa bata deti. Shayad kuch bhi nahi badalta. Woh jaan nahi sakta tha, aur isi uncertainty ko saath lekar chalna use seekhna tha.

Ek shaam, train ka intezaar karte hue baarish shuru ho gayi. Platform ki lights geeli patriyon par dhundhli pad rahi thi. Haruki ne unki chat kholi, jokes aur photos ke aage scroll kiya, aur aakhri message par ruk gaya.

Is baar usne uska matlab nikaalne ki koshish nahi ki.

Usne use wahi rehne diya jo woh tha: aakhri cheez jo Aoi use de paayi thi, na ki uski poori kahani.

Use ab bhi afsos tha ki Aoi ne explain nahi kiya. Woh ab bhi jaana chahta tha ki kya woh behtar ho rahi thi. Kisi ki care karna sirf isliye khatam nahi ho jaata ki ab us care ko rakhne ki koi jagah nahi bachi.

Train halki si hawa ke shor ke saath aa gayi. Haruki ne phone lock kiya aur train mein chadh gaya.

Kuch endings darwaza patak kar aati hain. Kuch paanch shaant lafzon ki tarah aati hain, aur peeche reh jaane wale ko us khaali jagah ke ird-gird apni zindagi dobara banani padti hai.

Woh ab bhi seekh raha tha ki kaise.
