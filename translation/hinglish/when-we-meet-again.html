Chapter One Khamoshi Ke Rang

Us raat ke do saal baad, jab sab kuch toot gaya tha, Akito Mori ne apni zindagi ko ek aise mod par la diya tha jahan sab kuch theek se chal raha tha. Usne degree poori kar li thi, doosre shehar mein shift ho gaya tha, aur ek chhote photography studio mein internship mil gayi thi. Uska room saada tha: ek bed, ek desk, camera bag aur kitaabon ki ek shelf jinhe woh padhne ka iraada rakhta tha. Woh rent dene aur camera ke liye film khareedne jitna kama leta tha. Kenta se aksar har hafte baat hoti thi. Uski photography series, *Colours of Silence*, dheere-dheere popular ho gayi thi. Khaali stations, band classrooms, baarish ke baad ek bench. Woh aisi jagahon ki photos leta tha jo aisi lagti thi jaise koi abhi-abhi wahan se gaya ho. Log tasveeron ko khoobsurat kehte. Akito tareef maan leta, bina yeh bataye ki editing karte waqt uske khayalon mein kitni baar ek khaas chehra aa jaata tha. Mio bhi graduate ho chuki thi. Woh doosre shehar ki ek chhoti publishing company mein junior editor thi. Ab woh apne lafzon ko pehle se zyada soch-samajh kar chunti thi, aur usne seekh liya tha ki guilt mehsoos karna aur galti sudhaarna ek hi baat nahi hai. Usne Akito se contact nahi kiya tha. Phir unke purane college town ki ek gallery se invitation aaya. Curator ne *Colours of Silence* ki kuch photos group exhibition ke liye select ki thi. Akito ne lagbhag mana kar diya. Kenta ne use samjhaya ki past se judi har jagah se door rehna, aage badhne ke barabar nahi hota. Isliye woh gaya. Gallery roshan thi, bheed se bhari thi aur har taraf baatein ho rahi thi. Akito deewar ke paas hi ruka raha, jab tak usne apni ek photo ke saamne khadi pehchaani hui shakl nahi dekhi. Mio. Woh sardi ke mausam mein nange pedon ke neeche rakhi ek bench ki photo dekh rahi thi. Akito ne yeh tasveer unki aakhri baat-cheet ke kaafi baad li thi, lekin jagah wahi thi jahan woh kabhi ek mushkil din ke baad saath baithe the. Caption tha: *Jahan shuru hua. Jahan khatam hua.* Mio ne use bahut der tak dekha. Jab woh mudi, unki nazrein mil gayin. Kuch pal tak dono mein se koi nahi hila. Phir Akito uske paas chala gaya. “Hello, Mio.” “Hello, Akito.” Do saal ke unsent messages, afsos aur mann mein banayi gayi baatein chaar aam lafzon mein simat gayin. “Kaise ho?” usne poochha. “Kaam kar raha hoon. Photos le raha hoon.” “Maine series online dekhi thi. Bahut khoobsurat hai.” “Thank you.” Usne nahi poochha ki photos uske baare mein thi ya nahi. Akito ne nahi poochha ki woh kyun aayi thi. Kuch minute tak unhone kaam, gallery aur us town ke baare mein baat ki jo dono ki umeed se kam badla tha. Jab woh alag hue, Akito andar se hila hua tha—lekin toota nahi tha. Past ne unhe ek hi room mein la khada kiya tha. Pehli baar, dono mein se kisi ne use dobara likhne ki koshish nahi ki.

Chapter Two Woh Maafi Jis Par Koi Haq Nahi Tha

Agle din Mio ne ek message bheja. *Kal mujhse baat karne ke liye thank you. Main baar-baar contact nahi karungi. Bas kehna chahti thi ki tumhe achha karte dekhkar khushi hui.* Akito ne message padha, kuch der socha aur jawab diya: *Mujhe bhi khushi hai ki tum achhi ho.* Baat chhoti thi. Isse unka relationship dobara shuru nahi hua. Bas dono ko yeh maan lene ka mauka mila ki woh apni zindagi ke sabse bure pal ke baad bhi jee rahe do insaan the. Ek hafte baad Akito ek print lene gallery wapas gaya. Mio bhi wahan thi, ek colleague ke saath agle event ki taiyaari kar rahi thi. Aakhir mein dono saath station ki taraf chal pade. Mio ne poochha ki kya woh uski ek theek se maafi sunna chahega—maafi isliye nahi ki woh maafi ki umeed karti thi, balki isliye ki ab woh pehle ki tarah dhundhle lafzon ke peeche nahi chhupna chahti thi. Akito ne socha. “Theek hai,” usne kaha. “Lekin main promise nahi kar sakta ki uske baad mujhe kaisa lagega.” “Main samajhti hoon.” Woh station ke paas ek shaant café mein baithe. Mio ne saaf-saaf sach bataya. Use kisi naye insaan ke chahe jaane ka ehsaas achha laga tha. Use Akito ko khone ka darr tha, isliye woh apne relationship ka comfort bhi rakhna chahti thi aur Ryo ke attention ka excitement bhi. Usne baatein chhupayi, Akito ki fikr ko nazarandaaz kiya, aur maafi tab maangi jab sach ko chhupana mumkin nahi raha. “Main khud se kehti rahi ki main confused hoon,” usne kaha. “Main confused thi, lekin maine faisle bhi liye. Maine tumhe hurt kiya, aur tum iske haqdaar nahi the.” Akito bina beech mein roke sunta raha. “Main sirf afsos mehsoos karne ki wajah se tumse trust karne ko nahi keh sakti,” woh aage boli. “Mujhe pata hai ki apne faislon ki keemat mujhe maan-ni hogi.” Kuch der tak Akito apni coffee se uthti bhaap dekhta raha. “Ab aakhirkaar saaf-saaf kehne ke liye thank you,” usne jawab diya. “Mujhe lagta hai tum sach keh rahi ho. Lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki hum pehle jaise ho sakte hain.” “Mujhe pata hai.” Dono café se alag-alag nikle. Akito ko achanak koi sukoon nahi mila. Koi filmy pal nahi aaya jisme past be-asar ho gaya ho. Lekin us maafi ne usse Mio ko tasalli dene ya uska guilt mitaane ko nahi kaha tha. Usne sach ko uska sahi naam diya tha. Yeh maayne rakhta tha.

Chapter Three Bina Shortcut Ke Dobara Bharosa

Unki agli mulaqat ittefaq se hui, aur uske baad ek aur jaan-boojh kar. Unhone aam baaton par kabhi-kabhi messages karne shuru kiye: Akito ki exhibition mein lagi ek photo, Mio ki edit ki hui kitaab, ya koi café jo dono ko yaad tha. Koi bhi turant reply ki demand nahi karta. Agar kisi ko space chahiye hoti, toh woh keh deta. Akito ne dhyaan rakha ki kindness ko promise na samjhe. Mio ne bhi dhyaan rakha ki contact ko forgiveness na samjhe. Shuru mein unki baat-cheet formal thi. Dheere-dheere woh natural hone lagi. Unhe yaad aaya ki saath hansna kaisa lagta hai, halanki ab woh hansi ek-doosre ke baare mein jaane mushkil sach ko mita nahi deti thi. Ek shaam Mio ne poochha ki kya woh Akito ka chhota studio dekh sakti hai. Akito maan gaya aur use woh prints dikhaye jo woh ek nayi series ke liye taiyaar kar raha tha. Woh ek khaali room mein dhoop ki lakeer wali photo ke saamne ruk gayi. “Yeh alag lagti hai,” usne kaha. “Kaise?” “Aisa nahi lagta ki koi abhi-abhi yahan se gaya hai. Lagta hai koi aane wala hai.” Akito ne photo ko phir dekha. “Maine is tarah nahi socha tha.” “Shayad isi liye mujhe yeh pasand hai.” Woh muskura diya. Use ehsaas hua ki bina khud ko defend kiye kisi baat par charcha karna use kitna yaad aaya tha. Trust sirf isliye wapas nahi aaya kyunki dono chahte the. Woh chhote, nazar aane wale faislon se badha. Mio sach bolti, chahe usse woh buri lage. Akito kehta jab use bechaini mehsoos hoti, bajaye yeh dikhane ke ki sab theek hai. Dono boundaries par baat karte, bina unhe saza samjhe. Kuch din purana dard wapas aa jaata. Aisa hota toh woh yeh dikhawa nahi karte ki dard gayab ho gaya hai. Woh dheere ho jaate, imaandari se baat karte aur bina zabardasti koi jawab nikaale us feeling ko guzarne dete. Dobara bharosa banana toote hue vase ko jodne jaisa kam, aur aisi zameen par naya ghar ban sakta hai ya nahi—yeh jaanne jaisa zyada tha jo kabhi hil chuki ho. Dono mein se kisi ko nahi pata tha ki iska anjaam kya hoga. Pehli baar, na jaan-na ka matlab bhaag jaana nahi tha.

Chapter Four Sardi Mein Ek Mulaqat

January mein Akito Mio ke shehar gaya. Uske paas ek weekend khaali tha aur ek sawaal tha jo woh saamne baithkar poochhna chahta tha. Usne Mio ko pehle hi bata diya ki woh aa raha hai. Woh bina bataye uske darwaze par nahi pahuncha aur na hi is mulaqat ko koi test banaya. Dono ne nadi ke paas ek park mein milne ka faisla kiya. Mio grey coat aur wahi scarf pehenkar aayi jo college ke dino mein uske paas tha. Use dekhkar Akito ko woh ladki yaad aayi jo woh pehle thi—aur woh insaan bhi jo ab ban chuki thi. Dono nange pedon ke neeche chale. Hawa itni thandi thi ki unki saans dikh rahi thi. “Mujhe nahi pata hum phir se waise ban sakte hain ya nahi, jaise pehle the,” Akito ne kaha. Mio ne uski taraf dekha. “Mujhe nahi lagta humein bilkul waise banne ki koshish karni chahiye.” Akito ko laga tha woh ek aur mauka maangegi. Iske bajaye, woh use yeh faisla karne ki jagah de rahi thi ki ek mauke ka matlab kya ho sakta hai. “Mujhe ab bhi tumhari parwah hai,” usne maana. “Lekin sirf isliye jo hua use nazarandaaz nahi karna chahta kyunki tumhari yaad aati hai.” “Tumhe nahi karna chahiye.” “Aur main promise nahi kar sakta ki mujhe dobara hurt hone ka darr kabhi nahi lagega.” “Main promise nahi kar sakti ki tumhe kabhi dard nahi hoga. Main yeh promise kar sakti hoon ki tumse sach bolungi, chahe woh sach mushkil hi kyun na ho.” Woh nadi ke paas ruk gaye. Sardi ki roshni mein paani behta raha, jaise kinare par liye ja rahe faislon se use koi farq na padta ho. Akito ne saans li. “Main tumhe dobara jaanne ki koshish karna chahta hoon. Dheere-dheere. Aise nahi jaise kuch hua hi nahi.” Mio ki aankhon mein aansu aa gaye, lekin woh uski taraf daudkar nahi aayi aur na hi usse is faisle ko usse bada banane ko kaha. “Main bhi yahi chahti hoon,” usne kaha. Dono phir chalne lage. Unke haath ek baar takraaye, phir dobara. Mio ne unke beech ki jagah ko dekha, faisla Akito par chhodte hue. Akito ne uska haath pakad liya. Mio ne use pyaar se thaam liya. Yeh past mein lautna nahi tha. Yeh kisi aisi cheez ki taraf ek chhota, sambhal kar uthaya gaya kadam tha jiske anjaam ki guarantee kisi ke paas nahi thi. Filhaal, itna kaafi tha.

Chapter Five Phir Milenge

Purane college town mein spring laut aayi. Station road ke saath cherry trees phoolon se bhar gaye, aur students bag kandhon par uchhalte hue unke neeche se jaldi-jaldi guzarte. Akito aur Mio ne yeh announce nahi kiya ki woh phir saath hain. Woh abhi bhi seekh rahe the ki unka relationship kya ban sakta hai. Woh plans banate aur unhe poora karte. Kuch galat lagta toh baat karte. Mushkil sawaalon ko chup karane ke liye affection ka istemaal nahi karte. Kuch din aasaan hote. Kuch nahi. Jab Akito ke purane doubts laut aate, woh yaad karne ki koshish karta ki ehtiyaat aur yaqeen ek hi cheez nahi. Jab Mio se galti hoti, woh use chhupakar badi problem banne dene ke bajaye maan-na seekhti. Dono past ko badal nahi sakte the; lekin aage kya karna hai, yeh chun sakte the. Ek dopahar woh Seihoku College gaye. Cafeteria mein naye tables the, aur purani vending machines gayab ho chuki thi. Auditorium Akito ki yaad se chhota lag raha tha. Mio us seat ke paas khadi hui jahan woh pehli baar mile the. “Tum kitne chup rehte the,” usne kaha. “Tum hum dono ke liye kaafi bolti thi.” “Abhi bhi bolti hoon.” “Kuch cheezein nahi badalti.” Woh muskuraayi. “Kuch badalti hain.” Dono bahar nikle aur cherry trees ke neeche baith gaye. Kuch pankhudiyan raaste par girin. Akito ko pehla saal yaad aaya, jab future aasaan lagta tha kyunki unhe pata hi nahi tha ki woh unse kya maangega. “Kya tumhe mujhse milne ka afsos hai?” Mio ne poochha. Usne sawaal ka imaandari se jawab dene ke liye socha. “Nahi. Mujhe afsos hai ki humne ek-doosre ko jis tarah hurt kiya. Jo baatein humne chhupayi, unka afsos hai. Lekin tumse milne ka afsos nahi.” Mio ne sir hila diya. Usne jawab ko jaisa tha waisa hi liya, usse zyada matlab nikaalne ki koshish nahi ki. Dono ne dopahar ko guzarne diya. Aakhir mein Mio ne apna kandha uske kandhe se tika diya. Akito ne use wahin rehne diya. Future ab bhi uncertain tha. Unhe ek-doosre ka trust lagataar kamaana hoga, aur bade hone ki mehnat se pyaar unhe chhoot nahi dega. Lekin ab woh yeh dikhawa nahi kar rahe the ki doosra mauka milne ka matlab sab bhool jaana hai. Jab woh station pahunche, Mio ne campus ki taraf mudkar dekha. “Phir milenge,” usne kaha. Akito muskuraaya. “Abhi toh mile.” “Mera matlab agli baar.” “Agli baar bhi.” Train aa gayi. Woh saath chadhe—isliye nahi ki unki kahani perfect ho gayi thi, balki isliye ki unhone aankhen khuli rakhkar use aage badhane ka faisla kiya tha. Kuch log milte hain, alag ho jaate hain aur phir kabhi ek-doosre ki zindagi mein nahi laut-te. Kuch dobara milte hain aur samajhte hain ki past ne badal diya hai ki woh ab ek-doosre ko kya de sakte hain. Aur kabhi-kabhi, sach keh dene aur waqt ko apna shaant kaam karne dene ke baad, do log phir se shuru karne ka faisla karte hain—shuruaat se nahi, balki jahan woh sach mein khade hain wahan se. Phir milenge. Jab hum dobara milenge.
