PrologueThe Girl Who Knew His Old Name

Hanako ne phone dekha.

Koi naya message nahi.

Woh conversation itni baar padh chuki thi ki use har punctuation mark yaad ho gaya tha.

Bench par ek saaya pada.

Hanako ne nazar uthaayi.

Hanako use pehchanti nahi thi.

Ladki ne can ko dekha.

Phir Hanako ko.

“Tumhe yeh karna band kar dena chahiye.”

Hanako ki ungliyan phone par kas gayin.

“Kyun?”

Aoi ka expression badal gaya.

“Hanako.”

Woh chalna band kar gayi.

“Tum chahte kya ho ki main kya kahoon?”

“Main jaanna chahta hoon ki tum kya soch rahi ho.”

Woh lagbhag hans hi padi.

Kitni seedhi si baat thi.

Woh bhi toh usse yahi chahti thi.

“Main soch rahi hoon ki tumse sawaal kaise poochhoon, bina yeh mehsoos kiye ki main tumse woh cheez maang rahi hoon jo tum dena nahi chahte.”

Kaito ka chehra narm pad gaya.

“Tum mujhse kuch bhi poochh sakti ho.”

“Kya tum jawab bhi doge?”

Usne munh khola.

Phir band kar liya.

Hanako ne nazar pher li.

“Mujhe yahi laga tha.”

Woh aage chal di.

Kaito uske peeche aaya, lekin dono ke beech ka faasla bana raha.
