PrologueThe Girl Who Knew His Old Name

Coffee thandi ho chuki thi.

Hanako Shimizu bench par apne paas rakhe can ko dekh rahi thi. Uski side par condensation ki ek patli lakeer dheere-dheere neeche sarak rahi thi.

Usne woh aadat mein khareedi thi.

Black coffee. Bina sugar.

Kaito Mizuno ki usual choice.

Vending machine se can uthane mein use teen seconds lage the. Aur yeh yaad karne mein ek second se bhi kam ki Kaito wahan nahi tha.

Use yeh baat nafrat dilati thi.

Use yeh bhi nafrat thi ki haath woh cheez yaad rakh sakta tha jise dil bhoolne ki koshish kar raha ho.

Station platform ke doosri taraf train ka announcement goonj utha. Log bags lekar jaldi-jaldi guzar rahe the, phones par baat kar rahe the, apni normal zindagi jee rahe the—aisi zindagi jo Hanako ko had se zyada simple lag rahi thi.

Hanako ne phone dekha.

Koi naya message nahi.

Screen par unki aakhri conversation abhi bhi khuli thi.

Hanako: Kal aa rahe ho?

Kaito: Mujhe nahi lagta mujhe aana chahiye.

Hanako: Tumhe aisa lagta hai, ya tum aana nahi chahte?

Kaito: Hanako—

Hanako: Chhodo.

Yeh chaar din pehle hua tha.

Woh conversation itni baar padh chuki thi ki use har punctuation mark yaad ho gaya tha.

Bench par ek saaya pada.

“Tum abhi bhi uske liye coffee khareedti ho?”

Hanako ne nazar uthaayi.

Uske saamne ek ladki khadi thi, haath mein muda hua umbrella tha.

Woh Hanako ki hi umar ki lagti thi. Uske dark baal dheele se peeche bandhe the, aur uske chehre par ek shaant expression tha jo is bheed-bhaad wale station mein ajeeb lag raha tha. Uski uniform anjaan thi. Bag se ek chhota silver charm latak raha tha.

Hanako use pehchanti nahi thi.

Ladki ne can ko dekha.

Phir Hanako ko.

“Tumhe yeh karna band kar dena chahiye.”

Hanako ki ungliyan phone par kas gayin.

“Excuse me?”

Ladki bina permission maange bench ke doosre sire par baith gayi.

“Woh ab yeh brand nahi peeta.”

Hanako bilkul chup ho gayi.

“Tumne kya kaha?”

Ladki uski taraf mudi.

Pehli baar uska composed expression hil gaya.

Woh darr nahi tha.

Kuch regret jaisa tha.

“Sorry,” usne dheere se kaha. “Mujhe yeh nahi kehna chahiye tha.”

Hanako khadi ho gayi.

“Tum kaun ho?”

Ladki ne apne haathon ki taraf dekha.

“Mera naam Aoi Kanzaki hai.”

“Tum Kaito ko kaise jaanti ho?”

Aoi ne turant jawab nahi diya.

Phir usne aisi baat kahi ki Hanako ko laga station ki saari awaazein ek pal ke liye band ho gayin.

“Main use tab se jaanti hoon jab usne khud ko Kaito kehna shuru bhi nahi kiya tha.”

Hanako ko thandi coffee ka can apni hatheli par mehsoos hua.

Jab usne khud ko Kaito kehna shuru bhi nahi kiya tha?

Woh use mahino se jaanti thi.

Use uska favourite drink, uski aadatein, thakne par dheemi ho jaane wali awaaz, aur woh halki si smile pata thi jo woh tab karta tha jab use lagta tha koi dekh nahi raha.

Hanako ko laga tha ki woh dheere-dheere uske woh pehlu jaan rahi hai jo usne sabse chhupa rakhe the.

Lekin shayad uski ek poori zindagi thi jo Hanako se pehle ki thi.

Aoi khadi hui aur bag ka strap theek kiya.

“Agar tum abhi bhi uski parwah karti ho,” usne kaha, “toh filhaal mujhse uske baare mein mat poochhna.”

Hanako use dekhti rahi.

“Kyun?”

Aoi ka expression badal gaya.

Ek pal ke liye woh kisi mysterious stranger se zyada aisi insaan lag rahi thi jo ek aise kamre ke bahar khadi ho jismein woh jaana chahti ho, lekin ja nahi sakti.

“Kyunki,” usne fusfusa kar kaha, “mujhe nahi pata woh tumhe kya batayega.”

Phir woh chali gayi.

Hanako bench ke paas hi khadi rahi.

Coffee thandi thi.

Phone khamosh tha.

Aur Kaito ke usse bharosa karne ko kehne ke baad pehli baar, Hanako ne socha ki kya usne kabhi use poora sach bataya bhi tha.

Part ITHE DAYS WE THOUGHT WOULD LAST

Chapter OneA Relationship Is Not a Magical Cure

Kaito ki girlfriend hona hairatangez taur par normal tha.

Hanako ko laga tha yeh zyada complicated hoga. Koi instruction manual nahi tha, koi orientation meeting nahi hui thi, aur apne boyfriend ke phir se umbrella bhool jaane ki report karne ke liye student council ka koi form bhi nahi tha.

Usne aisa form draft kiya tha. Submit nahi kiya.

Isliye nahi ki woh samajhti thi relationships aasaan hoti hain, balki isliye ki Kaito simple situations ko bhi confusing bana dene mein kamaal ka tha.

Misal ke taur par, public mein uska haath pakadne ki permission hai ya nahi, yeh decide karne mein usne gyarah minute laga diye.

Woh school se ghar ja rahe the jab aakhir Hanako ka patience khatam ho gaya.

“Tum phir se wahi kar rahe ho.”

Kaito ne uski taraf dekha.

“Kya?”

“Mere haath ko dekhna.”

“Main road dekh raha tha.”

“Road doosri taraf hai.”

Usne saamne dekha.

“Tum sahi keh rahi ho.”

“Is tarah mujhse agree mat karo!”

“Mujhe laga tum chahti ho main apni galti maanun.”

“Chahti hoon. Bas us tone mein nahi.”

“Kaunsi tone?”

“Wahi!”

Kaito muskuraya.

Hanako ne nazar hata li, isse pehle ki woh uski smile bhi dekh leta.

Phir usne khud hi haath badha kar Kaito ka haath pakad liya.

Uski ungliyan Hanako ki ungliyon ke gird band ho gayin.

Kuch der tak dono kuch nahi bole.

Yeh ek chhoti si baat thi.

Bilkul normal si baat.

Aur shayad isi wajah se yeh important thi.

Apni zindagi ke zyadaatar hisse mein Hanako maanti aayi thi ki affection ko khud ka announcement karna chahiye. Jo log care karte hain, unhe kehna chahiye. Jo kuch chahte hain, unhe maangna chahiye. Aur agar kisi ko problem ho, toh use explain karna chahiye, na ki sabse umeed rakhni chahiye ki woh koi puzzle solve karein.

Kaito ne use sikhaya tha ki log itne seedhe nahi hote.

Hanako ne use sikhaya tha ki khamoshi bhi dard de sakti hai, chahe uska iraada achha hi kyun na ho.

Dono abhi bhi seekh rahe the.

Lunch ke waqt Hiro ne unke jude hue haath dekhe aur apne drink par lagbhag ghut gaya.

“Ugh, yeh toh disgusting hai.”

Hanako ne turant Kaito ka haath chhod diya.

“Kya?”

“Tumhari happiness. Poori classroom se romance novel jaisi smell aa rahi hai.”

“Kya main tumhe bahar jaane mein help karun?”

“Dhamkiyan bhi? Main is relationship ko teen hafton ka time deta hoon.”

Kaito ne socha.

“Isse hum agle mahine ke beech tak pahunch jayenge.”

Hiro use ghoorta reh gaya.

“Tumne mere breakup prediction ko schedule ki problem samajh kar accept kar liya.”

“Main bas timeline clear kar raha tha.”

“Main tum dono se nafrat karta hoon.”

Hanako ne smirk kiya.

“Nahi, nahi karte.”

Hiro ne uski taraf ungli uthayi.

“Uski lines chori mat karo. Shuruaat aise hi hoti hai.”

Kaito hans pada.

Hanako ne use dekha.

Use uski hansi pasand thi.

Isliye nahi ki woh ab rare thi, balki isliye ki woh familiar hoti ja rahi thi.

Aur Hanako ko samajh aa raha tha ki familiarity se pyaar karna kitna risky hota hai.

Woh normal lamhon ko yaadon mein badalne ki taaqat de deti hai.

Us dopahar Kaito use station tak chhodne aaya.

Alag hone se pehle usne Hanako ki taraf dekha.

“Kya main tumse ek baat poochh sakta hoon?”

“Depends.”

“Agar mujhe Saturday cancel karna pade toh kya tum upset hogi?”

Hanako ka expression badal gaya.

“Kyun?”

“Mera medical appointment hai. Kaafi pehle schedule hua tha, lekin unhone time change kar diya.”

Woh purane jawab ka intezaar kar rahi thi.

Yeh kuch nahi hai.

Fikr mat karo.

Main theek ho jaunga.

Lekin is baar Kaito ne baat jaari rakhi.

“Mujhe nahi pata kitna time lagega. Uske baad shayad main thak jaaun.”

Hanako ke andar kuch naram pad gaya.

“Toh hum kisi aur din kuch kar lenge.”

“Tum gussa nahi ho?”

“Hona chahiye?”

“Mujhe nahi pata.”

“Toh sabse bura assume karne se pehle yeh seekho ki pata kaise karte hain.”

Woh halkasa muskuraya.

“Thik hai.”

Hanako ek pal hichkichaayi, phir uska haath daba diya.

“Mujhe batana kaisa gaya.”

“Bataunga.”

“Aur sirf isliye appointment skip mat karna ki tumhe lagta hai isse mujhe inconvenience hogi.”

“Nahi karunga.”

Woh use jaate hue dekhti rahi.

Pehli baar, use uski baat par bharosa hua.

Us shaam ek message aaya.

Kaito: Appointment phir shift ho gaya. Saturday shayad theek rahega.

Hanako: Tumhe zabardasti karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Kaito: Main jaana chahta hoon.

Hanako ne un teen words ko jitni der dekha, woh khud bhi maan na chahti thi.

Phir usne reply kiya.

Hanako: Thik hai. Lekin lunch tum khilaoge.

Kaito: Mujhe laga jagah tum choose karogi.

Hanako: Jagah main choose karungi. Payment tum karoge.

Kaito: Yeh legally questionable lag raha hai.

Hanako: Authorities se baat kar lo.

Kaito: Hiro ki authorities?

Hanako: Bilkul.

Usne phone rakh diya.

Kuch minute tak woh bas wahin baithi rahi, khush mehsoos karti hui.

Isliye nahi ki sab kuch perfect tha.

Balki isliye ki cheezein imperfect thi, aur dono milkar unhe sambhalna seekh rahe the.

Use zara bhi andaaza nahi tha ki woh ehsaas use kitna yaad aane wala tha.

Chapter TwoThe Girl at the Station

Aoi budhwaar ko saamne aayi.

Hanako ne use turant pehchaan liya.

Wahi dark hair. Wahi silver charm. Wahi shaant aankhein.

Woh school gate ke bahar ek teacher se baat kar rahi thi.

Teacher ke chale jaane ke baad Aoi ne building ki taraf dekha.

Dono ki nazrein mili.

Aoi ne halka sa sir hila kar greet kiya.

Hanako ne jawab nahi diya.

Pichhle teen din se woh khud ko samjhane ki koshish kar rahi thi ki station par hui mulaqat koi khaas baat nahi thi.

Ab woh ladki uske school ke bahar khadi thi.

Yeh jaan-boojhkar kiya hua lag raha tha.

Lunch ke waqt Hanako ne Kaito se uska zikr kiya.

“Kya tum Aoi naam ki kisi ladki ko jaante ho?”

Uski chopsticks munh tak pahunchne se pehle ruk gayin.

Bas ek second se bhi kam ke liye.

Lekin Hanako ne notice kar liya.

“Kyun?” usne poochha.

“Aaj subah woh school ke bahar thi.”

Kaito ne chopsticks neeche rakh di.

“Usne tumse kya kaha?”

Sawaal bahut jaldi aa gaya.

Hanako ka pet kas gaya.

“Toh tum use jaante ho.”

Usne Hanako ki taraf dekha.

“Haan.”

“Kahan se?”

Ek pal ki khamoshi.

“Yahan aane se pehle se.”

“Yeh jawab nahi hai.”

“Mujhe pata hai.”

Usne apna angutha lunch container ke kinaare par ragda.

“Mujhe pehle usse baat karni hogi.”

Hanako use dekhti rahi.

“Kyun?”

“Kyunki mujhe kuch samajhna hai.”

“Kis baare mein?”

“Hanako.”

Use nafrat thi jab Kaito uska naam us tarah leta tha, jaise woh chahta ho ki woh sawaal poochhna band kar de.

Isliye nahi ki uska lehja sakht hota tha.

Balki isliye ki woh narm hota tha.

Jaise woh pehle se hi kisi aisi baat ke liye maafi maang raha ho jise usne abhi samjhaya bhi nahi.

“Thik hai,” usne kaha.

Kaito ke chehre par rahat dikhayi di.

Isse baat aur bhi buri ho gayi.

Woh khadi hui, apna lunch uthaya aur chali gayi.

Kaito uske peeche nahi aaya.

Baaki dopahar woh khud ko samjhati rahi ki woh reasonable ban rahi hai.

Logon ko apne past ke dost rakhne ka haq hai. Log kuch baatein private rakh sakte hain.

Kaito ki zindagi ki har detail jaanna zaroori nahi tha.

Yeh baat usne khud Kaito se kahi thi.

Lekin privacy ek cheez thi.

Aoi ka naam sunte hi Kaito ke chehre par aaya expression kuch aur tha.

Us shaam Kaito ka message aaya.

Kaito: Lunch ke liye sorry.

Hanako usse dekhti rahi.

Hanako: Kis baat ke liye?

Kaito: Tumhe aisa mehsoos karane ke liye jaise main tumhe apne se door rakh raha hoon.

Usne teen alag replies type kiye aur teeno delete kar diye.

Aakhir mein usne bheja:

Hanako: Kya tum mujhe bataoge ki yeh sab kis baare mein hai?

Uska jawab aane mein kai minute lag gaye.

Kaito: Haan. I promise.

Hanako ne saans chhodi.

Phir ek aur message aaya.

Kaito: Bas abhi nahi.

Woh screen ko dekhti rahi.

Ek promise, jiske saath condition lagi thi.

Ek darwaaza jo baad mein khulega.

Ek sach jo uska tha, jab tak woh decide na kare ki Hanako use jaan sakti hai.

Usne phone ulta karke rakh diya.

Unke relationship mein pehli baar, Hanako ne socha ki kaash Kaito ne koi promise kiya hi na hota.
