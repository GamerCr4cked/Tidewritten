PrologueThe Girl Who Knew His Old Name

Coffee thandi ho chuki thi. Hanako Shimizu bench par apne paas rakhe can ko dekh rahi thi. Uski side par condensation ki ek patli lakeer dheere-dheere neeche sarak rahi thi. Usne woh aadat mein khareedi thi. Black coffee. Bina sugar. Kaito Mizuno ki usual choice. Vending machine se can uthane mein use teen seconds lage the. Aur yeh yaad karne mein ek second se bhi kam ki Kaito wahan nahi tha. Use yeh baat nafrat dilati thi. Use yeh bhi nafrat thi ki haath woh cheez yaad rakh sakta tha jise dil bhoolne ki koshish kar raha ho. Station platform ke doosri taraf train ka announcement goonj utha. Log bags lekar jaldi-jaldi guzar rahe the, phones par baat kar rahe the, apni normal zindagi jee rahe the—aisi zindagi jo Hanako ko had se zyada simple lag rahi thi. Hanako ne phone dekha. Koi naya message nahi. Screen par unki aakhri conversation abhi bhi khuli thi. **Hanako:** Kal aa rahe ho? **Kaito:** Mujhe nahi lagta mujhe aana chahiye. **Hanako:** Tumhe aisa lagta hai, ya tum aana nahi chahte? **Kaito:** Hanako— **Hanako:** Chhodo. Yeh chaar din pehle hua tha. Woh conversation itni baar padh chuki thi ki use har punctuation mark yaad ho gaya tha. Bench par ek saaya pada. “Tum abhi bhi uske liye coffee khareedti ho?” Hanako ne nazar uthaayi. Uske saamne ek ladki khadi thi, haath mein muda hua umbrella tha. Woh Hanako ki hi umar ki lagti thi. Uske dark baal dheele se peeche bandhe the, aur uske chehre par ek shaant expression tha jo is bheed-bhaad wale station mein ajeeb lag raha tha. Uski uniform anjaan thi. Bag se ek chhota silver charm latak raha tha. Hanako use pehchanti nahi thi. Ladki ne can ko dekha. Phir Hanako ko. “Tumhe yeh karna band kar dena chahiye.” Hanako ki ungliyan phone par kas gayin. “Excuse me?” Ladki bina permission maange bench ke doosre sire par baith gayi. “Woh ab yeh brand nahi peeta.” Hanako bilkul chup ho gayi. “Tumne kya kaha?” Ladki uski taraf mudi. Pehli baar uska composed expression hil gaya. Woh darr nahi tha. Kuch regret jaisa tha. “Sorry,” usne dheere se kaha. “Mujhe yeh nahi kehna chahiye tha.” Hanako khadi ho gayi. “Tum kaun ho?” Ladki ne apne haathon ki taraf dekha. “Mera naam Aoi Kanzaki hai.” “Tum Kaito ko kaise jaanti ho?” Aoi ne turant jawab nahi diya. Phir usne aisi baat kahi ki Hanako ko laga station ki saari awaazein ek pal ke liye band ho gayin. “Main use tab se jaanti hoon jab usne khud ko Kaito kehna shuru bhi nahi kiya tha.” Hanako ko thandi coffee ka can apni hatheli par mehsoos hua. *Jab usne khud ko Kaito kehna shuru bhi nahi kiya tha?* Woh use mahino se jaanti thi. Use uska favourite drink, uski aadatein, thakne par dheemi ho jaane wali awaaz, aur woh halki si smile pata thi jo woh tab karta tha jab use lagta tha koi dekh nahi raha. Hanako ko laga tha ki woh dheere-dheere uske woh pehlu jaan rahi hai jo usne sabse chhupa rakhe the. Lekin shayad uski ek poori zindagi thi jo Hanako se pehle ki thi. Aoi khadi hui aur bag ka strap theek kiya. “Agar tum abhi bhi uski parwah karti ho,” usne kaha, “toh filhaal mujhse uske baare mein mat poochhna.” Hanako use dekhti rahi. “Kyun?” Aoi ka expression badal gaya. Ek pal ke liye woh kisi mysterious stranger se zyada aisi insaan lag rahi thi jo ek aise kamre ke bahar khadi ho jismein woh jaana chahti ho, lekin ja nahi sakti. “Kyunki,” usne fusfusa kar kaha, “mujhe nahi pata woh tumhe kya batayega.” Phir woh chali gayi. Hanako bench ke paas hi khadi rahi. Coffee thandi thi. Phone khamosh tha. Aur Kaito ke usse bharosa karne ko kehne ke baad pehli baar, Hanako ne socha ki kya usne kabhi use poora sach bataya bhi tha. ---

Part ITHE DAYS WE THOUGHT WOULD LAST

Chapter OneA Relationship Is Not a Magical Cure

Kaito ki girlfriend hona hairatangez taur par normal tha. Hanako ko laga tha yeh zyada complicated hoga. Koi instruction manual nahi tha, koi orientation meeting nahi hui thi, aur apne boyfriend ke phir se umbrella bhool jaane ki report karne ke liye student council ka koi form bhi nahi tha. Usne aisa form draft kiya tha. Submit nahi kiya. Isliye nahi ki woh samajhti thi relationships aasaan hoti hain, balki isliye ki Kaito simple situations ko bhi confusing bana dene mein kamaal ka tha. Misal ke taur par, public mein uska haath pakadne ki permission hai ya nahi, yeh decide karne mein usne gyarah minute laga diye. Woh school se ghar ja rahe the jab aakhir Hanako ka patience khatam ho gaya. “Tum phir se wahi kar rahe ho.” Kaito ne uski taraf dekha. “Kya?” “Mere haath ko dekhna.” “Main road dekh raha tha.” “Road doosri taraf hai.” Usne saamne dekha. “Tum sahi keh rahi ho.” “Is tarah mujhse agree mat karo!” “Mujhe laga tum chahti ho main apni galti maanun.” “Chahti hoon. Bas us tone mein nahi.” “Kaunsi tone?” “Wahi!” Kaito muskuraya. Hanako ne nazar hata li, isse pehle ki woh uski smile bhi dekh leta. Phir usne khud hi haath badha kar Kaito ka haath pakad liya. Uski ungliyan Hanako ki ungliyon ke gird band ho gayin. Kuch der tak dono kuch nahi bole. Yeh ek chhoti si baat thi. Bilkul normal si baat. Aur shayad isi wajah se yeh important thi. Apni zindagi ke zyadaatar hisse mein Hanako maanti aayi thi ki affection ko khud ka announcement karna chahiye. Jo log care karte hain, unhe kehna chahiye. Jo kuch chahte hain, unhe maangna chahiye. Aur agar kisi ko problem ho, toh use explain karna chahiye, na ki sabse umeed rakhni chahiye ki woh koi puzzle solve karein. Kaito ne use sikhaya tha ki log itne seedhe nahi hote. Hanako ne use sikhaya tha ki khamoshi bhi dard de sakti hai, chahe uska iraada achha hi kyun na ho. Dono abhi bhi seekh rahe the. Lunch ke waqt Hiro ne unke jude hue haath dekhe aur apne drink par lagbhag ghut gaya. “Ugh, yeh toh disgusting hai.” Hanako ne turant Kaito ka haath chhod diya. “Kya?” “Tumhari happiness. Poori classroom se romance novel jaisi smell aa rahi hai.” “Kya main tumhe bahar jaane mein help karun?” “Dhamkiyan bhi? Main is relationship ko teen hafton ka time deta hoon.” Kaito ne socha. “Isse hum agle mahine ke beech tak pahunch jayenge.” Hiro use ghoorta reh gaya. “Tumne mere breakup prediction ko schedule ki problem samajh kar accept kar liya.” “Main bas timeline clear kar raha tha.” “Main tum dono se nafrat karta hoon.” Hanako ne smirk kiya. “Nahi, nahi karte.” Hiro ne uski taraf ungli uthayi. “Uski lines chori mat karo. Shuruaat aise hi hoti hai.” Kaito hans pada. Hanako ne use dekha. Use uski hansi pasand thi. Isliye nahi ki woh ab rare thi, balki isliye ki woh familiar hoti ja rahi thi. Aur Hanako ko samajh aa raha tha ki familiarity se pyaar karna kitna risky hota hai. Woh normal lamhon ko yaadon mein badalne ki taaqat de deti hai. Us dopahar Kaito use station tak chhodne aaya. Alag hone se pehle usne Hanako ki taraf dekha. “Kya main tumse ek baat poochh sakta hoon?” “Depends.” “Agar mujhe Saturday cancel karna pade toh kya tum upset hogi?” Hanako ka expression badal gaya. “Kyun?” “Mera medical appointment hai. Kaafi pehle schedule hua tha, lekin unhone time change kar diya.” Woh purane jawab ka intezaar kar rahi thi. *Yeh kuch nahi hai.* *Fikr mat karo.* *Main theek ho jaunga.* Lekin is baar Kaito ne baat jaari rakhi. “Mujhe nahi pata kitna time lagega. Uske baad shayad main thak jaaun.” Hanako ke andar kuch naram pad gaya. “Toh hum kisi aur din kuch kar lenge.” “Tum gussa nahi ho?” “Hona chahiye?” “Mujhe nahi pata.” “Toh sabse bura assume karne se pehle yeh seekho ki pata kaise karte hain.” Woh halkasa muskuraya. “Thik hai.” Hanako ek pal hichkichaayi, phir uska haath daba diya. “Mujhe batana kaisa gaya.” “Bataunga.” “Aur sirf isliye appointment skip mat karna ki tumhe lagta hai isse mujhe inconvenience hogi.” “Nahi karunga.” Woh use jaate hue dekhti rahi. Pehli baar, use uski baat par bharosa hua. Us shaam ek message aaya. **Kaito:** Appointment phir shift ho gaya. Saturday shayad theek rahega. **Hanako:** Tumhe zabardasti karne ki zaroorat nahi. **Kaito:** Main jaana chahta hoon. Hanako ne un teen words ko jitni der dekha, woh khud bhi maan na chahti thi. Phir usne reply kiya. **Hanako:** Thik hai. Lekin lunch tum khilaoge. **Kaito:** Mujhe laga jagah tum choose karogi. **Hanako:** Jagah main choose karungi. Payment tum karoge. **Kaito:** Yeh legally questionable lag raha hai. **Hanako:** Authorities se baat kar lo. **Kaito:** Hiro ki authorities? **Hanako:** Bilkul. Usne phone rakh diya. Kuch minute tak woh bas wahin baithi rahi, khush mehsoos karti hui. Isliye nahi ki sab kuch perfect tha. Balki isliye ki cheezein imperfect thi, aur dono milkar unhe sambhalna seekh rahe the. Use zara bhi andaaza nahi tha ki woh ehsaas use kitna yaad aane wala tha.
