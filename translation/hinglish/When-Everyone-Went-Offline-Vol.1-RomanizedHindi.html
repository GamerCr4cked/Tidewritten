Chapter One Chhota Server, Badi Duniya

GamerCr4cked ko broken plugin theek karna usse kahin aasaan lagta tha jitna apni feelings samjhana. Shayad isi wajah se internet use apna sa lagne laga tha. Voice call par woh ghanton Minecraft ke baare mein baat kar sakta tha—permissions, hosting, builds, agla update, ya koi naya idea jismein uske available time se zyada mehnat lagni thi. Lekin saamne baithkar ek normal si conversation mein bhi use sahi words dhoondhne padte the. Server chhota tha. Late nights, udhaar ke patience aur use chalane wale teen logon ke bharose tikka hua. Technical lafde GamerCr4cked sambhalta tha. Jab baaki sab overexcited ho jaate, KingOP sabko shaant rakhta. Alone kisi bhi chaotic idea ko plan mein badal sakta tha—aur phir us plan ka mazaak udaate hue use banane mein bhi help karta. Woh plugins par behas karte, maintenance announce karna bhool jaate, aur kabhi-kabhi actual problem solve karne se zyada waqt server icon choose karne mein laga dete. Lekin jab koi naya player join karta, toh woh koshish karte ki use lage ki yahan uska welcome hai. Koi bada network nahi tha. Bas unki apni jagah thi. Bunny ek chhote project ko set up karne ke baare mein sawaal poochhte hue aayi. GamerCr4cked ne practical explanation de diya, sochkar ki baat wahin khatam ho jayegi. Lekin aisa nahi hua. Bunny agle din bhi aayi. Phir group call join ki, KingOP aur Alone ko ek bilkul bekaar si baat par behas karte suna, aur dheere-dheere itni regular ho gayi ki uska naam call ka natural hissa lagne laga. Woh bina zabardasti kiye funny thi. GamerCr4cked achanak chup ho jaata, toh woh samajh leti thi; har baar wajah poochhkar use explain karne par majboor nahi karti. Kabhi bas kisi build ya video ke baare mein message bhej deti aur conversation ko apni speed se aage badhne deti. GamerCr4cked ne khud maanane se pehle KingOP ne change notice kar liya. “Phir se member list check kar raha tha na?” KingOP ne call mein poochha. “Dekh raha hoon kisi ko help toh nahi chahiye.” “Haan, bilkul.” Alone ne sochne wali awaaz nikali. “Server ki monitoring aajkal kaafi romantic ho gayi hai.” “Tum dono mute hone wale ho,” GamerCr4cked ne kaha. Dono hans pade. Bunny ne sab sun liya tha; usne bas ek laughing emoji bhej diya. Chhoti si baat thi. Phir bhi uski poori shaam achhi ho gayi. Use abhi apni feelings ka naam dene ki zaroorat nahi thi. Bas itna pata tha ki Bunny online aati, toh server thoda zyada zinda lagne lagta.

Chapter Two Aadhi Raat Ke Messages

Unki dosti aam si cheezon se badhi: adhoori builds ke screenshots, aise clips jinka context na ho toh kuch samajh na aaye, school ki complaints, family ki expectations, aur woh irritating feeling ki sabko lagta hai unhe apni poori zindagi ka plan pehle se pata hona chahiye. Kuch raatein Minecraft ki baaton mein guzarti. Kuch raato mein uska zikr bhi mushkil se hota. Bunny ne GamerCr4cked se us project ke baare mein poochha jise woh kaafi time se taal raha tha. Usne Bunny ki ek technical problem solve karne mein help ki. Dono music share karte, baar-baar dohraye jaane wale jokes par haste, aur ek-doosre ki aadatein samajhne lage: kaun turant reply karta hai, kaun busy din mein gayab ho jaata hai, aur kaun “I'm fine” bolta hai jab use asal mein thodi space chahiye hoti hai. GamerCr4cked Bunny ko woh baatein batane laga jo aam taur par apne tak rakhta tha. Bunny bina har problem ko lecture mein badle sunti thi. Badle mein, jab uska din mushkil hota, toh GamerCr4cked uske saath rehne ki koshish karta—even jab use samajh na aaye ki perfect jawab kya hona chahiye. Dosti dheere-dheere itni important lagne lagi ki woh sambhal kar chalne laga. Woh is par pressure nahi daalna chahta tha, yeh maan kar ki dono ke liye iska matlab ek jaisa hi hoga. Kuch waqt tak chinta karne ki koi wajah nahi thi. Phir Bunny ke replies kam hone lage. Shuru mein baat samajh aati thi. Usne kaha woh busy hai. Ek call miss hui, phir doosri. Jo messages pehle lambi conversations ban jaate the, ab ek-do sentences ke baad khatam ho jaate. GamerCr4cked ne poochha kuch hua hai kya, toh Bunny ne kaha ki bahut kuch chal raha hai. Usne jawab maan liya, lekin uncertainty wahin reh gayi. KingOP ne kaha ki kuch missed messages se poori kahani mat bana. Alone ne suggest kiya ki ek baar seedhe aur clearly poochh le, phir Bunny ko jawab dene ki jagah de. Dono ki advice sensible thi. Follow karna mushkil tab hota jab har shaant shaam mein uska mann check karne ko karta ki Bunny online aayi ya nahi. Usne ek funny clip bheji. Reply nahi aaya. Usne poochha baad mein server join karegi kya. Bunny ne “maybe” kaha, phir aayi hi nahi. Kuch din baad Bunny group call mein wapas aayi aur Alone ke ek aur ghatiya joke par hans padi. GamerCr4cked ko itni jaldi relief mila jaise kuch minute ki normal baat-cheet uske saare sawaalon ko khatam kar degi. Lekin aisa nahi hua. Call ke baad khamoshi phir laut aayi. Use pata tha ki green online dot koi promise nahi hota. Logon ki screen ke bahar bhi zindagi hoti hai, aur kisi par yeh farz nahi ki woh hamesha available rahe. Takleef sirf is baat ki nahi thi ki Bunny busy thi. Takleef yeh thi ki jis insaan ke saath use samjha hua mehsoos hota tha, ab uske saamne apni jagah hi samajh nahi aa rahi thi. Usne dard ko jhooth bolkar chhupaye bina Bunny ko space dene ki koshish ki.

Chapter Three “Tum Kaun Ho?”

Kai hafton baad Bunny wapas aayi, aur usne aisi explanation di jiski GamerCr4cked ne umeed nahi ki thi. Usne bataya ki woh ek serious medical problem se guzar rahi thi aur uski surgery hui thi. Uske kehne ke mutabik, uski memory par asar pada tha. Zindagi ke kuch hisse yaad the, kuch nahi. GamerCr4cked ki pehli reaction fikr thi. Usne poochha ki kya woh safe hai aur kya uske aas-paas aise log hain jo use support kar sakte hain. Woh kisi aise insaan se sawaalon ki baarish nahi karna chahta tha jo shayad pehle hi kisi dara dene wali situation se guzar raha ho. Bunny ne chhota sa reply diya. Phir usne ek sawaal poochha. “Sorry… tum kaun ho?” Ek pal ke liye GamerCr4cked ko laga woh mazaak kar rahi hai. Usne Minecraft server, aadhi raat ki calls, screenshots aur midnight ke baad tak chalne wali conversations yaad dilane ki koshish ki. Usne message ko narm rakha, jaise sahi words use sab kuch yaad dila sakte hon. Lekin uska jawab door-door sa hi raha. Usne kaha use GamerCr4cked yaad nahi. KingOP aur Alone ne bhi contact kiya. Unhone bataya ki woh kaun hain aur Bunny ko kaise jaante hain. Bunny ne kaha use woh dono bhi yaad nahi. GamerCr4cked uski baat par bharosa karna chahta tha. Lekin woh sochna bhi band nahi kar pa raha tha. Bunny online community ke kuch hisson ko pehchanti hui lagti thi, lekin un logon ko nahi jo uske sabse kareeb the. Use Bunny ki medical reality ka pata nahi tha, na hi woh jaan sakta tha ki use kya-kya yaad tha. Aur uske experience ka faisla karne ka use koi haq bhi nahi tha. Kai possibilities thi jinmein farq karna uske bas mein nahi tha. Shayad uski memory waqai badal gayi thi. Shayad woh overwhelmed thi aur distance chahti thi. Shayad sach in dono explanations se zyada complicated tha. Woh jaan nahi sakta tha; zyada sochne se uncertainty fact nahi ban jaati. Kai din tak usne messages likhe aur delete kiye. Woh poochhna chahta tha ki Bunny wapas aakar bhi use ajnabi ki tarah kyun treat kar rahi thi. Woh chahta tha ki Bunny samjhe uski khamoshi se use kitni takleef hui thi. Lekin woh yeh bhi jaanta tha ki explanation ki demand karne se explanation mil hi jaaye, zaroori nahi. Aakhir mein usne ek chhota sa message bheja. “Umeed hai tum safe ho aur tumhe support mil raha hai. Reply karna zaroori nahi. Bas batana chahta tha ki hamari dosti mere liye maayne rakhti thi.” Message unread hi raha. Usne doosra message nahi bheja.

Chapter Four Goonj Se Bhara Server

Server chalta raha, lekin GamerCr4cked ke liye ab woh pehle jaisa nahi raha. Koi familiar channel Bunny ke kisi joke ki yaad dila deta. Adhoori build us conversation ki yaad laati jise ab aage nahi badha sakta tha. Kabhi bina soche member list khol leta, phir samajh aate hi band kar deta ki woh kar kya raha hai. KingOP aur Alone uske saath rahe. Unhone use zabardasti “move on” karne ko nahi kaha, aur na hi Bunny ko villain banane ke liye uksaya. Woh games khelte, projects par baat karte, aur jab GamerCr4cked ke paas kehne ko kuch nahi hota, toh use chup rehne dete. Aakhirkaar GamerCr4cked ne maan liya ki ab woh server manage nahi karna chahta. Usne kisi aur ko zimmedari dene ka intezaam kiya, community ko badlav ke baare mein bataya, aur woh saara kaam hand over kar diya jise woh itne waqt se sambhal raha tha. KingOP khush nahi tha. Alone ne poochha ki kya woh sure hai. “Break ki zaroorat hai, is baat par sure hoon,” GamerCr4cked ne kaha. “Hamesha aisa feel hoga ya nahi, pata nahi. Lekin abhi har channel mein aisa lagta hai jaise main kisi cheez ka intezaar kar raha hoon.” Usne gusse mein server nahi chhoda. Woh jagah uske liye sach mein important thi. Dosti, hasi-mazaak aur saath milkar kiya gaya kaam—sab maayne rakhta tha. Lekin woh is umeed mein nahi jee sakta tha ki shayad Bunny laut aaye aur sab kuch pehle jaisa kar de. Uske baad ke mahine kisi dramatic scene jaise nahi the. Na koi achanak revelation hua, na perfect closure mila. Kabhi-kabhi use ab bhi Bunny yaad aati. Woh sochta ki kya use woh log yaad the, kya woh theek thi, aur jo explanation usne di thi kya wahi poori kahani thi. Usne seekha ki kisi ki care karne ka matlab uski khamoshi ka solution dhoondhna nahi hota. Woh Bunny ke liye achha chah sakta tha, bina usse jawab ke liye chase kiye. Woh maan sakta tha ki use dard hua, bina yeh tay kiye ki Bunny ki niyat kya thi. Sabse zaroori baat, woh apni baaki dosti ke darwaze khule rakh sakta tha.

Chapter Five Woh Log Jo Saath Rahe

Ek shaam, server ka control kisi aur ke paas jaane ke kaafi waqt baad, GamerCr4cked ko purane screenshots ka ek folder mila. Usmein blocky builds thi, chat ke ridiculous messages, teenon owners ki ek tasveer jo aadhe bane spawn ke paas khade the, aur support channel mein Bunny ka pehla sawaal. Bas ek normal sa hello. Use zara bhi andaaza nahi tha ki usse kitni cheezein shuru ho jayengi. Woh folder scroll karta raha, har memory ko kisi clue mein badalne ki koshish kiye bina. Dosti maayne rakhti thi. Woh hansi-mazaak sach mein hua tha. Uska khatam hona us waqt ko mita nahi sakta tha jab usne uske din behtar bana diye the. Usne KingOP aur Alone ko message bheja. “Koi call ke liye free hai?” Alone sabse pehle join hua aur aate hi game mein hui kisi gadbad ki complaint shuru kar di. Ek minute baad KingOP aaya aur aise plugin par behas karne laga jise ab unmein se koi use bhi nahi karta tha. GamerCr4cked khud ko rokne se pehle hi hans pada. Call ki woh pehchaani hui awaaz kamre mein phail gayi. Woh ab bhi bheed-bhaad wali jagah se zyada screen ke peeche comfortable tha. Ab bhi kabhi-kabhi apni feelings samjhana mushkil lagta tha. Lekin woh seekh raha tha ki ek confusing ending ka matlab yeh nahi ki aage har naya connection unsafe lage. Shayad use kabhi theek-theek pata nahi chalega ki Bunny ke saath kya hua tha. Woh uski batayi hui baaton ki had ka respect kar sakta tha, maan sakta tha ki baaki sach ko verify nahi kar sakta, aur khud se aisa sawaal solve karne ki zid chhod sakta tha jo kisi aur ki zindagi ka hissa tha. Woh chahta tha Bunny theek ho. Umeed karta tha ki unki dosti ke achhe pal uske liye bhi maayne rakhte honge. Woh in umeedon ko apne saath rakh sakta tha, bina apni zindagi ko pause kiye. Purana server ab uske control mein nahi tha, lekin duniya uske saath khatam nahi hui thi. Ab bhi projects banane the, games khelne the, aur aise log the jo uske call karne par jawab dete the. Kabhi log offline jaate hain aur agli subah wapas aa jaate hain. Kabhi woh familiar username ke saath lautte hain, lekin unke aur tumhare beech aisi doori hoti hai jise koi message paar nahi kar sakta. Kabhi dosti bina kisi clear goodbye ke khatam ho jaati hai. GamerCr4cked yeh nahi chun sakta tha ki use kis tarah ka ending mila. Lekin agla kadam kya hoga, yeh woh chun sakta tha. Usne screenshots ka folder band kiya, call join ki, aur KingOP aur Alone ko bilkul bekaar baaton par behas karte suna. Kaafi arse baad pehli baar, woh kisi notification ka intezaar nahi kar raha tha. Woh pehle se hi wahin tha jahan rehna chahta tha.
