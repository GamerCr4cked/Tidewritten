Chapter One The Last Year at Seiran

Seiran mein mera final year Hina ke ek message ke saath shuru hua.

Tumhara high school ka third year. Mera junior high ka second year. Itne important mat ban jaana ki mere saath lunch bhi na kar sako.

Corridor mein message padhkar main apne phone ko dekhte hue idiot ki tarah smile karne laga.

Ek saal pehle main woh ladka tha jo school gate ko dekhta rehta aur un conversations ki practice karta jo kabhi hui hi nahi. Ab Hina meri habits, mere terrible jokes, aur woh exact time jaanti thi jab main weather check karna bhool jaata tha. Schedules allow karte toh hum cafeteria mein milte, aur aksar raat ko uske eleven o'clock limit ke paas tak baat karte.

Lekin ab graduation koi door ka idea nahi tha. Entrance exams, university applications aur shayad kisi doosre city mein shift hona—ye sab school year ke end par mera wait kar rahe the.

Maine koshish ki ki in baaton ke baare mein na sochun.

Lunch par Hina ek new navy-blue hairband pehen kar aayi. Maine turant notice kiya, par kuch nahi bola.

Paanch minute baad usne aankhein sikod kar poochha, “Tumne notice kiya tha, right?”

“Kya notice kiya?”

“Mera hairband.”

“Main bahut si cheezein notice karta hoon.”

“Apparently important cheezein nahi.”

Main hans pada. Woh annoyed dikhne ki koshish kar rahi thi, lekin uske lips ka corner uska secret bata raha tha.

“Achha lag raha hai,” maine aakhir kaha.

“Thank you.” Uski awaaz soft ho gayi. “Main soch rahi thi tum notice karoge ya nahi.”

Words simple the, lekin main unhe secret ki tarah class tak saath le gaya.

Us afternoon Daichi ne mujhe notes ko ghoorte hue pakda, jabki maine ek page bhi turn nahi kiya tha.

“Phir usi ke baare mein soch raha hai.”

“Main exams ke baare mein soch raha hoon.”

“Toh margin mein uska naam kyun likha hai?”

Maine notebook band kar di.

Woh hansaa, phir serious ho gaya. “Tujhe hamesha guess karte rehne ki zaroorat nahi hai, you know?”

Mujhe pata tha. Lekin jaanna aur kar paana do alag skills hain.

Do saalon mein Hina aur maine ek doosre ke saath comfortable rehna seekha tha. Hum worries share karte, ek doosre ki chhoti preferences yaad rakhte, aur silence ko bina excuses ke quiet rehne dete. Main use rush nahi karna chahta tha. Main apni friendship ko aisa question nahi banana chahta tha jiska answer use ready hone se pehle dena pade.

Lekin main honest hue bina graduate bhi nahi hona chahta tha.

Chapter Two The Rooftop

Daichi ne mujhe high-school building ke peeche wale rooftop ke baare mein bataya. Technically wahan jaana allowed nahi tha, though old access door aksar unlocked rehta tha. Hina ko invite karne se pehle maine rules check kiye; main nahi chahta tha ki hamari favourite jagah trouble ka reason bane. Hum sirf tab gaye jab allowed tha, aur edge se door rahe.

Wahan se Seiran chhota lagta tha. Courtyard, cafeteria, gate aur station ke paas wali narrow road—sab ek map ke parts jaise dikhte the, jin par hum hazaar baar chal chuke the.

Hina railing ke paas khadi late-afternoon light ko campus par settle hote dekh rahi thi.

“Yeh achhi jagah hai,” usne kaha.

“Mujhe laga tumhe pasand aayegi.”

“Tum mujhe view dikhane laaye ho?”

“Mostly.”

Woh meri taraf mudi. “Mostly?”

Maine apne haathon ki taraf dekha. “Main kisi quiet jagah par baat karna chahta tha.”

“Toh baat karo.”

Yeh instruction unfairly direct tha.

Main rooftop ke safe side mein wall se peeth laga kar baith gaya. Thodi der baad Hina bhi mere paas baith gayi, hamare beech thodi si space thi.

Humne graduation, uske upcoming exams, aur mere roz school mein na hone par campus kitna different lagega—inn sab ke baare mein baat ki. Usne maana ki woh bhi is baare mein soch chuki thi.

“Main nahi chahti sab kuch ek saath change ho,” usne kaha.

“Main bhi nahi.”

Sunset ke saath maine apna haath hamare beech ground par rakha, uske haath ko touch kiye bina.

Hina ne us gap ko dekha, phir apni ek finger mere haath ke side par lightly rakh di.

Gesture itna chhota tha ki koi aur miss kar sakta tha. Maine nahi kiya.

Humne kuch nahi kaha. Sky amber se pink, phir pale evening blue hota raha.

Stairs se neeche jaate waqt woh itni paas chal rahi thi ki hamari sleeves brush ho rahi thi.

Door par usne meri taraf dekha. “Tum mujhe phir yahan invite kar sakte ho.”

“Really?”

“Aise questions poochte rahe toh main apna mind change kar sakti hoon.”

Main hans pada, aur woh bhi.

Us evening mujhe samajh aaya ki future ab bhi mujhe daraata tha—but isliye nahi ki woh empty tha. Balki isliye ki kuch log aur jagahen thi jinhe main apne saath le jaana chahta tha, aur mujhe abhi nahi pata tha kaise.

Chapter Three Things We Had Not Said

Rooftop ke baad hamari friendship overnight change nahi hui. Bas thodi aur honest ho gayi.

Hum ab bhi music aur cafeteria food par argue karte. Hina mujhe strange kehti jab main overthink karta, aur main use yaad dilata ki usne hi ek bird draw karke uska naam apne naam par rakha tha. Lekin kabhi-kabhi hamari nazrein milti aur hum turant nazar nahi hataate.

Ek evening hum phone par everything and nothing ke baare mein baat kar rahe the. Teacher ne too much homework de diya tha; Yui ne new hobby shuru ki thi; aur Daichi ne itna bad joke sunaya tha ki woh funny ban gaya.

Aakhir Hina ne poochha, “Tumhe yaad hai hum pehli baar kab baat kiye the?”

“Gate ke paas.”

“Tum terrified lag rahe the.”

“Main kuch intelligent sochne ki koshish kar raha tha.”

“Kaam kiya?”

“Clearly nahi.”

Woh hans padi, phir uski awaaz quiet ho gayi.

“Main bhi nervous thi, you know.”

Main seedha baith gaya. “Tum?”

“Of course. Tum aur tumhare friends hamesha idhar dekhte rehte the. Mujhe samajh nahi aata tha tum mujh par hans rahe ho ya nahi.”

“Main kabhi tum par nahi hansa.”

“Ab mujhe pata hai.”

Maine un moments ke liye sorry kaha jab mere friends bahut loud ho jaate the. Usne kaha ki woh kabhi nahi chahti thi main unse friendship tod doon—bas itna chahti thi ki main yaad rakhun, woh nahi chahti thi ki sab uske baare mein joke karein.

“Mujhe pehle samajh jaana chahiye tha,” maine kaha.

“Tumne samjha tha. Tumne apna behaviour change kiya.”

Yeh baat mere liye praise se zyada matter karti thi.

October mein school festival wapas aaya. Hina ne poetry reading mein participate kiya. Hall lights ke neeche woh paper dono haathon mein pakde khadi thi. Uski awaaz steady thi, aur reading ke dauran usne sirf ek baar nazar uthaayi. Audience ke across uski aankhein meri aankhon se mili.

Baad mein main corridor mein wait karta raha.

“Woh poem kisi ke baare mein thi?” maine poochha.

“Maybe.”

“Kisi aise insaan ke baare mein jise main jaanta hoon?”

“Kisi aise ke baare mein jo pehle bahut quiet tha.”

“Yeh toh koi bhi ho sakta hai.”

“Toh phir guess karte raho.”

Woh mere reply se pehle chali gayi.

Main uske peeche nahi gaya. Main seekh raha tha ki har question ka answer turant zaroori nahi. Kabhi koi tumhe thoda sa truth deta hai, aur kindest response hota hai use carefully sambhal kar rakhna.

Winter paas aaya toh hum permission hone par rooftop par zyada milne lage. University, seekhne wali cheezon aur dekhne ki jagahon ke baare mein baat karte. Thand badhti toh Hina apna scarf mere saath share karti; main vending machine se hot drinks laata.

Ek hi scarf ke neeche hamare shoulders touch hote.

Ab main pretend nahi kar sakta tha ki meri feelings sirf temporary fascination thi. Woh dheere-dheere ek real friendship ke saath grow hui thi. Main Hina ko ek person ki tarah jaanta tha—not as a mystery, not as an imaginary story, balki aise insaan ki tarah jo mujhse disagree kar sakti thi, mujhe surprise kar sakti thi, aur apna path khud choose kar sakti thi.

Isi liye main use truth batana chahta tha.

Chapter Four A Notebook for Two

Winter break se pehle aakhri school day cold wind aur station ke paas roasted sweet potatoes ki smell ke saath aaya.

Lunch par Hina ne mujhe ek chhota wrapped package diya.

“Yahan mat kholna,” usne kaha.

“Main kholne wala nahi tha.”

“Tum bilkul kholne wale the.”

Main ghar pahunchne tak wait karta raha.

Andar handmade cover wali ek notebook thi, jis par branch par baithe tiny bird ka design tha. First page par Hina ki careful handwriting mein likha tha:

Tum pehle insaan the jisne is school ko thoda less ordinary bana diya.

Baaki pages blank the. Back cover ke andar ek chhota note tha.

Stories, thoughts, kuch bhi likhna. Maine first line likhi kyunki kisi ko toh start karna tha.

Maine use kai baar padha.

New Year's Eve par hum phone par baat kar rahe the, jab hamari families midnight ki preparations kar rahi thi. 11:55 par Hina ne poochha mera resolution kya hai.

“Exams pass karna.”

“Boring.”

“Umbrella bhoolna band karna.”

“Impossible.”

“Tumse baat karte rehna.”

Ek pause aaya.

“Yeh wala achha hai,” usne kaha.

Minutes beet gaye. Bahar door fireworks ki awaaz aane lagi. Humne saath countdown kiya, dono jaldi-jaldi bol rahe the aur count bhoolne par hans rahe the.

Midnight par Hina ne kaha, “Happy New Year, Haruto.”

“Happy New Year.”

“Aur… main chahti hoon tum ek baat jaano.”

Main wait karta raha.

“Tum mere liye matter karte ho. Jitna main usually keh paati hoon, usse zyada.”

Mera gala tight ho gaya. “Tum bhi mere liye matter karti ho.”

Humne jaldi se kuch aur kehne ki koshish nahi ki. Silence full tha, empty nahi.

January mein rooftop par woh mere paas baithi thi jab main uski di hui notebook mein likh raha tha. Woh apni book mein kuch sketch kar rahi thi.

“Ek minute move mat karna,” usne kaha.

“Kyun?”

“Kyunki main tumhe draw kar rahi hoon.”

Main itna freeze ho gaya ki woh hans padi.

Woh focus ke saath draw karti rahi, kabhi mere face ko, kabhi page ko dekhti. Jab usne sketch dikhaya, kuch careful lines mein main khud tha—mere messy hair, aankhon ka shape, aur woh chhoti smile jiska mujhe khud pata nahi tha.

“Tumne mujhe mujhse zyada kind bana diya,” maine kaha.

“Tum apne khayal se zyada kind ho.”

Mujhe reply samajh nahi aaya, toh maine notebook mein likha:

Kabhi-kabhi koi doosra tumhari achhai pehle dekh leta hai, usse pehle ki tum khud dekh pao.

Hina ne mere shoulder ke upar se padha.

“Achhi line hai,” usne kaha.

“Koshish karunga ki isse zyada proud na ho jaun.”

“Too late.”

Hum hans pade. Bahar winter sky pale aur wide tha. Notebook hamare beech rakhi thi, aur uske first pages ab blank nahi rahe the.

Chapter Five The Truth on the Page

February exam revision, cold mornings aur Valentine's Day decorations ke saath aaya jo raaton-raat appear ho gayi thi.

Maine koi grand gift nahi khareeda. Maine likha.

Hina ki notebook ke next blank page par maine woh chhoti cheezein likhin jinhe main treasure karne laga tha: uska details yaad rakhna, uska rainy lunch break ko warm bana dena, aur joke karna easy hone par bhi sach bolna.

Phir maine woh likha jise kehne se main darta tha.

Hina, I like you. Isliye nahi ki tumhe jaane bina maine tumhe nickname diya tha, aur na hi isliye ki main chahta tha har glance ka koi meaning ho. Main us person ko pasand karta hoon jise main ab jaan chuka hoon. Main nahi chahta tum sirf isliye answer do kyunki tumhe lagta hai tum mujh par owe karti ho. Main bas honest hona chahta tha.

Class ke baad maine notebook use wapas di.

Usne rooftop par page padha. Main wait karta raha aur possible endings ko baar-baar imagine karne se bachne ki koshish karta raha.

Jab woh finish hui, usne notebook apne coat se laga li.

“Tumne sach mein bahut time laga diya,” usne kaha.

“I know.”

“Main soch rahi thi tum kab bataoge.”

Maine uski taraf dekha. “Tum soch rahi thi?”

Usne nod kiya, ab thodi shy lag rahi thi. “Mujhe bhi tum pasand ho, Haruto. Main bas chahti thi hum samjhein ki hum kya keh rahe hain. Sirf isliye rush nahi karna chahti thi kyunki baaki sab har cheez ko label dene lagte hain.”

Mere andar jo relief aaya, woh itna quiet tha ki maine use almost calm samajh liya.

“Toh… ab kya hoga?” maine poochha.

“Hum apne jaise hi rahenge.” Woh smile ki. “Aur shayad ab hum pretend karna band kar dein ki ek earphone share karna accident hai.”

Main hans pada. Woh bhi hansi, aur hamare beech ki tension soft ho gayi.

Koi audience nahi thi, koi announcement nahi, koi dramatic music nahi. Bas winter coats pehne do students, beech mein ek notebook, aur ek truth jise sahi words milne mein saal lag gaye.

Hum railing ke paas khade the, haath ek doosre ke close. Is baar maine uska haath pakadne se pehle poochha.

“May I?”

Hina ne nod kiya.

Uska haath mere haath mein warm tha.

Chapter Six Growing Up

March exam schedules aur is uneasy feeling ke saath aaya ki har ordinary day ab countdown ban gaya tha.

Hina ke apne tests the. Mere university entrance exams the. Hum lunch par utni baar nahi mil paate, lekin messages chalte rahe: exam se pehle good-luck note, baad mein complaint, vending machine ke ridiculous drink ki photo, aur midnight se pehle so jaane ka reminder.

Physics exam ke baad main convinced tha ki maine apni zindagi mein jo kuch seekha tha sab bhool gaya hoon. School building ke peeche ek bench par Hina mera wait kar rahi thi.

Usne nahi poochha exam kaisa gaya. Woh mere paas baith gayi aur silence ko settle hone diya.

Kuch der baad usne apna haath aage kiya.

Maine pakad liya.

“Bad?” usne poochha.

“Very.”

“Toh aaj ka din bad tha. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aage ka har din aisa hoga.”

Maine uski taraf dekha. “Tum ise simple bana deti ho.”

“Simple nahi hai. Main bas yahan hoon.”

Hum kuch der wahan baithe rahe, school wall ke us paar traffic sunte hue. Uska haath mere haath mein raha—not as a promise ki kuch change nahi hoga, balki proof ki har difficult cheez ka saamna mujhe akela nahi karna.

Exams khatam hone par woh question aakhir saamne aaya jise hum avoid kar rahe the: main university ke liye kahan jaunga?

Mujhe same city mein engineering programme mein admission mil gaya, train se lagbhag forty minutes door. Jab maine pehli baar worry karna shuru kiya tha, future aisa imagine nahi kiya tha, lekin ab main is future ko imagine kar sakta tha.

Hina ne meri baat suni.

“Toh tum ab bhi nearby rahoge,” usne kaha.

“Visit karne jitna nearby.”

“Good.” Woh smile ki. “Main tumhe doosre city mein jaakar bhi umbrella bhoolne nahi doongi.”

Hum hans pade, lekin uski awaaz mein relief sun sakta tha. Mujhe bhi wahi feel hua.

Bade hone ka matlab yeh promise nahi ki kuch change nahi hoga. Matlab hai fear ko decisions lene dene se pehle change ke baare mein baat karna seekhna. Hina ki apni studies, friends, family aur plans the. Mere bhi apne the. Hum hamesha ek jagah, ek waqt par nahi honge.

Lekin humne important baatein kehna seekh liya tha.

Woh ek beginning thi, guarantee nahi. Aur woh kaafi tha.

Chapter Seven Where Our Story Begins

Seiran ka final day ek clear evening sky ke neeche aaya.

Mere friends courtyard mein photos le rahe the. Teachers last-minute advice de rahe the. Jo students saalon se school ki complaints karte aaye the, woh ab classrooms ke paas aise khade the jaise har wall ko yaad kar lena chahte hon.

Jaane se pehle main ek jagah jaana chahta tha.

Rooftop.

Campus khaali hone laga toh Hina mujhse wahan mili. Late sunlight railing ko gold bana rahi thi. Neeche courtyard tha jahan maine use pehli baar notice kiya tha, cafeteria jahan usne mere excuses ko challenge kiya tha, aur gate jahan usne poochha tha ki woh paas aati hai toh main quiet kyun ho jaata hoon.

Teen saalon ke ordinary moments us view mein sama gaye jaise.

“Mujhe laga tum yahin hoge,” usne kaha.

“Mujhe hope thi tum aaogi.”

Woh mere paas khadi hui. Hamare haath railing par the, close lekin touch nahi kar rahe the.

“Darr lag raha hai?” usne poochha.

“Thoda.”

“Mujhe bhi.”

Main uski taraf muda. “Mujhe exactly nahi pata next year kaisa hoga. Main promise nahi kar sakta ki hamare difficult days kabhi nahi aayenge.”

“Agar tum aisa promise karte toh main believe nahi karti.”

“Lekin main is relationship ko choose karte rehna chahta hoon. Busy hone par bhi baat karna. Worry hone par apne dimaag mein chup ho jaane ke bajay tumhe batana.”

Hina ne kaafi der tak mujhe dekha. Phir usne mera haath pakad liya.

“Main bhi yahi chahti hoon.”

Sun rooftops ke peeche neeche ja raha tha. Hum chupchaap khade rahe jab sky gold se orange, phir soft pink ho gaya. Maine us ladke ke baare mein socha jo main pehle tha—jo door se dekhta tha aur maanta tha ki feeling ko kabhi bolna na pade toh woh safer ho jaati hai.

Main ab bhi nervous tha. Mujhe ab bhi har answer nahi pata tha.

Lekin next step lene se pehle har answer jaanna ab zaroori nahi tha.

Hina ne apna forehead gently mere forehead se laga diya. Humne ek pal ke liye aankhein band kar li, din ki last light fade hote hue us quiet moment ko share kiya.

Jab woh peeche hati, woh smile kar rahi thi.

“Iske baare mein likhte waqt zyada dramatic mat bana dena,” usne kaha.

“Koi promise nahi.”

“Haruto.”

“Fine. Try karunga.”

Hum saath rooftop se nikle aur gate ki taraf chale. Usi jagah, jahan hamari story ek nervous question se shuru hui thi, hum alag-alag directions mein jaane se pehle ruk gaye.

Hina apni street ki taraf mudi. Main station ki taraf.

Kuch steps ke baad hum dono ne ek hi waqt par peeche dekha.

Woh hans padi. Main bhi.

Phir usne wave ki, aur maine bhi.

Maine use kabhi Little Bird kaha tha kyunki main use door se dekhta tha aur apni feeling ko samajhne ka doosra tareeka nahi jaanta tha. Ab main uska naam, uski laugh, uski stubbornness, uski quiet kindness, aur Seiran ki walls ke bahar le jaane wale dreams jaanta tha.

Woh meri imagination ki kabhi thi hi nahi. Woh hamesha apni khud ki thi.

Aur somehow, teen saalon ki chhoti conversations, awkward silences, rainy afternoons, shared songs aur aakhirkaar bole gaye words ke baad, usne mere saath chalna choose kiya tha.

School year khatam ho gaya. Hamari lives badalti rahengi, aur hum mein se koi promise nahi kar sakta tha ki aage ka har din easy hoga.

Lekin humne honestly bolna, ek doosre ko poori tarah apna rehne dena, aur time ko freeze karne ka pretend kiye bina ek doosre ko choose karna seekh liya tha.

Hamari story un ordinary days mein continue hogi jinhe humne abhi jiya bhi nahi. Yeh us chapter ka end tha jise shuru karne se main kabhi darta tha—and Seiran ke un saalon ka complete ending bhi, jinhone mujhe kisi ko idea ki tarah nahi, balki ek real person ki tarah pyaar karna sikhaya.
