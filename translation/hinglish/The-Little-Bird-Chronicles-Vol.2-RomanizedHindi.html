Chapter One The Girl at the Gate Again

Ek saal beet gaya.

Maine junior high khatam ki, entrance exams kisi tarah nikaale, aur Seiran Academy ke high-school division mein aa gaya. Classrooms doosri building mein the, assignments mushkil ho gaye the, aur har koi future ke baare mein aise baat karta tha jaise woh ek train ho jise humein nikalne se pehle pakadna hi pade.

Main formulas yaad karne aur chemistry samajhne ka dikhawa karne mein busy tha. Phir bhi, jab bhi station ke paas wali tang sadak se guzarta, mujhe woh ladki yaad aati jo kabhi ek kandhe par schoolbag latkaye mujhse aage chala karti thi.

Hina apne father ke transfer ki wajah se door chali gayi thi. Maine uske liye achha hi chaha tha. Sach mein.

Lekin kabhi-kabhi school gate ki taraf dekhne se main khud ko rok nahi paata tha.

Phir, August ki ek umas bhari subah, maine use dekha.

Summer break ke baad lautne wale students se neeche ka courtyard bhara hua tha. Entrance ke paas umbrellas se paani tapak raha tha, pedon mein cicadas shor macha rahe the, aur ek teacher sabko homeroom ki taraf bhejne ki koshish kar raha tha. Do chhote students ke beech ek jaani-pehchaani si ladki apne navy-blue bag ka strap theek kar rahi thi.

Hina.

Uske baal pehle se thode chhote the. Woh kuch lambi bhi lag rahi thi. Ab woh junior high ke second year mein thi, aur main usi campus ke doosre hisse mein high school ka first-year student ban gaya tha.

Mera phone lagbhag haath se chhoot gaya.

Woh wapas aa gayi.

Teen din tak maine use upper corridor se dekha, lekin baat karne ke liye kuch nahi kiya. Maine khud se kaha ki ab school ki buildings alag hain. Maine kaha ki main ab bada ho gaya hoon aur mujhe mature behave karna chahiye. Courtyard ko zaroorat se zyada baar dekhte hue maine khud ko kai bharosemand bahaane diye.

Chauthe din cafeteria ne mere saare bahaane khatam kar diye.

Main line mein khada phone scroll kar raha tha, tab ek jaani-pehchaani awaaz aayi, “Agar tum line par dhyaan doge, toh woh jaldi aage badhegi.”

Maine nazar uthayi.

Hina lunch box liye mere paas khadi thi. Uske chehre par wahi shaant shararat thi jo mujhe yaad thi.

“Tum…” maine kamaal ka jawab diya.

“Main,” usne kaha. “Hairaan ho?”

“Tum kab wapas aayi?”

“Pichhle hafte. Mujhe laga tum jaldi notice kar loge.”

“Maine pehle hi din notice kar liya tha.”

“Aur hello bolne mein chaar din laga diye?”

“Buildings alag hain.”

“Cafeteria toh nahi.”

Mere paas koi jawab nahi tha.

Usne bataya ki uske father ka transfer cancel ho gaya tha. Uska parivaar wapas isi area mein aa gaya tha aur usne Seiran mein dobara admission le liya. Usne yeh baat itni aasani se kahi, jaise mausam bata rahi ho.

“Tum wapas aayi, mujhe achha laga,” maine zyada sochne se pehle hi keh diya.

Usne ek pal mujhe dekha. “Apne liye ya mere liye?”

“Umeed hai, dono ke liye.”

Is baar woh sach mein hans padi.

Hum cafeteria ki khidkiyon ke paas baithkar khana khane lage. Usne apne lunch se tamagoyaki ka ek tukda mujhe diya, aur maine ek chhote se egg piece ke laayak se kahin zyada shukriya ke saath use le liya. Hum teachers, summer homework aur us jagah lautne ke ajeeb ehsaas ke baare mein baat karte rahe jo tumhare bina bhi chalti rahi ho.

Kuch dramatic nahi hua.

Lekin jab hum cafeteria se nikle, toh ek saal baad campus phir se apna sa lagne laga.

Chapter Two A Routine We Never Planned

Hamari nayi routine bina kisi meeting ya vaade ke ban gayi.

Lunch ke waqt main cafeteria jaata, Hina bhi wahin hoti. Kabhi line mein mulaqat ho jaati, kabhi woh apne friends ke saath table se mujhe bula leti. Main late hota, toh woh zaroor kehti ki meri timing bekaar hai. Aur agar woh late hoti, toh bolti ki use “junior-high ke important kaam” ne rok liya.

Uski friend Yui sab kuch notice karti thi.

“Toh yeh tumhare senior hain?” ek dopahar usne mujhe aate dekhkar poochha.

“Haruto,” Hina ne sudhaara. “Aur haan, senior hain.”

“Bas senior?”

“Thode ajeeb senior.”

Yui ne mujhe dekha. “Tum special lagte ho.”

“Yakeen maano, main mostly ordinary hoon.”

“Ajeeb log bilkul aisi hi baat kehte hain,” Hina ne kaha.

Mujhe lagne laga tha ki Seiran lautne ke baad woh aur bhi khatarnak ho gayi hai.

Ek baarish wale Thursday ko cafeteria bheed aur shor se bhar gaya. Main dono buildings ke beech bane covered walkway mein chala gaya, earphones lagaye aur chhat ke kinaare se girte paani ko dekhta raha.

Kisi ne mere kandhe par tap kiya.

Hina apna band umbrella pakde khadi thi.

“Kya sun rahe ho?”

“Aisa kuch jo logon ko ignore karne mein madad kare.”

“Toh kaafi achha hoga.”

Woh mujhse thodi door, sookhe ledge par baith gayi. Hum music, uski nayi class aur mere science stream chunne ke baare mein baat karne lage—jabki main likhne mein zyada achha tha, bolkar apni baat samjhaane mein nahi.

“Tum achha likhte ho,” usne kaha.

Main uski taraf muda. “Tumhe kaise pata?”

“Tumhari notebook se. Ek baar lunch ke waqt tum usmein likh rahe the.”

“Tumne padh li?”

“Bas woh line jo khuli hui thi. Main koi criminal nahi hoon.”

Main sharminda hokar hans pada. Woh khud se kaafi khush lag rahi thi.

Hawa ke jhonke se baarish chhat ke neeche aa gayi. Hina paani se bachne ke liye khiski, aur uska haath mere haath se chhoo gaya. Ek pal ke liye hum dono nahi hile. Phir woh peeche hati, hamare haathon ko dekha aur boli, “Tumhari ungliyan thandi hain.”

“Baarish ho rahi hai.”

“Gloves laaya karo.”

“August chal raha hai.”

“Toh bahaane banana band karo.”

Woh baarish ki taraf dekhne lagi, lekin uske kaanon ke sire gulaabi ho gaye the.

Maine yeh decide nahi kiya ki iska koi khaas matlab tha. Maine seekh liya tha ki umeed se bhara dimaag ek chhote se pal ko poora imagined future bana sakta hai.

Phir bhi, main us pal ko poori shaam yaad karta raha.

Chapter Three The Little Bird in Her Sketchbook

September aate-aate hamari dosti itni normal ho gayi thi ki friends bhi use notice na karne ka naatak nahi kar sakte the.

Daichi poochhne laga ki main har lunch break mein gayab kyun ho jaata hoon. Yui ne Hina se poochha ki agar woh apne lunch ka aadha hissa mujhe hi dene wali hai, toh phir lunch pack kyun karti hai. Hum dono ne aise jawab diye jin par kisi ko yakeen nahi hua.

Ek din main lunch lana bhool gaya. Hina ne bina koi baat banaye apna sandwich aadha kar diya. Agle din main uske liye ek extra canned coffee le aaya. Uske baad jo bhi hamare paas hota, use share karna ek aisa rule ban gaya jise kisi ne likha nahi tha.

Ek hafte baad hum cafeteria ke bahar bench par baithe the aur Hina apni chhoti sketchbook mein drawing bana rahi thi. Woh aam taur par use mujhse thoda door rakhti thi, isliye jab usne sketchbook meri taraf ghumaayi, toh mujhe hairaani hui.

Page par ek chidiya bani thi—chhoti si, satark, ek daali par baithi hui. Neeche saaf handwriting mein likha tha: Little Bird.

Main use dekhta reh gaya.

“Tumhe woh nickname yaad hai?”

“Bilkul.”

“Mujhe laga tha tumhe bura lagta hoga.”

“Mujhe woh ajeeb zaroor laga tha.” Usne sar tedha kiya. “Lekin kabhi bura nahi laga.”

“Maine woh naam sirf isliye rakha tha kyunki shuru mein mujhe tumhara naam nahi pata tha.”

“Mujhe pata hai. Daichi dheere se baat karne mein bahut kharaab hai.”

Maine ek haath se chehra dhak liya. “Mujhe usse serious baat karni padegi.”

“Please mat karna. Woh entertaining hai.”

Woh drawing par laut aayi aur chidiya ke pankhon par kuch bareek lines jod di.

“Tumne ise sambhaal kar kyun rakha?” maine poochha.

“Kyunki yeh yaad dilata hai ki hamari baat pehli baar kaise shuru hui thi.” Woh ruki. “Aur kyunki yeh aise naam jaisa lagta hai jo koi tumhe tab deta hai jab woh tumhare saath achha hona chahta hai, par ghabrahat ki wajah se normal baat nahi keh paata.”

Mujhe samajh nahi aaya ki us baat ka kya jawab doon, toh maine phir sketch ko dekha.

“Tum mujhe is naam se bula sakte ho,” usne kaha. “Bas poore courtyard mein announce mat kar dena.”

“Main khud par control rakhne ki koshish karunga.”

“Achha hai. Main apni reputation khona nahi chahti.”

Us shaam usne mujhe apna number diya. Usne kaha ki koi zaroori baat ho toh message kar sakta hoon, phir yeh bhi jod diya ki “bore ho raha hoon” bhi valid reason hai. Lekin raat ko uski mother phone check karti thi, isliye gyarah baje ke baad message nahi karna tha.

Hamari pehli conversation ek seedhe se Hey se shuru hui.

Teen minute baad uska reply aaya: Likhta bhi itna boring ho kya?

Agle kuch hafton tak dinner aur bedtime ke beech ke chhote se waqt mein hamare messages bhar gaye. Hum school, songs, books aur classmates ki ajeeb baaton ke baare mein baat karte. 10:55 par Hina kehti ki ab use jaana hai. 10:59 par woh ek aakhri message bhej deti—kabhi joke, kabhi random sticker, kabhi sirf Hmm.

Mujhe us aakhri message ka intezaar zaroorat se kahin zyada rehne laga.

Ek raat usne poochha, “Kabhi sochte ho ki agle saal hum kaise honge?”

Maine kai jawab type kiye aur sab delete kar diye.

Aakhir mein maine likha, “Umeed hai hum aise hi baat karte rahenge.”

Typing indicator aaya, gayab hua, phir wapas aaya.

10:59 par uska reply aaya.

Toh mujhse gayab mat ho jaana, Haruto.

Maine use do baar padha.

Chapter Four The Senior Who Came to Cheer

Seiran ka sports festival bright banners, loudspeakers aur itne josh ke saath aaya ki poora campus goonj utha.

High-school students stands ke ek section mein baithe har event ko barabar support karne ka naatak kar rahe the. Main yeh naatak khaas achhe se nahi kar paaya jab Hina ka badminton match shuru hua.

Woh court par aise focus ke saath utri jo main lunch ke waqt kam hi dekhta tha. Uske footwork tez the, returns bilkul sahi, aur chehra itna serious tha ki main lagbhag bhool gaya ki August mein gloves ko lekar behas karne wali ladki bhi wahi thi.

Usne seedhe games mein match jeet liya.

Baad mein jab usne stands ki taraf dekha, maine thumbs-up diya. Usne apni water bottle utha kar chhota sa salute kiya, phir teammates ke chhedne se pehle hi doosri taraf dekhne lagi.

Phir bhi teammates ne use chheda.

Baad mein Yui ne bataya ki Hina baaki dopahar muskurati rahi thi. Maine suna hi nahi, aisa dikhaya.

Kuch din baad mujhe cold ho gaya aur main lunch ke waqt high-school classroom mein hi raha. Maine Hina ko message kiya ki main theek hoon, mostly isliye kyunki mujhe pata tha woh poochhegi ki main kahan hoon.

Paanch minute baad darwaaze par knock hua.

Woh hallway mein ek chhota thermos liye khadi thi.

“Meri mother ne extra soup banaya tha,” usne kaha. “Yeh koi special delivery nahi hai. Isse weird mat banana.”

“Main aisa kuch karne wala nahi tha.”

“Tumhara chehra dekhkar toh lag raha hai karoge.”

Usne thermos meri desk par rakha aur jaane lagi. Uske neeche ek muda hua note tha.

Jaldi theek ho jao. Tumhare bina cafeteria boring hai.

Main itna muskuraya ki mere gaal dukhne lage.

October mein dono divisions ne school festival ki taiyaari mein madad ki. Main decorations committee ke posters bana raha tha; Hina supplies organise kar rahi thi aur kisi tarah sabko apne plan ke hisaab se chala rahi thi. Classes ke baad hum ek hi hall mein kaam karte—paper lanterns, rang-birangi strings aur fresh paint ki khushboo ke beech.

Ek teacher decorations ka box lekar guzri. Hum dono par nazar daali, phir khud hi muskura di.

Hina ne notice kar liya.

“Shuru mat hona,” usne mujhe warning di.

“Maine kuch kaha hi nahi.”

“Tum zor-zor se soch rahe the.”

“Aisa kuch nahi hota.”

“Tum karte ho toh hota hai.”

Hum hans pade aur teacher aage badh gayi.

Us dopahar mujhe samajh aaya ki kuch badal gaya tha. Ab har conversation mein main yeh nahi sochta tha ki kahin galat baat toh nahi keh di. Main bas Hina ke saath reh sakta tha, uski baatein sun sakta tha, aur din ko achha hone de sakta tha—bina is par zor diye ki use kuch aur banna hi chahiye.

Festival ke end par hum saath gate tak chale. Shaam ki hawa thandi ho gayi thi. Hina ne bag se ek earbud nikala aur meri taraf badhaya.

“Share karoge?”

Maine apne earphones mein se ek side use de di. Hamare beech ek purana gaana dheere se baj raha tha, campus se nikalte students ke shor ke neeche.

Hamare shoulders ek doosre se chhoo rahe the.

Hum dono mein se koi door nahi hata.

Chapter Five Our Kind of Normal

December aate-aate campus par thand jam gayi thi. Zelkova trees ke zyadaatar patte gir chuke the, aur students scarves ko coats mein daba kar buildings ke beech jaldi-jaldi chalte the.

Hina aur main ek-doosre ke din ka hissa ban gaye the. Mauka milta toh milte, nahi toh messages karte, aur un chhoti baaton ko share karte jo kisi aur ko shayad important na lagti.

Mujhe pata chala ki pareshan hone par woh draw karti hai, bahut meethi drinks pasand nahi karti, aur lunch ka aakhri bite baad ke liye bacha leti hai. Use pata chala ki main notes ke margins mein stories likhta hoon, weather forecast mein baarish hone par bhi umbrella bhool jaata hoon, aur sach bolne ki koshish karte waqt chup ho jaata hoon.

Ek shaam hum school gate ke bahar khade the aur aasman ka rang dheere-dheere winter blue ho raha tha.

“Agle saal ko lekar tension hai?” usne poochha.

“Mostly exams ki. Aur unke baad kya hoga, uski.”

“Tum figure out kar loge.”

“Tum aise keh rahi ho jaise tumhe pakka pata ho.”

“Pakka nahi pata.” Usne apna scarf theek kiya. “Bas keh rahi hoon kyunki mujhe lagta hai tum kar sakte ho.”

Main uske kehne ka exact tareeka yaad rakhna chahta tha.

Phir usne mujhe ek muda hua paper diya.

“Ghar jaakar kholna,” usne kaha.

Andar uski sketchbook wala Little Bird ka drawing tha, ab poora bana hua—bareek pankh aur patton se bhari daali ke saath. Neeche usne likha tha:

Jo Little Bird kabhi door lagta tha, use apni pehchaani daali mil gayi.

Maine use do baar padha, tab jaakar samajh aaya ki main muskurane laga tha.

Agle din maine use ek chhoti notebook di. Maine plain blue cover wali notebook chuni thi kyunki mujhe pata tha woh us par bhi drawing bana degi.

“Tumhari sketches ke liye,” maine kaha.

Usne notebook ko haathon mein palta. “Aur tum kis kaam mein laoge?”

“Shayad writing ke liye.”

“Toh hum pages compare kar sakte hain.”

“Yeh challenge hai?”

“Obviously.”

Hum pehli baar bina yeh dikhawa kiye gate se saath nikle ki yeh bas coincidence tha. Hina meri left side par chal rahi thi. Corner ke paas usne haath badhakar meri coat ki sleeve do ungliyon se pakad li.

Main ruk gaya. Woh bhi ruk gayi.

Ek pal ke liye laga jaise woh kuch kehna chahti ho. Lekin phir woh muskurayi aur sleeve chhod di.

Woh chhota sa touch tha, lekin station tak mere saath raha.

Humne apne beech ke rishte ko koi naam nahi diya tha. Hum abhi bhi students the—exams saamne the, families ki baatein sunni thi, aur future ka koi pakka pata nahi tha. Hina junior high ke second year mein thi; main high school ke first year mein. Humein badhne ka waqt tha, aur jo abhi samajh nahi aaya tha use jaldi naam dene ki koi zaroorat nahi thi.

Lekin ab hamare beech ki doori deewar jaisi nahi lagti thi.

Us raat 10:58 par mera phone roshan hua.

Good night, Haruto.

Ek doosra message aaya.

Aur kal umbrella mat bhoolna. Baarish hone wali hai.

Main dheere se hans pada aur phone notebook ke paas rakh diya.

Little Bird wapas aa gayi thi. Hamare aam dinon ne phir se apni rhythm pakad li thi.

Aur shared lunches, baarish wale corridors aur thand mein ek earbud share karne ke beech, main samajhne laga tha ki relationship hamesha confession ke saath shuru nahi hota.

Kabhi-kabhi woh chupchaap badhta rehta hai, jab tak do log khud ko ek-doosre ke saath ghar jaate hue nahi paate.

Filhaal, hamare liye normal ka matlab yahi tha.

Mujhe abhi nahi pata tha ki aam din kitni jaldi important ban sakte hain. Agla school year ek alag sawaal lekar aane wala tha: kya Hina aur main ek-doosre tak dobara pahunch sakte hain, yeh nahi—balki kya hum itne brave honge ki jo feelings hamare beech badhi hain, unke baare mein sach bol sakein.
