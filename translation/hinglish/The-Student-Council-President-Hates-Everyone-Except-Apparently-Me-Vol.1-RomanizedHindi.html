Chapter EightThe Empty Seat

Monday ko Kaito absent tha. Tuesday ko bhi. Wednesday tak Hanako classroom door ko itni baar dekh chuki thi ki Hiro ne apni chair uski line of sight mein kar di.

Hanako ke paas argument nahi tha.

Kaito ne nod kiya.

Kaito ne nazar neeche ki.

Hanako ko apni harshness ka regret hua. Usne softer voice mein kaha ki woh uski illness se angry nahi hai. Woh is baat se angry hai ki Kaito sochta hai honest hone se woh burden ban jaayega.

Woh halka sa smile kiya. Itna kaafi tha.

Hiro hamesha maanta tha ki friendship timing ke baare mein hai: kab bolna, kab chup rehna, kab joke kisi ko embarrassment se bacha sakta hai aur kab wahi joke use aur chhota feel karwa dega.

Chapter TenThe Truth About Breathing

Hanako ka drink girte-girte bacha. “Woh kyun la rahe ho?”

Chapter TwelveStay With Me

Monday morning ek broken pen se shuru hui. Hanako student council ke liye note likh rahi thi jab pen ne kaam karna band kar diya. Usne do baar click kiya, jaise formal warning se pen maan jaayega. Nahi maana. Zyada press kiya toh tip snap ho gayi.

Hanako ne pen ko dekha, phir classroom ke across Kaito ko, jo Hiro se baat karte hue hans raha tha.

This is ridiculous.

Hanako uski honest life ka part banna chahti thi—not nurse, not rescuer, aur na hi koi aisa person jise paas rehne ke liye useful hona zaroori ho.

Bas khud.

Lunch mein usne Kaito ko courtyard mein dhoondha. “Mere saath aao.”

Hanako ne kaha woh use pasand karti hai, lekin woh frustrating hai, cheezein chhupata hai, use worry karwata hai aur uski expectations ke mutabik react nahi karta. Use pata hai ki woh uski problems fix nahi kar sakti, uski health ki responsibility nahi hai, aur Kaito ko uske liye fine hone ka pretend nahi karna chahiye.

“Lekin main wahan rehna chahti hoon. Truth sunna chahti hoon, difficult ho tab bhi. Jab tum baat nahi karna chaho tab tumhare saath baithna chahti hoon. Coffee peena, stupid cheezon par argue karna aur—”

Woh ruk gayi.

Kaito ka expression soft ho gaya, aur sab aur difficult ho gaya.

Hanako ne nazar hata li. “So stay with me.”

Silence.

“Sirf aaj nahi. Mera matlab—”

Usne aankhein band ki. “Let's be together.”

Words ab wapas nahi liye ja sakte the.

Kaito use aise dekh raha tha jaise usne koi precious cheez di ho aur woh use gira dene se darr raha ho.

“Say something!” Hanako ne kaha.

Kaito ne breath li. “I like you, too.”

Hanako use stare karne lagi. “Phir aisa face kyun bana rahe ho?”

Kaito halki si hansi hansa, phir serious ho gaya. “Main tumhare saath rehna chahta hoon. Lekin mujhe tumhe apni life mein aane dena seekhna hoga. Kabhi galti karunga.”

Kaito nahi chahta tha Hanako uske liye responsible feel kare. Hanako ne kaha, “Mujhe tumhari responsibility nahi chahiye. Main tumhare saath rehna chahti hoon.”

Hanako ne use hug kar liya. Awkward hug tha: usne bahut tight pakda, Kaito ne return karne se pehle hesitate kiya, aur dono ko haath kahan rakhne hain nahi pata tha.

Alag hone par Hanako ne poochha, “Can I kiss you?”

Kaito smile kiya. “Yes.”

AfterwordA Small Thing, Repeated

Kuch stories grand gestures ke liye yaad rakhi jaati hain: rain mein dramatic confession, fireworks ke neeche promise, ya koi insaan poora city cross kare kyunki woh kuch unsaid chhod nahi sakta.

Is story mein bhi woh feelings hain, lekin sabse important gestures chhote hain.

Kisi ko girne se pehle haath dena.

Aisi apology jo forgiveness demand na kare.

Answer assume kiye bina question poochhna.

Woh friend jo jaanta ho kab joke band karna hai.

Woh ladka jo kisi aur ko notice karna pade usse pehle kehna seekhe, “I'm not feeling well.”

Aur desk par coffee ka can rakhna.

Hanako quiet person nahi bani. Kaito har feeling perfectly explain karne wala nahi bana. Dating shuru karne se unki problems gayab nahi hui, aur affection ne unhe life ke difficult parts se immune nahi kiya.

Badla bas yeh ki unhone pretend karna band karne ki koshish ki.

Hanako ne seekha ki control kiye bina care ki ja sakti hai.

Kaito ne seekha ki care accept karne ke badle kisi ko apni poori life ka qarz nahi dena padta.

Coffee cans, arguments aur broken pens ke beech unhe pata chala ki understood hone ke liye perfectly understood hona zaroori nahi.

Kabhi-kabhi bas kisi aur ko try karne ka chance dena kaafi hota hai.

Unki story ki beginning wahi thi.

Kiss wala moment nahi.

Confession wala moment bhi nahi.

Woh moment jab unhone ek doosre ke liye decide karna band kiya ki kaun kya handle kar sakta hai—and ek doosre ko khud choose karne diya.

One can of coffee. One honest answer. One day at a time.

Shuru karne ke liye itna kaafi tha.

Uski mother ne drawer band kar diya. “Aaj raat nahi.”

Kaito ne question chhod diya. Kuch baatein abhi bhi thi jinhe poochhna usne nahi seekha tha, aur ab woh maan-ne laga tha ki honesty ka matlab difficult conversations ko apne waqt par aane dena bhi hai.

Use nahi pata tha ki jis past par usne kabhi sawaal nahi kiya, woh wapas aa raha tha.
