CHAPTER ONE
The Girl by the Window

Middle school ka doosra saal chalk, purani desks aur entrance ke paas sookhte baarish ke paani ki jaani-pehchaani smell ke saath shuru hua. Sota ki seat ke upar wala ceiling fan thaki hui si tik-tik kar raha tha. Uski notebook mein aadhe page ke class notes aur ek spaceship ki drawing thi jo planet se bhaag rahi thi.

“Yeh period khatam hone mein aur kitni der hai?” usne dheere se poochha.

Uske paas baitha Yuto, baahon par gaal tikaye hue tha. “Bees minute baad poochhna. Tab tak main jawab jhel chuka hounga.”

Sota ki hansi nikalte-nikalte reh gayi. Use chhupane ke liye woh khidki ki taraf muda—and uski nazar doosri row mein baithi ladki par padi.

Airi Tanaka saaf aur sambhal kar likh rahi thi. Notebook par jhukte waqt uske baal aage aa jaate, aur woh bina nazar uthaye unhe kaan ke peeche kar leti. Woh koi khaas kaam nahi kar rahi thi. Bas apne kaam mein doobi baithi thi, jabki baaki classroom ek aur aam subah se dheere-dheere guzarta ja raha tha.

Sota ne lagbhag turant nazar hata li.

Bas classmate hai, usne khud se kaha. Kal tak shayad bhool jaunga.

Woh nahi bhoola.

Agle kuch hafton mein usne bina jaan-boojhkar uske baare mein chhoti-chhoti baatein jaan li. Airi class ke best students mein se thi, lekin help maangne par kisi ko bewakoof mehsoos nahi karati thi. Teacher ke poochhne par dheemi awaaz mein bolti, magar uske answers clear hote. Kabhi teacher agle topic par badh jaata, phir bhi woh notebook ke margins mein likhti rehti. Jo bhi likhti, use padhkar khud hi muskura deti.

Ek dopahar English teacher ne kisi volunteer se passage zor se padhne ko kaha. Poora room jhuki hui nazron aur achanak bahut interesting lagne wale pencil cases se bhar gaya.

Airi ne haath utha diya.

Uski awaaz shaant thi, lekin har lafz classroom ke aakhri kone tak pahunch raha tha. Sota khud ko uski baat par utna dhyaan dete hue paaya jitna usne poore din kisi cheez par nahi diya tha.

Woh khatam hui toh uski nazar ek pal ke liye Sota se mili. Sota turant apni notebook dekhne laga, jaise spaceship ka kaam achanak bahut zaroori ho gaya ho.

Kuch hua nahi tha. Phir bhi, kuch shuru ho gaya tha.

CHAPTER TWO
The Small Things That Stayed

School ka saal zyadaatar un aam cheezon se banta hai jinhe yaad rakhna mushkil hota hai: attendance, udhaar liye erasers, lunch bell aur classrooms ke beech lamba raasta. Sota ke liye, dheere-dheere yeh saari chhoti baatein Airi ke aas-paas jama hone lagi.

Ek subah use pata chala ki history notebook ghar par reh gayi. Teacher ne sabko classmate ke saath notes share karne ko kaha. Yuto ne apni notebook uthakar fusfusaya, “Mere paas hai. Meri use kar le.”

Sota ne uska thank you kiya, lekin uske pair use pehle hi Airi ki desk tak le gaye the.

“Tanaka-san? Thodi der tumhare notes dekh sakta hoon?”

Airi ne hairani se dekha, phir notebook dono ke beech kar di. “Haan, bilkul. Bas lunch se pehle lauta dena. Mujhe bhi chahiye.”

“Of course.”

Bas itna hi. Airi apne kaam par laut gayi aur Sota notes aise copy karne laga jaise use kisi desh ka raaz saunp diya gaya ho.

Ek baar science project ke liye class ko groups mein kaam karna tha. Airi diagram dikhane ke liye table par jhuki, usi waqt Sota ruler uthane laga. Unki ungliyan lagbhag chhoo gayin. Sota ne haath itni tezi se peeche kheench liya ki Yuto use ghoorne laga.

“Ab stationery se bhi darr lagne laga?” Yuto ne baad mein poochha.

“Chup reh.”

“Mainne kuch kaha hi nahi.”

“Apne chehre se sab keh diya.”

Yuto hans pada, lekin Airi ke saamne usne Sota ko chheda nahi. Is chhoti si meherbaani ke liye Sota shukrguzaar tha—aur pehle se bhi zyada sharminda.

Baarish ka mausam aaya toh Sota extra foldable umbrella lekar chalne laga. Usne khud se kaha ki yeh practical hai. Lekin Airi ko offer karne ka sahi waqt kabhi nahi mila.

School jaate hue woh aam sentences rehearse karta: Homework samajh aaya? Woh book interesting lag rahi hai. Aaj baarish kaafi tez hai, hai na?

Jab aakhir woh uske paas khada hokar bolne ki himmat karta, saare lafz ek polite “Good morning” ban jaate. Airi hamesha wahi narm smile dekar jawab deti aur apne raaste chali jaati.

Woh ab ek aisi smile ka intezaar karne laga tha jo sirf ek second tikti thi.

CHAPTER THREE
A Letter Folded Four Times

March tak school ka saal lagbhag khatam ho gaya tha. Exams ki wajah se classroom mein bechaini thi aur sab agle grade ki baat kar rahe the. Sota ki ek aur pareshani thi: agar usne kuch nahi kaha, toh saal khatam ho jayega aur sach uske andar hi phansa reh jayega.

School ke baad Yuto ne use teesri baar wahi sawaal ghumate hue suna.

“Kisi ko pasand karta hai toh bata de,” Yuto ne kaha. “Sabse bura yeh hoga ki uski feelings same nahi hongi. Planet thodi na phat jayega.”

“Tumhare liye kehna aasaan hai.”

“Haan, hai. Main woh nahi hoon jo woh pencil maangti hai toh apne dimaag mein speech likhne lagta hai.”

Sota ke paas koi jawab nahi tha.

Us raat woh chhote lamp ke neeche desk par baitha. Pehla draft kisi kharaab romance drama ka dialogue lag raha tha. Usne phaad diya. Doosra formal application jaisa tha. Woh bhi phaad diya. Teesra do pages ka ho gaya, jisme har chhota moment tha jo use yaad tha. Confession se zyada aisa lag raha tha jaise diary galti se kisi aur ke naam likh di ho.

Aakhir usne saaf kagaz liya aur seedhe lafzon mein likha:

Airi, mujhe nahi pata yeh kehna sahi hai ya nahi, lekin main ise aur chhupana nahi chahta. Main tumhe pasand karta hoon. Shayad kaafi waqt se. Tumhe bhi aisa mehsoos karna zaroori nahi, aur main tumse apni pasand ka jawab nahi maang raha. Bas chahta tha ki tumhe pata ho.

Aakhri line par uski handwriting kaanp gayi. Usne khat ko chaar baar moda aur pocket mein rakh liya. Raat bhar theek se so nahi paaya.

Agle din har corridor zaroorat se zyada bhara hua lag raha tha. Lunch ke waqt usne aakhir Airi se baat ki, jab woh apni books rakh rahi thi.

“Tanaka-san… jab time mile toh kya tum yeh padh sakti ho?”

Usne muda hua kagaz aage badhaya. Apna haath use apne hi jism se alag sa mehsoos ho raha tha.

Airi ne khat se uske chehre ki taraf dekha. Pehle hairani aayi, phir ek sambhal kar rakhi hui narmi.

“Theek hai,” usne dheere se kaha.

Us din baaki kisi teacher ne kya kaha, Sota ko kuch yaad nahi raha.

CHAPTER FOUR
Her Answer

Airi ne class mein khat ka zikr nahi kiya. Usne classmates ko nahi bataya aur na hi Sota ke saath alag tarah se pesh aayi. Agle do din school ki aam routine chalti rahi, aur har aam pal use aur nervous kar deta.

Teesre din dopahar ko usne poochha ki kya woh shoe lockers ke paas thodi der baat kar sakte hain. Wahan students outdoor shoes pehen rahe the aur corridor mein geele umbrellas ki halki si smell thi.

“Sach-sach batane ke liye thank you,” usne kaha. Muda hua khat notebook ke pages ke beech sambhaal kar rakha tha. “Mujhe hairani hui, lekin achha laga ki tumne mujh par itna bharosa kiya.”

Sota ne normal saans lene ki koshish ki. “Okay.”

Woh ruki aur lafz sochne lagi. “I'm sorry. Main abhi relationship mein nahi aana chahti. Bahut si cheezein hain jin par main focus karna chahti hoon, aur mujhe nahi lagta ki main tumhari feelings ka waisa jawab de sakti hoon jaisi tum umeed karte ho.”

Uski awaaz mein koi berukhi nahi thi. Woh hansi nahi, aur na hi baad mein haan kehne ka jhootha ishara diya sirf isliye ki pal aasaan ho jaye.

Isi wajah se jawab sunne mein meherbaan bhi tha aur mushkil bhi.

“Samajh gaya,” Sota ne kaha. Uski awaaz uske andar ke ehsaas se zyada stable thi. “Tumhe sorry kehne ki zaroorat nahi.”

“Umeed hai hum classmates toh reh sakte hain. Main nahi chahti ki tumhe mujhse bachkar chalna pade.”

Usne sir hilaya. “Reh sakte hain.”

Dono apni-apni routine mein laut gaye. Airi ne ek baar phir thank you kaha aur train pakadne chali gayi. Sota lockers ke paas khada raha, jab tak corridor khaali hone nahi laga.

Usne rejection ko ek dramatic scene samjha tha: lambi speech, achanak toofaan, ek aisa pal jo zindagi ko pehle aur baad mein baant de. Lekin yeh toh school ke ek aam din ke aakhir mein do students ke beech hui shaant si baat thi.

Agle din Airi ne use hamesha ki tarah greet kiya. Sota ne jawab diya aur woh class chali gayi.

Airi ke liye kuch nahi badla tha—or kam se kam woh kuch badla hua dikhana nahi chahti thi. Sota ke liye wahi classroom tha, bas uske andar ek nayi doori aa gayi thi.

CHAPTER FIVE
The Same Face, Years Later

Kai saal baad, ek late spring afternoon mein Sota apne purane school laut aaya. Ab woh college mein tha, nayi sadkon, anjaan chehron aur aise schedules ke beech rehta tha jo school bell ke hisaab se nahi chalte the. Mahino se usne Airi ke baare mein nahi socha tha—or kam se kam itna nahi ki khud se maan le.

Phir uski nazar gate par padi.

Deewarein yaad se chhoti lag rahi thi. Sadak ke kinaare ke zelkova ped aur ghane ho gaye the, aur courtyard us duniya se chhota lag raha tha jise kabhi apne andar sameta hua tha. School ka event chal raha tha, isliye gate khula tha. Bina soch-samajh ke Sota andar chala gaya.

Woh shoe lockers, dopahar ki dhoop pakadne wali khidkiyon aur us corridor se guzra jahan usne woh sentences rehearse kiye the jo kabhi keh nahi paaya. Classroom ki seedhiyon ke paas uski chaal dheemi ho gayi.

Purana room khaali tha. Desks seedhi rows mein thi aur dhoop ka halka aayatakar tukda floor par pada tha. Use doosri row, khidki wali seat aur woh jagah yaad aa gayi jahan Airi notebook kholkar baitha karti thi.

Sota peeche apni purani seat par baith gaya.

Ek pal mein sab yaad aa gaya: pehli baar use notice karna, history ke notes udhaar lena, science project ke waqt ungliyon ka lagbhag chhoo jaana, pocket mein kaanpta khat aur shoe lockers ke paas uska narm inkaar.

Use hairani hui ki sab kuch itni saaf tarah yaad tha. Waqt ke saath feeling badal gayi thi, lekin woh kabhi aisi kahani nahi bani jisse use sharm aaye. Usne kisi ko pasand kiya, imaandari se bataya aur maan liya ki uski feelings same nahi thi. Dard hua tha, lekin usne yeh bhi sikhaya ki sach bolna sirf tabhi valuable nahi hota jab jawab wahi mile jo hum chahte hain.

Bahar courtyard mein ek ball uchhalti hui aayi. Ek student hans kar uske peeche bhaaga, aur awaaz khuli khidki se andar aa gayi.

Sota muskura diya. Woh utha, apni trousers se thodi dhool hataayi aur khidki ke paas khaali desk ko ek baar phir dekha.

Woh nikalne hi wala tha ki corridor se ek jaani-pehchaani awaaz aayi.

“Mori-kun?”

Woh muda. Airi classroom ke darwaaze ke paas khadi thi, saath mein ek aur former student. Woh badi ho gayi thi, bilkul expected tha, lekin uske chehre par ab bhi wahi shaant, dhyaan dene wali warmth thi. Ek second ke liye dono ko samajh nahi aaya ki apne past ke kis version ko pehle greet karein.

Phir Airi muskuraayi. “Bahut waqt ho gaya.”

“Haan. Kaafi.”

Shuru mein baat thodi awkward thi. Unhone college, teachers aur classroom ke yaad se chhota lagne ke baare mein baat ki. Saath aaya former student Airi ko event mein help karne ke liye bulaane laga, toh usne maafi maang kar jaane ki baat kahi. Koi dramatic baat nahi hui. Purani feelings achanak wapas nahi aayi, aur darwaaze ke peeche koi naya confession bhi nahi tha.

Jaane se pehle Airi ne poochha, “Tum ab bhi Instagram use karte ho? Class ke sab logon ke saath contact mein rehna chahiye.”

“Haan,” Sota ne kaha. “Bilkul.”

Unhone contact details exchange kiye aur ek group chat join ki, jo Yuto ne shayad kisi se poochhe bina bana diya tha. Dopahar ke end tak chat mein baarah members, teen alag names aur agle weekend khana kahan khana hai is par debate chal rahi thi.

“Yeh toh abhi se mess hai,” Sota ne likha.

Airi ne ek chhote rabbit ka sticker bheja jiske sign par likha tha: please cooperate.

Woh hans pada. Hansi pehchaani si thi, lekin ab woh present ki thi—not us ladke ki jo kabhi hafton tak ek sentence rehearse karta raha tha.

Agle kuch mahino mein unke messages ordinary ho gaye. Woh jokes share karte, stories ka reply dete aur friends ke saath outings par jaate. Woh akele kam hi milte; group hamesha hota, restaurants par behas karta, trains miss karta ya aisi photos leta jahan koi na koi aankh band kar leta. Sota aur Airi dost ban gaye—not isliye ki uski purani feelings jaadu se mit gayi thi, balki isliye ki usne uske jawab ka respect karne aur rishta sachchai ke saath jo ban sakta tha, use banne dene ka choice kiya.

Kabhi-kabhi koi memory use achanak pakad leti. Kabhi Airi ki kindness khidki wali ladki yaad dila deti. Lekin woh seekh raha tha ki kisi feeling ko yaad karne ka matlab yeh nahi ki use phir se pursue karna zaroori hai.

Use nahi pata tha aane wale saal kya laayenge. Filhaal messages ke jawab dene the, doston se milna tha aur aam din the jo dheere-dheere apni yaadein ban rahe the.

Ehsaas badal gaya tha.

Lekin yaadon ko samajhna sikhane wali kahani abhi khatam nahi hui thi.

Do saal baad, Sota ko samajh aayega ki kisi ka dost ban jaana, un feelings se poori tarah aage badh jaana nahi hota jo kabhi us insaan ne jagayi thi.
