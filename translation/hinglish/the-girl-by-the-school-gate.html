Chapter One The Girl by the School Gate

Seiran Academy Japan ke ek shaant shehar ke kinaare bana private combined junior-high aur senior-high school tha. Junior-high students ki uniform mein alag variations the, senior students pehli bell se pehle classroom ki khidkiyon ke paas jama hote, aur sab genkan mein outdoor shoes chhodkar indoor slippers pehente. School ki deewar ke us paar local train line khadkhadaati hui city centre ki taraf jaati thi.

Seiran Academy mein high school ka mera doosra saal apni usual rhythm mein dhal chuka tha: subah ki bell, bheed-bhare corridors, exams ki yaad dilate teachers, aur mere friends jo har aam din ko zaroorat se zyada shor-sharaba wala banane ke naye tareeke dhoondh lete the.

Main ordinary rehne mein achha tha. Apna kaam poora karta, sab hanste toh main bhi hans deta, aur ghar laut jaata bina kisi aisi baat ke jo kisi ko batane layak ho. Meri zindagi ka koi dramatic background music nahi tha. Zyadaatar din main isse khush tha.

Phir June ki shuruaat ki ek dopahar, Seiran Academy ke bahar sadak par, baarish se geeli zelkova ki shaakhon ke neeche, meri nazar ek ladki par padi.

Yeh proper mulaqat bhi nahi thi. Mere ek dost ko us ladki ki ek jhalak dekhni thi jo use pasand thi, isliye classes ke baad hum mein se kuch gate ke paas ruk gaye. Ek ladki, jiske paas milta-julta bag tha, sadak par aayi aur humne poore confidence se maan liya ki wahi woh ladki hai jiska hum intezaar kar rahe the. Agle din pata chala ki hum galat the.

Baat wahin khatam ho jaani chahiye thi. Ek chhoti galti, group ke liye ek joke, bas.

Kuch din baad main station ki taraf jaane wala shaant raasta lene laga. Raasta lamba tha, lekin ek chhote shrine, vending machines ki line aur ek residential street se guzarta tha jahan shaam ka traffic kam hota. Ek ladki saamne se aayi, uska chehra mask se aadha chhupa tha. Hum guzre toh maine ek nazar uski taraf dekhi, phir sadak par dhyaan de diya.

Bina kisi samajh aane wali wajah ke, woh pal mere dimaag mein reh gaya.

Yeh first sight par pyaar nahi tha. Mujhe uska naam, class ya uske baare mein ek bhi baat nahi pata thi. Bas itna jaanta tha ki us chhoti si mulaqat ne mere dimaag ke roz ke shor ko ek pal ke liye rok diya tha.

Mainne khud ko samjhaya ki main bewakoofi kar raha hoon, aur us baat ko bhool gaya—or bhoolne ki koshish ki.

Agle din subah homeroom shuru hi hua tha. Courtyard mein dhoop padi thi, aur students indoor uwabaki pehen kar jaldi-jaldi classrooms mein ja rahe the. Main aadha soya hua tha, teacher ko exams ke baare mein yaad dilate hue sun raha tha.

Tabhi ek late student gate se bhaagti hui aayi aur junior-high ki ladkiyon ki line mein ja mili.

Wahi ladki thi.

Is baar usne mask nahi pehna tha. Pehli baar maine uska chehra saaf dekha, aur bechain courtyard jaise us ek aam si detail ke aas-paas simat gaya. Woh kisi ka attention kheenchne ki koshish nahi kar rahi thi. Bas classmates ke saath khadi thi, apne bag ka strap theek karte hue, jabki hamare homeroom teacher bolte ja rahe the.

Kisi ke notice karne se pehle maine nazar hata li.

Ya kam se kam mujhe aisa hi laga.

Uske baad main use school day ke important hisson ke beech ke chhote khaali moments mein notice karne laga: seedhiyon ke paas, courtyard paar karte hue, gate par intezaar karte hue. Main kabhi jaan-boojhkar use dhoondhne nahi nikalta tha. Bas mera dhyaan khud hi use dhoondh leta, isse pehle ki main tay kar paata ki kahan dekhna hai.

Aakhir mujhe pata chala ki uska naam Hina tha. Woh junior high ke first year mein thi; main high school ke second year mein. Hum school ke alag stages par the, alag routines aur alag duniyaon ke saath. Yeh doori important thi, chahe mera uljha hua dimaag abhi samajh na pa raha ho ki is feeling ka kya kare.

Main use jaanta hi kitna tha ki is feeling ko koi bada naam de doon? Woh ek aise insaan ke liye halki si dilchaspi thi jo abhi bhi mere liye ajnabi thi. Isliye maine chupke se is feeling ko ek naam de diya.

The Little Bird.

Woh bheed ke beech aise nazar aati aur gaayab ho jaati jaise kisi se notice hone ki umeed hi na ho. Yeh naam meri imagination ka tha, uska nahi. Maine ise apne tak rakha, kyunki mujhe pakka yakeen tha ki bol diya toh friends ise joke bana denge.

Phir bhi unhe pata chal hi gaya.

Ek dopahar Daichi ne use playground ke kinaare se guzarte dekha. Uski grin lafzon se pehle aa gayi.

“Lo, aa gayi,” usne mere kandhe ko kohni maari. “Tumhari Little Bird.”

“Awaaz dheemi rakh,” maine budbudaaya.

“Kyun? Aise lag rahe ho jaise final exams ka result announce ho gaya ho.”

“Tum bas kahani bana rahe ho.”

“Bilkul. Aur tumne ek minute mein sirf chhe baar gate ki taraf dekha.”

Baaki sab hans pade. Maine naraaz dikhne ki koshish ki, lekin isse unhe aur mauka mil gaya.

Jab Hina door se guzri, maine nazar zameen par rakhi. Kisi ajnabi ko uncomfortable karne ka risk lene se behtar tha friends ki teasing jhelna. Mujhe usse kuch bhi expect karne ka haq nahi tha—na smile, na conversation, na itna sa ishara bhi ki use pata ho main exist karta hoon.

Phir bhi, kabhi-kabhi hamari nazrein mil jaati.

Bas ek second ke liye. Woh nazar hata leti; main baaki din us pal ko yaad karta. Itni chhoti cheez ko itna matlab dena embarrassing tha, aur mera ek hissa yeh jaanta bhi tha.

Phir ek dopahar woh gate ke paas ruk gayi.

Mere friends mere saath the, lekin is baar unmein se koi bhi moment bigaadne ke liye jaldi nahi bol paaya. Hina ne mujhe thodi uljhan ke saath dekha, jaise kisi suni hui baat se mere chehre ko mila rahi ho.

“Tum high school ke second year mein ho na?” usne poochha.

“Haan.” Meri awaaz meri umeed se dheemi nikli.

Usne mere friends ki taraf dekha, jo sun na rahe hon aisa dikhane ki bahaduri bhari koshish kar rahe the. “Tum log bahut hanste rehte ho.”

Uski awaaz mein halki si smile thi. Main jawab soch paata, usse pehle woh aage badh gayi.

Puri baat shayad das second bhi nahi chali. Maine use ghanton tak dimaag mein dobara chalaya.

Isliye nahi ki iska matlab tha woh mujhe pasand karti thi. Isse aisa kuch prove nahi hota tha. Bas pehli baar jis insaan ko main chupke se notice karta tha, usne mujhse aise baat ki jaise main bhi ek real person hoon, bheed ka ek aur chehra nahi.

Din ko alag mehsoos karane ke liye itna kaafi tha.

Chapter Two Small Conversations

Uske baad main dardnaak tareeke se notice karne laga ki hamare raaste kitni baar milte hain. Kabhi Hina seedhiyon ke paas dikhti, kabhi courtyard mein friends ke saath hoti, kabhi hum bina bole ek-doosre ke paas se guzar jaate.

Maine har coincidence ko clue banane ki koshish nahi ki. Khud ko yaad dilaya ki hum ek hi school mein the, wahi corridors use karte the aur hamare schedules overlap karte the. Humein ek-doosre ka dikh jaana normal tha.

Mere friends ko, afsos, reasonable banne mein koi interest nahi tha.

“Aaj baat hui?” ek subah Daichi ne poochha.

“Nahi.”

“Use dekha?”

“Abhi tumhe dekh raha hoon. Medal chahiye?”

“Yeh jawab nahi hai.”

“Bas itna hi milega.”

Woh itni zor se hans pada ki teacher ne hamari taraf dekha. Maine faisla kiya ki agar embarrassment school ka subject hota, toh Daichi class mein top karta.

Kuch din baad morning homeroom ke baad main courtyard ke drinking fountain par ruk gaya. Koi khaas wajah nahi thi; courtyard mein bheed thi aur mujhe shor se ek pal door rehna tha. Thodi der baad Hina apni bottle lekar aa gayi.

Kuch seconds tak hum dono tap ko bahut dhyaan se dekhte rahe.

Phir usne meri taraf dekha. “Tum hamesha friends ke saath hi rehte ho, hai na?”

“Zyadaatar waqt,” maine kaha. “Aadat ho gayi hai.”

“Tum sab maza karte lagte ho.”

“Zyadaatar woh mera mazaak banate hain.”

Woh dheere se hans di. Bottle bharne ke baad maine raasta dene ke liye side li toh usne thank you kaha aur friends ke paas laut gayi.

Yeh koi turning point nahi tha. Koi secret signal bhi nahi. Bas do students ke beech ek normal conversation thi, aur main samajhne laga tha ki normal cheezein bhi meaningful ho sakti hain, bina kuch aur banne ki zaroorat ke.

Uske baad hello kehna aasaan ho gaya. Kabhi woh pehle greet karti, kabhi meri himmat khatam hone se pehle main keh deta. Raaste milte toh kuch lafz badal lete, zyada der nahi. Mujhe pata chala ki use courtyard garden ke paas friends ke saath baithna pasand hai, jahan hydrangeas khilne lage the, aur woh jawab dene se pehle dhyaan se sunti thi.

Maine uske baare mein sab kuch jaanne ki koshish nahi ki. Zaroorat bhi nahi thi. Kisi mein dilchaspi lena aur use solve karne wali mystery samajhna alag baatein hain, aur main dheere-dheere yeh line samajh raha tha.

Ek dopahar woh meri classroom ke paas se guzri aur ruk gayi.

“Tumhara naam Haruto hai na?”

Main aadha second zyada der tak use dekhta raha. “Haan.”

“Kisi ko tumhe us naam se bulate suna tha.”

“Woh Daichi hoga. Woh mujhe bahut saare naam se bulata hai.”

Woh hans di, phir friends ke saath chalne ke liye jaldi se aage badh gayi.

Baaki din main bina wajah muskuraane se bachne ki koshish karta raha.

Us shaam maine socha ki notice kiye jaane aur sach mein jaane jaane mein kya farq hai. Usne mera naam suna tha aur ab use yaad tha. Chhoti si baat thi, lekin school ajnabiyon ki bheed se thoda kam lagne laga.

Kuch hi din baad main akela ghar ja raha tha jab peeche se apna naam suna.

“Haruto.”

Main muda. Hina kuch kadam door khadi thi, dono haathon se schoolbag ka strap pakde hue.

“Tum shayad jaldi school badalne wale ho na?” usne poochha.

“Shayad. Agle stage ke liye kahin aur jaana padega.”

Usne sir hilaya aur sadak ki taraf dekha. “Shayad aisa hi hota hai. Aakhir sab aage badh jaate hain.”

Main kuch clever kehna chahta tha, aisa kuch jo baat ko lamba kar de. Lekin bas itna hi keh paaya, “Haan. Shayad.”

Kuch pal hum chup rahe. Khamoshi awkward thi, lekin buri nahi. Phir usne good evening kaha aur apne raaste chali gayi.

Main ghar tak sochta raha ki hum ek-doosre ke baare mein kitna kam jaante hain, aur ek chhoti conversation aam sadak ko kitna ajnabi bana sakti hai.

Mere dimaag mein uska private naam wahi raha: Little Bird. Lekin ab us naam ke saath ek yaad bhi thi. Woh meri likhi kahani ka character nahi thi jise main apni marzi se chala sakun. Woh ek insaan thi, jiski zindagi ke baare mein mujhe lagbhag kuch nahi pata tha.

Chapter Three The Question I Couldn't Dodge

Agle kuch din main sochta raha ki hamari conversations ka matlab kya tha. Kya hum dost ban rahe the? Kya main bas isliye khush tha kyunki jis insaan ko maine notice kiya tha, woh bhi mujhe notice karne lagi thi? Mere paas saaf jawab nahi tha, aur shayad abhi jawab hona zaroori bhi nahi tha.

Phir ek shaant dopahar, hum station ki taraf jaane wali us patli sadak par mile—wahi sadak jahan maine use pehli baar notice kiya tha.

Hina akeli chal rahi thi. Main side mein hokar use jaane dene ka soch raha tha, jaise pehle kai baar kiya tha. Is baar main thodi doori par ruk gaya aur dekha ki kya woh baat karna chahti hai.

Woh bhi ruk gayi.

“Ek sawaal poochh sakti hoon?” usne kaha.

“Haan.”

Uske chehre par jigyaasa thi, gussa nahi. “Jab main paas aati hoon toh tum hamesha chup kyun ho jaate ho?”

Koi aasaan joke nahi tha jo mujhe bacha leta. Maine sadak ki taraf dekha, phir uski taraf.

“Main nervous ho jaata hoon,” maine maan liya.

“Meri wajah se?”

“Thoda.”

Woh mere chehre ko dekhne lagi, jaise tay kar rahi ho ki main sach bol raha hoon ya nahi. Phir usne poochha, “Tumhare friends mujhe Little Bird kehte hain na?”

Mera pet jaise doob gaya. “Kehte hain.”

“Tum bhi mujhe wahi bulate ho?”

“Sirf apne dimaag mein,” maine jaldi se kaha. “Bas ek nickname tha jo maine socha. Mera matlab tumhe uncomfortable karna nahi tha.”

Woh kuch der chup rahi. Maine bahane dekar khamoshi bharne ki ichchha ko roka.

“Naam thoda ajeeb hai,” aakhir usne kaha.

“Mujhe pata hai.”

“Lekin kam se kam tumne sach toh bataya.”

Maine saans chhodi, pata bhi nahi tha ki rok rakhi thi. “Mujhe tum achhi lagti ho. Bas. Samajh nahi aata tha kaise bolun ki sab awkward na ho jaye.”

Hina ne aage ki sadak ki taraf dekha. “Main relationship nahi dhoondh rahi,” usne seedhe kaha. “School hai, friends hain, aur apni zindagi ki cheezein samajhni hain.”

“Samajh gaya.”

“Lekin jab milen toh baat kar sakte hain. Agar tum chaho.”

“Main chahta hoon.”

Uski smile chhoti thi, lekin asli thi. Humne goodbye kaha aur alag-alag raaste chale gaye.

Kai hafton mein pehli baar mujhe sukoon mehsoos hua. Koi vaada nahi hua tha. Kisi cheez ko label nahi diya gaya tha. Usne bata diya tha ki woh kis baat mein comfortable hai, aur maine suna tha. Yeh un imaginary answers se zyada important tha jo main har nazar mein dhoondhta raha tha.

Daichi ko jab maine bataya toh woh bench se girte-girte bacha.

“Tune sach mein usse baat ki?”

“Haan.”

“Aur behosh nahi hua?”

“Abhi soch raha hoon, bas tujhe chup karane ke liye.”

Woh hans pada, lekin phir uska chehra thoda narm hua. “Seriously, achha kiya.”

“Kya karne ke liye?”

“Aakhir kuch bolne ke liye, dimaag mein poori film banane ke bajaye.”

Main behas karna chahta tha, lekin woh sahi tha.

Uske baad maine aam conversations se kam darna shuru kiya. Hina dikhti toh hello kehta, uska din kaisa tha poochhta. Kabhi woh friends ki kahani sunati; kabhi jaldi mein hoti aur bas haath hila deti. Maine har shaant din ko setback samajhna chhod diya.

Hum achanak alag na hone wale dost nahi ban gaye. Na hi hum kisi chhupi kismat ke liye bane the. Hum do students the jo ek-doosre ko pehchanne lage the, ek chhoti conversation ke baad doosri.

Filhaal, itna kaafi tha.

Chapter Four A Different Kind of Familiar

Hamari conversation ke baad Seiran Academy nahi badla. Wahi teachers exams ki warnings dete, wahi bell lessons khatam karti, aur Daichi ab bhi lagbhag har baat ko joke bana deta.

Lekin Hina aur mere beech ki jagah badal gayi thi. Ab hum aise dikhawa nahi karte the jaise kabhi baat hi nahi hui. Corridor mein milte toh koi hello keh deta. Kabhi ghar jaane se pehle gate ke paas ek minute baat kar lete.

Ek din uski friend Yui ne use school cafeteria ki taraf bulaya, jahan lunch ki line lagne lagi thi. Hina mudkar jaane lagi, phir mujhe paas khada dekha.

“Hi, Haruto.”

“Hey.”

Yui ne uski taraf aur phir meri taraf aise dekha jaise usne pehle hi poori kahani likh li ho. Hina ne us nazar ko ignore kiya aur poochha ki mere exams kharaab ja rahe hain kya.

“Normal ja rahe hain,” maine kaha. “Matlab tension ho rahi hai.”

Woh hans di. “Haan, yeh toh expected hai.”

Phir woh Yui ke saath chali gayi aur main class mein laut gaya.

Ek waqt tha jab main aise moment ko baar-baar sochta rehta, jab tak woh kuch aisa ban na jaata jo asal mein tha hi nahi. Ab maine use wahi rehne diya jo woh tha: school ke ek aam din ki achhi conversation.

Mere friends mujhe chhedte rahe, obviously. Hina dikhte hi Daichi use announce karna apna farz samajhta. Jab jokes bahut loud hone lage, main use rokne laga. Agar main chahta tha ki Hina mere aas-paas comfortable rahe, toh mujhe dhyaan rakhna tha ki friends use aise show ka centre na bana dein jisme shaamil hone ke liye usne kabhi haan hi nahi ki.

Hina ne notice kiya.

“Ab tum unhe shaant hone ko kehte ho,” ek dopahar usne kaha.

“Woh hamesha sunte nahi.”

“Phir bhi, tum koshish karte ho.”

“Woh idiots hain.”

“Yeh toh obvious hai.”

Hum hans pade, aur pehli baar mujhe moment ke peeche koi chhupa matlab dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi lagi.

Term aage badha toh main future ke baare mein zyada sochne laga: exams, results aur graduation ke baad ke choices. Har koi future ki baat aise karta tha jaise hum sab ek hi darwaaze se saath guzrenge, halaanki hum sab alag-alag jagah pahunchne wale the.

Ek dopahar Hina ne poochha ki school chhodne ko lekar nervous hoon kya.

“Thoda,” maine kaha. “Itne waqt se yahin hoon ki agali jagah imagine nahi hoti.”

“Aadat ho jayegi.”

“Tum kaafi confident lag rahi ho.”

“Nahi hoon. Bas keh rahi hoon taaki tum zyada tension na lo.”

Uska jawab meri umeed se zyada der tak yaad raha. Meherbaan tha, bina dramatic hue; imaandaar tha, bina badle mein kuch maange.

Kabhi Hina khush rehti, kabhi apne mein gum. Maine har badlav ka matlab nikaalna chhod diya. Use aisa mood rakhne ka haq tha jiska mujhse koi lena-dena na ho. Baat obvious honi chahiye thi, lekin use samajhne se duniya zyada badi lagne lagi.

Main ab bhi apne khayalon mein use Little Bird kehta tha. Yeh naam meri zindagi ke ek khaas daur ka nishaan ban gaya tha: woh saal jab school ki aam routine ne mujhe dhyaan dena, imaandari se bolna aur doosre ke choices ke liye jagah chhodna sikhaya.

Woh uski poori kahani nahi thi. Bas uski kahani ka chhota sa hissa tha jo meri kahani se takra gaya tha.

Chapter Five The Last Afternoon

Waqt chalta raha, chahe main chahta tha kuch din aur lamba thehrein.

Term ke end tak Hina se baat karna stage par chadhne jaisa nahi lagta tha. Milte toh hello kehte, kabhi ghar jaane se pehle gate ke paas kuch minute ruk jaate. Hamare beech koi grand confession nahi tha, koi vaada nahi ki future humein kareeb laayega. Bas ek shaant apnayat thi, aur maine use bina zyada maange value karna seekh liya tha.

Ek dopahar main gate ke paas khada tha, jab mere zyadaatar friends ja chuke the. Main kisi khaas insaan ka intezaar nahi kar raha tha. Bas station ki taraf jaane wali patli sadak lene se pehle wahan rukne ki aadat ho gayi thi.

Hina aayi, schoolbag ek kandhe par tha. Woh soch mein doobi lag rahi thi.

“Ab tum poori tarah chup nahi ho jaate,” usne kaha.

“Improvement ho rahi hai.”

“Dheere-dheere.”

“Chalega.”

Woh muskuraayi, phir bag ka strap theek kiya. “Kal main school change kar rahi hoon.”

Ek pal ke liye mujhe laga maine galat suna.

“Kal?”

“Family ka decision hai. Humein doosre area mein shift hona hai.”

Maine aisa sawaal dhoondhne ki koshish ki jo khabar ko kam final bana de, lekin koi bhi cheez badalne wali nahi thi. Purani instinct laut aayi—kuch dramatic kehne ka, use na jaane ko kehne ka, moment ko apni feelings ke baare mein bana dene ka.

Iske bajaye maine poochha, “Tum theek ho is baat se?”

Usne socha. “Mujhe lagta hai. Ajeeb hai. Friends ki yaad aayegi, lekin shayad nayi jagah achhi ho.”

“Haan,” maine kaha. “Umeed hai achhi hogi.”

Students hamare paas se guzarte rahe aur hum chup khade rahe. School ki awaaz bilkul pehle jaisi thi: kadmon ki aahat, door se aati hansi, gate khulne ki khar-khar. Hamare aas-paas kisi cheez ko pata nahi tha ki meri routine ka chhota sa hissa gayab hone wala hai.

“Pata hai,” Hina ne kaha, “shuru mein mujhe tumse baat karna mushkil lagta tha.”

“Fair hai.”

“Lekin jab mera mann nahi hota tha, tumne kabhi baat karne ka pressure nahi diya. Maine yeh notice kiya.”

Mujhe samajh nahi aaya kya kahun, isliye sach bol diya. “Main nervous tha. Lekin tumhe uncomfortable nahi karna chahta tha.”

“Achha laga.”

Un lafzon mein koi romantic confession chhupa nahi tha. Duniya achanak badli nahi thi. Bas ek insaan maan raha tha ki doosre ne kuch sahi karne ki koshish ki thi.

Woh jaane ke liye ek kadam peeche hui.

“Little Bird ko yaad rakhna,” usne halki smile ke saath kaha. “Jab kahani sunao toh kam se kam nickname ko thoda kam embarrassing banana.”

Main hans pada. “Uska promise nahi kar sakta.”

“Toh kahani kharaab mat sunana.”

“Koshish karunga.”

Usne haath hilaya aur sadak par chal padi. Kuch kadam baad ek baar phir mudkar haath hilaya, phir bheed ne use chhupa liya.

Main kuch der gate ke paas ruka raha. Main uske peeche nahi bhaaga. Maine koi promise nahi maanga, aur na hi yeh dikhawa kiya ki perfect lafz mil jaane se goodbye undo ho sakta hai. Uski zindagi aisi direction mein ja rahi thi jo sirf usne nahi chuni thi, aur sabse meherbaan kaam yahi tha ki uske faisle ko bojh banaye bina use jaane doon.

Aakhir main station ki taraf jaane wali patli sadak par chal diya.

Raasta bilkul waisa hi lag raha tha jaisa us dopahar laga tha jab maine use pehli baar notice kiya tha. Wahi uneven footpath, wahi shops, pedon ke beech dhoop ke wahi tukde. Main dheere chalta raha, sochta hua ki koi insaan bina dramatic shuruaat ke tumhari zindagi mein aa sakta hai aur phir bhi nishaan chhod sakta hai.

Maine use jaanne se pehle ek naam de diya tha. Waqt ke saath samjha ki naam ek memory ko sambhaal sakta hai, lekin poore insaan ko apne andar nahi samet sakta.

Hina sirf woh ladki nahi thi jise maine school courtyard mein notice kiya. Woh apne friends, apni pareshaniyon aur apne future wali student thi—aisa future jo meri feelings ke aas-paas nahi ghoomta tha. Main chahta tha naya school uske saath achha ho. Umeed thi use aise log milen jo use hansayein.

Aur main chahta tha ki is saal ko imaandari se yaad rakhun—not ek aisi love story ki tarah jo bas hone se reh gayi, balki bade hone ke baare mein ek chhote chapter ki tarah.

Kuch log rukne ke liye nahi aate. Kabhi woh tumhare raaste se guzarte hain, ek aam din ki shakal badalte hain aur phir aage badh jaate hain.

Agle din subah students ne uwabaki pehni aur hamesha ki tarah homeroom mein chale gaye. Aadat se maine junior students ki taraf dekha, phir khud ko yaad dilaya ki yeh aadat bhi dheere-dheere khatam ho jayegi. School bell baji. Mere friends baat karne lage. Din aage badhta raha.

Pehli baar mujhe samajh aaya ki ending ka matlab memory ko tragedy bana dena zaroori nahi.

Little Bird us aasman ki taraf ud gayi thi jiske peeche main nahi ja sakta tha.

Aur main use achha chaah sakta tha, bina yeh kahe ki woh palat kar aaye.

Mujhe laga tha kahani yahin khatam ho gayi. Mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek saal baad main phir school gate ki taraf dekhunga—not isliye ki past badal gaya tha, balki isliye ki zindagi ne chupke se Hina ko dobara meri nazar mein la diya tha.
