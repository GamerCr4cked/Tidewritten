CHAPTER ONE
Do Saal, Teen Notifications

CHAPTER THREE
11:48 Par Read

Online doston se chat karne ke kuch rules hote hain. Sota ne kabhi likhe nahi the, lekin pretend kar sakta tha ki use pata hain.

Is baar memories us future ka saboot nahi lag rahi thi jo usse chhoot gaya. Woh ek aisi zindagi ka saboot thi jo aage badh chuki thi.

CHAPTER EIGHT
Woh Sawaal Jo Usne Kabhi Nahi Poochha

Autumn dheere-dheere winter mein badal gaya: thande train platforms, dono haathon mein pakdi garam drinks, aur har shop window mein achanak scarves. Group chat assignments ki shikayat aur aise meals ki photos se bhar gaya jo dikhne mein taste se behtar the.

Sota aur Airi ab bhi baat karte. Kabhi sirf do messages, kabhi aadha ghanta kisi film, ajeeb advertisement, ya is baat par ki Yuto ne aakhir group calendar chalana seekha ya nahi. Unki dosti koi secret code nahi thi. Woh bas dosti thi, aur Sota koshish karta ki use kisi aur cheez mein badalne ki maang kiye bina enjoy kare.

Phir bhi, kuch shaamein mushkil hoti thi.

Ek raat laptop mein file dhoondhte hue use purane khat ki photo mili. Shayad fold karne se pehle usne photo li thi, taaki un lafzon ko yaad rakh sake jinhe likhne ki himmat hui thi. Asli kagaz kab ka gayab tha, magar photo mein uski tedhi handwriting, zyada zor se dabaya gaya crease aur woh jumla ab bhi tha jisme likha tha ki Airi ko same feel karna zaroori nahi.

Usne dobara padha. Har lafz tab bhi sach tha, aur ab bhi.

Lekin ek aur sach tha jo usne kabhi nahi likha: Airi ke jawab ke baad usne apne dil mein ek chhoti si jagah khuli chhod di thi. Usne uska intezaar karne ka faisla nahi kiya tha, na dobara confess karne ka plan banaya. Bas usne apne zehan ka darwaaza band nahi kiya, kyunki use band karna aisa lagta tha jaise kahani shuru hone se pehle hi khatam maan lena.

Do saal beet gaye. Woh Airi ke saath hansa, assignment mein madad ki, group outings ki aur use un tareekon se confident hote dekha jinhe woh pasand karta tha. Saath hi usne chhote lamhon ka ek private collection bana liya tha, jinhe tanha hone par yaad karta. Un lamhon ka woh matlab nahi tha jo Sota chupke se chahta tha.

Yeh samajhna dardnaak tha, lekin ismein Airi ki galti nahi thi.

Usne ek khaali document khola:

Maine kabhi doosre chance ke liye nahi poochha. Shayad mujhe darr tha ki tum uncomfortable ho jaogi, aur yeh bhi ki wahi jawab dobara sunna padega. Shayad maine khud se kaha ki agar kaafi der intezaar karunga toh umeed aur qabooliyat mein se kisi ek ko chunna nahi padega.

Thoda ruk kar usne aage likha:

Lekin na poochhne ka matlab yeh nahi ki main umeed nahi kar raha tha. Bas maine woh umeed apne tak rakhi.

Usne document save nahi kiya, na bheja. Yeh Airi ke liye confession nahi tha; yeh ek sach tha jise khud se maan lena zaroori tha.

Agle din Yuto ka call aaya, lunch par aane ke liye. Sota ne haan kaha. Phir Yuto teen minute tak samjhata raha ki naye restaurant ka menu “structurally suspicious” kyun hai. Sota itna hansa ki phone kaan se door karna pada.

Zindagi use aam kaam deti rahi. Woh samajhne laga ki aage badhna shayad aise hi hota hai—ek bade faisle se nahi, balki hazaar chhote lamhon se jinmein insaan us din ko jeena chunta hai jo asal mein uske paas hai.

CHAPTER NINE
Please Galat Mat Samajhna

Message Wednesday shaam aaya, group chat mein Mei ke jale hue pancakes ki photo aur Yuto ke reminder ke beech ki agle outing ki date ab tak decide nahi hui.

Sota abhi ghar aaya tha. Bag desk ke paas tha, shoes ab bhi pehne hue the, aur table par aadha glass paani tha. Usne bina soche Instagram khola.

Airi Tanaka ne aapko message bheja.

Hey, kya main ek baat keh sakti hoon? Umeed hai tum galat nahi samjhoge.

Sota baith gaya. Bilkul. Kya hua?

Typing ke teen dots aaye, gayab hue, phir aaye. Sota unhe zaroorat se zyada der tak dekhta raha.

Phir Airi ka message aaya:

Main kuch waqt se is baare mein soch rahi hoon. Bas chahti hoon ki hum dono ek hi baat samjhein. Mujhe tumse baat karna aur sabke saath hang out karna sach mein pasand hai, lekin main nahi chahti ki tum kisi baat ko galat samjho. Hum bas dost hain, theek hai? Main tumhe galat umeed nahi dena chahti.

Sota ne ek baar padha, phir dobara. Message mein koi berahmi nahi thi, na ilzaam, na mazaak, na yeh kehna ki usne kuch galat kiya. Airi ehtiyaat se baat kar rahi thi. Shayad kisi ne phir mazaak kiya tha, shayad usne Sota ke behaviour mein kuch notice kiya, ya shayad woh dosti complicated hone se pehle saaf karna chahti thi. Sota nahi jaanta tha, aur woh uski wajah ka faisla nahi kar sakta tha.

Use bas yeh pata tha ki lafz us tak kaise pahunche.

Do saal pehle Airi ne kaha tha ki woh uski feelings ka jawab nahi de sakti. Sota ne qabool kiya tha. Usne dobara nahi poochha, use manane ki koshish nahi ki, aur alag jawab ki demand nahi ki. Dheere-dheere woh dost bane. Sota ne khud se kaha tha ki dosti kaafi hai, kyunki woh Airi ki maujoodgi ki qadr karta tha aur use uncomfortable nahi karna chahta tha.

Use pata hi nahi chala ki uske andar ek chhoti, bina kahi possibility ab tak khuli thi.

Ab aisa lag raha tha jaise us sawaal ke liye doosri baar reject kiya gaya ho jo usne kabhi poochha hi nahi.

Usne phone rakha, phir uthaya. Woh aisa jawab dena chahta tha jo shaant ho, Airi ko guilt se aur khud ko embarrassment se bachaye. Har sentence ya toh zyada thanda lagta ya zyada sachcha.

Aakhir usne likha:

Samajh gaya. Fikr mat karo, tumne kuch galat nahi kiya. Mujhe pata hai hum dost hain, aur main kabhi nahi chahta tha ki tum par pressure ho. Mere saath honest hone ke liye shukriya.

Usne dhyaan se padh kar send kar diya.

Ek minute baad Airi ka jawab aaya: Samajhne ke liye thank you. Main hamari dosti ki sach mein qadr karti hoon.

Sota “dosti” lafz ko dekhta raha, jab tak woh lafz rehna band hua aur ek aisi boundary ban gaya jo ab use saaf dikh rahi thi.

Phone phir vibrate hua. Group chat active thi. Haru ne aise sandwich ki photo bheji thi jo beech se dhans gaya tha. Mei bread ko blame kar rahi thi. Yuto scientific investigation propose kar raha tha.

Sota lagbhag hans diya. Aam duniya ab bhi wahan thi, apni chhoti bewakoofiyon ke saath. Lekin uske andar kuch khamosh ho gaya tha—sukoon wali khamoshi nahi, balki us possibility ke khatam hone ki khamoshi jise usne kabhi kisi ke saamne maana hi nahi tha.

Woh Airi se naraaz nahi tha. Airi honest thi. Sota ne uske jawab ki izzat karne ka waada kiya tha aur ab bhi us par qayam tha. Dard is baat ka tha ki woh ek aise future ka gham mana raha tha jo kabhi uska tha hi nahi.

Usne screen band ki aur andhere kamre mein baitha raha. Pehli baar usne poori disappointment ko mehsoos hone diya, bina foran use samjhane ki koshish kiye. Usne umeed ki thi, chahe dikhaya na ho. Use parwah thi, chahe woh ehtiyaat se pesh aaya. Ab use unke beech ki jagah ko aisa sawaal samajhna chhodna tha jiska jawab kabhi badal sakta tha.

Bahar buildings ke paar se train guzri, uski khidkiyan andhere mein chhote, door ke kamron ki tarah sarakti gayin.

CHAPTER TEN
Woh Chehra Jo Main Kabhi Nahi Bhoola

Agli subah Sota kisi naye insaan ki tarah nahi utha.

Woh ab bhi bistar se uthne se pehle phone check karta, chaabiyan kahan rakhi bhoolta, aur shopping list ke bajaye lunch ke baare mein sochte hue class ke liye galat notebook khareed leta. Duniya us umeed ke khamosh end ka jashn manane ke liye nahi ruki thi jise usne kabhi zubaan nahi di.

Kuch din tak Airi ke messages aate toh woh jawab deta, lekin conversation shuru karne ke bahaane dhoondhna band kar diya. Use saza dene ya yeh sochne par majboor karne ke liye nahi ki usne kya kiya; use bas apne khayal sunne ke liye jagah chahiye thi, bina har notification ko chhota test banaye.

Ek shaam station se chalte hue usne Yuto ko sach bataya.

“Uska message aaya tha,” Sota ne kaha. “Woh chahti thi mujhe pata rahe ki hum bas dost hain.”

Yuto ka chehra narm pad gaya. “Tumne bataya ki tum zyada ki umeed kar rahe the?”

“Nahi. Pehli baar ke baad maine kabhi nahi poochha.”

“Phir yeh doosra rejection kyun lag raha hai?”

Sota ne traffic lights badalte dekhe. “Kyunki shayad maine apne dimaag mein ek chance zinda rakha. Uske saath kuch kiya nahi. Bas… use poori tarah jaane nahi diya.”

Yuto kuch der chup raha. “Yeh dardnaak lagta hai.”

“Hai.”

“Kya tum usse nafrat karte ho?”

“Nahi,” Sota ne turant kaha. “Usne mujhe kuch promise nahi kiya. Pehli baar bhi saaf thi. Shayad main apni feelings se utna aage nahi badha tha jitna samajhta tha.”

Yuto ne sir hilaya. Usne koi clever solution nahi diya, aur pehli baar udaasi ko joke mein badalne ki koshish nahi ki.

“Aaj hi dosti band karna zaroori nahi,” usne kaha. “Bas dosti se woh banne ki umeed karna band karna hai jo woh hai hi nahi.”

Sota woh baat ghar le gaya.

Ek hafte baad woh akela purane school lauta. Alumni afternoon ke baad classroom ab kisi shrine se zyada ek aam jagah lagta tha. Woh use ek baar aur dekhna chahta tha—jawab dhoondhne nahi, balki us version ko goodbye kehne jo samajhta tha ki himmat ka matlab kisi ko apni feelings bata dena hai.

Room khaali tha, desks dobara sajaye gaye the. Dhoop second row par thi, jahan Airi kabhi notebook khol kar baithti thi. Sota darwaaze ke paas khada raha, phir peeche ja kar apni purani seat par baith gaya.

Use chaar baar fold kiya khat yaad aaya, Airi ka soch-samajh kar diya jawab, aur woh chhote pal jo uske liye keemti bane: udhaar ke notes, room ke paar ek smile, science project mein ungliyon ka lagbhag chhoo jaana. Use baad ke saal bhi yaad aaye—jokes, group photos, raat ki proofreading, penguin plush aur woh messages jinhone unhe aam dost bana diya tha.

Woh yaadein sach thi. Unka maayne romantic hona zaroori nahi tha.

Woh samajhta tha ki aage badhne ka matlab chehra dhundhla pad jaana hai, ya yaad itni halki ho jaana ki dard na ho. Shayad baat kabhi woh thi hi nahi. Airi uske liye ek yaad reh sakti thi. Woh kisi ko sachchai se chahne ki uski pehli koshish ka hissa thi, aur is sabaq ka bhi ki feelings sincere ho sakti hain, chahe saamne se wapas na milen.

Lekin use yaad rakhna, uska intezaar karna nahi tha.

Usne phone nikala. Group chat agle outing ka plan bana rahi thi. Mei bookstore jaana chahti thi, Haru sasta khana, aur Yuto ne aisi jagah suggest ki jo na bookstore thi na sasti. Airi ne door dekhte rabbit ka sticker bheja.

Sota muskura diya. Jab taiyaar ho, woh sabke saath ja sakta tha. Woh chhupe matlab dhoondhe bina Airi ke saath achha bartaav kar sakta tha. Zaroorat ho toh thoda distance le sakta tha, aur jab dosti phir se sachchi lage toh laut sakta tha. Aage badhne ke liye kisi ko villain banana ya past ko be-maayne kehna zaroori nahi.

Usne group chat mein likha:

Pehle bookstore. Phir khana. Aur agar kisi ne tax document jaisa menu wale restaurant ka naam liya, toh main nikal jaunga.

Yuto ne turant jawab diya: Woh Haru ke liye keh raha hai.

Haru ne menu ki photo bhej di.

Mei ne likha: Main tum sabko mute kar rahi hoon.

Sota hans pada. Apni hansi ko itna normal sun kar woh khud hairaan hua.

Classroom se nikalne se pehle usne khidki ki taraf aakhri baar dekha. Ab use kamre se alag ending nahi chahiye thi. Khat likhne wala ladka bahadur tha. Ab yahan baitha jawaan bhi bahaduri dikha sakta tha: jawab ko waada banane ki zid kiye bina qabool karna, aur baaki zindagi ko aage chalne dena.

Bahar dopahar roshan thi. School gate khula tha aur students shor karte groups mein courtyard paar kar rahe the. Sota bina peeche dekhe gate se nikal gaya.

Woh Airi Tanaka ko yaad rakhega. Shayad bahut waqt tak. Shayad hamesha.

Lekin jis chehre ko woh kabhi nahi bhoola, zaroori nahi ki usi ka intezaar zindagi bhar karta rahe.

Kuch feelings hamare saath rehti hain. Kuch umeedon ko chhodna padta hai. Kabhi bade hone ka matlab us insaan ko bhoolna nahi hota jisne zindagi badli, balki use yaad rehne dena hota hai—bina usse apna mustaqbil banane ki maang kiye.

Woh feeling be-maayne nahi thi.

Bas ab khatam ho chuki thi.
