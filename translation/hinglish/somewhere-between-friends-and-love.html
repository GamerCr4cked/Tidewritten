Chapter One The Empty Seat

Akito Mori ke paas college survive karne ka ek fixed system tha: jaldi pahunchna, peeche ki taraf seat chunna, earphones laga lena, aur bina zaroorat kisi se baat na karna. Atharah saal se yahi tareeka kaam kar raha tha. Phir Seihoku College ke orientation day par ek ladki late aayi. Uske iced coffee ka aadha hissa gir chuka tha, aur chehre par aisa expression tha jaise subah ke saath uski pehle hi behas ho chuki ho. Auditorium ki har seat bhari hui thi—sirf Akito ke bagal wali seat khaali thi. “Yeh seat khaali hai?” usne dheere se poochha. Akito ne sir hila diya. “Great. Agar main so jaun, toh important part se pehle jaga dena.” “Tumhe pata hai important part kya hai?” “Nahi. Isi liye toh tumhari zaroorat hai.” Uska naam Mio Tachibana tha. Dean ki speech ke beech woh dheere-dheere comments karti rahi, auditorium ke kharaab air-conditioning par serious announcement diya, aur kisi tarah do ghante ke orientation ko Akito ke liye yaad rehne layak bana diya. Program khatam hua toh Akito ne earphones nikaalne ke liye haath badhaya. “Main lunch karne ja rahi hoon,” Mio ne kaha. “Chaaho toh tum bhi aa sakte ho.” Uska usual jawab no hota. Room unpack karna tha, schedule set karna tha. Woh akele khaana khane mein bilkul comfortable tha. Lekin uske munh se nikal gaya, “Theek hai.” Mio aise muskuraayi jaise use pehle se pata tha ki woh haan kahega. Cafeteria shor se bhara tha, curry bas theek-thaak thi, aur Mio ne dono ki taraf se baat kar li. Usne poochha Akito kahan se aaya hai, kya padh raha hai, aur kya woh hamesha aisa dikhta hai jaise kisi bhi waqt room se nikalne wala ho. “Main aisa nahi dikhta.” “Bilkul aise hi dikhte ho.” Akito apni hansi rok nahi paaya. Use abhi pata nahi tha, lekin jab unke beech sab kuch complicated hone lagega, tab use isi hansi ki chhoti si yaad baar-baar aayegi.

Chapter Two A Habit Called Mio

Mio agle din bhi aayi. Aur uske agle din bhi. Pehla hafta khatam hote-hote Akito ke bagal wali seat unofficially uski ho chuki thi. Agar Akito pehle aata, toh bagal wali chair par apna bag rakh deta. Agar Mio pehle aati, toh use aise haath hila kar bulati jaise apne ghar mein table reserve kar rahi ho. Unki dosti baar-baar milne se badhne lagi. Lunch roz ka ritual ban gaya. Woh saath padhte, lecturers ki complaints karte, aur library se wapas aate waqt lamba raasta lete, kyunki Mio ka kehna tha ki garden ke paas wali vending machines mein drinks zyada achhe milte hain. Woh machine ke saare drinks try karti. Akito black coffee peeta. “Tum har baar wahi order karte ho,” Mio ne kaha. “Kyunki mujhe pata hai mujhe woh pasand hai.” “Tumhe kaise pata ki baaki pasand nahi aayenge?” “Meri aankhein hain.” “Professor Mori, taste aise kaam nahi karta.” Raat ko messages aane lage: uske barbaad instant noodles ki photo, assignment ka sawaal, ya aadhi raat ko bina kisi explanation ke bheja gaya meme. Akito bina soche apna phone check karne laga. Uske roommate Kenta ne notice kar liya. “Aajkal tum bahut available rehte ho.” “Main messages ka reply deta hoon.” “Pehle tum messages ko tax office ke letters jaisa treat karte the.” Akito ne uski taraf pillow phenka. Woh ise aadat kehta tha. Yeh ek convenient word tha. Aadat hone par use yeh sochna nahi padta tha ki Mio ki hansi uska bura din kaise achha kar deti hai, ya uske na hone par shaant cafeteria itna khaali kyun lagta hai. Use abhi samajh nahi aaya tha ki ek routine dheere-dheere tumhari zindagi mein kisi insaan ke liye bani jagah ban sakta hai.

Chapter Three Best Friends, Apparently

Pehla semester khatam hote-hote Seihoku mein sab Akito aur Mio ko ek saath dekhne ke aadat ho chuke the. Woh dating nahi kar rahe the. Unhone dating ke baare mein baat bhi nahi ki thi. Lekin lecture hall ke doosre kone se ek nazar milakar poori conversation samajhne ki skill unhone zaroor develop kar li thi. Mio apna student card bhool jaati toh Akito gate par uska wait karta. Akito report finish karne ke liye lunch skip karta toh Mio extra rice ball lekar aa jaati, aur uske chehre ka expression dekhkar lagta ki behas ki koi gunjaish nahi. “Tum sirf coffee pe zinda nahi reh sakte,” usne kaha. “Main try toh kar sakta hoon.” “Fail bhi kar sakte ho.” Unke friends obvious baat ko lekar itne cautious nahi the. Kenta ne Akito se poochha ki woh Mio se shaadi karne ka plan bana raha hai ya agle chaar saal tak bas uske bagal wali seat reserve rakhega. Mio ki friend Yuna aur bhi buri thi. “Tum dono ya toh secretly relationship mein ho, ya phir duniya ke sabse slow log ho.” “Hum friends hain,” Mio ne jawab diya. Usne baat halke andaaz mein kahi. Akito ne notice kiya ki kehne ke baad usne ek second ke liye uski taraf dekha. Us raat Akito chhat ko dekhte hue us pal ke baare mein sochta raha. Woh aisi details notice karne laga tha jinhe yaad rakhne ki koi wajah nahi thi: focus karte waqt Mio ka apne kaan ke peeche baal karna, sach mein pareshan hone par uska chup ho jaana, aur hamesha poochhna ki usne khaana khaya ya nahi. Samajh dheere-dheere aayi—aur phir ek hi pal mein sab clear ho gaya. Use Mio pasand thi. Sirf ek funny classmate ki tarah nahi. Sirf roz ke routine ki tarah nahi. Use woh insaan pasand thi jo uske bagal wali khaali seat par baithi thi aur uski carefully organised zindagi ko pehle se zyada zinda bana gayi thi. Mushkil yeh thi ki unki dosti uske liye bahut keemti ho chuki thi. Apni feelings ko naam dekar woh sab kuch badal dene se darta tha.

Chapter Four The Space Between Us

Spring aayi toh campus ke raaste cherry blossoms se bhar gaye. Lecture ke baad Mio, Akito ko bahar kheench laayi. Usne declare kiya ki jo insaan har dhoop wale din ko ghar ke andar bitata hai, uski complaint honi chahiye. Woh ek phoolon se bhare ped ke neeche baithe aur convenience store se li hui sweets ka packet share kiya. “Ek baat poochhun?” Mio ne kaha. “Depend karta hai.” “College ke baad kya hoga, kabhi sochte ho?” “Job. Rent. Shayad aur coffee.” “Main serious hoon.” “Main bhi. Coffee mehengi hoti hai.” Mio ne uske kandhe ko halka sa dhakka diya, lekin zyada der tak hansi nahi. Usne bataya ki use darr lagta hai ki graduation ke baad sab apni-apni jagah chale jayenge, aur jo dosti shared schedules par bani hai woh schedules badalte hi khatam ho sakti hai. Akito use batana chahta tha ki woh use khona nahi chahta. Lafz uthe, gale tak aaye, aur wahin ruk gaye. “Log koshish karein toh contact bana reh sakta hai,” usne aakhir kaha. Mio ne uski taraf dekha. “Tum karoge?” “Haan.” Yeh confession nahi tha. Uski feelings samjhane ke liye kaafi bhi nahi tha. Lekin Mio ka expression naram pad gaya aur woh ped ke sahare peeche tik gayi. “Achha. Main bhi karungi.” Woh pal kai hafton tak uske saath raha. Usne ajeeb bane bina zyada care dikhane ki koshish ki. Jab dono ki classes saath khatam hoti, toh woh use station tak chhodta. Use uska pasandida drink yaad rehta. Kabhi-kabhi woh pehle message bhejta, chahe kehne ke liye kuch khaas smart baat na ho. Mio ne badlaav notice kiye. Ek thandi shaam jab Akito ne use garam canned drink diya, toh usne pehle drink ko aur phir Akito ko dekha. “Aajkal tum thoughtful ho rahe ho.” “Afwaah mat phailana.” “Ab toh der ho gayi. Main sabko bataungi.” Woh muskuraayi, lekin uski aankhon mein jaise koi sawaal bhi tha. Pehli baar Akito ne socha ki kya Mio bhi uske munh se kuch sunne ka intezaar kar rahi thi.

Chapter Five Say It Before the Train

Pehla saal halki garmiyon ki baarish ke beech khatam hua. Exams khatam ho gaye, dormitory hall mein suitcases dikhne lage, aur students ghar jaane ke plans banane lage. Mio agle subah ja rahi thi. Uska parivaar kai prefectures door rehta tha, aur chhuttiyon mein woh hafton tak alag rehne wale the. Packing ke baad dono station ke bahar mile. Baarish ab halki phuhaar ban gayi thi. Mio ke paas transparent umbrella tha; Akito ne Kenta se umbrella udhaar liya tha. “Tum aise lag rahe ho jaise speech dene wale ho,” Mio ne kaha. “Mujhe kaha gaya hai ki mujhe zyada baat karni chahiye.” “Kisne?” “Sabne.” “Samajhdaar log hain.” Akito hansa, phir chup ho gaya. Platform se train ka announcement sunayi diya. “Mio, tum mujhe pasand ho.” Lafz khoobsurat nahi the. Woh jaldi se nikal aaye, jaise use darr ho ki uski himmat khatam ho jayegi. Mio ki muskaan ruk gayi—dukhi hone ki wajah se nahi, balki isliye ki woh samajhne ki koshish kar rahi thi ki usne sahi suna hai ya nahi. “Tum mujhe pasand ho,” usne phir kaha. “Ek dost se zyada. Tumhe abhi jawab dene ki zaroorat nahi. Bas main nahi chahta tha ki tumhe bataye bina tum chali jao.” Kuch seconds tak umbrella par baarish ki halki awaaz aati rahi. Phir Mio ne nazar neeche ki, aur itni dheere se muskuraayi ki Akito lagbhag miss kar deta. “Akito, tumne sach mein bahut der laga di.” Uska dil ek pal ko atak gaya. “Kya yeh buri baat hai?” “Nahi.” Usne upar dekha. “Yeh bahut achhi baat hai.” Mio ne bataya ki woh bhi use pasand karti thi. Use pehle kehne se darr lagta tha—kahin unki dosti mein sirf woh hi kuch aur imagine toh nahi kar rahi thi. Train aa gayi. Woh achanak bilkul alag log nahi ban gaye. Ab bhi awkward the, ab bhi ek-doosre ko chhedte the, aur ab bhi nahi jaante the ki relationship shuru kaise kiya jaata hai. Lekin jab Mio train mein chadhi, toh darwaze par mudkar boli. “Aaj raat phone karna.” “Karunga.” “Aur kal bhi.” “Theek hai.” “Aur yeh mat kehna ki bhool gaye.” “Nahi kahunga.” Darwaze band ho gaye. Train Mio ko le gayi, aur Akito platform par khada raha—sleeves par baarish aur chehre par aisi muskaan jise woh chhupa nahi pa raha tha.

Chapter Six Phir Milenge

Unka relationship bina kisi announcement ke shuru hua. Na koi dramatic post, na cafeteria mein declaration. Unke friends ko tab pata chala jab summer break ke baad Mio wapas aayi, seedha Akito ke bagal wali seat par baithi, aur table ke neeche uska haath pakad liya—jaise yeh duniya ki sabse natural baat ho. Kenta ki chai lagbhag gale mein atak gayi. “Tum dono itne time se dating kar rahe the?” “Poore time se nahi,” Akito ne kaha. Mio ne sar tedha kiya. “Yeh depend karta hai tum counting kab se shuru karte ho.” Unke din pehle se zyada warm ho gaye, lekin perfect nahi. Music, study schedules aur Akito ki coffee ko proper drink maana ja sakta hai ya nahi—inn sab par ab bhi behas hoti thi. Unhone seekha ki har misunderstanding ko competition banane ke bajaye sorry kaise bolte hain. Ek dopahar baarish ne unhe cafeteria mein rok liya. Mio khidki par behte paani ko dekh rahi thi. “Bahar chalna hai?” usne poochha. “Baarish ho rahi hai.” “Wah, kya observation hai.” Paanch minute baad dono courtyard mein khule aasman ke neeche hans rahe the. Mio ne baahen phaila kar ek chakkar lagaya, aur Akito use us pyaar ke saath dekhta raha jise woh aakhirkaar chhupana band kar chuka tha. Mio uske paas lauti. Uske baalon par baarish thi aur aankhon mein hansi. “Us pehle din mere saath lunch par aane ka ab bhi afsos nahi hai?” “Haan,” usne kaha. “Bilkul nahi.” Mio ne apna maatha halka sa uske maathay se laga diya. Baarish unke aas-paas girti rahi aur campus dhundhla hokar chaandi jaisa dikhne laga. Pehla saal khatam hone par garmiyan unhe phir alag shehron mein le jaayengi. Dono jaante the ki mushkil hogi. Yeh bhi jaante the ki woh koshish karna chahte hain. Station par Mio ne apni train aate dekhkar haath hilaaya. “Jaldi milte hain,” usne kaha. Akito muskuraaya. “Phir milenge.” Woh dobara milenge. Abhi dono mein se kisi ko nahi pata tha ki aage chal kar in lafzon ka kitna matlab hoga—ya doori ke chalte aam si imaandari bhi kitni mushkil ho sakti hai.
