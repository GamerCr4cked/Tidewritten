Chapter One The Bus Stop

Arjun Sato ki subah minute ke hisaab se chalti thi. Uska alarm 7:15 par bajta. Woh 7:16 par uthta, 7:30 tak taiyaar hota, 7:45 par ghar se nikalta aur 8:05 tak bus stop pahunch jaata. Bus 8:12 par aati. TechNova Solutions mein uski desk saatvin manzil par thi, aur use nau baje tak wahan hona hota. Dedh saal se lagbhag kuch bhi nahi badla tha. Arjun kisi dramatic tareeke se dukhi nahi tha. Uske paas stable job, chhota sa apartment aur itne paise the ki har bill dekhkar tension na leni pade. Bas woh apne din aise guzaarta tha jaise kisi checklist ke tasks poore kar raha ho. Zaroorat padne par baat karta, baaki waqt headphones laga kar rakhta. Ek bheed-bhari subah bus aate hi kisi ne use dhakka dekar paar kiya. Uska pair footpath ke kinaare fisla. Woh sadak par girta, usse pehle kisi ne uska baazu pakadkar use wapas footpath par kheench liya. Use pakadne wali ladki tez saans le rahi thi. Uska bag ek kandhe se phisal gaya tha aur uski aankhon mein shock saaf dikh raha tha. “Tum theek ho?” Arjun ne apni hatheli par lagi kharoch dekhi, phir uski taraf. “Main theek hoon. Thank you.” Woh bus ki taraf badh gaya. Ladki wahin khadi reh gayi, hairaan ki koi uski help ke baad itni jaldi headphones laga kar aage kaise badh sakta hai. Uska naam Nisha Fujimoto tha, aur TechNova mein uska pehla din tha. Dono ek hi bus mein chadhe, halaanki Nisha usse kuch rows peeche baithi. Office pahunchkar use pata chala ki bus stop wala shaant ladka uski hi team mein kaam karta hai. Unke colleague Sahil ne introduction karaya. “Arjun, yeh Nisha hai. Nisha, yeh Arjun. Kaam mein achha hai, small talk mein disaster.” “Main sun sakta hoon,” Arjun ne kaha. “Toh introduction successful hai.” Nisha muskura di. Arjun ne halka sa sir hilaya aur screen par laut gaya. Nisha ne use girne se bachaya tha. Usne thank you kaha tha. Uske hisaab se baat khatam. Lekin Nisha sochne lagi thi ki aakhir kaisa insaan hota hai jo kisi ki madad ko itni doori se accept karta hai.

Chapter Two The Lunchbox

Nisha ka pehla hafta kaafi busy tha. Usne software seekha, office ka layout yaad kiya aur samajhne ki koshish ki ki printer ko hamesha tabhi paper jam kyun hota hai jab sabse zyada jaldi ho. Usne yeh bhi notice kiya ki Arjun lunch akele karta tha. Pehle use laga ki shayad use akele rehna pasand hai. Phir usne dekha ki woh aksar lunch ke waqt bhi kaam karta aur desk par sirf coffee lekar laut aata. Ek dopahar usne Arjun ke keyboard ke paas ek chhota lunchbox rakh diya. “Tum khana khana bhool gaye,” usne kaha. “Main bhoola nahi.” “Toh jaan-bujhkar nahi khaya. Yeh toh aur bhi bura hai.” Usne bataya ki uski maa ne zaroorat se zyada khana pack kar diya tha. Bahana tha, aur dono jaante the. Arjun lunchbox ko aise dekh raha tha jaise kisi important document ka review karna ho. “Thank you,” usne kaha. “Bas dabba wapas kar dena. Yeh mera favourite hai.” Agle din lunchbox dhula hua, achhe se sukha hua laut aaya. Dhakkan ke neeche ek chhota note tha: *Thank you. Khana achha tha.* Nisha ne note sambhaal kar rakh liya. Uske baad unki routine mein ek-ek minute karke badlav aane laga. Nisha project ke baare mein sawaal karti, Arjun kaam ke jawab deta. Woh poochhti ki chai piyega? Woh haan kehta. Kabhi-kabhi woh lunch ke waqt paas baith kar apni book ya train mein dekhi ajeeb cheezon ke baare mein batati. Arjun apni personal life ke baare mein kam hi batata tha, lekin dhyaan se sunta tha. Nisha ne ek important presentation ka zikr kiya toh use date yaad rahi, aur baad mein usne poochha ki sab kaisa gaya. “Tumhe yaad tha?” Nisha ne poochha. “Tumne bataya tha.” “Zyadaatar log bhool jaate hain.” “Mujhe samajh nahi aata kyun bhoolun.” Yeh Arjun ki taraf se compliment ke sabse kareeb cheez thi. Nisha apni desk par lauti toh uske chehre par zaroorat se zyada badi smile thi.

Chapter Three Rain, Cake, and Small Changes

Nisha ne Arjun ko extrovert banane ki koshish nahi ki. Woh bas use zindagi ke un chhote hisson mein shaamil hone ka mauka deti rahi jinhe woh nazarandaaz karta tha. Jab baarish ne unhe office ke bahar rok liya, usne apna umbrella share kiya. Station ke paas naya café khula toh poochha ki kya woh coffee judge karne mein uski help karega. Office party mein Arjun khidki ke paas khada tha; Nisha uske bhaagne se pehle uske liye plate le aayi. Badle mein Arjun bhi use notice karne laga. Nisha sabke saath friendly thi, lekin woh har waqt khush rehne ka natak nahi karti thi. Mushkil dinon mein woh shaant ho jaati aur apni pareshani ko dhyaan se suljhati. Woh ambitious thi, kabhi-kabhi ziddi bhi, aur galti hone par maan leti thi. Uski kindness koi performance nahi thi; woh roz kiya jaane wala ek choice tha. Ek shaam Arjun ko apni desk par cake ka ek slice mila. Na note, na announcement. Bas cake. Usne Sahil se poochha kisne rakha. Sahil ne monitor se nazar uthayi. “Tum hi batao. Tumhe khana laakar kaun deta rehta hai?” Arjun ne office ke doosre kone mein dekha. Nisha ek colleague se baat kar rahi thi aur screen par kuch dekhkar hans rahi thi. Pehli baar usne socha ki Nisha kaisi hoti hogi jab woh use apni duniya mein shaamil karne ki koshish nahi kar rahi hoti. Woh do logon ke liye chai banane laga. Usne uske weekends ke baare mein poochhna shuru kiya. Office se akele nikalne ke bajaye uske saath station tak chalne laga. Uska timetable badal raha tha—not isliye ki Nisha ne demand ki, balki isliye ki woh khud uske liye jagah banana chahta tha. Phir Kaito naam ka ek naya employee team mein aaya. Woh confident, funny aur Nisha ki help karne ke liye hamesha ready rehta. Arjun ne dono ko project par hans kar baat karte dekha aur use ek ajeeb si bechaini hui jiska matlab woh samajh nahi paaya. Sahil ne notice kar liya. “Jealous ho.” “Nahi.” “Das minute se ek hi spreadsheet ko ghoor rahe ho.” Arjun ne screen dekhi. Usne ek bhi cell nahi padha tha. Woh samajhne laga tha ki jab koi insaan tumhare liye important ho jaata hai, toh duniya pehle jaisi predictable nahi rehti.

Chapter Four The Question He Avoided

Kuch din tak Arjun phir se door-door rehne laga. Usne khud ko samjhaya ki woh sensible ban raha hai. Nisha ki apni zindagi thi, aur use uske attention ki umeed rakhne ka haq nahi tha. Apne purane routine mein laut jaana, kuch chahne se darne ki baat maan lene se aasaan tha. Nisha ne badlav notice kar liya. “Mainne kuch kiya?” ek shaam lift mein usne poochha. “Nahi.” “Toh mujhse bach kyun rahe ho?” “Kaam zyada hai.” “Tum hamesha busy hote ho. Pehle kam se kam meri baat sunte waqt meri taraf dekhte toh the.” Lift ground floor par pahunch gayi. Dono turant bahar nahi nikle. Arjun koi aur bahana bana sakta tha. Is baar usne maan liya ki woh apni feelings ko lekar confused tha aur samajh nahi paa raha tha ki unka kya kare. Nisha ka chehra narm hua, lekin usne uski taraf se conversation ko aasaan nahi banaya. “Aaj raat hi sab decide karna zaroori nahi,” usne kaha. “Lekin jis baat ke baare mein tumne mujhe bataya hi nahi, uski saza mujhe mat do.” Arjun ne maafi maangi. Dono saath station ki taraf chale. Crossing par Nisha ne apne purane difficult relationship ke baare mein bataya aur kaise us experience ne use un logon se cautious kar diya tha jo sach bolne ke bajaye chup ho jaate hain. “Mujhe nahi chahiye ki tum har waqt cheerful raho,” usne kaha. “Bas mujhe pata hona chahiye ki main tumhari zindagi mein kahan khadi hoon.” Alag hone ke baad bhi Arjun is baat ke baare mein sochta raha. Agli subah usne pehle message bheja. *Aaj mere saath lunch karogi? Office ke bahar kahin?* Uska reply lagbhag turant aa gaya. *Mujhe laga tha tum kabhi poochhoge hi nahi.*

Chapter Five A Better Kind of Ordinary

Unki lunch dates simple thi. Station ke paas ek chhota restaurant. Nadi ke kinaare walk. Ek bookstore jahan Nisha ek ghante tak books dekhti aur Arjun dikhata aise tha jaise use bhi maza nahi aa raha. Unhone andaza lagane ke bajaye poochhna seekha. Arjun ko shaanti chahiye hoti toh woh bata deta. Nisha hurt hoti toh wajah samjhati, yeh umeed nahi karti ki Arjun khud samajh jayega. Unke differences gayab nahi hue, lekin unhe samajhna aasaan hone laga. Ek shaam Nisha ne apni friend Sneha ke baare mein bataya, jisne use Arjun ki khamoshi ko beparwahi na samajhne ki warning di thi. “Usne kaha tum mujhe aise dekhte ho jaise meri har harkat yaad karne ki koshish kar rahe ho.” Arjun ke haath se chopsticks lagbhag gir hi gaye. “Yeh exaggeration hai.” “Achha?” Usne jhooth bolne ka socha. Phir sir hila diya. Nisha muskuraayi. Jeetne wali smile nahi—raahat wali. Kuch hafton baad unka pehla serious argument hua. Arjun ne kaam ki wajah se last minute par plan cancel kar diya aur Nisha ko tab bataya jab woh pehle hi pahunch chuki thi. Nisha naraaz thi—not isliye ki kaam important tha, balki isliye ki usne bina explanation ke use intezaar karne diya. Arjun ne apna bachav kiye bina maafi maangi. Dono ne busy dinon ko handle karne ke baare mein baat ki, aur agali baar jab kaam ka pressure badha, Arjun ne apni baat par amal kiya. Tab Nisha ko uske badlav par bharosa hone laga. Bade-bade promises ki wajah se nahi, balki isliye ki Arjun sach mein present rehna seekh raha tha. Ek baarish bhari shaam woh station ke shed ke neeche khade the. Nisha ne aasman ki taraf dekha. “Yaad hai hum pehli baar kab mile the?” “Tumne mujhe girne se bachaya tha.” “Aur phir tum aise chale gaye jaise maine tumhe receipt pakda di ho.” “Mainne thank you kaha tha.” “Bilkul politely. Jaise customer service email.” Arjun hans pada. Nisha khush dikh rahi thi, phir achanak thodi sharma gayi. “Achha hua mainne tumhe pakad liya,” usne kaha. “Mujhe bhi.” Arjun ne dheere se uska haath pakadne ke liye badhaya, itni dheere ki woh mana kar sakti thi. Nisha ne apna haath uske haath mein rakh diya. Baarish unke aas-paas girti rahi. Dono mein se kisi ko jaldi nahi thi.

Chapter Six Alive

Kuch mahine baad bhi Arjun ki routine 7:15 se shuru hoti thi. Woh ab bhi lagbhag 8:05 par bus stop pahunchta aur nau baje se pehle office aa jaata. Lekin ab schedule uski zindagi ke ird-gird bani deewar nahi lagti thi. Kuch subah woh Nisha ke saath chalta. Kuch shaam dono milkar khana banate. Arjun ghar lautte waqt photos lene laga tha—dukaan ki khidkiyon par padti roshni, baarish ke baad aasman ke rang. Nisha use photos sambhaal kar rakhne ke liye kehti, chahe woh unhe ordinary kehkar taal de. “Yeh tumhari ordinary zindagi hai,” woh kehti. “Isi liye sambhaalne layak hai.” Unka relationship sabse kam dramatic tareeke se official hua: Nisha ne poochha ki kya woh ek friend ke dinner par Arjun ko apna boyfriend kehkar introduce kar sakti hai. Arjun ne haan kaha. Phir dono das minute tak behas karte rahe ki kaunsi train leni chahiye. Ek saal baad woh usi bus stop par laut aaye jahan pehli baar mile the. Bench ko dobara paint kiya gaya tha aur footpath repair ho chuka tha. Shehar pehle ki tarah unke aas-paas chalta raha. Nisha ne jagah ko dekhkar hansi. “Us subah tumne mudkar dekha tak nahi.” “Sharm aa rahi thi.” “Tum impossible the.” “Pata hai.” Usne Arjun ka haath pakad liya. Arjun ne bhi uska haath thaam liya, us chhote se haath ko yaad karte hue jisne use girne se pehle sadak se wapas kheench liya tha. Bus aa gayi. Darwaaze khul gaye. Dono nahi hile. “Yahi bus leni hai?” usne poochha. Nisha ne apna sir uske kandhe par halka sa tika diya. “Abhi nahi.” Bus unhe wahin chhodkar chali gayi, aur kuch pal ke liye stop shaant ho gaya. Arjun kabhi sochta tha ki achhi zindagi woh hoti hai jo time par chale aur koi unexpected cheez na maange. Nisha ne use koi nayi personality nahi di thi. Usne bas use woh zindagi notice karna sikhaya jo pehle se uske aas-paas ho rahi thi—saath khana, sachchi maafi ke baad theek kiye gaye plans, do logon ke liye banayi chai, aur bina wajah bataye thaama gaya haath. Ghar ka raasta lamba tha, lekin ab woh dono saath chalte the. Woh ab sirf din kaat nahi raha tha. Woh sach mein jee raha tha.
