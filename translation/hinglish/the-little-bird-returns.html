Chapter One Gate Par Woh Phir Se

Ek saal guzar gaya.

Maine junior high khatam ki, entrance exams ko kisi tarah paar kiya, aur Seiran Academy ke high-school division mein aa gaya. Classrooms doosri building mein the, assignments zyada mushkil the, aur har koi future ke baare mein aise baat karta tha jaise woh ek train ho jise humein pakadna hi pade, warna woh humein chhodkar nikal jaayegi.

Main formulas seekhne aur chemistry samajhne ka naatak karne mein busy tha. Phir bhi, jab bhi station ke paas wali patli sadak se guzarta, mujhe woh ladki yaad aati jo kabhi ek kandhe par schoolbag latkaye mujhse aage chali thi.

Hina apne father ke transfer ki wajah se door chali gayi thi. Maine uske liye achha chaha tha. Sach mein chaha tha.

Lekin isse main kabhi-kabhi school gate ki taraf dekhna band nahi kar paaya.

Phir, August ki ek chipchipi subah, maine use dekha.

Summer break ke baad lautne wale students se neeche ka courtyard bhara hua tha. Entrance ke paas umbrellas se paani tapak raha tha, pedon mein cicadas shor kar rahe the, aur ek teacher sabko homeroom ki taraf bhejne ki koshish kar raha tha. Do chhote students ke beech ek pehchaani si shakhsiyat navy-blue bag ka strap theek kar rahi thi.

Hina.

Uske baal thode chhote ho gaye the. Woh thodi lambi bhi lag rahi thi. Ab woh junior high ke second year mein thi, jabki main usi campus ke doosre hisse mein high school ka first-year student ban chuka tha.

Mera phone lagbhag haath se gir gaya.

Woh wapas aa gayi.

Teen din tak maine use upper corridor se dekha aur usse baat karne ke liye kuch bhi nahi kiya. Maine khud se kaha ki ab school ki buildings alag hain. Maine kaha ki main ab bada ho gaya hoon aur mujhe usi tarah behave karna chahiye. Courtyard ko zaroorat se kahin zyada baar check karte hue maine khud ko kai convincing bahaane diye.

Chauthe din cafeteria ne mere saare bahaane khatam kar diye.

Main line mein khada phone scroll kar raha tha, tab ek pehchaani awaaz aayi, “Agar tum line ko sach mein dekho, toh yeh jaldi aage badhegi.”

Maine nazar uthayi.

Hina mere paas lunch box liye khadi thi. Uske chehre par wahi shaant shararat thi jo mujhe yaad thi.

“Tum…” maine kamaal ki baat kahi.

“Main,” usne kaha. “Hairaan ho?”

“Tum kab wapas aayi?”

“Pichhle hafte. Mujhe laga tum jaldi notice kar loge.”

“Maine pehle din hi notice kar liya tha.”

“Aur hello bolne mein chaar din laga diye?”

“Buildings alag hain.”

“Cafeteria toh nahi.”

Mere paas koi jawab nahi tha.

Usne bataya ki uske father ka transfer cancel ho gaya tha. Uska parivaar wapas is area mein aa gaya tha aur usne Seiran mein dobara admission le liya. Usne yeh baat itni saadgi se kahi, jaise mausam bata rahi ho.

“Tum wapas aayi, mujhe achha laga,” maine sochne se pehle hi keh diya.

Usne ek pal mujhe dekha. “Apne liye ya mere liye?”

“Umeed hai dono ke liye.”

Is baar woh sach mein hans padi.

Hum cafeteria ki khidkiyon ke paas baithkar khana khane lage. Usne apne lunch se tamagoyaki ka ek tukda mujhe diya, aur maine ek chhote se egg piece ke laayak se kahin zyada shukriya ke saath le liya. Hum teachers, summer homework aur us jagah lautne ke ajeeb ehsaas ke baare mein baat karte rahe jo tumhare bina bhi chalti rahi ho.

Kuch dramatic nahi hua.

Lekin jab hum cafeteria se nikle, campus ek saal baad phir se jaana-pehchaana laga.

Chapter Two Ek Routine Jiska Koi Plan Nahi Tha

Hamari nayi routine bina kisi meeting ya vaade ke ban gayi.

Lunch ke waqt main cafeteria jaata. Hina bhi. Kabhi line mein mulaqat ho jaati, kabhi woh apne friends ke saath table se mujhe bula leti. Main late hota toh woh zaroor kehti ki meri timing bekaar hai. Woh late hoti toh kehti use “junior-high ke zaroori kaam” ne rok liya tha.

Uski friend Yui sab kuch notice karti thi.

“Toh yeh tumhara senior hai?” usne ek dopahar mujhe aate dekhkar poochha.

“Haruto,” Hina ne sudhaara. “Aur haan, senior hai.”

“Bas senior?”

“Thoda ajeeb senior.”

Yui ne mujhe dekha. “Tum khaas lagte ho.”

“Yaqeen maano, main zyada tar aam hi hoon.”

“Ajeeb log bilkul yahi kehte hain,” Hina ne jawaab diya.

Mujhe shak hone laga ki Seiran lautne ke baad woh aur bhi khatarnaak ho gayi thi.

Ek baarish wale Thursday ko cafeteria bheed aur shor se bhar gaya. Main buildings ke beech covered walkway mein nikal aaya, earphones lagaye aur chhat ke kinaare jama hote paani ko dekhta raha.

Kisi ne mere kandhe par tap kiya.

Hina apna band umbrella pakde khadi thi.

“Kya sun rahe ho?”

“Aisi cheez jo logon ko ignore karne mein madad karti hai.”

“Toh kaafi achhi hogi.”

Woh mujhse thodi door sookhe ledge par baith gayi. Hum music, uski nayi class aur mere science stream chunne ke baare mein baat karne lage, jabki main bolkar samjhaane se zyada likhne mein achha tha.

“Tum achha likhte ho,” usne kaha.

Main uski taraf muda. “Tumhe kaise pata?”

“Tumhari notebook. Ek baar lunch ke waqt tum usmein likh rahe the.”

“Tumne padhi?”

“Bas woh line jo khuli hui thi. Main koi criminal nahi hoon.”

Main sharminda hokar hans diya. Woh khud se khush lag rahi thi.

Hawa ka ek jhonka baarish ko chhat ke neeche le aaya. Hina paani se bachne ke liye khiski aur uska haath mere haath se chhoo gaya. Hum dono ek pal ke liye nahi hile. Phir woh peeche hui, hamare haathon ko dekha aur boli, “Tumhari ungliyan thandi hain.”

“Baarish ho rahi hai.”

“Gloves laaya karo.”

“August chal raha hai.”

“Toh bahaane banana band karo.”

Woh phir baarish dekhne lagi, lekin uske kaanon ke sire gulaabi ho gaye the.

Maine yeh faisla nahi kiya ki iska koi matlab tha. Main seekh chuka tha ki umeed se bhara dimaag ek chhote se pal ko poora kalpanik future bana sakta hai.

Phir bhi, mujhe woh pal poori shaam yaad raha.

Chapter Three Uski Sketchbook Mein Little Bird

September tak hamari dosti itni normal ho gayi thi ki friends ab use notice na karne ka naatak nahi kar sakte the.

Daichi poochhne laga ki main har lunch break mein gayab kyun ho jaata hoon. Yui ne Hina se poochha ki woh lunch pack kyun karti hai jab aadha mujhe de deti hai. Hum dono ne bekaar ke bahaane diye.

Ek din main lunch lana bhool gaya. Hina ne bina koi drama kiye apna sandwich aadha kar diya. Agle din main uske liye ek extra canned coffee le aaya. Uske baad, jo kuch bhi hamare paas hota use share karna ek aisa rule ban gaya jise kisi ne likha nahi tha.

Ek hafte baad hum cafeteria ke baahar bench par baithe the aur Hina chhoti sketchbook mein drawing bana rahi thi. Woh aam taur par book ko mujhse door rakhti thi, isliye mujhe hairaani hui jab usne use meri taraf ghumaya.

Page par ek chidiya bani thi—chhoti, satark, ek shaakh par baithi hui. Neeche saaf handwriting mein likha tha: Little Bird.

Main use dekhta reh gaya.

“Tumhe woh nickname yaad hai?”

“Bilkul.”

“Mujhe laga tha tumhe bura laga hoga.”

“Mujhe laga tha ajeeb hai,” usne sar tedha kiya. “Lekin kabhi nafrat nahi hui.”

“Maine bas isliye naam rakha kyunki shuru mein mujhe tumhara naam nahi pata tha.”

“Mujhe pata hai. Daichi dheere bolne mein bahut kharaab hai.”

Maine ek haath se chehra dhak liya. “Mujhe usse serious baat karni padegi.”

“Please mat karna. Woh entertaining hai.”

Woh drawing par laut gayi aur chidiya ke pankhon par kuch bareek lines jod di.

“Tumne ise sambhaal kar kyun rakha?” maine poochha.

“Kyunki yeh mujhe yaad dilata hai ki humne baat karna kaise shuru kiya.” Woh ruki. “Aur kyunki yeh aise naam jaisa lagta hai jo koi tab deta hai jab woh kind banna chahta ho, lekin normal baat kehne se bahut nervous ho.”

Mujhe us sentence ka kya jawaab dena chahiye, samajh nahi aaya. Isliye maine phir sketch dekha.

“Tum yeh naam rakh sakte ho,” usne kaha. “Bas courtyard mein chillana mat.”

“Main khud ko control karne ki koshish karunga.”

“Achha. Main apni reputation khona nahi chahti.”

Us shaam usne mujhe apna number diya. Usne kaha ki agar koi zaroori baat ho toh message kar sakta hoon, phir kaha ki “bore ho raha hoon” bhi valid reason hai. Lekin raat ko uski mother phone check karti thi, isliye eleven ke baad message nahi bhejna tha.

Hamari pehli conversation ek seedhe se Hey se shuru hui.

Teen minute baad uska reply aaya: Likhta bhi itna boring ho kya?

Agle kuch hafton tak dinner aur bedtime ke beech ka chhota sa waqt hamare messages se bhar gaya. Hum school, songs, books aur classmates ki ajeeb baaton ke baare mein baat karte. 10:55 par Hina kehti ki ab jaana hai. 10:59 par ek aakhri message bhejti—kabhi joke, kabhi random sticker, kabhi bas Hmm.

Main us aakhri message ka intezaar zaroorat se kahin zyada karne laga.

Ek raat usne poochha, “Kabhi sochte ho agle saal hum kaise honge?”

Maine kai jawaab type kiye aur sab delete kar diye.

Aakhir mein likha, “Umeed hai hum aise hi baat karte rahenge.”

Typing indicator aaya, gayab hua, phir laut aaya.

10:59 par uska jawaab aaya.

Toh mujhse gayab mat ho jaana, Haruto.

Maine use do baar padha.

Chapter Four Cheer Karne Aaya Senior

Seiran ka sports festival chamakte banners, loudspeakers aur itne josh ke saath aaya ki poora campus kaanp raha tha.

High-school students stands ke ek section mein baithe har event ko barabar support karne ka naatak kar rahe the. Main yeh naatak tab bilkul achhe se nahi kar paaya jab Hina ka badminton match shuru hua.

Woh court par aise focus ke saath aayi jo main lunch ke waqt kam hi dekhta tha. Uske footwork tez the, returns precise the, aur chehra itna serious tha ki main lagbhag bhool gaya ki August mein gloves ko lekar mujhse behas karne wali bhi wahi thi.

Usne seedhe games mein match jeet liya.

Baad mein jab usne stands ki taraf dekha, maine thumbs-up diya. Usne apni water bottle utha kar chhota sa salute kiya, phir teammates ke chidhane se pehle mud gayi.

Phir bhi unhone use chheda.

Baad mein Yui ne bataya ki Hina baaki dopahar muskurati rahi. Maine suna hi nahi, aisa dikhaya.

Kuch din baad mujhe sardi ho gayi aur main lunch ke waqt high-school classroom mein hi raha. Maine Hina ko message kiya ki main theek hoon, zyada tar isliye kyunki mujhe pata tha woh poochhegi main kahaan hoon.

Paanch minute baad darwaaze par knock hua.

Woh corridor mein ek chhota thermos liye khadi thi.

“Meri mother ne extra soup banaya tha,” usne kaha. “Yeh koi special delivery nahi hai. Isse weird mat banana.”

“Main aisa karne wala nahi tha.”

“Tum aise hi lag rahe ho.”

Usne thermos meri desk par rakha aur jaane lagi. Uske neeche ek muda hua note tha.

Jaldi theek ho jao. Tumhare bina cafeteria boring hai.

Main itna muskuraya ki gaal dukhne lage.

October mein dono divisions ne school festival ki taiyaari mein madad ki. Main decorations committee ke liye posters bana raha tha; Hina supplies organise kar rahi thi aur kisi tarah sabko apne plan par chala leti thi. Classes ke baad hum ek hi hall mein kaam karte, jahan paper lanterns, rang-birangi strings aur fresh paint ki khushboo thi.

Ek teacher decorations ka box lekar hamare paas se guzri. Usne hum dono ko dekha aur khud hi muskura di.

Hina ne notice kar liya.

“Shuru mat hona,” usne mujhe warning di.

“Maine kuch kaha hi nahi.”

“Tum zor se sochte ho.”

“Aisi koi cheez nahi hoti.”

“Tum karte ho toh hoti hai.”

Hum hans diye aur teacher aage badh gayi.

Us dopahar mujhe ehsaas hua ki kuch badal gaya hai. Ab main har conversation ke baad yeh nahi sochta tha ki kahin galat toh nahi bol diya. Main bas Hina ke saath reh sakta tha, uski baatein sun sakta tha, aur din ko achha rehne de sakta tha bina use kisi aur cheez mein badalne ki zid kiye.

Festival ke baad hum saath gate tak chale. Shaam ki hawa thandi ho gayi thi. Hina ne bag se ek earbud nikala aur meri taraf badhaya.

“Share karoge?”

Maine apne earphones ka ek side use de diya. Hamare beech ek purana gaana bajne laga, campus se nikalte students ke shor ke neeche dheere se.

Hamare kandhe chhoo rahe the.

Hum dono mein se koi door nahi hua.

Chapter Five Hamari Tarah Ka Normal

December tak thand campus par chhaa gayi thi. Zelkova ke pedon se lagbhag saare patte gir chuke the, aur students coats mein scarves daba kar buildings ke beech jaldi-jaldi jaate.

Hina aur main ek-doosre ke din ka hissa ban gaye the. Mauka milta toh milte, nahi toh messages karte, aur woh chhoti baatein share karte jo kisi aur ko bekaar lagti.

Mujhe pata chala ki woh pareshan hoti toh draw karti, bahut meethi drinks pasand nahi karti, aur lunch ka aakhri bite hamesha baad ke liye bachati. Use pata chala ki main notes ke margins mein stories likhta, forecast mein baarish hone par bhi umbrella bhool jaata, aur kuch honest kehne ki koshish mein chup ho jaata.

Ek shaam hum school gate ke baahar khade the, jab aasman winter blue mein badal raha tha.

“Agle saal ko lekar tension hai?” usne poochha.

“Zyada exams ko lekar. Aur unke baad kya hoga.”

“Tum figure out kar loge.”

“Tum aise keh rahi ho jaise sure ho.”

“Main sure nahi hoon.” Usne apna scarf theek kiya. “Main yeh isliye keh rahi hoon kyunki mujhe lagta hai tum kar sakte ho.”

Main uske kehne ka exact tareeqa yaad rakhna chahta tha.

Phir usne mujhe ek muda hua kagaz diya.

“Ghar jaakar kholna,” usne kaha.

Andar sketchbook wali Little Bird ki drawing thi, ab nazuk pankhon aur patton se bhari shaakh ke saath poori ki hui. Neeche usne likha tha:

Jo Little Bird kabhi door lagti thi, use ek pehchaani shaakh mil gayi hai.

Maine use do baar padha, tab jaakar samjha ki main muskura raha tha.

Agle din maine use ek chhoti notebook di. Maine saadha blue cover chuna tha, kyunki mujhe pata tha woh us par bhi drawing bana degi.

“Tumhari sketches ke liye,” maine kaha.

Usne notebook ko haathon mein ghumaya. “Aur tum iska kya karoge?”

“Shayad likhne ke liye.”

“Toh hum pages compare kar sakte hain.”

“Challenge hai kya?”

“Obviously.”

Hum pehli baar bina yeh naatak kiye gate se saath nikle ki yeh bas coincidence tha. Hina meri left side chal rahi thi. Corner ke paas usne haath badhakar meri coat ki sleeve do ungliyon se pakad li.

Main ruk gaya. Woh bhi ruk gayi.

Ek pal ke liye laga woh kuch kehne wali hai. Iske bajaay woh muskurayi aur chhod diya.

Woh chhota sa touch tha, lekin station tak mere saath raha.

Kisi cheez ko formally naam nahi diya gaya tha. Hum abhi bhi students the—exams, families aur aise future ke saath jise hum predict nahi kar sakte the. Hina junior high ke second year mein thi; main high school ke first year mein. Hamare paas bade hone ka waqt tha, aur jis cheez ko hum abhi samajh nahi paaye the use jaldi karne ki zaroorat nahi thi.

Lekin hamare beech ki doori ab deewar jaisi nahi lagti thi.

Us raat 10:58 par mera phone roshan hua.

Good night, Haruto.

Doosra message aaya.

Aur kal umbrella mat bhoolna. Baarish hogi.

Main dheere se hans diya aur phone notebook ke paas rakh diya.

Little Bird laut aayi thi. Hamare aam dinon ne phir apni rhythm dhoondh li thi.

Aur shared lunches, baarish wale corridors aur thand mein ek earbud share karne ke beech, main samajhne laga tha ki relationship hamesha confession ke saath nahi aata.

Kabhi-kabhi woh chup-chaap badhta hai, jab tak do log khud ko ek-doosre ke saath ghar jaate hue nahi paate.

Filhaal, hamara normal aisa hi tha.

Mujhe abhi nahi pata tha ki aam din kitni jaldi important ban sakte hain. Agla school year ek alag sawaal lekar aane wala tha: kya Hina aur main ek-doosre ko phir se dhoondh sakte hain, yeh nahi, balki jo hamare beech badha tha uske baare mein sach bolne ki himmat hum mein hai ya nahi.
