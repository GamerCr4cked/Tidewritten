Chapter One The Wrong Flat

Aarav Mehta do suitcases, ek backpack aur ek plan ke saath B-204 pahuncha: Delhi mein apni pehli raat bina kuch kiye guzaarni hai.

Woh freelance designer tha. Kaam kabhi ek saath dher saara milta, kabhi bilkul nahi; paise apne hi unpredictable schedule par aate the. Apartment se uski bas teen expectations thi—ek desk, reliable Wi-Fi aur itni shanti ki woh araam se soch sake. Broker Pandey ne use yakeen dilaya tha ki flat ekdum perfect hai.

“Sir, tension ki koi baat nahi hai.”

Aarav ko usi waqt shak kar lena chahiye tha. Jo insaan poochhne se pehle hi kehta hai ki tension ki koi baat nahi, us par bharosa karna mushkil hota hai.

Usne darwaza khola, bags neeche rakhe aur bedroom mein ek pink suitcase dekha.

Tabhi main door khula.

Ek young woman groceries lekar andar aayi. Aarav ko dekhte hi woh ruk gayi.

“Tum kaun ho?”

“Main yahan rehta hoon.”

“Nahi, tum nahi rehte.”

Dono ne apne rental agreements compare kiye. Flat same, move-in date same, broker bhi same. Do security deposits ek hi dhokhebaaz deal mein gayab ho chuke the.

Owner phone nahi utha raha tha. Pandey ka phone switched off tha. Raat hone wali thi, aur dono ke paas hotel mein rukne ke liye extra paise nahi the.

Us woman ne apna naam Zara Khan bataya. Woh junior architect thi, shifting se thaki hui thi, aur uske ghar mein achanak aa gaye kisi anjaan bande ke saath achha banne ka uska bilkul mood nahi tha.

Aarav bhi kuch alag feel nahi kar raha tha.

Dono rooms, keys aur is baat par behas karte rahe ki pehle kaun aaya tha. Aakhir mein practical baat jeet gayi: owner se contact hone tak unhe flat share karna padega, aur har cheez ka record rakhna hoga.

Unhone ek paper par rules likhe aur fridge par chipka diye. Kisi ke room mein jaane se pehle knock karna. Jo cheez use karo, use replace karna. Bina poochhe kuch borrow nahi karna. Raat ke baarah baje ke baad work calls dheere rakhna.

Zara ne ek aur rule add kiya.

“Dost banne ka natak nahi karenge.”

“Easy,” Aarav ne kaha.

Yahi pehla promise tha jise nibhana dono ke liye mushkil hone wala tha.

Chapter Two Rules and Exceptions

Pehla hafta ghar ke kaamon ke zariye chalne wali ek cold war jaisa tha.

Aarav ne apni shelves label kar di. Zara ne apni. Dono baari-baari se kitchen saaf karte, jaise kisi border par diplomats negotiation kar rahe hon. Unki baat-cheet zyada tar groceries aur Wi-Fi router ki complaints tak hi limited thi.

Phir chhoti-chhoti galtiyan unke beech aane lagi.

Zara ek building model par der raat tak kaam karti rahi aur khana khana bhool gayi. Aarav ne uske darwaze ke bahar chawal aur sabzi ki plate rakh di. Phir message bheja ki extra khana waste ho raha tha. Agli subah Zara ne thank you kaha, lekin yeh nahi bataya ki usne ek-ek bite kha li thi.

Ek baar Aarav poori shaam ek ziddi design file theek karne mein laga raha. Zara ne raat ke do baje uske darwaze ke neeche se roshni aati dekhi aur chai ka mug lekar knock kiya.

“Solution nahi hai,” usne kaha. “Bas chai hai.”

“Chai aam taur par aise hi kaam karti hai.”

Zara lagbhag muskura di.

Dheere-dheere dono ek-doosre ki routine samajhne lage. Zara focus karte waqt halki awaaz mein gaati thi. Aarav client ke reply ka wait karte waqt room mein chakkar lagata tha. Zara ko windows khuli rakhna pasand tha; Aarav fan chalana chahta tha. Temperature ko lekar behas hoti, phir dono ko pata chala ki dinner ke baad woh zyada reasonable ho jaate hain.

Aakhir owner ka reply aaya. Usne broker ke fraud ki jaanch karne ka promise kiya. Paise wapas milne mein waqt lagne wala tha, aur filhaal dono turant shift afford nahi kar sakte the.

Tab tak flat pehle jitna hostile nahi raha tha.

Aarav ne Zara ka heavy model uske studio tak le jaane mein help ki. Zara ne Aarav ka portfolio review kiya aur bataya ki uske best designs woh the jo usne apne liye banaye the—un clients ke liye nahi jo har idea ko bas expensive dikhana chahte the.

“Tum achha kaam karte ho,” usne kaha. “Bas log tumse jo banwana chahte hain, uske peeche tum khud ko chhupa lete ho.”

Zara ke room se jaane ke baad bhi Aarav ko woh baat yaad rahi.

Woh ab bhi khud ko temporary roommates kehte the. Lekin jab Aarav ghar aaya aur Zara ko ek bekaar cooking video par haste dekha, toh uska mann hua ki woh bhi use apne din ke baare mein bataye.

Fridge par rules ab bhi chipke hue the.

Bas exceptions badhti ja rahi thi.

Chapter Three The Almost

Kuch mahine beet gaye. Aarav ka kaam stable hone laga aur Zara ko architecture firm mein zyada responsibilities milne lagi. Chhoti victories celebrate karne ke liye dono balcony mein takeaway food khate.

Aarav ne Zara ko presentation ki taiyaari karne mein help ki. Zara ne use ek difficult client contract negotiate karne mein help ki. Jab kisi ka din kharab hota, doosre ko pata hota ki advice deni hai, chai banani hai ya bas chup-chaap saath baithna hai.

Ek shaam power cut ho gaya aur poora flat bahar ke shehar ki roshni se jagmaga raha tha. Zara sofa par uske paas baithkar Aarav ke purane designs ka folder dekh rahi thi.

“Jab tum yeh sochkar cheezein banate the ki koi unhe khareedega ya nahi, usse pehle tum zyada khush lagte the,” usne kaha.

“Rent artistic fulfilment se pay nahi hota.”

“Nahi, lekin uske chakkar mein tumhe apni pasand ki har cheez bhi nahi khona chahiye.”

Aarav jawab dene ke liye muda. Zara uski expectation se zyada paas thi. Ek pal ke liye dono bilkul nahi hile. Unke beech ki khamoshi mein woh sab tha jis par unhone kabhi baat nahi ki thi.

Phir Zara khadi ho gayi.

“Mujhe apni presentation finish karni hai.”

“Haan, bilkul.”

Dono ne aise behave kiya jaise kuch hua hi nahi.

Ek hafte baad Zara apne confident colleague Dev ko date karne lagi. Dev ko pata tha ki room mein enter karte hi sabka attention kaise lena hai. Aarav ne supportive rehne ki koshish ki. Usne khud ko samjhaya ki disappointed feel karne ka uska koi haq nahi tha; usne Zara ko kabhi bataya hi nahi tha ki woh uske liye kya maayne rakhti hai.

Dev flat par aane laga. Logon ke saamne woh charming tha, lekin Aarav ko kuch chhoti baatein pareshan karti thi: Dev Zara ke kaam ko halka samajhta, uske waqt ko apni cheez ki tarah treat karta, aur Zara un baaton ke liye bhi sorry bolti jo uski galti nahi thi.

Aarav ne apni concerns apne tak rakhi. Woh jealous roommate nahi banna chahta tha jo apni feelings ko hi sachchai samajh baithe.

Jab Zara ne aakhir poochha ki Dev ke baare mein uska kya opinion hai, Aarav ne soch-samajhkar jawab diya.

“Mujhe lagta hai tum aise insaan ko deserve karti ho jisse disagree karne par tumhe guilty feel na karaya jaaye.”

Zara ne nazar hata li.

“Mujhe pata hai woh perfect nahi hai.”

“Main bhi nahi hoon. Baat woh nahi hai.”

Pehli baar Zara ko laga ki Aarav behas jeetne ki koshish nahi kar raha tha. Woh uski fikr kar raha tha.

Chapter Four The Other Guy

Sach ek Thursday raat saamne aaya.

Zara der se ghar lauti, bag zameen par chhod diya aur kitchen table par bina light jalaye baith gayi. Aarav paani lene aaya toh usne Zara ko wahan dekha.

Zara ko pata chala tha ki Dev kisi aur ko bhi date kar raha tha. Jab usne Dev se saamna karke poochha, toh Dev ne ulta use hi blame kiya—busy rehne ke liye, zyada kaam karne ke liye, use enough attention na dene ke liye. Dev ne sorry tab kaha jab Zara ne keh diya ki woh relationship khatam kar rahi hai.

Aarav ne uski baat suni. Woh kehna chahta tha ki use pehle se kuch galat lag raha tha. Lekin usne bas poochha ki Zara chahti hai woh paas rahe ya use akela chhod de.

“Ruko,” Zara ne kaha.

Aarav ne chai banayi. Kitchen ki ghadi aadhi raat paar kar gayi, tab tak Zara bolti rahi. Aarav ne apni feelings ka zikr nahi kiya—jab Zara khud hurt thi aur samajhne ki koshish kar rahi thi ki hua kya hai, tab nahi.

Agale kuch dino mein usne Zara ko Dev ka chhoda hua samaan wapas karne mein help ki aur yaad dilaya ki relationship khatam karna koi failure nahi hota. Zara dheere-dheere phir se apni jaisi lagne lagi. Woh zyada hansne lagi aur har kuch minute mein phone check kiye bina kaam karne lagi.

Phir ek shaam usne Aarav se poochha ki usne kabhi kisi ko date kyun nahi kiya.

“Pata nahi,” Aarav ne kaha. “Shayad main nahi chahta ki koi mujhe sirf isliye chune kyunki main convenient hoon.”

Zara use dekhti reh gayi.

“Kya tumhe lagta hai hamare saath bhi aisa hi hai? Ki hum bas ek-doosre ke liye convenient hain?”

“Nahi. Mujhe lagta hai saath rehne ki wajah se comfort ko kisi aur cheez samajhna aasaan ho gaya hai.”

“Aur tum kya feel karte ho?”

Aarav mazaak ke peeche chhup sakta tha. Lekin is baar usne sach bol diya: woh Zara ki care karta tha, lekin usse jawab ki umeed nahi thi. Woh nahi chahta tha ki Dev ke hurt karne ke baad Zara sirf isliye use choose kare.

Zara ki aankhon mein narmi aa gayi. “Mujhe samajhne ke liye thoda time chahiye ki main kya feel karti hoon.”

“Le lo.”

Pehli baar dono ne agla chapter jaldi shuru karne ki koshish nahi ki.

Chapter Five A Split in the Road

Waqt ke saath jawab aur saaf ho gaya—lekin woh nahi jiski Aarav ne chupke se umeed ki thi.

Zara ne use bataya ki woh uski bahut care karti hai. Flat uske liye ghar isliye bana kyunki Aarav wahan tha. Woh us par bharosa karti thi, uski izzat karti thi aur uske aas-paas safe feel karti thi. Lekin woh imaandari se nahi keh sakti thi ki woh Aarav se pyaar karti hai.

“Main jo hamare beech hai, use khona nahi chahti,” usne kaha. “Lekin jhooth bhi nahi bolna chahti.”

Aarav ko disappointment hui. Usne us feeling ko aane diya, lekin use saza dene ka tareeka nahi banaya.

“Toh phir hum pretend nahi karenge.”

Dono ne decide kiya ki lease khatam hote hi unmein se koi ek shift ho jayega. Isliye nahi ki kisi ne kuch galat kiya tha, balki isliye ki same arrangement mein rehkar alag zindagi banana mushkil hota ja raha tha.

November mein shifting hui. Zara ko apne office ke paas ek chhota apartment mil gaya. Aarav ne uski books aur plants pack karne mein help ki. Flat mein unka aakhri dinner zameen par hua, kyunki table pehle hi shift ho chuki thi.

Darwaze par Zara ne use hug kiya.

“Thank you, tumne kabhi mujhe aisa feel nahi karaya ki mujhe tumhara kuch chukana hai,” usne kaha.

Aarav ne woh baatein nigal li jo usne pehle se soch rakhi thi, aur imaandari se jawab diya.

“Thank you, tumne sach bataya.”

Dono dost rahe, halanki unki baat-cheet dheere-dheere kam ho gayi. Kuch waqt baad Zara ne phir dating shuru ki. Aarav ne apne kaam par focus kiya, portfolio strong banaya aur apna pehla bada branding award jeeta.

Kuch mahine baad Zara ka phone aaya. Woh abhi-abhi single hui thi aur soch rahi thi ki kya dono dobara koshish kar sakte hain. Aarav ne shaanti se uski baat suni. Use samajh aa gaya ki ab woh phir se us sawaal mein nahi lautna chahta ki shayad Zara kabhi use choose karegi. Aakhirkaar usne aisi zindagi bana li thi jise sahi mehsoos karne ke liye kisi aur ki approval ki zaroorat nahi thi.

“Main tumhari care karta hoon,” usne kaha, “lekin mujhe nahi lagta ki wapas wahi jaana mere liye sahi hoga.”

Phone par khamoshi chha gayi. Phir Zara ne kaha ki woh samajh gayi.

Call ke baad Aarav ne chai banayi aur balcony mein khada ho gaya. November ki dhoop uske naye desk aur shelf par rakhe framed award par pad rahi thi.

Woh akela tha, lekin ab akelapan use is baat ka saboot nahi lagta tha ki uski zindagi mein kuch kami hai.

Us flat ne unki zindagi ko pehle aur baad mein baant diya tha. Dhoke ne Zara ko sikhaya ki woh ab kya bardasht nahi karegi. Us adhoore rishte ne Aarav ko sikhaya ki kisi ki care karne ka matlab hamesha intezaar karte rehna nahi hota ki woh tumhe choose kare.

Use woh ending nahi mili jo kabhi woh chahta tha.

Lekin use kuch aisa mila jiski use zaroorat thi: khud woh.

Kuch log zindagi mein hamesha ke liye aate hain. Kuch humein samjhate hain ki hum khud kahan khade hain. Aur kabhi-kabhi sabse imaandaar ending badla lena ya dobara milna nahi hota—bas bina koi kadwahat saath liye aage badhne ka faisla hota hai.

Ek flat. Do log. Do alag futures.

Aur aakhirkaar, dono apni-apni zindagi ke malik.
