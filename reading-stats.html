Privacy and storage

Reading statistics are kept in this browser's local storage. They include total active reading time, daily time totals, completed chapter IDs and titles, and completed volume details. A chapter is recorded only after you reach its end and spend a minimum amount of active time in that chapter. The time threshold is estimated from its word count (about one-quarter of a 220-words-per-minute reading estimate), with a 15-second minimum and a 90-second cap. Simply scrolling to the end is not enough. A volume is complete only when every detected chapter meets this rule.

Time is counted only while this page is visible, focused, and you have interacted within the last 60 seconds. Chapter time is attributed to the chapter at the reader's current position. A page left idle does not keep adding time. Switching language editions uses the same canonical volume record. After this update, earlier chapter and volume completion totals are reset once because the previous rule could count fast-scrolling as reading; accumulated reading time and daily activity are preserved.
