PROJECT INFORMATION

About Tidewritten

Tidewritten is an independent light-novel reading project. Its Library brings stories together by series and volume, with available language editions presented through a shared reading experience.

Browse the Library ↗ Reading guide ↓ Editorial & credits ↓

A series-based Library

Related books are grouped into series. A standalone title opens directly, while a multi-volume series provides volume selection and navigation between available volumes.

A focused reading experience

Individual books use a long-form layout with adjustable text size, typography, line spacing, reading width, and display theme. Available language editions can be selected from the reader.

A distinct visual identity

The interface uses an aquatic cyan palette, restrained gradients, and clear typography. Decorative elements support navigation and reading rather than competing with the story.

Reading guide

  • Find a story: Start on Home for featured picks, or visit the Library to browse available titles by series.
  • Choose a volume: Series pages group related volumes. Use the volume selector and previous/next navigation where available to read in order.
  • Set your theme: Use the theme control in the top navigation to switch between dark and light appearance.
  • Customize the reader: On story pages, use Customize to adjust text size and available reading appearance settings. Use Translate to select an edition when one is available.
  • Return to a story: Home can show recently opened stories when reading history is available. This history is stored in the current browser, so it may not be available on another device or in a different browser.
  • Review story information: Stories may explore a range of themes. Check the opening and available story information if you want to understand a title's subject matter before reading.

Editorial principles

  • Stories are organized by series and volume; language editions remain attached to the corresponding work.
  • Story wording, chapter titles, character names, and authorial intent are protected during site maintenance.
  • Translations are presented as editions of their source work and should preserve meaning, sequence, and tone.
  • Interface copy is concise and informative so the catalogue and reading experience remain the focus.
  • Story-specific authorship and rights belong to the respective work and its credited author or rights holder. Maintaining the platform does not imply ownership of story content.

Tidewritten is an independent project. Story content should be understood according to the author and edition credited with each work.
