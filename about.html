A LITTLE ABOUT THIS PLACE

Welcome to Tidewritten.

A home for emotional stories, immersive light-novel editions, complicated friendships, quiet romance, and the moments that keep echoing after the final line.

📚

Stories first

Tidewritten is built around reading: follow a series, open a volume, and give each story room to breathe.

🌊

Why “Tidewritten”?

A tide comes and goes, but it leaves traces behind. The name reflects the feelings, memories, and small moments that stories leave with us.

✨

Different kinds of stories

Expect friendship, romance, growing up, distance, mystery, and speculative ideas. Each series has its own mood and reading journey.

Tidewritten is a creative reading project. Story characters and events may be fictional or adapted for storytelling; a story’s emotional truth does not mean every event is a real-life account.
