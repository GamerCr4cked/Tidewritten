PROJECT OVERVIEW

About Tidewritten.

Tidewritten is an independent light-novel reading project featuring original stories across romance, friendship, coming-of-age, contemporary drama, and speculative fiction.

Explore the Library ↗Reading Guide →
📚

Reading platform

The catalogue is organized by series and volume. Open a standalone title directly or select a volume from a multi-volume series.

🌊

Project name

The name combines the imagery of a tide with written stories, reflecting the project's aquatic visual identity and focus on long-form fiction.

✨

Story catalogue

The catalogue includes romance, friendship, coming-of-age, contemporary drama, mystery, and speculative fiction.

Tidewritten is a creative reading project. Story characters and events may be fictional or adapted for storytelling; a story’s emotional truth does not mean every event is a real-life account.
