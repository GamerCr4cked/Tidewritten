**Result:** The previously reported 298-versus-280 discrepancy is not, on its own, a translation-completeness defect. It reflects different paragraph segmentation. The reviewed narrative sequence is present in both editions. This audit did not perform a line-by-line professional copy-edit of every translated sentence; any future language polishing should be a separate, explicitly scoped task.
