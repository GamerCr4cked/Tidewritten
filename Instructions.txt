DESIGN AND EDITORIAL IDENTITY
- Tidewritten is a polished, professional, cyan/aquatic light-novel reading website.
- Keep the interface concise, informative, welcoming, and uncluttered.
- Avoid generic dramatic/sentimental marketing slogans, vague promotional claims, filler, forced poetic expressions, and repetitive AI-sounding copy.
- This interface-copy rule does not apply to story prose or author-written content.
- Preserve the Japanese light-novel-inspired long-form reading experience. No chat bubbles or unnecessary character cards.
- Use existing design tokens and conventions in assets/css/style.css and _layouts/light-novel.html. See Checks/Notes.md for exact tokens, typography, and reader design details.

SITE STRUCTURE
- The catalogue is named Library, not Stories.
- Library cards represent canonical series, not translations or individual volume entries.
- Multi-volume series use a volume chooser; single-volume series open directly.
- Use Volume, not Season.
- Keep canonical /book/... routes and previous/next volume navigation valid.
- /about/ is the consolidated About page. /creator/, /reading-guide/, and /editorial/ remain legacy redirects to /about/.
- index.html and index.md have separate required roles: index.html redirects / to /home/; index.md generates /home/. Do not remove either without proving a replacement.
- Keep CNAME, _config.yml, icon.png, 404.html, coming-soon.html, active layouts, assets, stories, and translation folders unless the owner explicitly requests a change and dependencies are verified.

STORY AND TRANSLATION PROTECTION
Unless explicitly requested, never change story wording, punctuation, names, original titles, chapter titles, numbering, order, IDs, anchors, scenes, dialogue, plot, or authorial intent.
Preserve paragraph structure, scene breaks, emphasis, links, navigation, and end matter. Use semantic <p> elements; do not nest paragraphs or put block elements inside them.
- Hinglish: Latin script, modern natural conversational Hindi-English; avoid textbook/formal or awkward literal phrasing.
- Banglish: Bangla in Latin script, modern natural conversational Banglish; keep it distinct from Hinglish.
- Translation must preserve source meaning, tone, dialogue turns, and scene sequence; do not summarize or omit.
- For substantial translations use two-chapter batches, compare with the matching English source, track progress, and separately check prologues/interludes/epilogues/afterwords.
- Keep original chapter titles and character names.
- Use translation/hinglish/ and translation/banglish/ and the established series-volume filename conventions documented in Checks/Notes.md.
- English story URL remains canonical during in-place translation switching. Preserve chapter and scroll position, reader settings, and per-volume language preference. Only offer editions that exist.

READER AND LIBRARY
Preserve the shared reader controls and behavior: dark/light theme, in-place Translate, reading progress, saved position/resume, text sizing, appearance customization, edge fades, and applicable previous/next navigation.
Reader controls must not obstruct story text. Check desktop/mobile, both themes, keyboard/touch operation, focus visibility, reduced motion, and accessibility where relevant.
Library metadata derives from canonical English pages; available edition choices derive from actual translation mappings. Never create duplicate translation cards.
Use only approved genre vocabulary and explicit publication status. Do not invent content_updated dates; keep Recently updated unavailable until trustworthy dates exist.
Reading statistics are browser-local; do not describe them as account-synced or cross-device.

COVERS AND OPTIONAL PRESENTATION
- 17 canonical volumes currently have WebP covers in assets/images/covers/.
- Use portrait 2:3 covers, stable lowercase kebab-case names, root-relative paths, and matching edition metadata.
- Keep the same canonical cover path across language editions; localize alt text where possible.
- Keep semantic title/volume text outside artwork. Do not hotlink cover assets.
- Missing optional metadata/assets must fall back gracefully to CSS-built series art or text-only introduction.
- Author notes and illustrations are optional; never invent them. Validate illustration paths and chapter IDs.
- Full standards and metadata schema are in Checks/Notes.md.

GITHUB WORKFLOW
- Use latest main as base unless the owner specifies otherwise.
- Create a dedicated branch for substantial work.
- Fetch current file content and SHA before editing; re-fetch before sequential updates.
- Review the full diff for unintended changes and verify moved files and references.
- Run relevant checks/builds and inspect actual GitHub Actions results.
- Commit with a clear message and open a PR for every substantial change.
- After reviewing the complete diff and confirming the relevant automated checks/build/workflow pass, merge the PR without waiting for a separate owner approval. The owner has explicitly delegated routine PR merging as the default workflow.
- Do not pause for approval merely to merge a routine, scoped, verified PR. Pause only when a genuinely destructive or irreversible action is involved, requirements are materially ambiguous, a required check fails, or a security/privacy/legal risk needs owner direction.
- Distinguish commit, PR, merge, successful build, deployment, owner-reported browser QA, and independently performed browser QA.
