PROJECT INFORMATION

Editorial & Credits

This page records who maintains the Tidewritten platform, the principles used to present its catalogue, and the external services acknowledged by the project.

Project creator & maintainer

GamerCr4cked is the creator and maintainer of the Tidewritten website and its public repository.

Tidewritten source repository ↗

Editorial principles

  • Stories are organized by series and volume; language editions remain attached to the corresponding work.
  • Story wording, chapter titles, character names, and authorial intent are protected during site maintenance.
  • Translations are presented as editions of their source work and should preserve meaning, sequence, and tone.
  • Interface copy is concise and informative so the catalogue and reading experience remain the focus.
  • Story-specific authorship and rights belong to the respective work and its credited author or rights holder. Platform maintenance does not imply ownership of story content.

Platform acknowledgements

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting and publication.
  • Jekyll — static-site generation.
  • Google Fonts — hosted typefaces used by the site's interface and reader.

Additional work-specific acknowledgements should be shown with that work when its source information is available.
